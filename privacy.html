Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Parslia Kitchen OS (“Parslia”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at parslia.app, our web app, and our iOS / Android apps.

Contact for privacy requests: hello@parslia.app.

1. Information we collect

  • Account data — name, email, company/kitchen name, role, and login credentials.
  • Kitchen operational data — recipes, menus, allergens, stock, suppliers, rota, logs, labels, and related kitchen records you enter.
  • Support messages — content you send via early-access or contact forms.
  • Device & usage data — app version, device type, approximate region, crash/diagnostic events, and basic analytics needed to keep the service reliable.
  • AI feature inputs — prompts, voice queries (for AI Voice Finder), and images you choose to submit (for AI Image). Microphone audio is processed only when you use Voice Finder.

2. How we use information

  • Provide and improve Parslia Kitchen OS features (recipes, menus, allergens, stock, rota, logs, AI Image, AI Voice Finder).
  • Authenticate users, secure accounts, and prevent abuse.
  • Respond to support and early-access requests.
  • Meet legal, safety, and compliance obligations where required.
  • Send service notices (for example, security or material product updates). Marketing emails are optional and can be declined.

3. AI providers

AI Image and AI Voice Finder may send the content you submit to third-party AI providers (for example OpenAI or similar) solely to generate the requested result. Do not submit sensitive personal data in AI prompts unless necessary for your kitchen workflow. We do not sell AI inputs or kitchen data.

4. Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Infrastructure and hosting providers that process data on our behalf.
  • AI providers when you use AI features, as described above.
  • Authorities when legally required.

We do not sell personal data.

5. Cookies and similar tech

The marketing site and apps may use essential cookies or local storage for login sessions and basic preferences. Analytics, if enabled, is used to understand product usage and improve reliability.

6. Retention

We keep account and kitchen data while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterward for backups, disputes, and legal requirements. You may request deletion (see below).

7. Your rights & deletion

Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, export, or deletion of your personal data. Email hello@parslia.app with the subject “Privacy request”. We will verify the request and respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

Parslia is a B2B kitchen operations product for professional use. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (HTTPS, access controls, and least-privilege practices). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a strong unique password.

10. International transfers

Data may be processed in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area, United States, or other regions where our providers operate. We take steps appropriate to the transfer mechanism required.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as Parslia evolves. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Material changes will be highlighted on this page or by email when appropriate.
