Parslia Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Who we are

Parslia provides kitchen planning, recipe, menu, allergen and operational tools on the web, Microsoft Store and Apple App Store. Privacy enquiries: hello@parslia.app.

Information we process

  • Account information such as name, email address and authentication records.
  • Kitchen workspace content, including recipes, menus, notes, photographs and files you choose to upload.
  • Subscription status and transaction identifiers supplied by the storefront you used. Apple processes App Store payments. Web checkout is processed by Hercules Commerce. Microsoft Store listing distribution is handled by Microsoft. Parslia does not receive full payment-card details from Apple or Microsoft.
  • Device, security, diagnostic and usage information needed to operate and protect the service, including Windows, web and iOS clients.
  • Microphone or camera content only when you deliberately use a voice or image feature and grant permission on that device.

Purposes and legal bases

We process information to provide the contracted service, verify subscription access, support customers, maintain security, comply with law and improve reliability. Where consent is required, you may withdraw it through device settings or by contacting us.

Service providers

Information may be processed by Apple for iOS subscriptions, Microsoft for Microsoft Store distribution, hosting and communications suppliers, Hercules Commerce for web checkout, and AI providers when you deliberately use an AI feature. We require appropriate contractual and security protections and do not sell personal information.

Live generative AI

AI Image and AI Voice Finder send the content you submit for that feature to an AI provider so the feature can run. Outputs can be incomplete or inaccurate. You can report inappropriate AI content by emailing hello@parslia.app with “Report AI content” in the subject. We review reports and take action where needed.

Customer content

You retain ownership of content you upload. You give Parslia the limited permission needed to host, process, back up and display it to provide the service. You are responsible for having permission to upload it.

Retention, controls and rights

We retain information only as long as needed for the service, security, legal obligations and dispute handling. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children, security and changes

Parslia is a professional kitchen service and is not directed to children. We use proportionate safeguards but no system is risk-free. Material changes will be posted here.
