Aam Papad Challi
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

A zesty fusion of tangy aam papad and crunchy nuts, delivering a burst of sweet and sour flavors in every delightful bite.

Cuisine: Indian  Course: Snacks and Starters  Prep: 0-5 minutes  Cook: 11-15 minutes  Serves: 4  Taste: Sweet & Sour  Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients
• Few pieces regular aam papad
• Few pieces kala aam papad
• 1 corn on cob
• Oil for drizzling
• Butter for brushing
• 1 teaspoon chaat masala + for sprinkling
• 2 tablespoons lemon juice

Method
1. Peel corn on cob and roast directly on high flame.
2. Heat a non-stick grill pan.
3. Cut roasted corn on cob into 3 portions, halve and place on the hot pan.
4. Drizzle some oil and toss well.
5. Brush the corn pieces with some butter, add red chilli powder, black salt, chaat masala and lemon juice.
6. Top with regular and kala aam papad pieces and cook till soft.
7. Transfer into a serving bowl, sprinkle chaat masala and serve immediately.

Almond Milk Smoothie
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Enriched with almonds and garnished with sabza and blueberries, this smoothie is a power house of essential nutrients.

Cuisine: Fusion  Course: Beverages  Prep: 6-7 hour  Cook: 0-5 minutes  Serves: 4  Taste: Sweet  Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients
• 1 cup almonds, soaked overnight and drained
• 2 medium ripe banana
• 1 cup fresh blueberries + for garnish
• 1 teaspoon vanilla essence
• 3 tablespoons honey
• Fresh mint sprigs for garnish
• 2 tablespoons basil seeds (sabza), soaked

Method
1. Put soaked almonds in a grinder jar, add 2 cups warm water and grind till mixture is smooth.
2. Line a strainer kept on a bowl with muslin cloth, pour the ground almond mixture into it and strain the almond milk into the bowl.
3. Grind the residue again with 1 cup warm water to get more almond milk and strain this into the same bowl.
4. Peel the bananas, break into half and put in the grinder jar.
5. Add blueberries, vanilla essence, honey, almond milk and grind to a smooth mixture.
6. Skew blueberries and a mint sprig on each skewer.
7. Put some basil seeds in each serving glass and pour the smoothie over them.
8. Place one skewer on each glass and serve immediately.

Aloo Masala Cheese Toast
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Here, we’re giving a delicious makeover to leftover aloo sabzi with our easy recipe for 'Aloo Masala Cheese Toast.' So, follow our step-by-step guide to easily recreate it and enjoy a delightful snack.

Cuisine: Fusion  Course: Snacks and Starters  Cook: 6-8 minutes  Serves: 4  Taste: Mild  Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients
• 1½ cups leftover aloo sabzi
• Grated mixed cheese to sprinkle
• 4 white bread slices
• 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
• 1½ tablespoons tomato ketchup
• 2 teaspoons chopped fresh parsley + to sprinkle
• Black sesame seeds to sprinkle

Method
1. Take leftover aloo sabzi in a bowl.
2. Add mayonnaise, tomato ketchup and parsley and mix well.
3. Preheat the oven at 180°C.
4. Arrange the bread slices on baking tray and spread the potato mixture evenly.
5. Sprinkle mixed cheese and bake in preheated oven for 6-8 minutes.
6. Sprinkle black sesame seeds and parsley.
7. Cut into halves and serve.

Aloo Muri
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Satisfy your snack cravings with 'Aloo Muri', a beloved street-side delight from Meghalaya. This irresistible recipe features crispy puffed rice, spiced mashed potatoes and a special homemade masala.

Cuisine: Meghalaya  Course: Snacks and Starters  Cook: 6-8 minutes  Serves: 4  Taste: Tangy  Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients
• 3 medium potatoes (aloo), boiled and peeled
• 4 cups puffed rice (muri)
• 2 tablespoons coriander seeds
• 1 tablespoon cumin seeds
• 1 medium onion, chopped
• 2 green chillies, chopped
• 3 tablespoons mustard oil
• 3 tablespoons chopped fresh coriander leaves + to sprinkle
• ¼ cup aloo bhujiya + to sprinkle
• ½ lemon

Method
1. Heat a nonstick pan.
2. Add coriander seeds, cumin seeds and sauté for 2-3 minutes.
3. Transfer in a mortar and crush to fine powder with a mortar.
4. Take a bowl, cut the potatoes into medium sized pieces and add to the bowl.
5. Add onion, green chillies, puffed rice, 2-3 tbsps ground spice mix, tamarind water, mustard oil and mix well.
6. Add coriander and aloo bhujiya and mix well.
7. Squeeze lemon and mix.
8. Transfer in serving bowls, sprinkle coriander, aloo bhujiya and serve immediately.

Anjeer Toffee
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

A healthy sweet made without the addition of sugar – ideal for growing children.

Cuisine: Indian  Course: Mithais  Prep: 3-4 hour  Cook: 26-30 minutes  Serves: 4  Taste: Sweet  Difficulty: Moderate

Ingredients
• 5 dried figs (anjeer), soaked and drained
• 1 tablespoon ghee + for greasing
• 1 cup seedless dates (khajur), chopped
• 5 walnuts
• 2 tablespoons cashewnuts, chopped
• 2 tablespoons pistachios, chopped
• 2 tablespoons almonds, chopped
• ½ cup gum resin (gond), fried

Method
1. Put the figs in a grinder jar, add a little water and grind to fine paste.
2. Heat ghee in a deep non-stick pan and add fig paste and sauté on medium heat for 3-4 minutes.
3. Add dates, mix well and cook for 10-15 minutes or till excess moisture dries up.
4. Chop walnuts and add to the pan along with cashewnuts, pistachios and almonds.
5. Mix well and cook till mixture begins to leave the sides of the pan.
6. Add gond and mix well.
7. Cook for 3-4 minutes on medium heat.
8. Set aside in a bowl and allow to cool.
9. Refrigerate for 30 minutes.
10. Grease your palms with ghee and shape the mixture into oblong or any other desired shape.
11. Take strips of parchment paper and place a toffee on each paper and roll them.
12. Store the toffees in a glass jar or serve.

Palak Paneer
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Cottage cheese cooked in spinach-based gravy and fresh Indian spices is a household staple. Master the art of making this simple, wholesome and nutritious veg dish by following this recipe.

Cuisine: Punjabi  Course: Main Course-Veg  Prep: 11-15 minutes  Cook: 11-15 minutes  Serves: 4  Taste: Mild  Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients
• 250 grams paneer (cottage cheese)
• 2 medium bunches of spinach
• 1 large onion
• 1 green chilli
• 1 tablespoon chopped garlic
• 2 tablespoons oil
• 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
• 4 teaspoons garam masala
• 2 tablespoons fresh cream + for garnish
• A large pinch of dried fenugreek leaves (kasuri methi) powder
• Parantha to serve

Method
1. Finely chop onion and cut paneer into 1-inch cubes.
2. Boil sufficient water in a deep pan.
3. Add spinach leaves and blanch for 1 minute.
4. Drain into iced cold water.
5. Drain the spinach leaves and squeeze out excess water and put into a mixer jar.
6. Add green chilli and 1/2 cup water and grind to a fine paste.
7. Heat oil in a non stick pan, add cumin seeds and let them change colour.
8. Add the chopped onions and sauté till translucent.
9. Add garlic, mix and cook for a minute.
10. Add the ground spinach paste, mix and cook for 1-2 minutes.
11. Add salt, garam masala powder, paneer cubes and fresh cream and gently mix.
12. Cook for 1 minute.
13. Add dried fenugreek leaves powder, mix and cook for 1 minute.
14. Take the pan off the heat.
15. Garnish with fresh cream and serve hot with parantha.

Paneer Matar Makhana
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

This vegetarian main course recipe is made with cottage cheese, green peas and lotus seeds cooked in a rich velvety gravy. You can enjoy this with hot paranthas, chapatis or rotis.

Cuisine: Indian  Course: Main  Prep: 11-15 minutes  Cook: 26-30 minutes  Serves: 4  Taste: Mild  Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients
• 250 grams paneer (cottage cheese), cut into small cubes
• ¼ cup green peas, boiled
• 2 cups lotus seeds
• 5 tablespoons ghee
• 1 onion
• 1 tomato
• 1 green chilli
• 1 inch ginger, roughly chopped
• 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
• 1 teaspoon red chilli powder
• 1 tablespoon coriander powder
• 1 teaspoon cumin powder
• ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
• ¼ teaspoon garam masala powder
• ¼ teaspoon dried fenugreek leaves (kasoori methi) powder
• 2 tablespoons fresh cream
• 1 tablespoon honey
• Fresh coriander sprigs for garnish

Method
1. Heat 1 tsp ghee in a non-stick pan, add lotus seeds and saute on low heat for 5-6 minutes or till crisp.
2. Transfer onto a plate and set aside.
3. Heat 2 tsps ghee in the same pan, add onion and saute till golden brown.
4. Add tomato, green chilli and ginger and saute well.
5. Add salt and mix well.
6. Cover and cook for 5-6 minutes.
7. Set aside to cool slightly.
8. Transfer the mixture into a blender jar and add ¼ cup water and blend to a smooth paste.
9. Heat remaining ghee in the same pan, add cumin seeds and sauté till their colour changes.
10. Add the ground paste, mix well, cover and cook for 5-6 minutes.
11. Add red chilli powder, coriander powder, cumin powder and turmeric powder, mix well and saute for a few seconds.
12. Add ¾ cup water, mix, and cook for a minute.
13. Add green peas, cottage cheese and the roasted lotus seeds and mix well.
14. Sprinkle garam masala powder, dried fenugreek leaves powder and fresh cream and mix well.
15. Add honey and mix and take the pan off the heat.
16. Transfer into a serving bowl, garnish with coriander sprigs and serve hot with paranthe and lemon wedges.
