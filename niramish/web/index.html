What stays

Milk, cheese, cream, butter, ghee, yogurt, curd, buttermilk and paneer.

What never goes in

  • Onion, garlic, chives, spring onion, green onion, leeks, shallots, scallions and the rest of that family
  • Meat, poultry, fish and seafood
  • Eggs and mayonnaise
  • Honey, gelatin, lard and other animal products that are not milk or cheese

How the database is built

Each photo is read, forbidden foods are swapped, quantities are nudged, and the method is rephrased. You confirm the house version before it is saved.
