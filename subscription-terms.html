Parslia Subscription Terms

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Plans and prices

Parslia offers paid kitchen plans. Prices shown at checkout control. Typical UK list prices are:

  • Starter Monthly: £39 per month.
  • Starter Annual: £390 per year.
  • Professional Monthly: £79 per month.
  • Professional Annual: £790 per year.
  • Business Monthly: £149 per month.
  • Enterprise: from £299 per month, arranged through contact sales rather than an in-app subscription.
  • AI Image Booster: £9.99 per month, adding 50 AI recipe images to an active paid plan.

Apple purchases are charged at the price shown in the customer’s App Store storefront. Web purchases are processed by Hercules Commerce, and any applicable tax is shown during checkout. Microsoft Store users may pay through Microsoft billing or the same secure web checkout, as shown in the product at the time of purchase.

14-day introductory trial

Eligible new customers receive 14 days without charge on a core Parslia plan. On iOS, eligibility is determined by Apple and normally applies once per subscription group. On web and Windows, eligibility is shown at checkout. The AI Image Booster does not include a separate trial. When a trial ends, the storefront charges the displayed price unless the subscription is cancelled in time.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically for the same duration and at the displayed price unless cancelled before renewal. Manage or cancel in the storefront you used. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Access continues until the entitlement expires; paid features lock when the storefront no longer reports an active entitlement.

  • Apple: cancel in Apple Account subscription settings at least 24 hours before renewal.
  • Web: follow the Hercules Commerce receipt or email hello@parslia.app.
  • Microsoft: use Microsoft account subscription settings when Microsoft billed the purchase.

Changes, upgrades and billing issues

The billing provider for your storefront handles payment, upgrade and downgrade timing, billing recovery, refunds and price-consent requirements. Business takes precedence over Professional, and Professional takes precedence over Starter, while the higher entitlement is active. The AI Image Booster is a separate add-on and does not grant access to a core plan.
