Parslia Subscription Terms

Last updated: 12 August 2026

Plans and prices

  • Starter Monthly: £39 per month.
  • Starter Annual: £390 per year.
  • Professional Monthly: £79 per month.
  • Professional Annual: £790 per year.
  • Business Monthly: £149 per month.
  • Enterprise: from £299 per month, arranged through contact sales rather than an in-app subscription.
  • AI Image Booster: £9.99 per month, adding 50 AI recipe images to an active paid plan.

Apple purchases are charged at the price shown in the customer’s App Store storefront. Web purchases are processed by Hercules Commerce, and any applicable tax is shown during checkout.

14-day introductory trial

Eligible new customers receive 14 days without charge on a core Parslia plan. Eligibility is determined by Apple and normally applies once per subscription group. The AI Image Booster does not include a separate trial. When a trial ends, Apple charges the full displayed price for the chosen period unless the subscription is cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically for the same duration and at the price Apple displays unless cancelled at least 24 hours before renewal. Manage or cancel in Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Access continues until the entitlement expires; paid features lock when Apple no longer reports a verified active entitlement.

Changes, upgrades and billing issues

Apple handles payment, upgrade and downgrade timing, billing recovery, refunds and price-consent requirements. Business takes precedence over Professional, and Professional takes precedence over Starter, while the higher entitlement is active. The AI Image Booster is a separate add-on and does not grant access to a core plan. Restoring purchases re-checks the Apple Account’s entitlements.
