Parslia Subscription Terms

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Plans and prices

  • Starter Monthly: £29 per month.
  • Starter Annual: £290 per year.
  • Pro Monthly: £59 per month.
  • Pro Annual: £590 per year.

14-day introductory trial

Eligible new customers receive 14 days without charge. Eligibility is determined by Apple and normally applies once per subscription group. When the trial ends, Apple charges the full displayed price for the chosen period unless the subscription is cancelled at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically for the same duration and at the price Apple displays unless cancelled at least 24 hours before renewal. Manage or cancel in Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Access continues until the entitlement expires; paid features lock when Apple no longer reports a verified active entitlement.

Changes, upgrades and billing issues

Apple handles payment, upgrade and downgrade timing, billing recovery, refunds and price-consent requirements. A Pro entitlement takes precedence over Starter while active. Restoring purchases re-checks the Apple Account’s entitlements.
