Parslia Support

For account, subscription, accessibility, privacy or technical help, email hello@parslia.app. Include the device, operating system or browser, and a description of the problem. Never email passwords or full payment details.

Windows and web

Open the kitchen workspace from parslia.app/app.html or the Microsoft Store listing. If the workspace asks you to sign in, use your Parslia account. Microsoft Store reviewers should use the demo account supplied in Notes for certification.

Subscriptions

Restore or manage purchases in the storefront where you subscribed.

  • Apple: use Restore Purchases in the iOS app, then Manage Subscriptions. Apple handles App Store billing and refund requests.
  • Web: manage or cancel through the Hercules Commerce checkout receipt or by emailing support.
  • Microsoft Store: use your Microsoft account subscription settings if the purchase was made through Microsoft billing, or the same web checkout path if the Windows app uses Hercules Commerce.

Report AI content

AI Image and AI Voice Finder create live generative AI output. To report inappropriate, harmful or unexpected AI content, email hello@parslia.app with “Report AI content” in the subject and a short description. We aim to acknowledge support within two business days.

Privacy requests

Email the same address with “Privacy request” in the subject for access, correction, export or deletion requests. We handle privacy requests within applicable legal time limits.
