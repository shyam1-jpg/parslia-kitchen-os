Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026 · Effective for Parslia Kitchen OS (web and iOS)

Parslia (“we”, “us”) provides Parslia Kitchen OS, professional kitchen software for recipes, menus, allergens, stock, rota, logs, labels, and AI-assisted features (including AI Image and AI Voice Finder). This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. Contact: hello@parslia.app.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data — name, email, organisation/kitchen name, role, authentication credentials or tokens.
  • Kitchen operational data — recipes, menus, allergen notes, stock/supplier records, rota, compliance logs, labels, and related files you upload.
  • AI feature data — prompts, voice audio (for AI Voice Finder), images or references you submit for AI Image, and generated outputs needed to deliver the feature.
  • Device & usage data — app version, device type, approximate diagnostics, crash/error logs, and basic analytics needed to keep the service reliable.
  • Payment data — if you purchase a plan, billing details are processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers on Parslia servers.
  • Support messages — content you send to hello@parslia.app or in-product support forms.

2. How we use data

  • Provide, secure, and improve Parslia Kitchen OS.
  • Run AI Image and AI Voice Finder using third-party AI processors when you use those features.
  • Authenticate users, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.
  • Send service and account emails (and marketing only if you opt in).
  • Meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

3. AI providers

When you use AI Image or AI Voice Finder, relevant prompts, audio, or images may be sent to AI service providers (for example OpenAI or other configured model providers) solely to generate the requested output. Do not submit sensitive personal data in AI prompts unless necessary for your kitchen workflow. We do not sell your kitchen data or AI prompts.

4. Microphone, camera, and photos (iOS)

  • Microphone — used only for AI Voice Finder when you choose to speak a search or command.
  • Camera / photo library — used only if you choose to capture or upload images for kitchen or AI Image workflows.

You can deny these permissions in iOS Settings; core non-AI kitchen features continue to work where possible.

5. Cookies and similar technology

We use essential cookies/local storage for sign-in, session security, and preferences. We may use limited analytics cookies or equivalents to understand product usage. You can control cookies via your browser or device settings.

6. Sharing

We share data only with:

  • Hosting, auth, database, email, payments, and AI processors that help run the product under contract.
  • Your organisation’s authorised users (data you put in a shared kitchen workspace).
  • Authorities when required by law.

We do not sell personal data.

7. Retention

We keep account and kitchen data while your organisation uses Parslia and for a reasonable period afterward for backups, disputes, and legal requirements. You may request deletion (see below). AI feature inputs/outputs may be retained briefly for delivery, abuse prevention, and quality, subject to provider policies.

8. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (HTTPS, access controls, and hosting security practices). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use strong passwords and protect device access in kitchen environments.

9. International transfers

Data may be processed in the UK, EEA, US, or other countries where our providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate transfer safeguards.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete personal data, and to object to certain processing. Email hello@parslia.app with the subject “Privacy request”. We may need to verify your identity and kitchen affiliation before acting.

11. Children

Parslia Kitchen OS is a business/kitchen product and is not directed at children under 16.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The “Last updated” date will change, and material changes may be announced in-app or by email when appropriate.
