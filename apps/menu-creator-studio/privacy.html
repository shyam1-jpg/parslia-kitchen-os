Menu Creator Studio privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026. This policy covers the standalone Menu Creator Studio app for iPhone and iPad, not Parslia Kitchen OS.

Who is responsible

The publisher is Shyam Prasad, trading as Parslia. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@parslia.app with “Menu Creator Studio” in the subject.

Your menus and PDFs

Menu titles, text and selected print styles are stored on your device. The app does not require an account and does not upload your menu content to a Parslia server. PDF previews and exports are created on your device. Device backups may include app data, depending on your Apple settings. When you share a PDF, the destination you choose receives that copy and its own privacy policy applies.

You can delete drafts from the menu library. Deleting a draft does not remove PDFs you already exported, shared copies or backups. Use your device and backup settings to manage those copies.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional one-time Menu Creator Plus purchase. The app checks Apple-verified purchase information to enable ad removal and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple keeps transaction records under its own policy. Use Restore purchases in the editor with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Refund requests can be made at reportaproblem.apple.com; eligibility depends on the transaction and applicable consumer rights.

Advertising and consent

The free editor uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. The app requests non-personalised ads, disables Google's publisher first-party ID, and does not request access to Apple's advertising identifier through App Tracking Transparency. Non-personalised ads still involve data processing.

Google's advertising SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, app- or device-scoped identifiers, information about ads displayed and interactions, app interaction information, crash reports and performance diagnostics. These support ad delivery, measurement, security, fraud prevention, analytics and service improvement. The app does not pass your menu text to Google and does not request location permission.

Where required, a consent message explains advertising choices before ads are requested. When available, use “Advertising privacy choices” in the editor to revisit your choices. Withdrawing consent affects future processing; it does not erase records already lawfully processed. Menu editing and PDF export remain available when advertising is unavailable. Plus removes ad requests while its entitlement is active; it does not erase previously collected advertising data.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps and Google's SDK data disclosures for details.

Support, purposes and legal bases

If you contact us, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and resolve support issues. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information in menu examples. We process support and purchase-related requests to provide the service, fulfil legal duties, and pursue legitimate interests in troubleshooting and preventing misuse. Advertising consent is requested where legally required; providers explain their purposes and legal bases in their notices.

Retention, providers and international processing

Drafts remain on your device until you delete them or remove app data, subject to your backups. We keep support correspondence only as needed to resolve enquiries, maintain relevant business records or meet legal obligations. Apple and Google apply their own retention periods and may process information in countries outside your country of residence, including the United States. Their privacy policies describe applicable safeguards and retention controls. Contact us for help with a request about data we hold.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain your personal data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent. Contact hello@parslia.app to exercise rights concerning data we control. We may need to verify your request. For data controlled by Apple or Google, use their privacy controls or contact them directly. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Audience and changes

Menu Creator Studio is a general menu-writing tool, not a service directed to children. We do not ask for dates of birth or create user profiles. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us. We may update this policy when app features or providers change; the effective date will be updated.
