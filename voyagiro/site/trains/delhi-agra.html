Delhi to Agra by train

About this route

Delhi to Agra is a classic day trip to the Taj Mahal. Fast trains such as the Gatimaan Express and Shatabdi services make it practical. Indian Railways tickets are sold officially through IRCTC; apps such as ixigo help you check availability and waitlist status. Book ahead, especially at weekends.
