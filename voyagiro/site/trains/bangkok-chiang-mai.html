Bangkok to Chiang Mai by train

About this route

Overnight sleeper trains run between Bangkok's Krung Thep Aphiwat Central Terminal and Chiang Mai, a scenic alternative to flying that saves a night's hotel. Lower berths are usually more comfortable, and sleeper seats sell out in peak season, so book early.
