Last updated: 31 Oct 2026. This is a template, not legal advice — have it reviewed by a qualified lawyer.

Voyagiro (voyagiro.com) is operated by Shyam Prasad (“we”, “us”). Contact: voyagiro@mail.grokbot.com.

Data we collect

  • Inquiry form: not connected. Submitting it stores nothing and sends no acknowledgement. If you email us, we use the message only to reply. Marketing mail is separate and is not sent from this form today.
  • Chat assistant: messages you type. In FAQ mode they stay in your browser. If the AI mode is enabled, messages are sent to our server function and an AI model provider to generate a reply; do not share sensitive personal data in chat.
  • Click tracking: a record of partner links you click (partner, page, time) is stored in your browser's localStorage only.
  • Analytics: if enabled, Google Analytics 4 collects pseudonymous usage data via cookies (see Cookie Policy).

Data may be transferred to the USA (Formspree, Google, AI provider) under appropriate safeguards.
