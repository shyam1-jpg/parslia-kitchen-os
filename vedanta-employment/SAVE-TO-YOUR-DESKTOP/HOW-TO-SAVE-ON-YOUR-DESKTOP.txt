Save these Word files on YOUR Desktop
=====================================

These are Microsoft Word files (.docx). Open them in Microsoft Word.

Yashar — start with this one:
  Yashar_Daily_Follow_Sheet.docx

Also here:
  Vedanta_Kitchen_Work_Formula.docx
  Vedanta_Kitchen_Employment_Template.docx

On your computer (Windows):
1. Download the .docx file.
2. Open it in Microsoft Word.
3. Click File.
4. Click Save As.
5. Click This PC.
6. Click Desktop.
7. Click Save.

Or:
1. Download the .docx file (it often goes to Downloads).
2. Right-click the file.
3. Click Cut or Copy.
4. Right-click an empty space on your Desktop.
5. Click Paste.

On a Mac:
1. Download the .docx file.
2. Open it in Microsoft Word.
3. Click File > Save As.
4. Choose Desktop.
5. Click Save.

Do not open the file only in a web preview. Open it in Word, then Save As to Desktop.
