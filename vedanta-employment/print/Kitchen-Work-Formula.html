1. What one dish really is

One dish is not one job. A single dish can have around ten elements: collect and date-check; wash; peel; cut; weigh to the recipe; pre-cook; sauce; garnish; plate; wash and label.

Starter to dessert is often six items. Each needs a recipe calculated for that day’s guests, then every ingredient collected, then prepped.

6 items × 10 elements = 60 kitchen jobs before guest number, extra diets, washing, date checks, menu typing and close.

2. Programme — step by step

Step 1 — Open safe

Temps, date-check every open label, spot-check stock, report faults. HACCP every day — 10 guests or 50.

Step 2 — Lock the number and the diets

Confirm covers (they change). Write allergens and extra diets down. A late diet is a new line of work, not a garnish.

Step 3 — Menu for this day

Menu matches the guest and the staff who are in. Type it. Print it. No support means a simpler menu.

Step 4 — Calculate every recipe

Qty today = recipe per portion × confirmed guests × yield, plus each extra diet line.

Step 5 — Collect, then prep

Pick-list first. Then the ten elements. Prep is the long part. Cooking is the visible part.

Step 6 — Breakfast, lunch, dinner

Recipe, temperature, allergen check, handover on time, Kitchen pot wash. Restaurant owns guest-facing buffet and refreshment.

Step 7 — Order, plan, print, clean

Shortages, 48-hour plan against the next retreat number, menu print if it changed, clean and date-check at close.

Step 8 — Close — or keep going if the board is empty

No one on the board means the person who opened at 07:00 is still finishing dinner. That is a gap, not a model.

3. Guest factor

GuestsFactorOn the floor
101.0Full HACCP and menu cycle. Diet add-ons still hurt.
201.6Normal house. Wash and prep are constant.
302.2One chef cannot also be the porter.
503.2Peak. Close runs late unless support is real.

Work score = (items × elements × guest factor) + diet add-ons. Example at 20 guests: 6 × 10 × 1.6 = 96, plus diets ≈ 99. The guests are 20. The jobs are not 20.

Do not roster the same chef 21:00 then 07:00. Do not ask one person to open at 07:00 and finish a 50-cover dinner.

6. What I need agreed

  1. Plan from the formula, not from “it is only 20 people”.
  2. Recommended cover first; lean option if money is tight; never empty-board 07:00–21:00 as the plan.
  3. Guest number and diet list in writing, and tell me when it changes.
  4. Menu typing, recipe calculation, ordering, date checking and cleaning are daily Kitchen work.
  5. At 30 or 50 we add hands or simplify the menu — not full menu and no support.

I can run this house. I have done it for years. I am asking for a programmed way of covering it so the food stays safe and I am not the whole rota.
