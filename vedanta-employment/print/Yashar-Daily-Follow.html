Yashar — what you have to do

Follow Head Chef Shyam’s kitchen programme at The Vedanta. Learn it, then do it in this order every shift.

You follow the daily. You do not skip date-check. You do not skip the recipe. 20 guests is not 20 jobs.

1. What you have to do

  1. Open the kitchen safe (temps + date check) before you cook.
  2. Confirm today’s guest number and today’s diets — they change (10, 20, 30, 50).
  3. Follow today’s menu and recipes — do not guess.
  4. For every dish: calculate → collect → prep → cook → label → wash.
  5. Help breakfast, lunch and dinner be ready on time.
  6. Clean as you go. Date-check again at close.
  7. If you are the only person on the board, say so early. Do not silently work 07:00–21:00 as if that is normal.
  8. Kitchen = food on the pass. Tea, buffet dressing and dining-room wash are not your default job.

3. How to follow the daily (in order)

  1. Arrive: clock in, uniform, hands, read the day board.
  2. Open safe: temps, date-check, pull out-of-date, report faults.
  3. Lock number and diets: written on the board before big prep.
  4. Menu for this day: this guest count, this staff. Alone = simpler menu.
  5. Calculate recipes: portion × today’s guests + diet lines.
  6. Collect, then prep: pick-list first, then wash / cut / weigh.
  7. Three services: breakfast, lunch, dinner — handover on time, Kitchen pots washed.
  8. Close: label, date-check, clean. Not 21:00 then 07:00 next day.

5. This week

Day 1–2: shadow open (temps + date-check). Count ten elements on one dish.
Day 3–5: calculate one recipe; collect a full pick-list; run Kitchen wash in service.
Week 2: lead one prep block; write a handover; diet swap only from the written list.
