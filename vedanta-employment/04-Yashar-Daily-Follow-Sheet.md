## 1. What you have to do (short)

1. Open the kitchen **safe** (temps + date check) before you cook.  
2. Confirm **today’s guest number** and **today’s diets** — they change (10, 20, 30, 50).  
3. Follow **today’s menu and recipes** — do not guess.  
4. For every dish: **calculate → collect → prep → cook → label → wash**.  
5. Help get breakfast, lunch and dinner **ready on time**.  
6. Keep the kitchen clean as you go. Date-check again at close.  
7. If you are the only person on the board, tell Shyam / the Duty Manager early. Do not silently work 07:00–21:00 as if that is normal.  
8. Restaurant food on the pass is Kitchen. Guest tea, buffet dressing and dining-room wash is **not** your default job.

That is the job. Everything below is how you follow it **daily**.

## 2. What you have to learn from there

Learn from Shyam’s pack — 35 years as chef, senior chef and executive chef (Avanti and Vedanta), and 5½–6 years running this kitchen. You are not copying his title. You are copying his **order of work**.

| Learn this | Why it matters for you |
|------------|------------------------|
| One dish ≈ **10 jobs** (check, wash, peel, cut, weigh, pre-cook, sauce, garnish, plate, wash/label) | If you only “cook”, you are late. |
| Starter to dessert is often **six items** | Six recipes, not one pot. |
| Guests are **not fixed**: 10 / 20 / 30 / 50 | Prep for *today’s* number, not last week’s 50. |
| Extra diets / allergens = **extra work lines** | Write them down. Do not add a new full dish in the middle of service unless Shyam says so. |
| **Recipe first** | Calculate each ingredient, then collect, then prep. |
| **HACCP every day** | Temps and date-check — 10 guests or 50. |
| Kitchen vs Restaurant | You cook and hand over. You do not own buffet show, tea station or routine FOH wash. |
| Empty board | No KA/KP means you cook *and* wash, and the day runs long. Ask for a short wash window or a simpler menu. |

You are ready to work when you can do this without being asked:

- Date-check a fridge and pull anything out of date.  
- Read the day board: covers, allergens, menu.  
- Weigh a recipe for today’s guest count.  
- Follow a dish through all ten elements.  
- Label leftovers with name, date and allergen.  
- Hand over what is left, what is short, what the next person must know.

## 3. How to follow the daily

Do the steps **in this order**. Do not jump to cooking first.

### When you arrive

1. Clock in. Clean uniform, apron, hair, hands.  
2. Read the **day board**: guest number, diets, menu, who is on with you.  
3. If the number or diets are not written, **ask** before you start a big prep.

### Step 1 — Open safe (every day)

- Fridge / freezer temperatures. Write them.  
- Date-check open labels (use-by, cooked-on, defrost).  
- Pull anything out of date.  
- Tell Shyam if a machine is broken.

### Step 2 — Lock the number and the diets

- Today’s covers: 10, 20, 30 or 50 — or whatever is on the board.  
- Today’s extra diets / allergens — **written**, not only spoken.  
- If a diet arrives late: simpler substitute if Shyam agrees — not a new 10-element dish with no time.

### Step 3 — Menu for *this* day

- Cook **this** menu, **this** guest count, with **this** staff.  
- If you are alone, the menu must be simpler. That is Shyam’s rule, not laziness.

### Step 4 — Calculate the recipes

For each dish:

`quantity today = recipe per portion × today’s guests`  
then add each extra diet line.

If you skip this you waste food or you run out.

### Step 5 — Collect, then prep

- Collect every ingredient on the list **first**.  
- Then the ten elements: wash, peel, cut, weigh, and so on.  
- Prep is the long part. Cooking is the visible part.

### Step 6 — Breakfast, then lunch, then dinner

For each service:

- Finish to the recipe.  
- Temperature and allergen check.  
- Hand over to Restaurant **on time**.  
- Wash Kitchen pots and reset for the next service.

### Step 7 — Order, plan, print, clean

- Write shortages on the order list.  
- Help with tomorrow if Shyam asks (next retreat number can jump).  
- Clean, label, date-check again.

### Step 8 — Close

- Kitchen close list done.  
- Morning person must not inherit last night’s pots.  
- If you finish at 21:00, you are **not** on at 07:00 the next day.

## 4. Your day on the clock (how to follow it)

Use the window you are **rostered**. Do not invent extra hours.

| If you are on | Clock (normal house) | Your follow |
|---------------|----------------------|-------------|
| Morning chef / with Shyam | 07:00–16:00 | Open safe → breakfast + lunch prep and handover → lunch overlap is for handover and next prep, not two people both lost on a 20-cover lunch. |
| Late / dinner | 12:00–21:00 | Take handover → dinner → Kitchen close. Not 07:00 tomorrow. |
| Early support (KA/KP) | 07:00–15:00 | Help prep at **today’s** quantities, deliveries, labels, Kitchen wash, clean. Not Restaurant buffet or tea fruit as your routine. |
| Late support (KA/KP) | 15:00–22:00 | Dinner support, Kitchen pot wash, Kitchen close. Leave it clean for 07:00. |
| Relief | Only when called | Cover that window, or a named job (deep clean, delivery). Do not invent work. |

**Guest number changes the pace, not the order.**  
10 guests: same steps, fewer portions.  
20 guests: normal house — still need wash/prep help.  
30 guests: you cannot be chef and porter.  
50 guests: peak — more trays; if no extra hands, Shyam must simplify the menu.
