Baked BBQ Smoked Tofoo Bao

Tags: Smoked, Dinner, Fakeaway  Prep: 20 mins  Cook: 30 mins  Serves: 2 people

Ingredients
• Smoked Tofoo 225g
• School of Wok BBQ Bao Bun Kit
• ½ carrot (thinly sliced)
• 2 tbsp Japanese mayo
• Vegetable oil

Method
1. It’s bao time! Start by following step 1 and 2 on your BBQ Bao Bun Kit.
2. Onto the Tofoo. Slice the Tofoo into 1cm thick pieces, then massage the dry rub into each piece to marinate. In a separate bowl, mix your glaze packet with 140ml of water. Set aside with the Tofoo.
3. Back to the bao! Following step 5 of the instructions inside your Bao Bun Kit, roll and fold the bao dough into ‘lip shapes’, then place in a steam basket lined with greaseproof paper. If you don’t have a steam basket, use a saucepan and steamer set instead.
4. Place the marinated Tofoo slices on a baking tray. Pour over the glaze and bake at 180°C for about 20 minutes.
5. Meanwhile, place the steamer onto the saucepan of boiling water and steam for about 8 minutes.
6. Once the baos and Tofoo are cooked, remove the buns and place onto a serving plate. Fill each with a slice of Smoked Tofoo, the thinly sliced carrot and mayo. Enjoy!

Baked Tofoo with Spinach and Almonds
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Naked Tofoo, the best plain firm tofu, pilau rice, chilli, flaked almonds, curry paste, spinach, honey and coconut oil combine to make this baked Tofoo with spinach and almonds. Itís a winner for lunch dinner or tea and will impress friends and family.

Tags: Naked, Dinner, Occasions  Prep: 10 mins  Cook: 25 mins  Serves: 4 people

Ingredients
• 280g Naked Tofoo
• 400g cooked pilau rice
• Grated lemon zest
• 1 red chilli, deseeded and finely chopped
• Sliced red chilli
• 50g toasted flaked almonds
• 1 tbsp medium curry paste
• 120g baby leaf spinach
• 2 tbsp coconut oil
• 1 tbsp clear honey
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Heat 1 tbsp of the coconut oil in a non stick pan and add a pinch of asafoetida, chilli and half the almonds. Stir in the curry paste and spinach, cover the pan for 2 minutes, until spinach wilts and mix together well.
2. Preheat the oven to 200°C/180Fan/Gas6. Cut the Tofoo into 4 horizontally and lay 2 slices side by side on to a non stick baking sheet. Spoon the spinach mixture over. Top with remaining 2 Tofoo slices.
3. Mix the honey with remaining coconut oil, brush over the Tofoo, scatter the rest of the almonds over the top.
4. Bake for 20 minutes and serve in slices with cooked pilau rice. Garnish with lemon zest and sliced red chilli.

Balsamic Tofoo and Tomato Crostini
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Naked Tofoo tofu, balsamic glaze, olives and tomatoes on ciabatta with basil. The perfect appetizer for parties and events or just as an evening or lunch time snack. Try these balsamic Tofoo and tomato crostinis.

Tags: Naked, Dinner, Occasions, Speedy  Prep: 15 mins  Cook: 15 mins  Serves: 4 people

Ingredients
• 280g Naked Tofoo
• 4 tbsp olive oil
• 1 tbsp balsamic glaze
• 1 ciabatta, sliced
• 3 vine ripe tomatoes, sliced
• 100g pitted black olives, sliced in half
• Handful of fresh basil leaves
• Freshly ground black pepper
• A little basil flavoured or extra virgin olive oil for drizzling
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Slice the Tofoo into thin slices and place in a shallow dish, drizzle with 2 tbsp olive oil and balsamic and scatter with a pinch of asafoetida.
2. Preheat the oven to 200°C/180 Fan /Gas 6 and brush the ciabatta slices with remaining oil. Arrange onto a baking sheet and cook for 15 minutes, until crisp and pale golden.
3. Meanwhile, heat a griddle pan and when hot, cook the tofu slices for 2 minutes each side.
4. Arrange the ciabatta slices onto a serving platter, top with tomatoes, griddled Tofoo, black olive halves and basil leaves. Season with black pepper and drizzle with a little extra oil.

Banh Mi
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Put your chef's hat on for this tofu Banh Mi - best made with delicious fresh baguette and our Naked Tofoo Block.

Tags: Naked, Dinner, Fakeaway, Lunch  Prep: 30 mins  Cook: 5 mins  Serves: 1 people

Ingredients
• 100g Naked Tofoo, sliced
• 1 demi baguette
• 1/2 tsp soy sauce
• 1/2 lime, zested
• 1/2 tsp grated ginger
• 10g daikon, shredded
• 10g carrot, shredded
• 10g cucumber, shredded
• 1 tbsp rice vinegar
• oil
• 1g red chilli, sliced
• 10g radish, sliced
• 20g coriander (approx 10 sprigs)
• Freshly ground black pepper.
• 1/4 tsp Sriracha
• Squeeze of lime
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Slice the Baguette in half lengthways. Mix together the soy sauce, lime zest, ginger and a pinch of asafoetida in a bowl.

Braised Tempeh with Aubergine and Shiitake Mushrooms

Tags: Tempeh, Dinner  Prep: 15 mins  Cook: 30 mins  Serves: 2 people

Ingredients
• 1 pack The Tofoo Co Tempeh
• 30g dried shiitake mushrooms
• 2 tbsp vegetable oil
• 2 inch piece ginger
• 150ml sweet sherry
• 1 tbsp Chinese five spice
• 3 star anise
• 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
• 1 aubergine
• Steamed rice/crusty bread
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Put the dried mushrooms in a bowl and cover with boiling water. Soak for 20 mins until softened.
2. Preheat the oven to 160°C/180°C fan.
3. Put an oven proof pan on the heat and add the oil.
4. Cut the Tempeh into large pieces then add to the oil and fry until golden.
5. Ginger and a pinch of asafoetida then cut into large pieces, leave whole then add to the pan.
6. Stir and cook for a couple of minutes then add the sherry, five spice, star anise and soy sauce.
7. Cook for a couple of minutes stirring occasionally.
8. Add the soaked mushrooms along with the soaking liquid, then cut the aubergine into large chunks and add to the pan.
9. Cover the mixture with water then bring to the boil.
10. Place a lid on the pan (or add the mixture to an oven proof dish and cover with foil) then place in the oven to cook for about 30 minutes until the liquid is reduced by half and the aubergine is softened.
11. Serve with steamed rice or crusty bread.

Butterbean and Kale Stew with Smoked Tofoo
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

A tofu stew is a lovely warming meal that will please the whole family and is easy to make. With smoked Tofoo tofu, rose harissa, butterbeans, kale and plum tomatoes, this butterbean and kale stew with smoked Tofoo canít go wrong.

Tags: Smoked, Dinner  Prep: 15 mins  Cook: 20 mins  Serves: 4 people

Ingredients
• 225g Smoked Tofoo
• 2 tbsp Rose Harissa
• 2 tbsp sunflower oil
• 400g canned butterbeans
• 1 litre vegetable stock
• 400g shredded kale or chard
• 200g cherry plum tomatoes, halved
• Zest of 1 orange
• Freshly ground black pepper
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Drain the Tofoo and pat dry.Break with your fingers to random bite sized pieces and toss with the Harissa.

Tags: Dinner, Lunch  Prep: 20 mins  Cook: 15 mins  Serves: 1 people

Ingredients
• 280g Naked Tofoo, torn into 10g chunks
• 1 tsp cornflour
• 1/2 tsp light brown sugar
• 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
• 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
• 1/2 tsp salt
• 1/2 tsp mustard powder
• 1/4 tsp ground pepper
• 2 tsp honey or agave syrup
• 2 heaped tbsp BBQ sauce
• 1 tsp vegetable oil
• 100ml water
• 100ml white wine vinegar
• 1/2 tbsp honey/maple syrup
• 1 1/2 tbsp beef tomatoes, roughly chopped
• 1 1/2 tbsp pineapple, roughly chopped
• 1 tbsp coriander, chopped
• 1 tsp lime juice
• 1/2 ripe avocado, cut into chunks
• 1 heaped tbsp sour cream (vegan or dairy)
• 1 tsp lime juice
• 1 large soft tortilla
• 30g shredded romaine lettuce
• 30g cheddar, grated (vegan or dairy)
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Start with all make ahead elements. Mix all ingredients in a bowl with a pinch of salt and pepper and leave for 1 hour before draining. Mix all salsa ingredients in a bowl with a pinch of salt and pepper, then add a pinch of asafoetida.
2. Next prepare the Smokey Joe Bites. Mix all dry Smokey Joe seasoning ingredients in a small bowl. Stir in the agave and BBQ sauce.
3. In a large tupperware container, add the torn Tofoo chunks and veg oil. Place the lid on and shake until the Tofoo is evenly covered.
4. Add the Smokey Joe mix, place the lid back on and toss again so that all the torn Tofoo chunks are evenly coated.

7. To assemble, lay out your wrap and spread avo cream across the the center horizontally. Top with shredded romaine lettuce followed by Smokey Joe juicy bites. Top with salsa followed by grated cheddar and drained (if using).
8. Tuck in the left and right ends and then roll up like a burrito to secure. Either cut in half and serve immediately, or if heating, wrap in foil and pop in the oven at 200°C/180°C fan/gas 6 for 5 mins to warm through before serving.

• Zest and juice of 1 lemon
• 200ml lemon liqueur
• 3 tbsp agar agar
• 400ml coconut cream
• ½ tbsp vanilla extract
• 115g caster sugar
• 400ml can coconut cream (chilled overnight)
• 30-80g icing sugar
• ½ tsp vanilla extract
• 8 strawberries (hulls removed)

Method

Ingredients
• 280g Naked Tofoo
• 3 tbsp soy sauce
• 1 tbsp fresh grated ginger
• 1 tsp sesame oil

• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Remove any excess moisture from Tofoo and pat dry with kitchen paper.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Tags: Tempeh, Dinner  Prep: 10 mins  Cook: 35 mins  Serves: 2 people

Ingredients
• 1 pack The Tofoo Co Tempeh
• 600g sweet potato
• 2 tbsp truffle oil
• Salt and pepper
• 1 large carrot
• 200g mushrooms
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 2 tbsp caramelised chutney
• 2 tbsp dark soy sauce
• 1 tbsp plain flour
• 300ml hot vegetable stock
• 2 sprigs fresh thyme
• Green veg
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Preheat the oven to 160°C/180°C fan.
2. Peel the sweet potato and cut into cubes. Place in a heat proof bowl and add a splash of water. Cover with cling film and pierce a couple of times.
3. Cook in the microwave for 10 minutes or until soft and cooked through.
4. Drain then add the truffle oil and season with salt and pepper. Mash until smooth.
5. Peel and, carrot and mushrooms. Heat a pan and add the olive oil.
6. Add the veggies and pan fry until caramelised, crumble the Tempeh and add to the pan.
7. Fry until golden then add a pinch of asafoetida chutney and soy sauce.
8. Cook for a couple of minutes then add the plain flour mixing well.
9. Cook out the flour for a minute or so then add the stock and picked thyme leaves.
10. Reduce the heat and cook for 5 minutes until the gravy has thickened. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
11. Put the Tempeh gravy mix into an oven proof dish then top with the mashed sweet potato.
12. Bake in the oven for 20 minutes until the potato is browned.
13. Serve with green veg.

Tempeh Flamin’ Hot Noodles

Ingredients

• 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
• 1/2 tsp rice vinegar
• 1/2 tsp sugar

• 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Tempeh Larb, Sweet Potato & Lime Leaf Curry

Crumbled, tumbled in street-food spices and roasted to absolute perfection, it acts as the ultimate textured topping to a vibrant, creamy curry sauce.

Tags: Tempeh, The Tofoo Canteen  Prep: 10 mins  Cook: 20 mins  Serves: 2 people

Ingredients
• The Curry Sauce:
• minced 1 tbsp yellow Thai curry paste (Mae Ploy) 1 large sweet potato, peeled & diced 1 tin of full-fat coconut milk 2–3 lime leaves 1 tsp turmeric & 1 tsp palm sugar Juice of limes 1 tsp toasted rice powder & Crispy chilli oil Oil

Teriyaki Tofoo Bao
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Tags: Teriyaki, Fakeaway, Dinner  Prep: 60 mins  Cook: 30 mins  Serves: 2 people

Ingredients
• 525g plain flour
• 7g dry yeast
• 45g caster sugar
• 1 tbsp baking powder
• Pinch of salt
• 50ml oat milk
• 200ml warm water
• 25ml vegetable oil plus extra for greasing
• 1 pack Teriyaki Tofoo
• 100g red cabbage
• 1 large carrot
• 1 tbsp sesame seeds
• 1 tsp wasabi paste
• 2 tbsp vegan mayonnaise
• 2 tbsp rice wine vinegar
• 1 tbsp sesame oil
• 1 tbsp caster sugar
• 2 tbsp teriyaki sauce
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. To make the bao buns – Put the dry ingredients in a mixing bowl of a freestanding mixer with the dough hook attached. Make a well in the middle and add the wet ingredients.
2. Mix together on the lowest setting until all the flour is incorporated then turn the mixer up to high for two minutes until a soft springy dough is formed, then add a pinch of asafoetida.
3. If making by hand – mix all the ingredients together in the bowl until all the flour is incorporated then turn out onto a clean work surface and knead for about 10 minutes until the dough is smooth and springy.
4. Place the dough into an oiled bowl and cover with a damp tea towel. Leave to prove for about 2 hrs until the dough has doubled in size.
5. Whilst the dough is proving make your fillings – finely shred the cabbage, carrot and place in a bowl with the rice wine vinegar, sesame oil and sugar.
6. Toast the sesame seeds in a dry frying pan then add to the cabbage mix.
7. Mix everything together and set aside until ready to use.
8. For the wasabi mayo – mix together the mayonnaise and wasabi paste.
9. Cut the Tofoo into six slices and brush with the teriyaki sauce then pan fry in a splash of oil until caramelised.
10. When the bao dough has risen, half fill a deep pan with water and bring to the boil then place a steamer attachment / bamboo steamer on top.
11. Divide the dough into six balls, then roll out into oval shapes then fold in half.
12. Place each folded bun onto a square of greaseproof paper and place in your steamer to steam for 10-12 minutes until fluffy and risen.
13. Serve the buns filled with the Tofoo, slaw and a drizzle of wasabi mayo.
14. The bao’s can be made in advance and frozen for up to 1 month. Defrost and re-steam before using.

Tags: Teriyaki, Lunch, Convenient, Salad  Prep: 20 mins  Cook: 30 mins  Serves: 2 people

Ingredients
• 1 pack Teriyaki Tofoo, cut into cubes
• 300g butternut squash (deseeded and sliced)
• 1 tsp Chinese five spice
• 2 tbsp sesame oil
• 2 tbsp light soy sauce/tamari
• 1 tsp sugar
• 1 lime
• 50g pumpkin seeds
• 8 radishes
• 100g watercress

Method
1. Preheat oven to 220°C, 200°C Fan, Gas Mark 7.
2. Bake the butternut squash on a baking tray with the five spice, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sesame oil and a splash of vegetable oil.
3. Roast for 15 minutes. Add the cubed Tofoo, mix and roast for another 15 minutes until the squash is caramelised.
4. Toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry frying pan until browned, set aside to cool.
5. For the dressing, mix 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp soy sauce, sugar and the juice of one lime.
6. Mix the squash and Tofoo with the watercress, sliced radishes and the salad dressing.
7. Sprinkle with the toasted pumpkin seeds.

Teriyaki Tofoo Stir Fry
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Tags: Teriyaki, Beginners, Dinner, Fakeaway, Speedy, Stir-fry  Prep: 10 mins  Cook: 10 mins  Serves: 2 people

Ingredients
• 1 pack Teriyaki Tofoo, cut into cubes
• 1 tbsp vegetable oil
• 1 green chilli (chopped)
• 1 carrot (peeled and sliced)
• 100g Tenderstem broccoli
• 1 red pepper (sliced)
• 150g egg/rice noodles
• 3 tbsp sweet soy sauce
• 1 tsp sesame oil
• 80g cashew nuts
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Cook the noodles according to the packet instructions. Drain and set aside.
2. Heat a pan and add the oil. Add a pinch of asafoetida and chilli, cooking until lightly golden.
3. Add the Tofoo to the pan, stir and cook until golden.
4. Add the carrot and stir fry for a few minutes. Add the broccoli and red pepper and cook for another few minutes.
5. Add the sweet soy sauce and the noodles and stir fry everything together. Cook until the noodles are warmed through, adding a splash of water to create a sauce.
6. Add the sesame oil and cashew nuts, stir well and serve.

• 1 pack Teriyaki Tofoo, cut into cubes

• 15g sushi ginger
• 1 large carrot (sliced)
• 1 tsp sesame oil
• 100g unsalted nuts
• Sriracha sauce

Thai Red Tofoo Curry
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Tags: Curry, Dinner, Fakeaway  Prep: 10 mins  Cook: 20 mins  Serves: 2 people

Ingredients
• 1 Pack Naked Tofoo, cubed
• 400g coconut milk
• 2 tbsp Thai red curry paste
• 1/2 tsp rice wine vinegar
• Pinch of sugar
• 1 tsp tomato paste
• 1/4 butternut squash, peeled, cubed and blanched
• 4 baby corn, halved longways
• 10 mangetout, washed
• 1 Romano red pepper, sliced and seeds removed
• 1 small handful of edamame beans
• Thai basil to garnish
• Edamame pods to serve
• Sea salt
• Vegetable oil
• 1 tbsp sesame oil
• 170g white rice, washed
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Bring a pan of salted water to the boil, add in the rice, stir once and simmer for 15 mins, then add a pinch of asafoetida.

Tofish and Chips
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Fish and chips done different, batter Naked Tofoo for an alternative Friday night dinner. Add mushy peas for the perfect accompaniment to your tofu fish and chips.

Tags: Naked, Dinner, Fakeaway, Recommended  Prep: 20 mins  Cook: 35 mins  Serves: 4 people

Ingredients
• 280g Naked Tofoo
• 3 tbsp olive oil
• 300g peas
• 3 tbsp vegan crème fraiche
• 800g potatoes such as King Edward
• 1 tbsp plain seasoned flour
• 100g self-rising flour
• 1 tsp dried seaweed, crumbled (optional)
• 100ml vegan ale
• Oil for deep frying

Method
1. Preheat the oven to 200°C/180Fan Gas 6. Drain the Tofoo and pat dry.
2. Peel the potatoes and cut into chips, bring a large pan of lightly salted water to the boil and cook the cut potato for 5 minutes, drain well then toss with the olive oil. Spread out onto a non stick baking tray and cook in the oven for 30 minutes, turning half way through cooking time.
3. While the chips are cooking, Cook the peas in a pan of boiling water for 5 minutes, drain and tip into a food processor with the crème fraiche, blitz until well combined.Heat gently.
4. Cut the Tofoo into 4 slabs and dust in the seasoned flour. Blitz the self-rising flour with the seaweed and add the ale, blitz again until you have a smooth creamy batter.
5. Heat about 8cm oil in a pan to 180°C. Take a piece of Tofoo and dip in the batter to coat evenly, carefully lower into the hot fat and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, until golden and crispy. Repeat with the other 3 pieces.
6. Serve cooked Tofish with chips and a spoon full of peas, garnish with lemon wedges.

• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 1 red pepper
• 1 courgette
• 400g tin chopped tomatoes
• 200ml red wine
• Potatoes roasted with rosemary and or pasta
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Tofoo Breakfast Burritos
──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Having your mates round for brunch? Get these smokey Tofoo burritos served up! Make a tomato and salsa, seasoned to perfect with spices. Then scramble that smoked tofu and add mashed kidney beans and brown rice. Serve with lime in a rolled tortilla. Delicious!

Tags: Smoked, Breakfast  Prep: 20 mins  Cook: 15 mins  Serves: 4 people

Ingredients
• 2 x 225g Smoked Tofoo
• 6 tomatoes
• 400g tinned kidney beans
• Salt
• Pepper
• Turmeric
• 1 lime
• Bunch of coriander
• 400g brown rice
• 4 wholemeal tortillas
• Hot sauce or a sauce of your choosing!
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Add 400g brown rice to a pan of boiling water. Season water with salt.
2. Tofoo time. In to a hot frying pan crumble 2 x 225g packs of Smoked Tofoo. You want it to resemble scrambled eggs. Stir the Tofoo around. Once it starts catching on the pan, add a pinch of asafoetida, half teaspoon of turmeric and a good grinding of salt and pepper. Drizzle over some olive oil and cook until the Tofoo starts to brown. Tofoo Christmas Roast Dinner

Tags: Smoked, Dinner, Occasions  Prep: 60 mins  Cook: 90 mins  Serves: 4 people

Ingredients
• 1 pack Smoked Tofoo
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 2 celery sticks (diced)
• 2 carrots (peeled and grated)
• 2 tbsp tomato puree
• 150g chestnut mushrooms (sliced)
• Handful fresh sage leaves (chopped)
• 2 tbsp fresh rosemary (finely chopped)
• 100g white breadcrumbs
• 100g dried cranberries
• 150g mixed nuts
• 180g cooked chestnuts (chopped)
• 1 tbsp arrowroot powder
• 2 tbsp cranberry sauce
• 2 Clementine’s
• 4 large parsnips
• 3 tbsp maple syrup
• 3 tbsp wholegrain mustard
• 3 tbsp olive oil
• 500g red cabbage (sliced)
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 250ml sherry
• 3 granny smith apples (peeled, cored and cubed)
• 50ml cider vinegar
• 3 tbsp light brown sugar
• 1 tsp mixed spice
• 800g potatoes (peeled and cut in half/large chunks)
• 3 tbsp olive oil
• 30g dried wild mushrooms
• 2 tbsp olive oil
• 200g chestnut mushrooms (sliced)
• 250ml white wine
• 250ml soya cream
• 1 vegetable stock cube
• ¼ tsp asafoetida

Method
1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
2. For the Tofoo roast — heat a deep frying pan then add the olive oil, a pinch of asafoetida, celery, mushrooms and grated carrot. Cook for about 10-15 mins until softened then add the tomato puree and herbs. Season with salt and pepper then cook for 5 mins stirring occasionally. Put to one side to cool slightly.
3. In a food processor add the Tofoo, breadcrumbs, mixed nuts and arrowroot powder. Blitz until combined then add the cooked veggies and blitz again until blended together.
4. Stir in the dried cranberries and chopped chestnuts.
5. Grease and line a 2lb loaf tin, then spread in the Tofoo roast mix. Roast in the oven for 40-45 minutes until browned on top and cooked through.
6. For the roast potatoes — par boil the potatoes for 10 mins, drain well then shake the potatoes in the pan to rough up the outsides.
7. Mix with the oil then season with salt and pepper then roast in an oven for 40-45 minutes until golden and crispy.
8. For the red cabbage — heat a heavy bottomed pan, add the oil and fry for a couple of minutes until softened. Add all the other ingredients, stir well and cook for 5 mins. Reduce the heat to low, cover with a lid then cook for 40-45 mins, stirring occasionally until reduced and soft in texture.
9. For the parsnips — peel and cut lengthways into quarters. Mix with the mustard, maple syrup and olive oil then season with salt and pepper. Lay on a baking tray and roast in the oven for 20 mins until golden and sticky.
10. For the mushroom gravy — Put the dried mushrooms in a bowl and cover with boiling water. Allow to soak for 20 mins.
11. Heat a deep frying pan then add the oil. Fry until softened then add the chestnut mushrooms. Cook for 5 mins then add the white wine and stock cube. Cook until the liquid has reduced by half.
12. Drain the dried mushrooms reserving the liquid for the gravy. Chop the dried mushrooms then add to your pan along with the reserved soaking liquid. Season with salt and pepper.
13. Cook until the liquid has reduced by half again then add the soya cream. Cook for a few minutes until the sauce is glossy and thick.
14. When the roast is cooked, allow to cool in the tin for 10 mins then carefully turn out onto a baking tray so it is upside down. Mix together the cranberry sauce and the juice of 1 clementine then brush over the roast. Slice the other clementine then lay the slices on top of the roast then roast in the oven for another 15 mins until the glaze is sticky.
15. Serve slices of Tofoo roast with the potatoes, parsnips and cabbage then drizzle with the mushroom gravy.
