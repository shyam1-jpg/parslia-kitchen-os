If you cannot see the folders
=============================

They are NOT on the Windows desktop.
They are INSIDE Outlook, on the LEFT side.

Last time: only a picture was made in GitHub. Your live
Hotmail (shyam_1@hotmail.co.uk) was not organised.
The helper files were on a pull request, not on this PC.

This time: double-click FILE-ALL-EMAIL-FOLDERS.bat


File the live mailbox
=====================

1. Double-click  FILE-ALL-EMAIL-FOLDERS.bat
2. Open the sign-in page it prints and enter the code
3. Sign in as shyam_1@hotmail.co.uk
4. Press Y
5. Refresh https://outlook.live.com/mail/
6. Look LEFT: Inbox → 01 Apple, 02 Google, 03 GitHub, 04 Cursor
   Inbox → Companies (other firms)
   Inbox → People (friends, family, your own mail)

If one email fills the whole page:
  Outlook → View → Folder Pane → Normal
  Web: click the folder icon on the far left.

To put this helper on your Desktop:
  Double-click  PUT-OUTLOOK-SETUP-ON-DESKTOP.bat


What gets filed
===============

Every company in the mailbox gets a folder:
  GitHub, Cursor, Cloud, Apple, Google, Microsoft,
  Brakes, Bidfood, Nisbets, HMRC, banks, parcels, …

Unknown firms with several emails get their own folder.
One-off shops go in  Other companies.
Friends and family go in Inbox → People (one folder each).
One-off people go in  Other people.
Your own copies go in  Me.
Failed deliveries go in  Mail failures.


Classic Outlook only
====================

If Hotmail sign-in fails and you have classic Outlook:
  Open Outlook, turn New Outlook OFF,
  then run SETUP-OUTLOOK-FOLDERS.bat
