# Simple Cookie Recipe: Vegan Vanilla Almond Sandies

**Diet:** vegan

Leela Cyd is a photographer and cookbook author. Her previous books include Food with Friends and Cooking Up Trouble. She lives in Santa Barbara with her husband and new baby son Izador Cosmo.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 1 cup almond meal or freshly ground almonds (I used Bob's Red Mill almond mealwith great results)
- 2/3 cup powdered sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup Earth Balance (or other vegan margarine)
- 1 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon almond extract
- 20 almonds

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350°. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. In a large bowl, stir together all dry ingredients. Pour this dry mixture into the bowl of a stand mixer or food processor. Blend in the vegan margarine, vanilla extract, and almond extract — just until the margarine is incorporated into the dough.
3. Shape into a 2-inch diameter log, and slice into 1/2 inch slices. Place cookie dough on sheets and gently press an almond into each cookie. Bake for approximately 20 minutes, remove from oven and cool on a wire rack. Serve warm with a glass of milk.
