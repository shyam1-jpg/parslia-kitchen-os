# Sweet Potatoes with Star Anise, Ginger, and Lime

**Diet:** vegan

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 1 pound sweet potatoes, scrubbed and cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 star anise, finely crushed
- 1 (1-inch) piece fresh ginger, finely grated
- 1 lime, zested and juiced
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- Freshly ground pepper

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 425°F.
2. In a large bowl, combine all ingredients and toss. Arrange potatoes in a single layer on a baking sheet.
3. Roast for 10 minutes, then shake or flip the potatoes over. Continue roasting for 10 minutes or until tender and slightly browned.
4. Serve warm or at room temperature.
