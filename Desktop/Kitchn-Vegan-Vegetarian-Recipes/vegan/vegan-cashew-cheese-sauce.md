# Vegan Cashew Cheese Sauce

**Diet:** vegan

1. Soak the cashews overnight in cold water to soften, unless you have a high power blender such as a Vitamix.

## Ingredients

- 2 large red bell peppers, roughly chopped
- 2 cups raw cashews
- 1 cup nutritional yeast
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 clove
- garlic, optional
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon red chile flakes (or less, depending on your spice tolerance)

## Method

1. Soak the cashews overnight in cold water to soften, unless you have a high power blender such as a Vitamix.
2. Combine all the ingredients in a blender, making sure the red peppers are on the bottom. Blend until a smooth sauce forms, adding a bit of water if the blender is having trouble blending.
3. Transfer to a sealed container and refrigerate until ready to use. The cheese sauce will keep for up to 5 days in the fridge.
