# Crispy Baby Potatoes Are the Perfect Go-to Side Dish

**Diet:** vegan

Everyone needs a simple, versatile side that’s easy to partner with whatever they're cooking for dinner.

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds baby Yukon gold or red potatoes (about 1-inch wide)
- 1 small bunch fresh rosemary or thyme
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 425ºF.
2. Halve 2 pounds baby Yukon gold or red potatoes. Finely chop fresh rosemary or thyme leaves until you have 2 tablespoons.
3. Place the potatoes on a rimmed baking sheet. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons olive oil and sprinkle with the herbs, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper. Toss to coat, then arrange the potatoes in a single layer cut-side down.
4. Roast until the potatoes are lightly browned on the bottom and they easily release from the pan with a flat spatula, 20 to 25 minutes. Toss the potatoes and roast until browned all over and tender, about 5 minutes more.
