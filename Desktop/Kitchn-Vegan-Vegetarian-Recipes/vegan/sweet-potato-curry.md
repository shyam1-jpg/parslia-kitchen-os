# Sweet Potato Curry

**Diet:** vegan

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon vegetable or coconut oil
- 1 tablespoon peeled and minced ginger
- 2 cloves
- garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons Thai red curry paste
- 1 pound sweet potatoes (2 medium), peeled and diced
- 1 (about 15-ounce) can
- chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 (about 13-ounce)
- can full-fat coconut milk
- 1/2 cup water
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more as needed
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 5 ounces baby spinach (5 packed cups)
- 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lime juice (1/2 medium lime), plus more as needed
- Cooked rice, for serving (optional)

## Method

1. Heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in a large saucepan over medium heat until shimmering. Add 1 tablespoon peeled and minced ginger and 2 minced garlic cloves. Cook, stirring often, until fragrant, about 1 minute. Stir in 3 tablespoons Thai red curry paste and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
2. Stir in 1 pound peeled and diced sweet potatoes, 1 (about 15-ounce) can drained and rinsed chickpeas, 1 (about 13-ounce) can coconut milk, 1/2 cup water, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, and bring to a simmer over high heat. Reduce the heat to maintain a simmer. Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until the sweet potatoes are tender, 12 to 15 minutes.
3. Stir in 5 ounces baby spinach a few handfuls at a time, and cook uncovered until just wilted, 1 to 2 minutes. Remove the saucepan from the heat. Stir in 1 tablespoon lime juice. Taste and season with more lime juice and kosher salt as needed. Serve over rice if desired.
