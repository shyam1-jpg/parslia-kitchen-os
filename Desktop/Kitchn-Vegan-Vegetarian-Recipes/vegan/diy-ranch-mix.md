# DIY Ranch Mix

**Diet:** vegan

Ranch seasoning mix is nothing more than a simple blend of dried herbs, spices, salt, and pepper.

## Ingredients

- 8 teaspoons dried parsley
- 4 teaspoons dried onion flakes
- 1 tablespoon dried dill
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 2 teaspoons dried chives
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Place all the ingredients in a jar. Seal and shake to combine.
2. Place all of the ingredients together in a bowl and whisk to combine.
