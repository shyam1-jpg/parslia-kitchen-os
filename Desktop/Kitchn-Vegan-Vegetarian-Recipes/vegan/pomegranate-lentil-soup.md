# Pomegranate Lentil Soup

**Diet:** vegan

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 1/2 cups diced onion
- 1/2 cup diced carrot
- diced carrot
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1 1/2 cups brown lentils, picked over and rinsed
- 1 1/2 cups finely chopped chard
- 6 cups water
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 1/2 cups pomegranate juice
- 1 large pomegranate, de-seeded

## Method

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add onion and carrot and sauté until onion is translucent.
2. Add cumin and coriander and sauté for another minute.
3. Add lentils, chard, water, and 1/2 teaspoon salt and stir to combine. Bring to a boil. Reduce heat and simmer until lentils are tender, about 30 minutes.
4. Stir in pomegranate juice and simmer for another 5 minutes.
5. Season to taste with salt and pepper.
6. To serve, ladle soup into bowls and sprinkle with pomegranate seeds.
