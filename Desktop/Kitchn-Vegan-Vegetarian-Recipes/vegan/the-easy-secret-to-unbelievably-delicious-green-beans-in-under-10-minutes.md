# The Easy Secret to Unbelievably Delicious Green Beans (in Under 10 Minutes!)

**Diet:** vegan

Title: The Easy Secret to Unbelievably Delicious Green Beans (in Under 10 Minutes!)

## Ingredients

- 1 pound fresh green beans, trimmed
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Heat an air fryer to 375°F. Place 1 pound trimmed green beans in a large bowl. Add 1 tablespoon olive oil, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, and toss to combine.
2. Air fry in batches if needed: Add the green beans to the air fryer basket and arrange into a single layer. Air fry until the green beans are crisp-tender, about 8 minutes. If you prefer your green beans a little more tender, give the basket a toss to redistribute the beans, then cook for 2 minutes more.
