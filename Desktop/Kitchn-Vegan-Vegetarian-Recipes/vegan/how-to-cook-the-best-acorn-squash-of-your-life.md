# How to Cook the Best Acorn Squash of Your Life

**Diet:** vegan

Emma is a former editor for The Kitchn and a graduate of the Cambridge School for Culinary Arts. She is the author of True Brews and Brew Better Beer. Check out her website for more cooking stories

## Ingredients

- 1 medium acorn squash
- Olive oil or melted unsalted butter
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the bottom third of the oven and heat the oven to 375°F.
2. Place 1 medium acorn squash on a cutting board. Using a chef’s knife, start on one side of the stem and cut straight through until the knife stops hitting resistance and you’ve cut through to the hollow middle. Continue cutting around the acorn, through the tip, ending up on the other side of the stem. _Do not try to cut through the stem._
3. Put down your knife and hold the squash in both hands. Pull the two halves away from each other, cracking the shell at the stem. The stem will stay with one half and leave the other half clean; you can trim away the stem if you want, but I usually leave it in.
4. Use a sturdy spoon to scrape out all the seeds and stringy bits from inside both squash halves. You can save these for roasting in the oven, if you like!
5. Place the acorn squash halves cut-side up, like bowls, in a roasting pan or on a rimmed baking sheet. Drizzle with a little olive oil or butter and use your fingertips to rub it all over the cut surface and inside of the squash.
6. Sprinkle both halves generously with salt and pepper.
7. Roast until the halves are caramelized and roasted around the edges, 45 to 60 minutes. You should be able to easily poke a fork or knife all the way through the flesh.
8. Eat immediately, or cool and refrigerate for up to 5 days.
