# Toasted Coconut & Almond Chocolate Clusters

**Diet:** vegan

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 12 ounces dairy-free dark chocolate (at least 70%), finely chopped
- 2 cups unsweetened coconut flakes
- 1 cup sliced almonds
- Flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 350°F. Line one rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper and place 40 paper mini muffin liners on another baking sheet.
2. Place the chocolate in a medium heatproof metal or glass bowl. Bring a large skillet of water to a boil. Turn off the heat and set the bowl in the water. Leave the chocolate to melt gently while you prepare the coconut and almonds.
3. Spread the coconut flakes and almonds out in an even layer on the parchment-lined baking sheet. Bake until lightly golden brown and toasted, 2 to 3 minutes.
4. Transfer the coconut and almonds into the melted chocolate and gently stir until completely coated. Spoon into the mini muffin cups.
5. Sprinkle the top of each cluster with a small pinch of flaky sea salt. Refrigerate until set, about 30 minutes.
