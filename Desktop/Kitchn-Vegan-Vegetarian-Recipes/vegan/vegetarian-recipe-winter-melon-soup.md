# Vegetarian Recipe: Winter Melon Soup

**Diet:** vegan

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups water
- 2 scallions, cut into 1-inch pieces
- Handful of cilantro (leaves and stems)
- 1 (1-inch) piece of ginger, peeled and thinly sliced
- 1 clove
- garlic, smashed with the side of a knife
- 6 white
- peppercorns, plus ground white pepper for garnish
- 1 1/2 pounds winter melon (can substitute fuzzy melon)
- 1/3 cup dried sliced shiitake mushrooms
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

## Method

1. Set aside 4 of the green scallion pieces and a few cilantro leaves for garnish. (The scallion pieces should be thinly sliced before serving.)
2. Fill a saucepan with 4 cups of water and the remaining scallions and cilantro plus the ginger, garlic, and 6 white peppercorns. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer, partially covered, for 30 minutes.
3. While the broth is simmering, peel the melon, remove the seeds and pulp, and cut into 1/2-inch chunks.
4. When the broth is ready, remove and discard the solids. Add the melon, mushrooms, soy sauce, and salt to the broth and simmer, partially covered, until melon is translucent and tender, about 20-30 minutes.
5. Taste and add more salt, if desired.
6. To serve, garnish with thinly sliced scallions, cilantro leaves, and ground white pepper.
