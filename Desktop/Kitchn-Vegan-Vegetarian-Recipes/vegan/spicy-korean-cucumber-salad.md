# Spicy Korean Cucumber Salad

**Diet:** vegan

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons rice vinegar
- 1-2 teaspoons
- gochugaru
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon sugar
- 1 scallion, chopped
- 2 Kirby cucumbers, sliced 1/8-inch thick

## Method

1. Combine all ingredients except cucumbers in a bowl. Taste and adjust seasonings, if desired.
2. Add cucumber slices and toss to coat (wear gloves and use your hands, or use tongs).
3. Serve room temperature or chilled.
