# My Mom’s Charoset Is the Best Part of the Seder Plate

**Diet:** vegan

Lisa Freedman was the Executive Lifestyle Director at The Kitchn. She has never met a cheese or a washi tape she didn't like. She lives in New York state with her husband and their pup, Millie.

## Ingredients

- 2 medium Granny Smith apples, peeled, cored, and diced into small pieces
- 8 ounces toasted walnuts (about 2 cups), chopped
- 1 1/2 teaspoons granulated sugar or honey
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- Pinch
- kosher salt
- 1/3 cup kosher sweet wine, such as Manischewitz concord grape
- Matzo, for serving

## Method

1. Place 2 chopped medium Granny Smith apples and 8 ounces chopped toasted walnuts in a large bowl.
2. Add 1 1/2 teaspoons granulated sugar or honey, 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon, and a pinch of kosher salt, and stir to combine. Add 1/3 cup kosher sweet wine and stir to combine. Let the charoset sit until the flavors meld, at least 30 minutes. Serve with matzo.
