# Learn How to Cook (And Eat!) Artichokes

**Diet:** vegan

Alexandra Shytsman is a recipe developer, food writer, photographer, and creator of The New Baguette, a plant-based recipe site. Her mission is to inspire people to cook more healthy, plant-centered meals at home.

## Ingredients

- 2 medium artichokes (about 1 3/4 pounds total)
- 1 medium lemon
- #### For the garlicky mayo dip (optional):
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh cilantro or parsley leaves
- 1 clove
- garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 teaspoon freshly squeezed lemon juice
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Bring a few inches of water to a boil in a large pot that fits a steamer insert with a lid. Meanwhile, prepare the artichokes.
2. Using a chef’s knife, cut the stem off at the base of 2 medium artichokes.
3. Cut off about 1 inch from the top of the bulbs (right around where it starts to taper). Pull off any small, dark leaves near the base and discard.
4. Using a pair of kitchen shears, trim off about 1/3 inch from each leaf to remove the sharp points.
5. Rinse the trimmed artichokes under cold running water while gently separating the leaves with your fingers to remove any dirt hidden between the layers. If your artichokes are going to sit out for more than a few minutes before cooking, submerge them in a large bowl of acidulated water (water plus the juice of 1 medium lemon) to keep them from turning brown.
6. Fit a steamer basket or heatproof colander or strainer in the pot and place the artichokes inside cut-side up. Reduce the heat to a gentle simmer, cover the pot, and steam for 40 to 60 minutes.
7. The artichokes are ready when you can pull off an outer leaf with tongs without much effort. Remove the artichokes from the pot and let stand until cool enough to handle. Meanwhile, make the dipping sauce if desired.
8. Make the dipping sauce (optional). Place 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh cilantro or parsley, 1 minced garlic clove, 1/2 cup mayonnaise, and 1 teaspoon lemon juice in a bowl. Season with black pepper and stir to combine.
9. Serve the artichokes warm with the dipping sauce.
