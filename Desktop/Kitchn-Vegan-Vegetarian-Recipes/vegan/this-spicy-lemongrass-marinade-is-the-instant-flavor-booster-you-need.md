# This Spicy Lemongrass Marinade Is the Instant Flavor Booster You Need

**Diet:** vegan

This versatile marinade is made with lemongrass, chile-garlic sauce, fresh ginger, shallots, and lime juice.

## Ingredients

- 1 (2-inch) piece
- ginger
- 1/2 small shallot
- 1 (6-inch) piece
- lemongrass
- 1 medium lime
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 2 tablespoons chile-garlic sauce
- 2 tablespoons packed light brown sugar

## Method

1. Prepare the following, adding them to a blender as you complete them: Coarsely chop 1 (2-inch) piece unpeeled ginger. Coarsely chop 1/2 small shallot. Coarsely chop 1 (6-inch piece) lemongrass. Juice 1 medium lime (about 2 tablespoons).
2. Add 1/2 cup vegetable oil, 2 tablespoons chile-garlic sauce, and 2 tablespoons packed light brown sugar. Blend until smooth.
