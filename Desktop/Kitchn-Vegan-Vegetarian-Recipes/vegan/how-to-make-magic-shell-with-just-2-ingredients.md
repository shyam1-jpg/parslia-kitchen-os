# How To Make Magic Shell with Just 2 Ingredients

**Diet:** vegan

Love this recipe? Our small but mighty editorial team made this free recipe perfect for you. Support our work and get everything we offer.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips (about 12 ounces)
- 1/4 cup refined coconut oil
- #### Equipment
- Measuring cups
- Rubber spatula
- Microwave-safe bowl
- Microwave

## Method

1. Measure the chocolate and coconut oil.**Place 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips and 1/4 cup refined coconut oil in a medium microwave-safe bowl.
2. Melt the chocolate.** Microwave on HIGH in 30-second bursts, stirring between each burst, until the chocolate is fully melted, 1 to 1 1/2 minutes total.
3. Let cool 20 minutes.** Let the mixture cool uncovered, stirring every 5 minutes so it cools evenly, until the chocolate is barely warm, about 20 minutes.
4. Use immediately.** Immediately pour over ice cream and sprinkle with chopped nuts, sprinkles, or other toppings. Let sit for 30 seconds to harden before serving.
