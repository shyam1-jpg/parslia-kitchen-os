# Move Over, Coleslaw: Everyone Who Tries My Cabbage Salad Says It's Even Better

**Diet:** vegan

Title: Move Over, Coleslaw: Everyone Who Tries My Cabbage Salad Says It's Even Better

## Ingredients

- #### For the salad:
- 8 cups finely shredded green cabbage (3/4 medium head)
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt (optional, if salting the cabbage), plus more as needed
- 1 medium cucumber (7 ounces), seeds removed, cut into matchsticks (1 cup)
- 1/2 cup thinly sliced red onion (1/2 medium)
- 2 medium scallions, thinly sliced (1/4 cup)
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh parsley leaves
- #### For the dressing:
- 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1 teaspoon packed dark or light brown sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, plus more as needed
- 1/4 cup neutral oil, such as canola or vegetable oil

## Method

1. _\_Optional:\__ Salt the cabbage (not required, but it helps it stay crisp a little longer). Place 8 cups finely shredded cabbage in a colander, sprinkle with 1 tablespoon kosher salt, and toss to combine. Place the colander on a plate or in the sink and let sit for 30 to 60 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, whisk 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar, 1 teaspoon packed brown sugar, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper together in a large bowl. While whisking constantly, slowly pour in 1/4 cup neutral oil and whisk until incorporated.
3. Squeeze as much moisture as you can from the cabbage with your hands (no need to rinse). Add the salted cabbage or 8 cups shredded cabbage, 1 matchstick-cut medium cucumber, 1/2 cup thinly sliced red onion, 2 thinly sliced medium scallions, and 2 tablespoons finely chopped fresh parsley leaves to the dressing. Toss to coat. Taste and season with more kosher salt and black pepper as needed.
