# Almond Butter Granola

**Diet:** vegan

A near perfect granola, with lots of clusters and the sweet flavor of almond butter and maple syrup.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1 cup sliced almonds
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 cup smooth almond butter
- 1/3 cup maple syrup
- 1/4 cup coconut oil
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup dried tart cherries

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 300°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper; set aside.
2. Place the oats, almonds, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl and stir to combine; set aside.
3. Place the almond butter, maple syrup, and coconut oil in a small saucepan over low-medium heat. Stir constantly until smooth and blended together, about 2 minutes. Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla.
4. Pour the almond butter mixture over the oat mixture and stir until the oats are fully coated. Transfer to the prepared baking sheet and spread into an even layer.
5. Bake until golden-brown and starting to crisp, 40 to 45 minutes total, stirring once about halfway through. Remove from the oven and let cool completely on the baking sheet. Transfer to a clean, dry bowl, break into clusters and add the cherries.
