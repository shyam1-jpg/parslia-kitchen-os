# How To Make Dried Pineapple Flowers

**Diet:** vegan

Love this recipe? Our small but mighty editorial team made this free recipe perfect for you. Support our work and get everything we offer.

## Ingredients

- 1 large pineapple
- #### Equipment
- Chef's knife
- Paper towels
- 2 to 3
- wire cooling racks
- Aluminum foil
- Rimmed baking sheet
- Muffin tin or glass

## Method

1. Set up your oven for drying:** Arrange a rack in the lowest part of the oven, remove all the other racks, and heat to 200°F or its lowest setting. Make 12 aluminum foil balls, each about the size of a golf ball, and flatten slightly.
2. Peel and thinly slice the pineapple:** Place the pineapple on its side on a cutting board and cut off the top and bottom. Set the pineapple upright. Working your way around the pineapple, cut off the rind in strips. Cut deep enough to remove the "eyes," but save as much of the flesh as possible. When you're done, trim off any eyes or bits of rind you may have missed. Thinly slice the pineapple crosswise into 1/8-inch-thick rounds.
3. Dry the pineapple slices on paper towels:** Lay the pineapple slices in a single layer on paper towels and pat dry with additional paper towels.
4. Move the pineapple slices to the drying racks:** Lay the pineapple slices in a single layer on 2 to 3 wire cooling racks, making sure they do not touch. Stack the racks on a rimmed baking sheet, using the foil balls as spacers in each corner between the racks.
5. Dry the pineapple:** Move the whole setup to the oven. Dry until the slices are dried but still pliable, 2 to 3 hours.
6. Shape the flowers:** While the slices are still warm, drape them over the back of a muffin tin or glass to form a cup shape and cool completely.
