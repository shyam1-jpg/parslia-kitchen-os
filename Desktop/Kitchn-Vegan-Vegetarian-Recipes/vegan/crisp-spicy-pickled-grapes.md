# Crisp & Spicy Pickled Grapes

**Diet:** vegan

Dana Velden's first book, Finding Yourself in the Kitchen: Kitchen Meditations and Inspired Recipes from a Mindful Cook (Rodale Books) is available where ever books are sold. She lives in Oakland, CA.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups apple cider vinegar
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1 cup water
- 6 fresh ginger slices, about 1/4-inch thick
- 3 star anise
- 1/2 teaspoon sichuan peppercorns
- 2 tablespoons coriander
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 pound red grapes, picked, rinsed clean and drained

## Method

1. In a non-reactive pan, combine everything but the grapes and bring to a simmer to dissolve the sugar.
2. Place the grapes in a bowl that can take heat and pour the brine over. Let steep until cool. Store in glass jars in the refrigerator up to one month.
