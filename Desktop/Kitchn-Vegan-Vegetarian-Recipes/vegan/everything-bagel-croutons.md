# Everything Bagel Croutons

**Diet:** vegan

Love this recipe? Our small but mighty editorial team made this free recipe perfect for you. Support our work and get everything we offer.

## Ingredients

- 1 teaspoon poppy seeds
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder (no salt added)
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 cups day-old bread cubes (about 1 inch)

## Method

1. Mix the poppy seeds, sesame seeds, garlic powder, onion powder, and salt in a small bowl; set aside. Cut the bread into roughly 1-inch cubes.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add the bread cubes in a single layer. Toast, tossing every minute or so, until the cubes are golden-brown on all sides, about 5 minutes total.
3. Remove from the heat and transfer to a large bowl. Immediately sprinkle with the spice blend and toss to combine. Serve immediately or let cool completely.
