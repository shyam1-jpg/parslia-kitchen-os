# White Noise Cocktail

**Diet:** vegan

Love this recipe? Our small but mighty editorial team made this free recipe perfect for you. Support our work and get everything we offer.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 ounces Cocchi Americano Bianco
- 1 ounce elderflower liqueur
- Ice
- 2 ounces club soda
- Grapefruit twist, for garnish
- #### Equipment

## Method

1. Place the Cocchi Americano Bianco and elderflower liqueur in a chilled double old fashioned glass. Fill the glass with ice, and then top with the soda water. Stir gently to incorporate. Hold the grapefruit twist with both hands between your thumb and forefinger. Twist the peel over the cocktail. Rub the peel on the rim of the glass for extra flavor. Drop the peel into the glass.
2. Pour the Cocchi Americano Bianco and elderflower liqueur into a pitcher.
3. Fill the pitcher with ice, then top with the soda water.
4. Stir gently to incorporate and garnish with several grapefruit twists. To serve, pour gently from the pitcher, making sure to spoon in some ice along with the liquid. Do not allow the drink to sit too long before serving, as the ice will melt and dilute it.
