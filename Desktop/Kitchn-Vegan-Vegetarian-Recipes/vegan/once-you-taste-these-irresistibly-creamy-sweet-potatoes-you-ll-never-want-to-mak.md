# Once You Taste These Irresistibly Creamy Sweet Potatoes, You’ll Never Want to Make Them Any Other Way.

**Diet:** vegan

Title: Once You Taste These Irresistibly Creamy Sweet Potatoes, You’ll Never Want to Make Them Any Other Way.

## Ingredients

- 4 sweet potatoes
- 1 cup water
- #### Equipment
- Fork
- Instant Pot or electric pressure cooker
- Steamer basket or heatproof trivet
- Measuring cup

## Method

1. Wash and scrub 4 similar-sized sweet potatoes, then pierce each one all over with a fork.
2. Set a heatproof trivet or steamer basket in an 6-quart Instant Pot or electric pressure cooker. Add 1 cup water and place the sweet potatoes on the trivet.
3. Lock the lid on and check that the valve is set to seal. Set to cook for 12 minutes under HIGH pressure for small potatoes (6 to 8 ounces each), 18 minutes under HIGH pressure for medium potatoes (8 to 12 ounces each), and 25 minutes under HIGH pressure for large potatoes (12 to 16 ounces each). It will take 7 to 10 minutes to come up to pressure.
4. When the cook time is up, let the pressure naturally release for 10 minutes. Quick release any remaining pressure. Open the pressure cooker and remove the sweet potatoes.
