# Orzo Tabbouleh

**Diet:** vegan

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup dry orzo pasta
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice (from 1 large lemon)
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 2 medium tomatoes, seeded and diced
- 2 cups loosely packed chopped fresh parsley leaves (from 2 large bunches)
- 1/2 cup loosely packed chopped fresh mint leaves (from 1 small bunch)
- 1 small cucumber, peeled, seeded, and diced
- 2 medium scallions, thinly sliced

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook until al dente, about 9 minutes or according to package instructions. Drain and run briefly under cool water to cool the pasta down. Drain well again.
2. Place the olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and a few grinds of black pepper in a large bowl and whisk to combine. Add the cooked pasta, tomatoes, parsley, mint, cucumber, and scallions and toss to combine. Taste and season with more salt and pepper as needed.
