# How To Make Spanish Red Pepper Sauce: Romesco

**Diet:** vegan

## Ingredients

- 1 (1 1/2-inch-thick) slice crusty bread (about 2 ounces)
- 7 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 1 medium red bell pepper, halved, cored and seeded, or 8 ounces jarred roasted red peppers, drained
- 1 plum or Roma tomato, halved and seeded
- 3 cloves
- garlic
- 1/2 cup whole, blanched almonds, toasted (about 3 ounces)
- 1/2 cup hazelnuts, toasted and skinned (about 2 ounces)
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Pinch red pepper flakes
- 1 tablespoon sherry vinegar
- #### Equipment
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Pastry brush
- Baking sheet
- Tongs
- Mixing bowl
- Pot lid, aluminum foil, or plastic wrap
- Food processor

## Method

1. Arrange oven racks and heat broiler.** Position the top oven rack as close as possible to the broiling element in the oven. Heat broiler to high.
2. Brush bread with oil and prepare vegetables.** Brush both sides of the bread with 1 tablespoon of the olive oil. Place on one side of a baking sheet. Place the tomato and bell pepper halves skin-side up on the other side of the baking sheet. Press down on the bell pepper with the heel of your hand to flatten it slightly.
3. Toast the bread and blacken the vegetables.** Place the baking sheet under the broiler. Flip the bread once the first side is lightly toasted, after about 1 1/2 minutes. Once the second side is toasted, after another 30 seconds, remove the bread from the baking sheet. Continue to broil the tomato and bell pepper until their skins blacken, about 9 minutes more. Broilers vary, so keep an eye on how quickly the ingredients cook.
4. Steam vegetables and peel.** Transfer the tomato and bell pepper to a bowl. Cover tightly with a pot lid, aluminum foil, or plastic wrap and set aside to steam until cool enough to handle, about 5 minutes. Peel and discard the skin from the tomatoes and bell peppers.
5. Blend the sauce.** Tear the toasted bread into bite-sized pieces and place in the bowl of the food processor fitted with the blade attachment. Add the garlic, almonds, hazelnuts, salt, paprika, and red pepper flakes. Process until the nuts are ground into a coarse meal, about 45 seconds. Add the bell pepper, tomato, and vinegar and blend until smooth, about 30 seconds. With the motor running, drizzle in the remaining 6 tablespoons of olive oil and process until incorporated.
