# Black Magic Sangria

**Diet:** vegan

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup fresh or frozen blackberries
- 1 cup black grapes, halved lengthwise if large
- 2 black plums, pitted and thinly sliced
- 1 (750-milliliter) bottle
- full-bodied red wine, such as shiraz, malbec, or cabernet sauvignon
- 1/2 cup brandy
- 1/4 cup crème de cassis (blackcurrant liqueur)
- 2 cups seltzer or club soda, chilled, plus more if needed
- Ice

## Method

1. Place the blackberries, grapes, and plums in a pitcher. Add the wine, brandy, and crème de cassis, and stir to combine. Cover and refrigerate until chilled, at least 4 hours or overnight.
2. When ready to serve, add the club soda and stir gently to combine. Taste and add more club soda if desired. Scoop a few pieces of fruit into ice-filled glasses before pouring the sangria.
