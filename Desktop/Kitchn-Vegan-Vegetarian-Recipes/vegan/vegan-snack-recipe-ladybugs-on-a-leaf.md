# Vegan Snack Recipe: Ladybugs on a Leaf

**Diet:** vegan

Kathy is the founder of HealthyHappyLife.com and FindingVegan.com. She is also the author of two cookbooks, 365 Vegan Smoothies and Healthy Happy Vegan Kitchen.

## Ingredients

- 3 teaspoons almond butter
- 3 large romaine lettuce leaves
- 3 teaspoons goji berries

## Method

1. Soften the almond butter for easy spreading — you can do this in a microwave or just let the almond butter come to room temperature.
2. Spread a teaspoon of the softened almond butter down the center of the leaf, then add a teaspoon of berries to the almond butter. Repeat with the other romaine lettuce leaves.
