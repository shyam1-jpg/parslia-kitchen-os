# How To Roast Peppers, 3 Ways

**Diet:** vegan

Love this recipe? Our small but mighty editorial team made this free recipe perfect for you. Support our work and get everything we offer.

## Ingredients

- 1 to 8
- bell peppers
- #### Equipment
- Tongs
- Outdoor grill or baking sheet--depending on method
- Heatproof bowl
- Plastic wrap or aluminum foil
- #### Roast pepper on a gas stovetop (best for 1 to 2 peppers)

## Method

1. Turn a burner to the highest setting and set your pepper directly on the flame. Use a pair of tongs to turn the pepper occasionally until the skin is completely blackened, about 8 minutes.
2. Heat a gas grill to high or prepare a charcoal grill for direct heat. Place the peppers directly over the heat.
3. Turn the peppers every 2 to 3 minutes until they are completely charred, about 10 minutes total.
4. Position an oven rack as close as you can to the broiling element in your oven, and turn the broiler on to high.
5. Place your peppers on a baking sheet and place it directly under the broiler.
6. Use a pair of tongs to turn the peppers occasionally until the skin is completely blackened, about 8 minutes total.
7. Put the still-warm pepper(s) in a heatproof bowl and cover either with plastic wrap, aluminum foil, or a pot lid.
8. The skin will loosen as the pepper steams, and once it's cooled down a bit, you can easily remove the skin with your fingers.
