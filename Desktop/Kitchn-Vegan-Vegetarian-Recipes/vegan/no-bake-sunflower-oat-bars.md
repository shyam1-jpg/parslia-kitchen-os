# No-Bake Sunflower Oat Bars

**Diet:** vegan

Rebekah is a Brooklyn- & Paris-based writer and food stylist. Her clients include The New York Times, Saveur, Real Simple, Rachael Ray, Epicurious, and the Food Network. Her book, Honey was released in April 2014.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups old-fashioned rolled oats (gluten-free, if needed)
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 cup raw sunflower seeds
- 1 cup banana chips or dried bananas, roughly chopped (see Recipe Note)
- 1/2 cup dried fruit (like raisins, blueberries, cherries, cranberries)
- 1 1/2 cups sunflower seed butter
- 1/2 cup agave nectar

## Method

1. Line an 8x8-inch pan with parchment paper.
2. Combine all dry ingredients in a bowl. Stir in sunflower seed butter and agave nectar until well combined. Press into the pan and refrigerate until firm. Cut into bars and enjoy. Store in fridge (or freezer for longer storage).
