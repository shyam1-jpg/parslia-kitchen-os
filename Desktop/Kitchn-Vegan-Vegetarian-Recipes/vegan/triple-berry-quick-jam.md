# Triple Berry Quick Jam

**Diet:** vegan

Megan is a freelance writer, recipe developer and cookbook writer. Her first book, Whole-Grain Mornings, (Ten Speed Press) is available in bookstores nationwide.

## Ingredients

- 1 pint
- fresh raspberries (about 12 ounces or 2 1/4 cups)
- 1/2 pint
- fresh blackberries (about 7 ounces or 1 1/2 cups)
- 8 ounces fresh strawberries (1 3/4 cups), hulled and thickly sliced
- 1 cup natural cane sugar, such as turbinado
- Pinch kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon finely grated lemon zest
- 1 teaspoon freshly squeezed lemon juice

## Method

1. Stir the raspberries, blackberries, strawberries, and sugar together in a large pot. Set aside to macerate until the sugar begins to dissolve into the fruit, about 10 minutes. Bring to a boil over medium heat. Add the salt, lemon zest, and lemon juice and reduce the heat to medium-low.
2. Gently simmer until the fruit breaks down and starts to cook down, thickening slightly, 15 to 20 minutes. If the fruit isn’t breaking down on its own, use the back of a wooden spoon or a potato masher to help it out. (I tend to like a chunkier jam so I don’t go to great lengths to do this.)
3. When almost done, the jam will still be loose (it’ll firm up more as it cools) but coat the back of a wooden spoon. Remove from the heat and pour into your favorite clean glass jars to cool until warm. Cover and cool completely. Refrigerate for up to 3 weeks, or freeze for up to 6 months.
