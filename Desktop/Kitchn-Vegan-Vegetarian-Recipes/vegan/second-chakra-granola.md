# “Second Chakra” Granola

**Diet:** vegan

Dana Velden's first book, Finding Yourself in the Kitchen: Kitchen Meditations and Inspired Recipes from a Mindful Cook (Rodale Books) is available where ever books are sold. She lives in Oakland, CA.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups rolled oats (not instant)
- 1 cup sliced almonds
- 1 cup large, dried coconut flakes
- 1 cup pumpkin seeds
- 1 cup sunflower seeds
- 1/4 cup sesame seeds
- 1/4 cup oat bran
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 cup hot water
- 1/2 cup maple syrup
- 1/2 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1 cup cornflakes
- 1 cup dried cranberries

## Method

1. Arrange 2 racks to divide the oven into thirds and heat to 275°F. Line 2 rimmed baking sheets with parchment paper and set aside.
2. Place the oats, almonds, coconut, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, sesame seeds, oat bran, and salt in a large bowl and stir to combine. Add the hot water and mix thoroughly; set aside.
3. Place the maple syrup, olive oil, and brown sugar in a small saucepan and bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring until the sugar is dissolved. Pour over the oat mixture and stir to combine. Add the cornflakes and stir gently until just combined.
4. Divide the mixture between the baking sheets and spread into an even layer. Bake, stirring every 15 minutes, until dark golden-brown, 45 to 60 minutes total. Let cool completely on the baking sheets. Transfer the granola to a large bowl, add the cranberries, and stir to combine.
