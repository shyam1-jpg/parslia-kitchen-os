# This Creamy Dip Is So Good, It’ll Convert Even the Biggest Eggplant Haters

**Diet:** vegan

Title: This Creamy Dip Is So Good, It’ll Convert Even the Biggest Eggplant Haters

## Ingredients

- 1 large eggplant (1 1/4 to 1 1/2 pounds), cut in half lengthwise
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt
- 2 cloves
- garlic, peeled and smashed
- 2 to 3 tablespoons
- fresh lemon juice, from 1 medium lemon
- 1/4 cup tahini
- Freshly ground black pepper
- Ground sumac, to garnish

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 425ºF. Oil the cut side lightly with olive oil and sprinkle generously with kosher salt. Roast, cut side up, for 1 hour, or until very soft and blackened around the edges. The eggplant halves will be puffy and expanded, but will collapse as they cool. Let cool for 1 hour.
2. Scoop the flesh away from the skin and discard the skin. Put the eggplant flesh in in a fine mesh strainer. Press out and drain any juice that will come out. Transfer to a mortar or food processor. Add 2 smashed garlic cloves, 2 to 3 tablespoons lemon juice, 1/4 cup tahini, and black pepper and blend thoroughly until very smooth. Taste and add additional lemon juice, olive oil, salt, or pepper to taste, if desired.
3. Refrigerate for at least one hour to let the flavors blend. Before serving dust lightly with ground sumac. Serve with warm pita bread, baked chips, or chopped raw vegetables.
