# Rainbow Chia Pudding Bowls Are My Favorite Spring Breakfast

**Diet:** vegan

Leela Cyd is a photographer and cookbook author. Her previous books include Food with Friends and Cooking Up Trouble. She lives in Santa Barbara with her husband and new baby son Izador Cosmo.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1 cup coconut yogurt
- 2 tablespoons honey (or agave, for a vegan bowl)
- 1 vanilla bean pod
- Pinch of fine sea salt
- 1/4 cup white chia seeds (black are fine too, but I used white for a lighter color)
- 1 medium apple, cored and sliced into slivers
- 3 dried figs, thinly sliced
- 1/4 cup goji berries
- 1/4 cup dried raspberries
- 1/4 cup crushed pistachios
- 1/4 cup pumpkin seeds
- 2 tablespoons hemp seeds
- 1 tablespoon lightly ground bee pollen
- 1 teaspoon turmeric

## Method

1. In a medium bowl, gently whisk the coconut milk, yogurt, honey, contents of vanilla bean pod, and sea salt together until combined. Stir in the chia seeds, cover with plastic wrap, and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.
2. When ready to serve, distribute into three bowls and top with the assorted garnishes.
