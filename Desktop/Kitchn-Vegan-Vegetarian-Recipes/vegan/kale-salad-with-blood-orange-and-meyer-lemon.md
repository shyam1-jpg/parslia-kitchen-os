# Kale Salad with Blood Orange and Meyer Lemon

**Diet:** vegan

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 1 bunch
- black kale (about 1 pound)
- 4 small blood oranges, segmented, juice reserved (about 3 tablespoons juice)
- Zest of 1 Meyer lemon (about two teaspoons)
- 1 large shallot, finely chopped (about 2 tablespoons)
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Wash kale and pat off excess water. If the center stems are tender enough to eat, simply trim the bottom inch or two. If the center stems are thick or tough, cut or tear them out _(see_ _Basic Technique: How to Prepare Chard Or Any Other Leafy Green)_ and discard or save for another dish. Slice leaves crosswise into 1/4-inch-wide ribbons.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the blood orange juice, Meyer lemon zest, shallots, olive oil, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and a good crack of black pepper. Add the kale and, using your hands, massage the dressing into the leaves. Add the blood orange segments and toss to combine.
3. Let stand for 20 minutes. Taste and season with more salt and pepper, if desired.
