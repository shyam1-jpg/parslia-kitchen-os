# Gluten-Free Dessert Recipe: No-Bake Sesame Coconut Ginger Cookies

**Diet:** vegan

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup unhulled sesame seeds, soaked (see Recipe Notes)
- 1/2 cup pitted dates (preferably a soft, moist variety such as Medjool)
- 3/4 cup unsweetened shredded coconut
- 2 teaspoons ground ginger
- Tiny dash of sea salt

## Method

1. Using a food processor, process the dates until they form a paste; it should be fairly smooth but some small pieces are fine. Add the sesame seeds, coconut, ginger, and salt, and process for about 30 seconds until the mixture comes together. It should hold together when pinched.
2. Shape into balls using 1 tablespoon for each cookie and place on a parchment-lined tray. Flatten the balls to about 1/3-inch thick using your palm or the bottom of a glass (lightly greasing it with coconut oil can help prevent sticking). Freeze until firm.
3. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to a week.
