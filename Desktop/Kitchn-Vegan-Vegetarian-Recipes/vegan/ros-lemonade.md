# Rosé Lemonade

**Diet:** vegan

1. Combine the rosé, lemonade, and lemon slices in a large pitcher filled with ice. Top with club soda.

## Ingredients

- Ice
- 1 (750-milliliter) bottle dry rosé wine
- 3 cups lemonade
- 1 medium lemon, sliced
- 1 (12-ounce) can club soda

## Method

1. Combine the rosé, lemonade, and lemon slices in a large pitcher filled with ice. Top with club soda.
2. Pour into 8 glasses, adding ice from the pitcher to each glass as needed.
