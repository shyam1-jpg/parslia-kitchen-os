# French Bread Pizza Is Impossibly Cheesy — and Easy to Make

**Diet:** vegan

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 400°F. Thinly slice 2 plum tomatoes.

## Ingredients

- 2 plum tomatoes
- 1 (12-ounce) loaf soft French or Italian bread
- 3/4 cup store-bought or homemade pizza sauce, divided
- 1 (7.1-ounce) package Daiya Mozzarella Cutting Board Cheeze Shreds
- 2 tablespoons coarsely chopped or torn fresh basil leaves

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 400°F. Thinly slice 2 plum tomatoes.
2. Cut 1 (12-ounce) loaf soft French or Italian bread in half crosswise, then cut each half lengthwise so you have 4 pieces. Arrange the bread cut-side up on a rimmed baking sheet. Spread 3 tablespoons pizza sauce evenly on each piece of bread. Sprinkle 1 (7.1-ounce) package Daiya Mozzarella Cutting Board Cheeze Shreds evenly over the bread, then arrange tomato slices on top.
3. Bake the pizzas in a 400°F oven until the cheeze is melted and edges of the bread are golden and toasted, 12 to 14 minutes. Top with 2 tablespoons coarsely chopped or torn fresh basil leaves and serve immediately.
