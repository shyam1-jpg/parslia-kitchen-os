# Adzuki Bean Salad

**Diet:** vegan

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked adzuki beans (drained)
- 2 scallions (white and green parts), sliced diagonally
- 1/4 cup cilantro leaves, coarsely chopped
- 1 1/2 teaspoons grated ginger
- 1 clove
- garlic, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon plus 1 teaspoon rice vinegar
- 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
- Dash white pepper
- Salt (optional)
- Avocado (optional)

## Method

1. Place the adzuki beans, scallions, and cilantro in a bowl.
2. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the ginger, garlic, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and white pepper. Pour over the beans and toss to combine.
3. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour to allow the flavors to combine. Taste and season with salt, if desired.
4. Optional: top with avocado slices or cubes just before serving.
