# Goodbye Summer Recipe: Malaga Gazpacho

**Diet:** vegan

Leela Cyd is a photographer and cookbook author. Her previous books include Food with Friends and Cooking Up Trouble. She lives in Santa Barbara with her husband and new baby son Izador Cosmo.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 standard-sized baguette, torn into 1-inch pieces
- 2 cups ice water, divided
- 2 cloves
- garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 3/4 cup blanched almonds (reserve a few to garnish finished soup)
- 2 medium-sized cucumbers, peeled and roughly chopped
- 2 cups green grapes
- 1 tablespoon sherry vinegar, or to taste
- 1/3 cup mild extra-virgin olive oil
- Chopped green onions (for garnish)

## Method

1. Place the torn bread pieces in a medium sized bowl, cover with 1 cup of the ice water and set aside (bread will become mushy) for about five minutes.
2. In the bowl of a blender, add the bread, water, and all the remaining ingredients (do this in batches if it all won't fit). Blend until smooth and chill for at least 30 minutes, to 4 hours (letting it chill and set allows flavors to come together). When serving, garnish with a little green chopped onion and almonds.
