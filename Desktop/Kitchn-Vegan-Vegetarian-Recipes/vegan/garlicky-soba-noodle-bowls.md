# Garlicky Soba Noodle Bowls

**Diet:** vegan

This quick cooking vegetarian noodle bowl is made with soba noodles, cucumbers, greens, and a light chili garlic sauce.

## Ingredients

- 8 to 9 ounces
- dried soba noodles
- 3 medium scallions, thinly sliced
- 2 tablespoons rice vinegar
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce or tamari
- 2 teaspoons Asian sesame oil
- 2 teaspoons sambal or Asian chile-garlic paste
- 1 clove
- garlic, minced
- 1/2 English cucumber, halved lengthwise, then thinly sliced
- 2 ounces baby kale (about 2 1/2 packed cups)
- Poached eggs, for serving (optional)

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to a boil. Add the soba and cook according to package directions. Meanwhile, prepare the dressing and vegetables.
2. Whisk the scallions, vinegar, soy sauce or tamari, sesame oil, sambal, and garlic together in a medium bowl; set aside. Place the cucumber and baby kale in a large bowl; set aside.
3. When the soba is ready, drain in a colander. Run cold water over the noodles until cooled, then drain well again. Transfer the noodles to the bowl of vegetables, add the dressing, and toss to combine. Divide among bowls and top with poached eggs if using.
