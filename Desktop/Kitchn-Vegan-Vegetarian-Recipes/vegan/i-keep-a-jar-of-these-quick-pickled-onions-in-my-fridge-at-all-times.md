# I Keep a Jar of These Quick-Pickled Onions in My Fridge at All Times

**Diet:** vegan

Dana Velden's first book, Finding Yourself in the Kitchen: Kitchen Meditations and Inspired Recipes from a Mindful Cook (Rodale Books) is available where ever books are sold. She lives in Oakland, CA.

## Ingredients

- 1 medium red onion (about 5 ounces), peeled and thinly sliced into 1/4-inch moons
- 1/2 teaspoon granulated sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 3/4 cup rice vinegar, white vinegar, or apple cider vinegar
- #### Flavorings (optional):
- 1 small clove of garlic, halved
- 5 black peppercorns
- 5 allspice berries
- 3 small sprigs of thyme
- 1 small dried chili

## Method

1. Start 2 or 3 cups of water on to boil in a kettle. In the container you will be using to store the onions, add 1/2 teaspoon granulated sugar, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, 3/4 cup vinegar, and optional flavorings. Stir to dissolve.
2. Place 1 thinly sliced medium red onion in the sieve and place the sieve in the sink. Slowly pour the boiling water over the onions and let them drain. Add the onions to the jar and stir gently to evenly distribute the flavorings.
3. The onions will be ready in about 30 minutes, but are better after a few hours. Store in the refrigerator. They will keep for a couple weeks, but are best within the first week**.
