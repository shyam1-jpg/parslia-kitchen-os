# Fall Recipe: Moroccan-Spiced Carrot & Sorghum Soup

**Diet:** vegan

Megan is a freelance writer, recipe developer and cookbook writer. Her first book, Whole-Grain Mornings, (Ten Speed Press) is available in bookstores nationwide.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup sorghum
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 1 medium white onion, chopped
- 2 teaspoons fennel seeds
- 1 1/2 teaspoons cumin seeds
- 1 1/4 pounds carrots, peeled and sliced into 1/4-inch thick coins
- 1/2 pound sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed (1 large or 2 medium)
- 6 cups low sodium vegetable broth
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 teaspoon fresh lemon juice
- Kosher salt, to taste
- Freshly ground pepper, to taste
- Plain yogurt, to serve (optional)

## Method

1. Rinse the sorghum and place in a pot with water. Bring to a boil, cover and reduce heat to a simmer until the grains are tender, about 45 minutes. Drain away any excess liquid and set aside.
2. In a large soup pot over medium heat, warm 2 tablespoons of the olive oil and cook the onion until soft and translucent, 4 to 5 minutes. Add the fennel and cumin seeds and cook, stirring until fragrant, 1 to 2 minutes. Add sweet potatoes, carrots and remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil and stir well to coat with onion mixture. Cook down for 5 minutes. Add broth, coriander and bay leaf.
3. Bring mixture to a very low boil. Cover, reduce heat and simmer until vegetables are tender, 25 to 30 minutes. Discard bay leaf and puree soup in batches in a food processor or with an immersion blender. Stir in the cooked sorghum and add lemon juice, salt and freshly ground pepper to taste. Serve hot with a generous swirl of plain yogurt on top.
