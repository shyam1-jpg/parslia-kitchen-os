# Elegant Raw Recipe: Fresh Figs with Cashew Cream

**Diet:** vegan

Leela Cyd is a photographer and cookbook author. Her previous books include Food with Friends and Cooking Up Trouble. She lives in Santa Barbara with her husband and new baby son Izador Cosmo.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups raw, unsalted cashews
- Scant one cup water
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/3 cup agave syrup
- 1 heaping tablespoon coconut oil
- 10 fresh figs, cut in half
- 1/3 cup unsweetened, large coconut shreds, lightly toasted
- 1 tablespoon shaved dark chocolate (for garnish)

## Method

1. Cover cashews in water and soak for 6 hours or overnight. When ready to make dessert, strain the soaked cashews. In a blender place cashews, scant one cup water, vanilla, salt, agave and coconut oil into the vessel. Blend for about 2 minutes at high speed, until completely smooth. Taste and add more sweetness or salt, if desired. Set aside.
2. Meanwhile, slice figs and toast coconut shreds in a medium, ungreased pan for about 4 minutes, until coconut is slightly brown and fragrant. Shave a chocolate bar or cut chocolate into very small pieces to garnish dish.
3. When serving, spread cashew cream onto base of a pretty platter, arrange fruit on top of cashew cream and finish with toasted coconut and chocolate. Serve chilled or room temperature.
