# How to Cook Asparagus Any Way You Like

**Diet:** vegan

Love this recipe? Our small but mighty editorial team made this free recipe perfect for you. Support our work and get everything we offer.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 pound asparagus, rinsed, trimmed, and dried
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt

## Method

1. Heat the oven.** Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 425°F.
2. Prepare the asparagus.**Place the asparagus on a rimmed baking sheet, drizzle with the oil, and season with the salt. Use your hands to coat the asparagus and arrange in a single layer.
3. Roast the asparagus.** Roast, flipping halfway through, until lightly browned and crisp-tender, 12 to 15 minutes total, depending on thickness.
