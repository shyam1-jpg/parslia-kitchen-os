# Fresh Tomato Purée Beats Store-Bought Any Day

**Diet:** vegan

Love this recipe? Our small but mighty editorial team made this free recipe perfect for you. Support our work and get everything we offer.

## Ingredients

- 4 pounds Roma or plum tomatoes
- #### Equipment
- Chef's knife
- Serrated vegetable peeler
- Spoon
- Cutting board
- Large pot
- Blender or food processor
- Quart zip top freezer bags

## Method

1. Wash the tomatoes.**Rinse 4 pounds Roma or plum tomatoes under cool water and pat dry.
2. Prepare the tomatoes.**If desired, peel the tomatoes with a serrated vegetable peeler or blanch them. Halve the tomatoes and trim away any imperfections, bruises, or soft spots. Remove the core from the tomatoes.
3. Seed the tomatoes.** Use a spoon to scrape out the seeds and any watery pulp.
4. Chop the tomatoes.** Coarsely chop the tomatoes.
5. Cook the tomatoes.** Place the tomatoes in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high heat and bring to a boil. Reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer until they've released some of their juices and are tender, about 10 minutes.
6. Purée.** Remove the pot of tomatoes from the heat. Let the tomatoes cool for 5 to 10 minutes. Purée in the pot with an immersion blender, or transfer to a blender or food processor fitted with the blade attachment and process until smooth.
7. Measure and freeze.** If not using immediately, divide the puréed tomatoes into 8- or 15-ounce portions and transfer to quart zip-top freezer bags. Press out the excess air, seal, label, and freeze the bags flat.
