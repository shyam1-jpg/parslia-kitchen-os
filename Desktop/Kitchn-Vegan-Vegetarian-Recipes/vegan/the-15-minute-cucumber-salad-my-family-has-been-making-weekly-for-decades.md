# The 15-Minute Cucumber Salad My Family Has Been Making Weekly for Decades

**Diet:** vegan

This refreshing salad comes together in less than 15 minutes and is packed with salty, sweet briny flavor.

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup distilled white vinegar
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 pound English or seedless cucumbers (1 1/2 to 2 medium), halved lengthwise and thinly sliced crosswise
- 1 small shallot, halved and thinly sliced (about 1/4 cup)
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 Thai or bird’s-eye chile, thinly sliced crosswise (optional)

## Method

1. Bring 1/4 granulated sugar, 1/4 cup distilled white vinegar, and 1/4 cup water to a boil in a small saucepan over medium-high, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Remove from the heat and let cool completely.
2. Place 1 pound halved and thinly sliced English cucumbers, 1 thinly sliced small shallot, and 1 thinly sliced Thai chile in a medium bowl. Stir in the vinegar mixture and 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt. Refrigerate for 2 hours. Use a slotted spoon to serve.
