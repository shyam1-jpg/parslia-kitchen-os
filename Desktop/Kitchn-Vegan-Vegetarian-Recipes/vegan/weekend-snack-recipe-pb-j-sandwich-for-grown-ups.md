# Weekend Snack Recipe: PB&J Sandwich for Grown-ups

**Diet:** vegan

Dana Velden's first book, Finding Yourself in the Kitchen: Kitchen Meditations and Inspired Recipes from a Mindful Cook (Rodale Books) is available where ever books are sold. She lives in Oakland, CA.

## Ingredients

- #### Jam:
- 3 tablespoons strawberry jam
- Small pinch of finely chopped fresh rosemary
- Small turn of the pepper mill
- #### Peanut Butter:
- 3 heaping tablespoons no-salt, natural-style peanut butter
- 1 scant tablespoon light, white miso
- 1 scant tablespoon butter or peanut oil, as needed
- Bread of your choice (I used a whole grain sandwich loaf here)

## Method

1. In a small bowl, mix the jam, rosemary and pepper and set aside.
2. Mix the peanut butter and miso in a bowl and taste. Adjust accordingly, adding more peanut butter or miso as needed. If the mixture is too crumbly, add a little butter or peanut oil for a softer consistency.
3. Taste the jam again and add more rosemary or pepper if needed.
4. Spread peanut butter-miso mixture on a slice of bread, following with the jam mixture. Cover with a second slice of bread and carefully cut into triangles.
