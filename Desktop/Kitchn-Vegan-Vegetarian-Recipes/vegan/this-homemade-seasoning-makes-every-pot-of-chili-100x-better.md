# This Homemade Seasoning Makes Every Pot of Chili 100x Better

**Diet:** vegan

This chili seasoning mix elevates any dish and only requires 6 ingredients that you probably have in your spice cabinet already.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons chile powder, such as ancho chile
- 2 teaspoons chipotle chile powder
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon dried Mexican oregano
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder

## Method

1. Place all the ingredients in a small bowl and whisk with a fork to combine.
2. Carefully transfer the chili seasoning mix to a resealable container and store.
