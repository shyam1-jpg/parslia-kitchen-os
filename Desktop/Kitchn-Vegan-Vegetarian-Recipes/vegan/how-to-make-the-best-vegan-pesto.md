# How To Make the Best Vegan Pesto

**Diet:** vegan

An easy step-by-step guide to making basil pesto that's naturally vegan, without cheese or dairy.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup raw pine nuts or walnuts (2 ounces)
- 2 cloves
- garlic
- 6 ounces fresh basil (2 healthy bunches)
- 1/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 medium lemon
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- #### Equipment
- Rimmed baking sheet
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Knife and cutting board
- Food processor

## Method

1. Toast the nuts.**Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 350ºF. Place 1/2 cup raw pine nuts or walnuts on a rimmed baking sheet. Toast in the oven, tossing halfway through, until golden brown, 5 to 7 minutes total. Transfer to a food processor fitted with the blade attachment and let cool.
2. Pick the basil leaves.** Pick the leaves from 6 ounces fresh basil to get about 6 lightly packed cups.
3. Coarsely chop the garlic and nuts.** Smash 2 garlic cloves. Add to the food processor and pulse until coarsely chopped, 5 to 6 pulses.
4. Coarsely chop the basil.** Add the basil leaves and pulse until coarsely chopped, 6 to 8 pulses.
5. Process with the olive oil, lemon juice, and salt.**Add 1/3 cup olive oil, the juice of 1 medium lemon (about 3 tablespoons), and 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt. Process, stopping to scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed, until just combined, about 10 seconds. The pesto should not be completely smooth but rather a rough but uniform paste.
