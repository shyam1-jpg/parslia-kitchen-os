# Salad Recipe: Hearts of Palm, Corn, Tomatoes & Watercress Salad

**Diet:** vegan

Leela Cyd is a photographer and cookbook author. Her previous books include Food with Friends and Cooking Up Trouble. She lives in Santa Barbara with her husband and new baby son Izador Cosmo.

## Ingredients

- 1 head butter lettuce, washed and torn into large pieces
- 1 bunch watercress, washed and trimmed
- 1 1/2 cups sliced hearts of palm
- 1 1/2 cups fresh corn
- 2 cups cherry tomatoes
- 1 shallot, sliced thin
- 2 cups roasted potatoes (I used a Russet but small, red potatoes would be more like what they served in Chile)
- 1 cup green olives
- olive oil and lemon to taste
- salt and pepper to taste

## Method

1. To assemble the salad, prep all the ingredients and plate keeping everything separate.
2. Dress with olive oil, lemon, salt and pepper to taste. Serve immediately.
