# The No Mayo Coleslaw You'll Want to Eat with Everything This Summer

**Diet:** vegan

No mayo here!** Coleslaw tends to fall into two camps – creamy and mayonnaise-based or tangy and vinegar-based. This coleslaw is completely mayo-free.

## Ingredients

- 1/3 cup apple cider vinegar
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1 tablespoon whole grain mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon celery salt
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 medium head
- green cabbage, finely shredded (about 8 cups)
- 2 medium carrots, peeled and grated on the large holes of a box grater (about 1 1/2 cups)
- 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced (about 2 cups)
- 2 medium scallions, thinly sliced (about 1/4 cup)

## Method

1. Place 1/3 cup apple cider vinegar, 2 tablespoons olive oil, 2 tablespoons honey, 1 tablespoon whole grain mustard, 1/2 teaspoon celery salt, 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper in a large bowl and whisk until smooth.
2. Add 1/2 finely shredded medium green cabbage, 2 grated medium carrots, 1/2 thinly sliced small red onion, and 2 thinly sliced medium scallions to the bowl of dressing. Toss to combine.
