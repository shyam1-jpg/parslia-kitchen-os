# Weekend Cooking: Braised Radishes (Again) – This Time with Rice Vinegar and Mint

**Diet:** vegan

Title: Weekend Cooking: Braised Radishes (Again) – This Time with Rice Vinegar and Mint

## Ingredients

- 1 large bunch red or French breakfast radishes
- 1/2 large red onion, well diced
- 2 cloves
- garlic, diced
- 1 small handful sweet mint, finely chopped
- Olive oil
- 1/3 cup rice vinegar
- 1/3 cup water
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Rinse the radishes and trim off the tops and root ends. Slice in half from top to bottom. Cut the onion, garlic and mint.
2. Heat a litte olive oil in a heavy skillet over medium heat. Add the diced onion and garlic and cook, stirring, until the onion is sweating and translucent. Add the radishes, placing them cut side down in the pan. Let them cook until the bottoms are slightly browned. Add the rice vinegar and water and cover. Simmer for 10 minutes, then remove the top. Turn the heat to medium high and cook, stirring, until any extra liquid has reduced. There shouldn't be much liquid, though.
3. Add the mint and cook, stirring, just until it's wilted. Season with salt and pepper. Serve.
