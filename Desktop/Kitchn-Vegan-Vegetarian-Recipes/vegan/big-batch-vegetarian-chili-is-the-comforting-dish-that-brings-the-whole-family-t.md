# Big-Batch Vegetarian Chili Is the Comforting Dish That Brings the Whole Family to the Table

**Diet:** vegan

Title: Big-Batch Vegetarian Chili Is the Comforting Dish That Brings the Whole Family to the Table

## Ingredients

- 3 (15- to 16-ounce) cans pinto or kidney beans, or a combination
- 1 (about 15-ounce) can whole kernel corn
- 2 (24-ounce) jars Prego®+ Plant Protein Traditional Italian Sauce
- 1 (4-ounce) can mild chopped green chiles
- 1 1/2 teaspoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- Serving suggestions: sliced avocado, lime wedges, shredded cheddar cheese, cilantro leaves, diced onion

## Method

1. Drain and rinse 3 cans pinto or kidney beans. Drain 1 can whole kernel corn.
2. Add 2 jars Prego®+ Plant Protein Traditional Italian Sauce, 1 can chopped green chiles, 1 1/2 teaspoons chili powder, 1 teaspoon dried oregano, 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, 1/8 teaspoon cayenne pepper if using, beans, and corn to a Dutch oven, and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered, stirring occasionally, until the chili thickens slightly, 20 to 30 minutes.
3. Reserve 3 cups chili for the Rollover Dinner. Serve the remaining chili with toppings of your choice.
