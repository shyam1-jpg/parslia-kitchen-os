# How To Make Date Caramels

**Diet:** vegan

## Ingredients

- 120 pitted dried dates, preferably Medjool (about 1 1/2 pounds)
- 1/3 cup almond flour
- 3 tablespoons almond butter
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- Flaky sea salt, for topping
- #### Equipment
- 8-inch baking pan
- Parchment paper
- Food processor
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Kitchen shears or sharp knife

## Method

1. Line the pan:** Cut a rectangular piece of parchment paper to fit in an 8-inch square pan that hangs off 2 sides, creating a sling that will make removing the caramels much easier.
2. Chop the dates:** Use kitchen shears or a sharp knife to finely chop the dates.
3. Blend the caramel:** Place the dates, almond flour, almond butter, vanilla, and kosher salt in a food processor fitted with the blade attachment. Process until a smooth, thick paste forms.
4. Press and freeze:** Transfer the date mixture to the prepared pan and press into an even layer. Freeze for 1 hour to set.
5. Cut the caramels:** Using the sling, remove the date caramel slab from the pan and place on a cutting board. Sprinkle with flaky salt. Cut the caramels into 1/2-inch squares. If desired, wrap the individual caramels in parchment paper. Store in the freezer.
