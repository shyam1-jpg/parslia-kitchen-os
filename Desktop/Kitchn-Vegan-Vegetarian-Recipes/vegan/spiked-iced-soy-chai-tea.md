# Spiked Iced Soy Chai Tea

**Diet:** vegan

## Ingredients

- 2 quarts
- cold water
- 4 to 6
- spiced or black chai tea bags (if using loose tea use 4 teaspoons per quart)
- 1 cup vanilla soy milk
- 8 ounces aged rum (bourbon or white rum may be substituted)
- honey or agave syrup, optional

## Method

1. Combine the tea bags and water in a large pitcher and cover. If you are using loose leaves a large French press for tea works great. Place the mixture in the refrigerator overnight, or at least 8 hours. The longer you leave the tea to cold brew the stronger it will become.
2. Remove the tea bags. Add the vanilla soy milk and the aged rum. Stir to combine.
3. _Sweeten if desired with honey or agave syrup, but be sure to taste first because vanilla soy milk is already sweetened._
4. Serve over ice.
