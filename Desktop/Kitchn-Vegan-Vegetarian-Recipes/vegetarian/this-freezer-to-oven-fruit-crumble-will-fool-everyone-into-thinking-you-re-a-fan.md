# This Freezer-to-Oven Fruit Crumble Will Fool Everyone Into Thinking You're a Fancy Baker

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: This Freezer-to-Oven Fruit Crumble Will Fool Everyone Into Thinking You're a Fancy Baker

## Ingredients

- #### For the fruit filling:
- 1 quart
- canned fruit, or 2 pounds frozen fruit (if you’re using sugared fruit, skip the additional sugar)
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- #### For the crumble:
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) cold unsalted butter, cut into small cubes
- 2 to 3 tablespoons
- milk

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Make the fruit filling:**Place the fruit into a 12-inch ovensafe skillet or 8x8-inch baking dish. Add the sugar and flour and toss to coat.
3. Make the crumble:**Place the flour, oats, brown sugar, salt, and cinnamon in a food processor bowl fitted with the blade attachment. Pulse until the dry ingredients are well mixed, 3 to 4 pulses. Add the cubed butter and pulse until the butter is distributed throughout the flour and oat mixture, 6 to 7 pulses.
4. With the motor running, stream in 2 tablespoons of the milk. If the topping still looks very dry, stream in the last tablespoon of milk.
5. Crumble the crisp topping on top of the fruit, taking care to keep the topping rough and nubbly so that you have plenty of opportunity for browning.
6. Bake until the fruit is bubbling up around the edges and the topping is firm and deeply browned, 35 to 40 minutes. Serve warm or at room temperature.
