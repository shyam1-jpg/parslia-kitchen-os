# My Secret for Making “The Best Roasted Carrots You’ll Ever Have”

**Diet:** vegetarian

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds carrots
- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 3 cloves
- garlic, minced
- 1 1/2 tablespoons honey
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons coarsely chopped fresh flat leaf parsley leaves
- Flaky salt (optional)

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 425°F.
2. Peel and trim 2 pounds carrots. If they are more than 1-inch thick, cut them in half lengthwise; otherwise, leave them whole. Cut the carrots on a slight diagonal into 2-inch pieces. Place on a rimmed baking sheet.
3. Melt 3 tablespoons unsalted butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Continue cooking, swirling the pan occasionally, until the butter has a nutty aroma and is a golden-tan color, about 3 minutes. Add 3 cloves minced garlic cloves and continue to cook, swirling occasionally, until the butter is toasty-brown, about 30 seconds more. Remove from the heat, add 1 1/2 tablespoons honey, and whisk to combine.
4. Drizzle half the brown butter sauce over the carrots and toss to coat. Sprinkle with 1 teaspoon kosher salt and 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper and toss to coat again. Spread the carrots into an even layer.
5. Roast 15 minutes. Flip the carrots, then roast until the carrots are tender and the edges are charred and crispy, 15 to 20 minutes more.
6. Transfer to a serving bowl. Drizzle the remaining half of the brown butter sauce over the carrots, add 2 tablespoons coarsely chopped fresh parsley, and toss to combine. Finish with a generous pinch of flaky sea salt, if using. Serve immediately.
