# I Can’t Stop Eating This Spinach Apple Salad (It’s Perfect with Everything)

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: I Can’t Stop Eating This Spinach Apple Salad (It’s Perfect with Everything)

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon honey
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/8 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 (5-ounce) bag
- baby spinach (about 5 packed cups)
- 1 large tart apple, such as Granny Smith or Honeycrisp, cored and thinly sliced (about 1 1/2 cups)
- 4 ounces feta cheese, crumbled (about 1 cup)
- 1/2 cup walnuts, toasted and coarsely chopped

## Method

1. Whisk 3 tablespoons olive oil, 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar, 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, 1/2 teaspoon honey, 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/8 teaspoon ground black pepper together in a large bowl.
2. Add 1 (5-ounce) bag baby spinach and toss gently to coat in the vinaigrette. Add 1 thinly sliced Granny Smith or Honeycrisp apple, 4 ounces crumbled feta cheese, and 1/2 cup coarsely chopped toasted walnuts and toss gently again to combine. Serve immediately.
