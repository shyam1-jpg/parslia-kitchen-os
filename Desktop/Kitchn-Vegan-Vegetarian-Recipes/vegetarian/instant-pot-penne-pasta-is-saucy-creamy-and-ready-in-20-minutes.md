# Instant Pot Penne Pasta Is Saucy, Creamy, and Ready in 20 Minutes

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 (about 24-ounce) jar
- marinara sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 12 ounces dried penne pasta
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1/2 cup heavy cream

## Method

1. Add 1 jar marinara sauce and 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt to a 6-quart or larger Instant Pot or electric pressure cooker and stir to combine. Scatter 12 ounces dried penne pasta over the sauce in an even layer. Do not stir from this point on.
2. Pour 1 1/2 cups water into the marinara jar, cover, and shake gently. Pour the water over the pasta. Remember — no stirring here. Lock the lid on and make sure the pressure valve is set to seal. Set to cook under HIGH pressure for 6 minutes. It will take about 13 minutes to come up to pressure.
3. When the cook time is up, quick release the pressure. Turn the pressure cooker off. Pour in 1/2 cup heavy cream and stir to combine.
