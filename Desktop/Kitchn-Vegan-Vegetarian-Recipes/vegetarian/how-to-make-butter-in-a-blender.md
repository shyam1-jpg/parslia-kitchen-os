# How To Make Butter in a Blender

**Diet:** vegetarian

Emma is a former editor for The Kitchn and a graduate of the Cambridge School for Culinary Arts. She is the author of True Brews and Brew Better Beer. Check out her website for more cooking stories

## Ingredients

- 1 pint
- heavy cream
- 1/4 teaspoon salt, if making salted butter
- #### Equipment
- Blender

## Method

1. Let the cream warm to room temperature:** Pour the cream into your blender and let it stand until warmed to room temperature, about 30 minutes. (Chilled cream can also be used, but will take longer to separate into butter.)
2. Blend until the cream separates:** Turn on the blender and let it blend the cream past the "whipped cream" stage, until big crumbs appear — that's the butter! This can happen in a minute or two with a powerful blender, or can take up to 10 minutes in an older blender.
3. Let the butter and buttermilk separate:** The butter will float to the top as you let this sit. Once you see two clear layers, continue.
4. Pour off the buttermilk:** Pour the buttermilk into another container, leaving the butter in the blender. Use a spoon to hold back the butter, if needed.
5. Wash the butter:** Add enough cool water to cover the butter in the blender. Cover and pulse a few times until the water turns cloudy. Let it stand a moment or two to separate, then pour off the liquid. Repeat once or twice until the liquid is mostly clear after blending. (This liquid contains buttermilk, but is less concentrated than the original buttermilk; you can combine the two or keep them separated for different uses.)
6. Add the salt:** If you're salting your butter, add it now and pulse a few times until it's combined. Taste and add more salt if you like.
7. Store the butter:** Scrape the butter into a storage container or butter keeper, or shape it into a log with plastic wrap and refrigerate. Butter will keep for at least a week at room temperature, several weeks in the fridge, or up to 3 months if frozen.
