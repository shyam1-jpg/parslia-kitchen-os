# Your Instant Pot Is the Secret to Fluffy, Perfectly Cooked Rice

**Diet:** vegetarian

_Editor’s Note:_**_If you’ve cooked this beloved recipe before, it might look a little different these days! We recently improved it to make it even easier to follow and more delicious._

## Ingredients

- #### For white rice:
- 2 cups white, basmati, or jasmine rice
- 2 1/4 cups water
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt (optional)
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter (optional)
- #### For brown rice:
- 2 cups long-grain brown rice
- 2 1/4 cups water
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt (optional)
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter (optional)
- #### For wild rice:
- 2 cups wild rice
- 2 cups water
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt (optional)
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter (optional)

## Method

1. Measure 2 cups rice into a fine-mesh strainer and rinse under cool running water for about a minute or until the water runs somewhat clearer. For wild rice, just give it a quick rinse. Drain well.
2. Transfer the rice into an Instant Pot or electric pressure cooker. Add the water (note that the water amounts are different depending on the rice you’re using!) and 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt if using, and stir to combine. Toss 1 tablespoon unsalted butter in if using, and don’t worry if it floats.
3. Lock the lid in place and make sure the valve is set to seal. Set to cook under HIGH pressure for the following times:
4. When the cook time is up, let the pressure naturally release for 10 minutes.
5. Quick release any remaining pressure. Uncover, fluff the rice with a fork, and serve immediately.
