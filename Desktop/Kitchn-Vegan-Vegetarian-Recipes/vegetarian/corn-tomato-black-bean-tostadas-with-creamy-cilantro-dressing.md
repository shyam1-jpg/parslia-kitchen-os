# Corn, Tomato & Black Bean Tostadas with Creamy Cilantro Dressing

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- #### For the dressing:
- 1 cup cilantro leaves and stems, roughly chopped
- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon lime juice
- Zest of 1 lime
- 2 tablespoons grapeseed or other neutral oil
- #### For the tostadas:
- 1 cup cooked black beans, drained
- 2 ears sweet corn
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes
- 4 (6-inch) corn tortillas, plus more to test oil
- Vegetable oil
- Salt

## Method

1. In a small food processor, combine all the ingredients for the dressing except the grapeseed oil and process until the mixture is very smooth. Drizzle in the oil and process until it is fully incorporated.
2. In a small bowl, combine the beans with half the dressing and stir. Set the remaining dressing aside.
3. Shuck the corn, and using a sharp knife, cut the raw corn kernels from the cob. Halve the cherry tomatoes.
4. Fill a heavy-bottomed pot with a 1-inch layer of oil and heat on the stove over medium-high heat until very hot. To test, place a small piece of tortilla in the oil — if furious bubbles form around the edges and it floats to the top of the oil, the temperature is right. One at a time, fry the tortillas for 1 to 2 minutes, flipping halfway through. The tortilla will puff up, become crispy, and turn golden-brown as it fries. (For a flatter tostada shell, press down with a metal spatula as it cooks.) Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate and sprinkle with a little salt. Repeat with the remaining tortillas.
5. To assemble, place two fried tortillas on each plate. Layer on the beans, corn, and tomatoes, dividing evenly between the four tortillas. Generously drizzle each tostada with more of the cilantro dressing and serve immediately.
