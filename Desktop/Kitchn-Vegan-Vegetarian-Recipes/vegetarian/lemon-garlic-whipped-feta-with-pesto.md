# Lemon-Garlic Whipped Feta with Pesto

**Diet:** vegetarian

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 1 pound feta cheese, preferably in brine
- 1/2 cup plain whole-milk Greek yogurt
- Finely grated zest of 1 medium lemon
- 1 clove
- garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon freshly squeezed lemon juice
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons basil pesto
- Pita chips, crostini, or crudités, for serving

## Method

1. Drain and crumble 1 pound feta cheese into a food processor. Pulse into small crumbs, 6 to 8 (1-second) pulses. Add 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt and process continually, scraping down the sides of the bowl as needed, until the mixture is whipped and very smooth, 2 1/2 to 3 minutes. (It will start out very clumpy but will eventually smooth and thin out.)
2. Add the finely grated zest of 1 medium lemon, 1 teaspoon lemon juice, 1 minced garlic clove, and a few generous grinds of black pepper. Process until combined, about 10 seconds more. Transfer to a serving bowl and drizzle with 2 tablespoons pesto. Serve with crackers, bread, or vegetables.
