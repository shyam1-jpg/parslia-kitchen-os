# These Herby, Garlicky, Buttery Mushrooms Are the Side You Need to Try

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 4 cloves
- garlic
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh herbs, such as rosemary, thyme, and chives, or a mixture
- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter
- 1 pound cremini mushrooms
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt

## Method

1. Mince 4 cloves of garlic. Chop until you have 1/4 cup fresh herbs, then place the garlic and herbs together in a bowl.
2. Melt 8 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add 1 pound cremini mushrooms and season with 1 teaspoon kosher salt. Cook undisturbed until browned on the bottom, about 5 minutes. Flip the mushrooms and cook undisturbed until browned on the second side and tender, about 5 minutes more. Add the garlic and herbs, and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.
