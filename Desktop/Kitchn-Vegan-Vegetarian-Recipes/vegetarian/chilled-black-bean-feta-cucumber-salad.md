# Chilled Black Bean, Feta & Cucumber Salad

**Diet:** vegetarian

Love this recipe? Our small but mighty editorial team made this free recipe perfect for you. Support our work and get everything we offer.

## Ingredients

- 3 (15-ounce) cans black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 (15-ounce) can whole-kernel corn (or about 1 1/2 cups fresh corn)
- 1/2 medium cucumber, finely diced
- 1 cup chopped cilantro
- 2 to 3
- limes, juiced
- 1/2 cup chopped celery or green onions
- 1 to 2 cups
- crumbled feta
- 1/3 cup red wine vinegar
- Sea salt, cracked black pepper, and cumin to taste

## Method

1. Combine the black beans, corn, cucumber, cilantro, celery, and feta in a large bowl and toss until mixed evenly. Add lime juice and vinegar, stir, and taste. You may want to add more lime to taste. Add a dash of salt, pepper, and cumin, and adjust to taste.
2. Chill for at least one hour before serving.
