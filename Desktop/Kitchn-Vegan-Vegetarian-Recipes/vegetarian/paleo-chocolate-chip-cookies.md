# Paleo Chocolate Chip Cookies

**Diet:** vegetarian

These cookies are soft and chewy, with just a touch of firmness around the edges, and have a very generous helping of melty chocolate bits.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups almond flour (not almond meal)
- 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup ghee, melted and cooled
- 1/4 cup coconut sugar
- 1 large
- egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3/4 cup dairy-free dark chocolate, chopped

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 350℉. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat; set aside.
2. Place the almond flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl and whisk to combine; set aside.
3. Place the ghee and coconut sugar in a large bowl and whisk until well-combined, about 1 minute. Whisk in the egg and vanilla. Scrape down the sides of the bowl if necessary. Add the flour mixture and stir until just combined. Fold in the chocolate chips.
4. Using a spoon or cookie scoop, measure 1 1/2 tablespoons of dough from the bowl. Roll between the palms of your hands to form a ball. Place on the prepared baking sheet about 1 1/2 inches apart, 12 cookies per sheet. Use a flat spatula or the bottom of a measuring cup to flatten each scoop into a roughly 1/2-inch-thick round disc.
5. Bake until the cookies are lightly browned around the edges, 8 to 10 minutes. Cool on the baking sheet for 2 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
