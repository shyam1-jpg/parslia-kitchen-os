# How To Make a Single Jar of Fruit Jam

**Diet:** vegetarian

Learn how to make a single jar of jam, a weekly ritual that will make the most of your fruit purchases.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups chopped fruit, such as strawberries and raspberries
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lemon juice
- #### Equipment
- Medium saucepan
- Wooden spoon
- Measuring cups and spoons

## Method

1. Wash and cut the fruit.** Wash and hull the fruit as needed. Cut large strawberries into quarters and peaches into bite-sized pieces.
2. Combine the fruit with sugar and mash.** Combine the fruit with the sugar and lemon juice in a medium, heavy-bottomed saucepan. Gently mash with a wooden spoon until the fruit is juicy and most of the sugar is dissolved.
3. Cook until jammy.** Cook over a medium heat, continuing to mashing down on the fruit with a wooden spoon. Bring to a rolling boil and continue to cook, stirring frequently, until the jam is thick and darkened in color, about 20 minutes.
4. Transfer the jam to a jar and refrigerate.** Carefully spoon into a very clean (8-ounce) jar and let cool to room temperature. Seal the jar and refrigerate.
