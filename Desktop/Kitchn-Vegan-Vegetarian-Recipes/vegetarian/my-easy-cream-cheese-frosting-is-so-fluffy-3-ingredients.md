# My Easy Cream Cheese Frosting Is So Fluffy (3 Ingredients!)

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces (2 sticks) unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 8 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- 1 pound powdered sugar (about 4 1/2 cups)
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt

## Method

1. Place 8 ounces room temperature unsalted butter in a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment. (Alternatively, use an electric hand mixer and large bowl.) Beat on medium speed until light and smooth, about 1 minute.
2. Add 8 ounces room temperature cream cheese and beat on medium speed until homogenous and light, about 3 minutes.
3. Stop the mixer and scrape down the sides of the bowl with a rubber spatula. Add 1 cup of the powdered sugar, return to medium speed, and beat until incorporated. Repeat scraping and beating in the remaining powdered sugar 3 to 4 more times.
4. Add 1 teaspoon vanilla extract and 1/2 teaspoon fine salt.
5. Beat the frosting again on medium speed until smooth and slightly increased in volume, 3 to 5 minutes.
