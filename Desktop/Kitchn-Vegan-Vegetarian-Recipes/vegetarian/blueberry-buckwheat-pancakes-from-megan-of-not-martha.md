# Blueberry Buckwheat Pancakes from Megan of Not Martha

**Diet:** vegetarian

Leela Cyd is a photographer and cookbook author. Her previous books include Food with Friends and Cooking Up Trouble. She lives in Santa Barbara with her husband and new baby son Izador Cosmo.

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup buckwheat flour
- 1/2 cup all–purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 2 tablespoons sugar
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted (I like to use Kerrygold Irish butter here, such a strong flavor)
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 2 eggs
- Zest from one lemon
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 to 3 cups
- blueberries (if using frozen, just make sure to thaw before using)

## Method

1. In a large bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, eggs, butter and vanilla. Set aside. In a smaller bowl, stir together all the dry ingredients. Pour the dry into the wet, mixing until just combined. Let sit for about five minutes. Meanwhile, put a large skillet on the stove on medium heat. When hot and sizzling, grease the pan and ladle about 1/4 cup of batter for each pancake. Carefully place blueberries into pancakes at this point, pressing them into the batter. Cook until bubbles are formed, flip and cook about 1 minute more. Keep warm in a 200°F oven until ready to serve.
2. Serve with plenty of maple syrup, extra blueberries and a cup of strong coffee.
