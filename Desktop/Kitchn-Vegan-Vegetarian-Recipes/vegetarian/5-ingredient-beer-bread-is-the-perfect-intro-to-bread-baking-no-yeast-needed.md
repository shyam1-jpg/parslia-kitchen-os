# 5-Ingredient Beer Bread Is the Perfect Intro to Bread Baking (No Yeast Needed!)

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: 5-Ingredient Beer Bread Is the Perfect Intro to Bread Baking (No Yeast Needed!)

## Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter, plus more for the pan
- 12 ounces room temperature beer (1 1/2 cups), such as lager or ale
- 1/4 cup honey
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- #### Equipment
- 9x5-inch loaf pan
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Mixing bowl
- Whisk
- Spatula
- Wire cooling rack
- Serrated knife

## Method

1. Heat the oven and prepare a loaf pan.**Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 350°F. Lightly coat a 9x5-inch loaf pan with butter, then line with a parchment paper sling that hangs off the 2 longer sides.
2. Melt the butter.**Cut 6 tablespoons unsalted butter into 6 pieces. Place in a large microwave-safe bowl and microwave in 10-second bursts until completely melted, 50 to 60 seconds total. (Alternatively, melt the butter on the stovetop and transfer to a large bowl.)
3. Combine the wet ingredients.**Add 12 ounces beer and 1/4 cup honey and whisk to combine.
4. Add dry ingredients and mix until just combined.**Add 3 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tablespoon baking powder, and 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and stir with a spatula just until no dry spots remain. Do not overmix, a few lumps are fine.
5. Transfer batter to the prepared pan.** Scrape the batter into the prepared pan and smooth the top.
6. Bake the bread.**Bake until browned and the center of the loaf springs back when gently pressed, 45 to 55 minutes.
7. Cool the bread.**Let the bread cool in the pan for 10 minutes. Remove the bread from the pan and cool completely on a wire rack.
