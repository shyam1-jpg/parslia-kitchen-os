# Caprese Avocado Toast

**Diet:** vegetarian

Love this recipe? Our small but mighty editorial team made this free recipe perfect for you. Support our work and get everything we offer.

## Ingredients

- 2 slices hearty sandwich bread, such as peasant bread, sourdough, whole-wheat or multi-grain
- 1 medium avocado, halved and pit removed
- 8 grape tomatoes, halved
- 2 ounces fresh _ciliegine_ or bite-sized mozzarella balls (about 12)
- 4 large fresh basil leaves, torn
- 2 tablespoons balsamic glaze

## Method

1. Toaste the bread. While the bread is toasting, mash the avocado in a small bowl.
2. Spread the mashed avocado over the toast. Top each slice with tomatoes, mozzarella balls, and basil leaves, then drizzle with balsamic glaze. Serve immediately.
