# This Cheap Pantry Ingredient Is the Secret to the Crispiest Fried Green Tomatoes

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: This Cheap Pantry Ingredient Is the Secret to the Crispiest Fried Green Tomatoes

## Ingredients

- #### For the tomatoes:
- 3 unripe green tomatoes (about 1 1/2 pounds total), cut crosswise into 1/4-inch thick slices
- 2/3 cup instant flour, such as Wondra, divided
- 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 3/4 teaspoon paprika
- 3/4 cup buttermilk
- 1 large egg white
- 1 1/2 teaspoons hot sauce, such as Louisiana or Tabasco
- 3/4 cup finely ground cornmeal
- 1/2 cup canola oil
- #### For the remoulade sauce:
- 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice (from 1 medium lemon)
- 1 tablespoon finely choppped fresh parsley leaves (from 2 sprigs)
- 1 medium scallion, finely chopped (optional)
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise, preferably Duke’s
- 1 tablespoon whole-grain or Creole mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon hot sauce, such as Louisiana or Tabasco
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 pinch
- cayenne pepper
- #### For the tomatoes:

## Method

1. Place 1/3 cup of the instant flour, 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt, and 3/4 teaspoon paprika in a wide, shallow bowl or pie plate. Stir or whisk to combine.
2. Place 3/4 cup buttermilk, 1 large egg white, and 1 1/2 teaspoons hot sauce in a second wide, shallow bowl. Whisk to combine.
3. Place the remaining 1/3 cup instant flour and 3/4 cup finely ground cornmeal in a third wide, shallow bowl. Stir or whisk to combine.
4. Fit a wire rack inside a rimmed baking sheet. Dip each tomato slice in in the following order, making sure it is completly coated before dipping in the next mixture: flour mixture, buttermilk mixture, and cornmeal mixture.
5. Place the breaded tomato slices on the rack in a single layer. Let sit so the coating sets while the oil heats.
6. Heat 1/2 cup canola oil in a large cast iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering, about 5 minutes. The oil should sizzle immediately if you sprinkle flour into it.
7. Fry in batches of 4 to 5 tomatoes slices: Carefully add to the hot oil and fry, turning occasionally, until golden-brown, 3 to 5 minutes per side. Transer the fried tomatoes to a paper towel-lined plate.
8. Serve the tomatoes immediately with the remoulade sauce.
9. Place 2 tablespoons lemon juice, 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh parsley, and 1 finely chopped medium scallion (if using), 1/2 cup mayonnaise, 1 tablespoon whole-grain or Creole mustard, 1/2 teaspoon paprika, 1/2 teaspoon hot sauce, 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder, 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1 pinch cayenne pepper in a small bowl. Whisk until combined. Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve.
