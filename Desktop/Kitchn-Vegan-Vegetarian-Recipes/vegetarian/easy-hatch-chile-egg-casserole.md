# Easy Hatch Chile Egg Casserole

**Diet:** vegetarian

The distinct smoky flavor of New Mexico Hatch Chiles in a cheesy baked egg casserole.

## Ingredients

- Cooking spray
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 medium russet potatoes (about 1 pound), peeled and grated on the large holes of a box grater
- 4 to 5
- medium roasted Hatch chiles, seeded and chopped (see Recipe Note)
- 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese
- 10 large eggs
- 1 cup whole or 2% milk
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt, divided
- #### For serving:
- Sour cream
- Sliced avocado
- Fresh cilantro

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 375°F. Lightly grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray or oil.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat until shimmering. Add the onion and cook, stirring frequently, until softened, about 5 minutes. Stir in the potatoes and chiles, and season with 1/2 teaspoon of the salt. Transfer the mixture to the baking dish and spread into an even layer. Top with the cheese.
3. Beat the eggs in a large bowl and whisk in the milk, sour cream, cilantro, and remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt. Pour over the vegetable mixture.
4. Bake until the top is light golden-brown and a knife inserted in the middle comes out clean, about 45 minutes. Let cool for 5 minutes before slicing and serving hot.
