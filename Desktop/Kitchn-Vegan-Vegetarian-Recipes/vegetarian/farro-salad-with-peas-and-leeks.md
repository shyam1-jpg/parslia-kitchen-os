# Farro Salad With Peas and Leeks

**Diet:** vegetarian

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup farro, soaked for one hour in water to cover and drained
- 2 cups water
- Salt
- 2 baby leeks (or 1 regular leek), white and light green parts only
- 1 pound peas, shelled (or 1 cup frozen peas)
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, divided
- 1/4 cup plain Greek-style yogurt
- Juice of half a lemon
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1 1/2 tablespoons coarsely chopped tarragon leaves

## Method

1. Cook and cool farro**: Combine farro, 2 cups water, and 1 teaspoon salt in a saucepan and bring to a boil. Cover, reduce heat, and simmer until tender, about 30 minutes. Drain farro and spread out on a baking sheet to cool.
2. Prepare leeks**: Meanwhile, clean the leeks, slice them in half lengthwise, and chop into 1/2-inch pieces. Sauté in 1 tablespoon olive oil until soft.
3. Blanch peas**: Have ready a bowl of ice water and a slotted spoon. Bring a pan of water to boil over high heat. Add a tablespoon of salt and the peas and boil just until bright green, about 30 seconds to 1 minute. Quickly remove the peas with a slotted spoon and plunge them into the ice bath to cool.
4. Assemble salad**: In a large bowl, whisk together 2 tablespoons olive oil, yogurt, lemon juice, and a few cracks of black pepper. Add the farro, leeks, peas, and tarragon and toss to combine. Season to taste and serve immediately or keep covered in the refrigerator.
