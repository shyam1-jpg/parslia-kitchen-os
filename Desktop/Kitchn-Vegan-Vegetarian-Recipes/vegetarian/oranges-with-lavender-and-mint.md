# Oranges With Lavender and Mint

**Diet:** vegetarian

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 2 teaspoons orange flower water
- 2 teaspoons honey
- 1/8 teaspoon dried lavender buds
- 3 medium Mandarin oranges
- 1-2
- mint sprigs

## Method

1. Combine orange flower water, honey, and lavender in a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer over low heat, stirring to dissolve the honey. Remove from heat.
2. Peel and cut the oranges into 1/4-inch thick slices and remove any seeds. Arrange on a plate and drizzle the syrup on top.
3. Garnish with mint and serve.
