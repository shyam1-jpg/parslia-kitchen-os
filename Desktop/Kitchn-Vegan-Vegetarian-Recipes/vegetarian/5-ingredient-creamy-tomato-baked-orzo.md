# 5-Ingredient Creamy Tomato Baked Orzo

**Diet:** vegetarian

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- Olive oil or cooking spray
- 1 (24-ounce) jar
- marinara sauce, such as Rao’s
- 2 cups water
- 1 pound dried orzo (about 2 1/2 cups)
- 1 (5-ounce) bag
- baby spinach (about 5 packed cups), coarsely chopped
- 1 cup whole-milk ricotta cheese (8 ounces)
- 1 (8-ounce) ball
- fresh mozzarella cheese, cut into 1/2-inch cubes

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 400°F. Coat a 9×13-inch baking dish with olive oil or cooking spray.
2. Place the marinara sauce and water in the baking dish and stir to combine. Add the orzo and spinach and stir to combine. Dollop the ricotta and sprinkle the cubed mozzarella evenly over the mixture and gently stir, keeping a few streaks of ricotta here and there. Spread the mixture into an even layer.
3. Cover the dish tightly with aluminum foil. Bake until the pasta is almost tender, about 30 minutes. Uncover and bake until the top is browned and has crisped up a bit, about 10 minutes more. Let cool for 5 to 10 minutes before serving.
