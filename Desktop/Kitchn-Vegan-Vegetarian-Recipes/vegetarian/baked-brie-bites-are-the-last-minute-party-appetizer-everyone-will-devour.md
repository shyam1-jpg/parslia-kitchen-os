# Baked Brie Bites Are the Last-Minute Party Appetizer Everyone Will Devour

**Diet:** vegetarian

This easy appetizer promises buttery puff pastry, creamy Brie, sweet-tart cranberry sauce, and toasted walnuts in every bite.

## Ingredients

- 1 (17-ounce) box
- frozen puff pastry (2 sheets)
- Cooking spray
- 6 ounces Brie cheese
- 1/2 cup cranberry sauce or chutney
- 1/4 cup chopped walnuts (optional)

## Method

1. Thaw 1 (17-ounce) box frozen puff pastry (2 sheets) in the refrigerator according to package directions. Refrigerate until ready to use.
2. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 400ºF. Lightly coat a mini muffin tin with cooking spray.
3. Cut 6 ounces Brie cheese into 1-inch cubes. Refrigerate while you prepare the puff pastry.
4. Unfold both sheets puff pastry. Cut each puff pastry sheet into 9 squares (18 squares total). Press each square into a mini muffin tin well. Add 1 Brie cheese cube to each well. Top each with 1 teaspoon cranberry sauce and 1/2 teaspoon chopped walnuts.
5. Bake until puffed and lightly browned around the edges, 18 to 20 minutes. Cool for 5 minutes before serving.
