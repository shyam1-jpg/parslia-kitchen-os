# Weekend Snack: Radish Toast

**Diet:** vegetarian

Leela Cyd is a photographer and cookbook author. Her previous books include Food with Friends and Cooking Up Trouble. She lives in Santa Barbara with her husband and new baby son Izador Cosmo.

## Ingredients

- Ficelle (or substitute a baguette, but I enjoy the smaller size of the ficelle for this recipe), cut into 1/2-inch-thick slices
- Generous pats of high-quality butter (I enjoy locally-made Larsen's Cremerie Classique butter)
- 6 radishes, finely sliced
- A few sprigs or flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
- Pinch of kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Toast your bread rounds to desired doneness (I like mine well-toasted). Spread butter on toast generously. Place radish slices on top of bread, and sprinkle with parsley, salt and pepper.
2. Delicious with tea or a glass of wine.
