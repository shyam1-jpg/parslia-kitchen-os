# Yogurt Barley Soup

**Diet:** vegetarian

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1 medium white or yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 1/2 cups cooked pearl barley
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh parsley, plus more sprigs for garnish
- 1 teaspoon dried mint
- 1 teaspoon salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 3 1/2 cups vegetable stock
- 1 1/2 cups plain yogurt, room temperature
- Sweet paprika for garnish (optional)

## Method

1. Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium-low heat. Add onion and sauté until translucent.
2. Add barley, parsley, mint, salt, and pepper and stir to coat.
3. Add vegetable stock and bring to a boil.
4. Remove from heat and stir in yogurt. If soup is too cool after the addition of the yogurt, you can gently heat it through, but don't boil it or the yogurt will curdle.
5. Serve hot or chilled. Garnish with parsley and paprika, if desired.
