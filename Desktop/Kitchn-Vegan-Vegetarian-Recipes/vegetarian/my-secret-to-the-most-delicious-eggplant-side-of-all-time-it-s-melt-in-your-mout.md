# My Secret to the Most Delicious Eggplant Side of All Time (It's Melt-in-Your-Mouth Tender)

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: My Secret to the Most Delicious Eggplant Side of All Time (It's Melt-in-Your-Mouth Tender)

## Ingredients

- 2 cloves
- garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh oregano (from 4 sprigs)
- 1/3 cup olive oil
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1 1/2 pounds globe eggplant (about 2 small or 1 medium), cut crosswise into 1/2-inch thick rounds
- 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar

## Method

1. Heat an outdoor grill for medium-high, direct heat (about 425ºF).
2. Stir 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 tablespoon finely chopped fresh oregano, 1/3 cup olive oil, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes in a small bowl until combined.
3. Place 1 1/2 pounds eggplant slices in a single layer on a baking sheet. Brush the olive oil mixture onto both sides of the slices.
4. ​​When the grill is ready, scrape the grill grates clean if needed. Place the eggplant on the grill in a single layer. Cover and cook until grill marks form on the bottom, 3 to 5 minutes. Flip the eggplant, cover, and grill until grill marks form on the second side and the eggplant is very tender, 2 to 3 minutes more. Transfer to a platter and drizzle with 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar.
