# Yogurt Pretzel Bark

**Diet:** vegetarian

Take everything you love about yogurt-covered pretzels and turn them into this strawberry-filled yogurt bark. It's the perfect after-school snack.

## Ingredients

- 1 pound chopped white chocolate (not white baking chips or morsels)
- 1/2 cup yogurt powder
- 1 cup mini pretzel twists, divided
- 1 ounce freeze-dried strawberries, divided

## Method

1. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper and set aside.
2. Microwave the white chocolate in a large microwave-safe bowl on high in 30-second bursts, stirring after each burst, until the white chocolate is mostly melted — a few chunks of unmelted chocolate are welcome.
3. Stir in the yogurt powder until completely incorporated. Add 1/2 of the pretzels, crushing them up a bit as you do, and half of the freeze-dried strawberries. Fold these into the yogurt-chocolate mixture. Pour the mixture out onto the prepared baking sheet.
4. Spread the yogurt-chocolate mixture out into a 9x13-inch rectangle. Top with the remaining pretzels and strawberries. Press the pretzels down gently to ensure they stick as the chocolate sets. Let sit at room temperature until set, at least 1 hour, before cutting into bite-sized pieces.
