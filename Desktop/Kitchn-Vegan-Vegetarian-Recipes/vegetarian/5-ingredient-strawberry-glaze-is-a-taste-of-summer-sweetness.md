# 5-Ingredient Strawberry Glaze Is a Taste of Summer Sweetness

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 pound strawberries (about 4 cups)
- 1/2 medium lemon
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Method

1. Hull and coarsely chop 1 pound strawberries (about 3 1/2 cups). Juice 1/2 medium lemon until you have 1 tablespoon.
2. Place 1/2 cup granulated sugar, 1/4 cup water, 2 teaspoons cornstarch, and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract in a medium saucepan and whisk until smooth. Add the strawberries. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, mashing the berries with a potato masher or the back of a wooden spoon, about 5 minutes. Continue to boil until the sauce is thickened enough to coat the back of a spoon, about 5 minutes. Add the lemon juice and stir to combine. Remove from the heat.
3. Place a fine-mesh strainer over a heatproof bowl. Strain the strawberry mixture, using a spatula to press on the solids to release all the juices and also scraping the underside of the strainer occasionally. Use warm or refrigerate until chilled and use cold.
