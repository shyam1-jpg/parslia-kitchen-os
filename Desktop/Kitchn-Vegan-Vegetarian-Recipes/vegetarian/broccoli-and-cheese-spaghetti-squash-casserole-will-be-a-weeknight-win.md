# Broccoli and Cheese Spaghetti Squash Casserole Will Be a Weeknight Win

**Diet:** vegetarian

Tender strands of spaghetti squash are tossed with crisp-tender broccoli and enveloped in a fluffy, cheesy custard, giving you all the best parts of the classic side.

## Ingredients

- 1 (2 1/2 to 3-pound) cooked, halved spaghetti squash
- 8 ounces sharp cheddar cheese, shredded (about 2 cups)
- 16 ounces cottage cheese (2 cups)
- 3 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 3 cups (1-inch) broccoli florets (from about 2 large heads)
- 2 tablespoons water

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 350°F. Meanwhile, scrape the strands from a cooked spaghetti squash out with a fork. Grate 8 ounces sharp cheddar cheese on the largest hole of a box grater (about 2 cups). Reserve 1/2 cup for sprinkling, then place the remaining cheddar cheese in a large bowl. Add 16 ounces cottage cheese, 3 large eggs, 1 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, and stir to combine.
2. Place 3 cups (1-inch) broccoli florets and 2 tablespoons water in a large ovenproof skillet. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the broccoli is crisp-tender and the water evaporates, 2 to 3 minutes. Remove the skillet from the heat. Add the spaghetti squash and cheese mixture and stir until combined. Smooth the top into an even layer, then sprinkle with the reserved cheddar cheese.
3. Bake until set and a thin knife inserted into the center comes out clean, about 1 hour.
