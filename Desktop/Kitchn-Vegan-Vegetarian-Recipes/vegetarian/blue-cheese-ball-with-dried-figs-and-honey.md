# Blue Cheese Ball with Dried Figs and Honey

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
- 8 ounces crumbled blue cheese
- 1/3 cup dried figs, finely chopped
- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1/4 cup toasted sliced almonds

## Method

1. In a large bowl, combine the softened cream cheese and crumbled blue cheese together using a rubber spatula. Fold in the chopped figs and honey.
2. Spread the sliced almonds on a large plate. Form the cheese mixture into a ball. Roll the ball on the plate with the almonds, making sure it is completely coated. Wrap the cheese ball in plastic wrap and refrigerate until firm (at least 2 hours). Serve with crispy baguette toasts or crackers.
