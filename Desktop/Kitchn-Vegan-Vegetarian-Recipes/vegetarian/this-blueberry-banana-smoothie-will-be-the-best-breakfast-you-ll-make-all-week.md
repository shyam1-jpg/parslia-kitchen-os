# This Blueberry-Banana Smoothie Will Be the Best Breakfast You'll Make All Week

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: This Blueberry-Banana Smoothie Will Be the Best Breakfast You'll Make All Week

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups frozen blueberries
- 2 peeled and roughly chopped medium frozen banana (about 4 ounces frozen banana)
- 2 pitted Medjool dates
- 2 tablespoons chia seeds
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon
- ground cinnamon
- Pinch
- kosher salt
- 1 1/2 cups unsweetened milk of choice

## Method

1. Add 1 1/2 cups frozen blueberries, 1 frozen banana, 2 pitted Medjool dates, 2 tablespoons chia seeds, 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon, pinch of salt, and 1 1/2 cups milk to a blender. Blend on high speed until smooth and creamy, about 1 minute.
2. Pour into 2 glasses and serve immediately.
