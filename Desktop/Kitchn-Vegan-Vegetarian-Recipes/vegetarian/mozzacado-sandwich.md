# Mozzacado Sandwich

**Diet:** vegetarian

Love this recipe? Our small but mighty editorial team made this free recipe perfect for you. Support our work and get everything we offer.

## Ingredients

- 4 thick slices
- sourdough bread
- 1 medium avocado
- 1 small ripe tomato
- 4 thick slices
- whole-milk mozzarella
- 6 to 8
- fresh basil leaves
- Flaky sea salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Lightly toast the bread. Meanwhile, smash the pitted avocado with a little salt and pepper. Spread a thick layer of smashed avocado on the warm bread. Layer the cheese with the basil and tomato over the avocado.
2. Make two sandwiches, slice, share with someone you love, and enjoy!
