# How To Make a Perfect Strawberry Milkshake

**Diet:** vegetarian

Packed with strawberry flavor.** Fresh strawberries and strawberry ice cream team up to give every sip plenty of sweet strawberry goodness.

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces fresh strawberries, hulled and thinly sliced, plus more for garnish
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3/4 to 1 cup
- whole milk
- 1 tablespoon malted milk powder or regular powdered milk (optional)
- 1 pint
- high-quality strawberry ice cream (no fake flavors or dye)

## Method

1. Place 8 ounces thinly sliced strawberries in a medium bowl, sprinkle with 2 tablespoons granulated sugar and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, and toss to combine. Place in the freezer for about 1 hour.
2. Meanwhile, place 2 pint glasses or 4 smaller glasses in the freezer to chill.
3. Place the strawberries in a blender, and scrape in all the syrupy juice that has accumulated at the bottom of the bowl. Add 3/4 cup whole milk and blend until the strawberries are pulverized. There should be no big chunks left at all.
4. Add 1 tablespoons malted milk powder or powdered milk if desired and blend. (_\_Note:\__ The malted milk powder gives just a bit of extra depth and a hint of malty flavor without turning the shake into a true malt. If you opt to use regular powdered milk this also will just add a bit of richness and creaminess. But the powders are not necessary.)
5. Take the blender jar off the base, and add 1 pint strawberry ice cream. Stir it into the milk and strawberries by hand. Blend thoroughly. If it won’t blend very well or gets stuck, carefully add up to 1/4 cup more milk. Stir or shake if necessary.
6. Pour the milkshake out into the chilled glasses and garnish with strawberries.
