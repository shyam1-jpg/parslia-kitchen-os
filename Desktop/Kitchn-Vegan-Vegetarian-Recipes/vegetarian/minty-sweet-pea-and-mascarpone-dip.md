# Minty Sweet Pea and Mascarpone Dip

**Diet:** vegetarian

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups fresh peas, shelled (or frozen peas, defrosted)
- 1 clove
- garlic, minced
- 1/2 lemon, zested and juiced
- 1/4 cup mint leaves, finely chopped
- 1/2 cup mascarpone cheese
- Salt and pepper
- Fresh vegetables, pita bread, or crackers to serve

## Method

1. If using fresh peas, add to a pot of salted boiling water, and cook for about 3 to 5 minutes, until tender. Drain the water and cool the peas completely.
2. Add peas, garlic, lemon zest and juice, and mint to the bowl of a food processor, and process until smooth. Stir in the mascarpone and season to taste with salt and pepper.
3. Serve with your favorite fresh vegetables, pita bread, or crackers.
