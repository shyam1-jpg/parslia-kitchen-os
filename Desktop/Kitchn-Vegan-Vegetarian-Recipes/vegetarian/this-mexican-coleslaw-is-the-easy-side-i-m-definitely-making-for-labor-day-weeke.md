# This Mexican Coleslaw Is the Easy Side I’m Definitely Making for Labor Day Weekend

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: This Mexican Coleslaw Is the Easy Side I’m Definitely Making for Labor Day Weekend

## Ingredients

- #### For the coleslaw:
- 1 tablespoon neutral oil, such as vegetable
- 3 cups fresh corn kernels (3 to 4 ears)
- 1 small green or red cabbage (about 2 pounds), cored and thinly sliced (10 cups)
- 1 medium red onion, thinly sliced (1 cup)
- #### For the dressing:
- 1/2 cup Mexican crema
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 2 ounces Cotija cheese, crumbled (1/2 cup), plus more for garnish
- 1/4 cup coarsely chopped fresh cilantro (optional)
- 1/4 cup freshly squeezed lime juice (2 medium limes)
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika, plus more for garnish
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more as needed
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, plus more for garnish
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- #### Char the corn:

## Method

1. Heat 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a medium 10-inch skillet over medium heat until shimmering. Add 3 cups corn kernels and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned in spots, 4 minutes. Transfer to a bowl and let cool, stirring occasionally, until room temperature, about 20 minutes. Meanwhile, make the dressing.
2. Stir 1/2 cup Mexican crema, 1/4 cup mayonnaise, 2 ounces crumbled Cotija cheese, 1/4 cup coarsely chopped fresh cilantro if using, 1/4 cup lime juice, 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, and 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper together in a large bowl until combined.
3. When the corn is ready, add 1 cored and thinly sliced small cabbage and 1 thinly sliced medium red onion to the dressing, and toss until evenly coated in dressing. Add the corn and toss to combine. Taste and season with more kosher salt as needed. Garnish with more chopped fresh cilantro, smoked paprika, black pepper, and Cotija cheese if desired.
