# How To Make a Pull-Apart Rugelach Ring

**Diet:** vegetarian

Tami is a writer, recipe developer, culinary educator, and caterer. She is a graduate of the Professional Culinary Arts program at the Institute for Culinary Education (ICE) in New York.

## Ingredients

- #### For the dough:
- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter (3 ounces), at room temperature
- 3 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 2/3 cups all-purpose flour, plus about 1/4 cup for dusting
- 3/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt
- Cooking spray
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 teaspoon water
- #### For the filling:
- 1 1/2 tablespoons unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1 1/2 teaspoons unsweetened natural cocoa powder
- 1/4 cup powdered sugar
- 1/8 teaspoon fine salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/3 cup very finely chopped semisweet or dark chocolate (about 2 ounces)
- Turbinado or other coarse sugar, for sprinkling
- #### Equipment
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Stand mixer or handheld electric mixer and mixing bowl
- Rubber spatula
- Mixing bowls
- Plastic wrap
- Rolling pin
- Rimmed baking sheet
- Parchment paper
- Pastry scraper
- Small sharp knife
- Pastry brush

## Method

1. Mix the butter, cream cheese, and sugar:** In a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, or with a handheld electric mixer and large bowl, beat the butter, cream cheese, and sugar on medium-low speed until very soft, completely smooth, and lighter in color and texture, 3 1/2 to 4 minutes. Stop the mixer and scrape down the sides of the bowl with a rubber spatula.
2. Add the egg and vanilla:** Add the egg and vanilla and beat on medium-low speed until combined. Stop the mixer and scrape down the sides of the bowl with the rubber spatula again.
3. Add the flour mixture:** Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt together in a medium bowl until combined. With the mixer on low speed, beat the flour into the butter mixture until fully incorporated, stopping the mixer and scraping the sides of the bowl once. This is a tacky dough that will gather around the paddle. Remove the paddle or beater and stir 2 to 3 times by hand with the rubber spatula to ensure that the cream cheese and butter mixture is fully incorporated and there are no streaks.
4. Shape and chill the dough:** Transfer the dough onto a large piece of plastic wrap. Shape and pat it into a 6x8-inch rectangle. Wrap it tightly in the plastic and refrigerate until thoroughly chilled, about 1 hour.
5. Make the filling:** Meanwhile, combine the butter, cocoa powder, sugar, salt, and cinnamon in a small bowl and mix with a spoon until smooth and well-incorporated. Cover with plastic wrap and set aside.
6. Prepare to bake:** Coat a rimmed baking sheet with cooking spray, line it with parchment paper, and set aside.
7. Prepare to roll:** Drizzle about 1/2 teaspoon of water on a work surface and place a piece of parchment paper on top. (The water will secure the paper so it does not move much, but it will dry as you work, allowing the parchment to help you shape the dough.) Heavily dust the parchment and a rolling pin with flour. Place the dough onto it and dust the top with a little flour.
8. Roll the dough:** Roll the dough into an 8x12-inch rectangle, between 1/8- and 1/4-inch thick.
9. Add the filling:** Spread the filling mixture with the rubber spatula over the rectangle, leaving a 1/2- to 3/4-inch border on the long sides. Scatter the chocolate evenly over the top.
10. Roll up the rugelach:** Starting with one of the long sides, roll the dough up and over the filling, jelly-roll style, to form a tube, using the parchment paper to assist you as necessary. Add a little more flour under any area that sticks. Dust off any excess flour on the outside, especially at the seam and the open edges at the ends.
11. Move the rugelach tube to the baking sheet:** Gently lift the rugelach tube onto the prepared baking sheet and place it seam-side down.
12. Refrigerate the dough:** Cover the pan with plastic wrap and refrigerate until the dough is chilled, about 30 minutes.
13. Partially cut the rugelach roll:** Uncover the rugelach and reserve the plastic wrap. Using a sharp knife, cut the log crosswise, making cuts that are 2/3- to 3/4-inch apart and 3/4 of the way through the log, cutting all the way to the bottom, but leaving a border of about 1/2-inch uncut on the other long side, so that the rugelach roll is still a single entity with a very thick fringe. You should have 16 slashes.
14. Form the cut tube into a ring shape:** Bend the cut tube into a ring, make sure that the exposed ends touch each other, and gently pinch them together. Dust off any excess flour with a pastry brush.
15. Spread the cookies apart:** Gently spread open the slashed portions about 1/4- to 1/2-inch apart so they do not touch each other.
16. Refrigerate the filled, cut ring:** Cover the rugelach again with the reserved plastic wrap and refrigerate until chilled, about 30 minutes. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 350°F.
17. Coat with egg wash:** Whisk the egg yolk and and 1 teaspoon water together in a small bowl. Brush the egg wash over the rugelach ring and between the cut pieces. Sprinkle turbinado sugar evenly over the top.
18. Bake the rugelach:** Bake for 20 minutes. Rotate the pan from front to back, and bake until the rugelach ring is a warm golden-brown, 8 to 10 minutes more. Cool completely on the baking sheet, about 1 hour. Carefully transfer to a large serving tray or dish and serve.
