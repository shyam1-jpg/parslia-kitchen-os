# How To Make Starbucks-Style Sweet Cream at Home

**Diet:** vegetarian

Love Starbucks' Sweet Cream for iced coffee? Here's how to make it yourself at home.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (1 pint) half-and-half
- 1/2 cup sweetened condensed milk
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- #### Equipment
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Pitcher or jar

## Method

1. Combine the half-and-half, sweetened condensed milk, and vanilla.** Pour the half-and-half into a large liquid measuring cup or bowl. Slowly pour in the sweetened condensed milk — it will sink to the bottom of the cup. Add the vanilla.
2. Whisk to combine.** Whisk to combine and break up the sweetened condensed milk until the mixture is homogenous, about 1 minute.
3. Transfer to a jar for long-term storage.** Use the sweet cream right away or transfer to a jar or airtight container for longer-term storage in the refrigerator.
