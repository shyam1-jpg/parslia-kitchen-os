# This Spiced Cranberry Sauce Is So Good, You’ll Want It on Everything

**Diet:** vegetarian

Megan is a freelance writer, recipe developer and cookbook writer. Her first book, Whole-Grain Mornings, (Ten Speed Press) is available in bookstores nationwide.

## Ingredients

- 12 ounces fresh or frozen cranberries (about 3 cups, do not thaw frozen)
- 1/2 cup honey
- 1/2 cup water
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped candied ginger
- 1 (about 3-inch)
- cinnamon stick
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/8 teaspoon ground cloves
- Finely grated zest of 1 medium orange (about 1 tablespoon)
- 1 tablespoon packed dark or light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon Grand Marnier (optional)

## Method

1. Stir 12 ounces cranberries, 1/2 cup honey, 1/2 cup water, 2 tablespoons finely chopped candied ginger, 1 (about 3-inch) cinnamon stick, 1/4 teaspoon ground cardamom, 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg, and 1/8 teaspoon ground cloves together in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring occasionally.
2. Reduce the heat to medium-low. Cover and simmer until the most of the berries have burst and the sauce thickens, 7 to 10 minutes.
3. Stir in the finely grated zest of 1 medium orange, 1 tablespoon packed brown sugar, and 1 tablespoon Grand Marnier if desired. Simmer uncovered until the flavors meld, about 2 minutes more. Turn off the heat. Remove and discard the cinnamon stick. Transfer to a serving bowl or airtight container and let cool completely. It will thicken up as it cools.
