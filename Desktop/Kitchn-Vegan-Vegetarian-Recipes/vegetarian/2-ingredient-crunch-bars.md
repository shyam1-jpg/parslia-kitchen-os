# 2-Ingredient Crunch Bars

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 16 ounces good-quality milk chocolate (not chocolate chips), chopped
- 1 cup crisped rice cereal
- Flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Line a 8x8-inch baking dish with parchment paper, leaving a 2-inch overhang on two opposite sides.
2. Melt the chocolate in a double boiler or medium saucepan over low heat, stirring regularly, until thin and glistening. Immediately add the rice cereal and stir until evenly coated. Transfer the chocolate mixture to the prepared pan and use a spatula to spread in an even layer. Sprinkle with the salt. Refrigerate until the chocolate is set, about 1 hour.
3. Grasp the excess parchment and lift the chocolate slab out of the pan and onto a cutting board. Cut into 24 pieces and serve.
