# It's Not Diwali Without My Mom's Sooji Halwa

**Diet:** vegetarian

Rachel is an immigrant from Mumbai, India who is now based out of Brooklyn, New York. She is a chef, photographer and food editor who loves to cook and develop delicious recipes from her culture and beyond to share on her social channels.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups water
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup ghee
- 1 cup sooji (rava or semolina flour)
- 1/4 cup dried, finely shredded (desiccated) unsweetened coconut
- 1/3 cup sliced almonds
- Fresh edible rose petals, for garnish

## Method

1. Place 3 cups water and 1 cup granulated sugar in a small saucepan and bring to a simmer over medium-high heat. When the sugar is dissolved, remove from the heat.
2. Heat 1/2 cup ghee in a large, heavy-bottomed saucepan over medium heat. Add 1 cup sooji and cook, stirring constantly, until deep golden brown, about 18 minutes.
3. Add the sugar water and cook until the liquid evaporates and the mixture starts resembling a thick cake batter, about 6 minutes. Remove from the heat, sprinkle with 1/4 cup desiccated coconut, and stir to combine.
4. Sprinkle with 1/3 cup sliced almonds and garnish with rose petals. Serve immediately.
