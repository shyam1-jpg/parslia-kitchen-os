# Easy Roasted Vegetable Enchiladas

**Diet:** vegetarian

These easy veggie-driven enchiladas are packed with loads of flavor. From a cilantro-lime yogurt topping to smoky roasted veggies, this naturally gluten-free meal is a lighter take on hearty enchiladas.

## Ingredients

- #### For the enchiladas:
- 1 medium sweet potato (about 6 ounces), small dice
- 1 cup corn kernels, thawed if frozen
- 2 cloves
- garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lime juice
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon chili powder
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more as needed
- 1/8 teaspoon ground black pepper
- 1 medium poblano pepper, peeled, seeded, and cut into small dice
- 1 (15-ounce) can black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup shredded Monterey Jack cheese (about 4 ounces), divided
- 10 (6- to 8-inch) corn tortillas
- 2 cups red enchilada sauce (16 ounces), such as Frontera Red Chile Enchilada Sauce, divided
- #### For the cilantro-lime yogurt sauce:
- 1 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 1 cup loosely packed fresh cilantro, plus more for garnish
- Finely grated zest of 1 medium lime
- 2 to 3 tablespoons
- freshly squeezed lime juice
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 400°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil. Place the sweet potatoes and corn on the baking sheet. Sprinkle with the garlic, oil, lime juice, cumin, chili powder, cayenne, salt, and pepper and toss to combine. Spread into an even layer. Roast until the sweet potatoes are tender, about 30 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, roast the poblano pepper on top of a gas burner until all sides are blackened. (If you don't have a gas burner, roast in a 450°F oven after the sweet potatoes are ready for 20 to 25 minutes.) After all sides are blackened, place the pepper in a bowl. Cover with plastic wrap and let sit for 15 minutes to steam. Rub off the blackened skin, remove the core and seeds, and cut the flesh into small pieces. Transfer to a large bowl.
3. Add the roasted vegetables, beans, and cheese, and stir to combine. Warm up your tortillas, one by one in a skillet, or all at once in a microwave so they don’t break when you bend them.
4. Spread 1/2 cup of the enchilada sauce in the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Working with 1 tortilla at a time, spread 1/4 cup filling on the lower third of the tortilla. Carefully roll it up, then place seam-side down in the baking dish. Repeat with the remaining tortillas and filling. Evenly pour the remaining enchilada sauce over the enchiladas.
5. Bake uncovered until heated through, about 15 minutes. Meanwhile, place all the sauce ingredients in a food processor or blender and blend until smooth.
6. Let the enchiladas cool for about 5 minutes. Drizzle with the yogurt sauce and top with cilantro more if desired before serving.
