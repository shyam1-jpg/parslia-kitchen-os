# What to Make When You Buy Rhubarb Like a Romantic and Don't Know How to Close the Deal

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: What to Make When You Buy Rhubarb Like a Romantic and Don't Know How to Close the Deal

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup cherry jam
- 1/4 cup Crème de Cassis
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 2 dried dates, pitted and thinly sliced
- 1 pound rhubarb, trimmed
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, cut into small pieces
- Salt

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 400°F.
2. Whisk the jam, Crème de Cassis, sugar, and dates together in a large bowl. Cut the rhubarb lengthwise into 1/2-inch long sticks, then cut crosswise into 3-inches pieces. Add to the jam mixture and toss, evenly coating each piece as best you can.
3. Pour the rhubarb into a 9x13-inch baking dish, dot with the butter, and sprinkle with a pinch of salt. Tightly cover with aluminum foil.
4. Bake until the rhubarb is knife tender, 20 to 25 minutes.The rhubarb will create a lot of liquid, which is lovely served with the compote. Any extra liquid can be reduced in a saucepan over the stove to create a syrup that's delicious for cocktails or pancakes.
