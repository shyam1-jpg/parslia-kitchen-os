# Classic PB&J Snack Cake

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- #### For the cake:
- Cooking spray or butter
- 2 1/3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 1/2 cup smooth peanut butter, at room temperature
- 3 large eggs
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup buttermilk or whole milk
- #### For the PB&J swirl:
- 1/2 cup smooth peanut butter, at room temperature
- 1/3 cup powdered sugar
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup grape jelly
- 1 teaspoon hot water

## Method

1. Make the cake**: Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 350°F. Generously coat a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray or butter; set aside.
2. Place the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl and whisk to combine; and set aside.
3. Place the sugar, oil, and peanut butter in the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment. (Alternatively, use an electric hand mixer and large bowl.) Beat on medium speed until smooth, about 2 minutes. Beat in the eggs one at a time, followed by the vanilla. On low speed, add 1/2 of the flour mixture and beat until just combined. Slowly pour in all of the buttermilk or milk and beat until just combined. Add the remaining flour mixture and beat just until smooth — do not overmix.
4. Pour into the baking dish and smooth out the top; set aside.
5. Make the swirl**: Place the peanut butter, powdered sugar, oil, and vanilla in a small bowl and whisk until smooth and combined; set aside. Place the jelly and water in a small bowl and whisk until loosened (it will not be completely smooth).
6. Dollop teaspoonfuls of the grape jelly onto the cake batter, spacing them about 1 inch apart. Dollop heaping teaspoonfuls of the peanut butter mixture between the jelly. Drag the tip of a table knife through the dollops of peanut butter and jelly to create a swirl pattern, making sure to drag the pattern to the edges of the baking dish.
7. Bake until a tester inserted in the center comes out clean, 30 to 40 minutes (metal pans will bake faster than ceramic or glass). Cool completely on a wire rack before cutting into squares.
