# Appetizer Recipe: Garlic & Coriander Quick-Pickled Baby Carrots

**Diet:** vegetarian

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 1 pound farmers' market baby carrots
- 1 cup water, plus more for blanching the carrots
- 1 cup white wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon kosher salt
- 2 tablespoons honey, or to taste
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 garlic clove, smashed
- 1 teaspoon coriander seeds, toasted
- 1 teaspoon mustard seeds, toasted

## Method

1. Trim and peel the carrots and cut them in half lengthwise.
2. Bring a saucepan of water to a boil. Add the carrots and blanch until crisp-tender, about 2 minutes. Immediately drain and run under cold water. Transfer the carrots to a bowl (or jars if you plan to keep the carrots for longer).
3. Combine 1 cup of water with the vinegar, salt, honey, bay leaf, garlic, coriander, and mustard seeds in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil and stir to dissolve the salt and honey. Pour over the carrots.
4. Let cool to room temperature and then refrigerate in an airtight container for at least an hour and up to a month.
