# I Tried Caramelizing My Strawberries for a Summer Salad, and I’m Never Going Back

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: I Tried Caramelizing My Strawberries for a Summer Salad, and I’m Never Going Back

## Ingredients

- #### For the dressing and strawberries
- 1/3 cup Boursin Garlic & Fine Herbs cheese (about 3 ounces), at room temperature
- 2 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 pound strawberries, hulled and halved or quartered if large
- 1 teaspoon balsamic vinegar
- 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice (1 medium lemon)
- 1 tablespoon water, plus more as needed
- 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
- 1 tablespoon poppyseeds
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- Kosher salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- #### For the salad base
- 2 medium romaine lettuce hearts, halved lengthwise and cut crosswise into 1-inch-wide strips (about 8 cups)
- 1 medium shallot, thinly sliced (about 1/4 cup)
- 1/3 cup fresh mint leaves, torn if large, divided
- 1/3 cup toasted almonds, hazelnuts, or pecans, coarsely chopped
- #### Make the dressing and strawberries

## Method

1. Heat 1 teaspoon of the extra-virgin olive oil in a medium nonstick skillet over medium-high until shimmering. Add 1 pound halved strawberries cut-side down (it’s ok if they don’t all lay flat) and cook undisturbed until browned on the bottom and slightly softened, about 3 minutes.
2. Remove the skillet from the heat. Add 1 teaspoon balsamic vinegar and gently stir to coat strawberries; let cool slightly or to room temperature.
3. Whisk 1/3 cup room temperature Boursin cheese, the remaining 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, 2 tablespoons lemon juice, 1 tablespoon water, 1 tablespoon granulated sugar, 1 tablespoon poppyseeds, and 1 teaspoon Dijon vinegar together in a small bowl. Thin out by whisking in a tablespoon or two of water if needed to loosen to a pourable consistency. Taste and season with kosher salt and black pepper as needed.
4. Place 2 sliced medium romaine lettuce hearts, 1 thinly sliced medium shallot, half of the fresh mint leaves (about 3 tablespoons), and 2/3 of the dressing (about 1/2 cup) in a large serving bowl, and toss to coat. Top with the caramelized strawberries and 1/3 cup coarsely chopped toasted almonds. Drizzle with the remaining dressing. Sprinkle with the remaining fresh mint leaves.
