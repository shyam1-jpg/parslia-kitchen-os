# Baked Apples with Cranberry Sauce

**Diet:** vegetarian

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 4 apples
- 1/2 lemon
- 2/3 cup cranberry sauce (or less, depending on size of apples)
- 1 tablespoon butter
- Optional additions to cranberry sauce: spices (allspice, cinnamon, cloves), ginger (ground, freshly grated, or candied), orange or lemon zest, dried fruit, chopped nuts

## Method

1. Preheat oven to 350°F.
2. Core apples (using an apple corer, melon baller, or paring knife), leaving 1/2 inch intact at the bottom. Peel top 1 inch of each apple and rub with lemon to prevent discoloration.
3. If desired, mix additional ingredients into cranberry sauce.
4. Fill apples with cranberry sauce. Top each with a 1/4 tablespoon pat of butter.
5. Place apples in a baking dish and fill with 1/2 inch water.
6. Bake, basting every 15 minutes, until apples are tender when pierced with a knife, about 45-60 minutes.
7. Serve warm. If desired, serve with yogurt, whipped cream, crème fraîche, or ice cream.
