# Pesto Tortellini Pasta Salad

**Diet:** vegetarian

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 2 (9-ounce) packages fresh cheese tortellini
- 2 pints
- cherry tomatoes (about 4 cups), halved
- 1 (12-ounce) jar fire-roasted red peppers, drained and chopped (1 heaping cup)
- 1/3 cup basil pesto

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the tortellini according to package instructions. Drain and run briefly under cool water to cool the tortellini down. Drain well.
2. Place the cooked tortellini, tomatoes, and peppers in a large bowl and toss to combine. Add the pesto and toss to combine.
