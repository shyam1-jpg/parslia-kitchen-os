# Hasselback Apples

**Diet:** vegetarian

The Hasselback technique isn't just for potatoes. Slicing thinly makes apples better for baking, and so does our oat topping.

## Ingredients

- 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
- 3 tablespoons packed brown sugar, divided
- 3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon, divided
- 2 large, firm apples, such as Honey Crisp or Pink Lady
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 1 tablespoon old-fashioned rolled oats
- Salt
- Cooking spray
- Ice cream and caramel sauce, for serving (optional)
- #### Equipment
- Knife
- Measuring spoons
- Small saucepan (optional)
- Small bowls
- Vegetable peeler
- Melon baller
- Pastry brush
- Small baking dish
- Aluminum foil

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 400°F.** Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Melt the butter.** Melt 1 tablespoon of the butter and cool. Add 1 tablespoon of the brown sugar and 1/2 teaspoon of the cinnamon and stir to combine; set aside.
3. Prepare the apples.** Peel the apples, then cut each in half through the stem. Use a melon baller or spoon to remove the apple cores.
4. Cut slices in the apples, leaving the bottom intact.** Place the apples cut-side down on a cutting board. Cut parallel slices into the apple, 1/4-inch apart, stopping just before you cut through the bottom. (This keeps the slices connected at the bottom of the apple.)
5. Brush the apples with the melted butter and sugar.** Brush the butter mixture over top of the apples and between the slices.
6. Bake the apples for 20 minutes.** Coat a small baking dish with cooking spray. Place the apples in the baking dish, cut-side down. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 20 minutes. Meanwhile, prepare the streusel.
7. Make the streusel.** Cut the remaining 2 tablespoons butter into cubes. Place in a small bowl and add the remaining 2 tablespoons brown sugar, remaining 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon, flour, oats, and a pinch of salt. Use your fingers or a fork to blend the ingredients together to form moist clumps.
8. Top the apples with streusel.** After the apples have finished baking, remove the baking dish from the oven and uncover. Use a fork to carefully sprinkle the streusel over top of the apples, working the streusel between the slices as well.
9. Increase the oven temperature and continue baking.** Increase the oven temperature to 425°F. Place the baking dish back in the oven and bake, uncovered, for 15 minutes more.
10. Cool the apples.** Remove the baking dish from the oven. Cool the apples for about 5 minutes before serving. Top with ice cream and caramel sauce if desired.
