# Cauliflower Cheese Sauce

**Diet:** vegetarian

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 1/2 sprigs
- fresh thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 head
- cauliflower, cut into florets
- 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 cup grated Gruyère cheese
- 1/2 cup grated sharp cheddar cheese
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Bring a large saucepan of salted water to a boil over medium-high heat. Add the garlic, thyme, and bay leaf and boil for 5 minutes to infuse the water.
2. Add the cauliflower and cook until very fork-tender, 7 to 10 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the cauliflower to a blender or food processor fitted with the blade attachment. Scoop out 3/4 cup of the cooking liquid and add it to the blender along with the oil. Blend until very smooth, about 1 1/2 minutes; transfer to a medium bowl.
3. Whisk the Gruyère, cheddar, mustard, salt, and pepper into the purée. Taste and season with more salt and pepper as needed.
