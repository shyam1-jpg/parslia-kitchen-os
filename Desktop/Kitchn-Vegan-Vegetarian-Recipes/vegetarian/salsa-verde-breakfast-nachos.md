# Salsa Verde Breakfast Nachos

**Diet:** vegetarian

Jerry runs the vegetarian and vegan blog, Cooking Stoned, and is the author of the cookbook, Holidazed.

## Ingredients

- #### For the salsa verde (makes about 1/2 cup):
- 1/2 pound tomatillos
- 1 medium avocado
- 1 medium red onion
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1 serrano chile
- 2 cloves
- garlic, peeled
- 1/4 cup cilantro
- Salt
- #### For the nachos:
- 4 ounces tortilla chips
- 1 15-ounce
- can of beans (I used pinto)
- 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, colby, jack, or a mix)
- 2 tablespoons sour cream
- 1 tablespoon chopped cilantro
- 1 medium jalapeño pepper, sliced
- 2 large eggs
- 1 medium avocado, sliced

## Method

1. Make the salsa verde:** Peel, wash, and chop the tomatillos into equal-sized pieces. Do the same for the red onion and serrano. Add everything to your food processor and process until smooth. Salt to taste.
2. Make the nachos:** On a small baking sheet (like a jelly roll pan), layer the tortilla chips, beans, and cheese, leaving a well for the eggs. Add 1/4 cup of the salsa verde to the well, along with the cracked eggs. Bake at 325°F for about 12 minutes, until the egg whites have set and the cheese is melted. Remove tray from oven, and top nachos with the remaining salsa, sour cream, jalapeño slices, cilantro, and avocado. And dig in!
