# Yotam Ottolenghi’s A’ja (Bread Fritters)

**Diet:** vegetarian

Emma is a former editor for The Kitchn and a graduate of the Cambridge School for Culinary Arts. She is the author of True Brews and Brew Better Beer. Check out her website for more cooking stories

## Ingredients

- 4 white bread slices, crusts removed (3 ounces in total)
- 4 extra-large free-range eggs
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon sweet paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 ounce chives, chopped
- 1 ounce flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 1/3 ounce tarragon, chopped
- 1 1/2 ounces feta cheese, crumbled
- Sunflower oil, for frying
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Soak the bread in plenty of cold water for 1 minute, then squeeze well.
2. Crumble the soaked bread into a medium bowl, then add the eggs, spices, ½ teaspoon salt, and ¼ teaspoon pepper, and whisk well. Mix in the chopped herbs and feta.
3. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in a medium frying pan over medium-high heat. Spoon about 3 tablespoons of the mixture into the center of the pan for each fritter and flatten it using the underside of the spoon; the fritters should be 3/4 to 1 1/4 inches thick. Fry the fritters for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until golden brown. Repeat with the remaining batter. You should get about 8 fritters.
4. Alternatively, you can fry all the batter at once, as you would a large omelet. Slice and serve warm or at room temperature.
