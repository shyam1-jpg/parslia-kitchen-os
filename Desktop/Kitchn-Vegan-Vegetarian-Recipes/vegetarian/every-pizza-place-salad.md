# Every Pizza Place Salad

**Diet:** vegetarian

A pizza place-inspired salad of iceburg lettuce, radicchio, cherry tomatoes, olives, and cheese that's tossed in a bright and tangy lemon caper dressing.

## Ingredients

- #### For the salad:
- 1 head
- iceberg lettuce
- 1 small head
- radicchio
- 1/2 cup pitted Kalamata olives, halved
- 4 ounces ricotta salata cheese, crumbled (about 1 cup)
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- Flaky salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- #### For the dressing:
- Juice of 1 medium lemon
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- Pinch
- granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons capers

## Method

1. Quarter the iceberg and radicchio through the core, then cut the cores from each piece. If you have a mandoline, use it to shave each quarter into very, very thin shavings. If you do not have a mandoline, very thinly slice the lettuce and radicchio crosswise with a very sharp knife, keeping the shavings as thin as possible. Place in a large bowl.
2. Add the olives and about 2/3 of the ricotta salata and toss to combine; set aside.
3. Make the dressing: Place all the ingredients in a measuring cup or bowl and whisk to combine. Drizzle over the lettuce, then gently toss until the lettuce is well-coated. Taste and season with salt and pepper as needed.
4. Transfer the salad to a platter. Scatter the cherry tomatoes and remaining ricotta salata over the top. Grind extra black pepper on top, and serve the salad very cold.
