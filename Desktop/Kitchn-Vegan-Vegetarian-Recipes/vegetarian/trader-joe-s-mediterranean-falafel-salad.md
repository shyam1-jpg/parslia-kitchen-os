# Trader Joe’s Mediterranean Falafel Salad

**Diet:** vegetarian

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 1 (16-ounce) box Trader Joe's Falafel Mix
- 1 (8.8-ounce) bag Trader Joe's 10-Minute Farro
- 1 (12.95-ounce) bag Trader Joe's Organic Mediterranean Style Salad Kit

## Method

1. Make a double batch of falafel according to box instructions.
2. While the falafel dough rests before baking, bring a large pot of water to a boil and cook farro according to package instructions. Drain farro, spread onto a rimmed baking sheet, and transfer to fridge to cool.
3. Form falafel dough into patties and bake on a parchment-lined baking sheet at 350°F until golden and puffed, 25 to 30 minutes.
4. Assemble the salad kit, divide it among 4 shallow bowls, and top with farro and falafel.
