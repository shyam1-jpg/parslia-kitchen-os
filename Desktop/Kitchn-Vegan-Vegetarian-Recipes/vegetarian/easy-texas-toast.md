# Easy Texas Toast

**Diet:** vegetarian

Learn how to make garlicky, buttery Texas toast at home with this easy step-by-step tutorial.

## Ingredients

- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 5 cloves
- garlic, minced
- 1/2 teaspoon dried parsley
- 1/2 teaspoon dried garlic
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 loaf
- soft white bread or Texas toast bread (14-inch, 12 ounces)

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of oven, place a rimmed baking sheet on the rack to preheat, and heat the oven to 375**°**F.
2. Melt the butter in a medium microwave-safe bowl until completely melted, about 1 minute. Add the olive oil, minced garlic, parsley, garlic powder, salt, and pepper and stir to combine. Set aside while you slice the bread.
3. If unsliced, slice the bread into 1-inch-thick slices. Use a pastry or basting brush to coat both sides of each slice with the garlic butter.
4. Place the bread on the preheated baking sheet in a single layer. Bake until golden-brown, about 12 minutes. Serve warm.
