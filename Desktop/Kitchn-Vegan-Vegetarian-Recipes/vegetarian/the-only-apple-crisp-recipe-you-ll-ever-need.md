# The Only Apple Crisp Recipe You'll Ever Need

**Diet:** vegetarian

This one-bowl apple crisp recipe is packed with lightly sweetened, warmly spiced apples and finished with a buttery oat topping.

## Ingredients

- #### For the filling:
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 2 pounds Granny Smith apples, peeled, cored, and cut into 1/4-inch thick slices
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- #### For the topping:
- 1 1/2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 12 tablespoons (1 1/2 sticks) unsalted butter, melted, plus more for the baking dish
- 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 350°F. Coat a 9-inch square glass baking dish with butter; set aside.
2. Whisk 1/2 cup granulated sugar, 2 tablespoons cornstarch, and 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon together in a large bowl to break up any lumps. Add 2 pounds sliced apples 1 tablespoon lemon juice and toss gently to coat. Transfer to the baking dish and spread into an even layer.
3. Place the 1 1/2 cups old-fashioned rolled oats, 1 cup all-purpose flour, 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar, and 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt in a medium or large bowl — it can be the bowl that was just used for the apples — and stir until combined. Drizzle 12 tablespoons melted unsalted butter and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract over the oat mixture and stir to combine.
4. Scatter the crisp topping evenly over the fruit mixture, leaving large clumps intact.
5. Bake until the fruit juices are bubbling around the edges of the baking dish and the topping is golden and firm to the touch, 50 to 60 minutes.
6. Let the crisp cool on a wire rack for at least 15 minutes before serving. If transporting to a picnic or party, let the crisp cool completely to give the fruit filling time to set.
