# This Buttery Recipe Is the Best Way to Cook Corn in Your Instant Pot

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter
- 6 ears
- fresh corn, husked and broken in half
- 3 cups water
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt

## Method

1. Place the butter in an Instant Pot or electric pressure cooker and use the sauté to melt it. Turn off the sauté function. Add the corn, followed by the water, milk, and salt.
2. Lock the lid on and make sure the valve is set to seal. Use the manual fucntion and set for 1 minute of HIGH pressure. The Instant Pot will take 12 to 15 minutes to come to pressure.
3. When the cook time is up, naturally release the pressure, which will take 20 to 25 minutes. Carefully open the Instant Pot and serve.
