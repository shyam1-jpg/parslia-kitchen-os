# Broccoli & Feta Pesto

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 pound broccoli, trimmed and cut into small florets
- 1/4 cup plus 2 tablespoons water, divided, plus more as needed
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon olive oil, plus more as needed
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 2 cloves
- garlic, minced
- 1/3 cup chopped fresh Italian parsley
- Juice of 1 medium lemon (3 tablespoons)
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese, divided

## Method

1. Steam the broccoli on the stovetop or in the microwavewith 2 tablespoons of the water. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
2. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large sauté pan over medium heat until shimmering. Add the onion and garlic and cook until it just begins to soften. Add the broccoli and cook for several minutes or until the onion turns translucent. Add the parsley and cook until it's well-wilted. Pour in the lemon juice and simmer for about 2 minutes.
3. Transfer to a blender and add 1/4 cup of the crumbled feta cheese and the remaining 1/4 cup water. Purée until smooth, adding a drizzle of olive oil and a little more water if it sticks and turns to a lump. Purée until as smooth or chunky as you prefer.
4. Taste and season with more salt and pepper as needed. Serve with garnish with the remaining 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese.
