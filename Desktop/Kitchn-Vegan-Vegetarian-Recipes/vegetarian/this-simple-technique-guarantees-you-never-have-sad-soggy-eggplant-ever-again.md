# This Simple Technique Guarantees You Never Have Sad, Soggy Eggplant Ever Again

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: This Simple Technique Guarantees You Never Have Sad, Soggy Eggplant Ever Again

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds eggplant (1 large or 2 medium globe)
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Trim and cut 1 1/2 pounds eggplant into 3/4-inch pieces (about 8 cups). Place on the baking sheet. Drizzle with 3 tablespoons olive oil and season with 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper. Toss to evenly coat and arrange into a single layer.
