# One Bowl Vanilla Cake: Ice Cream Cake

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 (9-inch) one-bowl vanilla cake (see Recipe Notes)
- 3 1/2 cups raspberry sorbet, thawed until scoopable, divided
- 2 cups frozen raspberries
- 1 cup cold heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- Fresh raspberries

## Method

1. Line a 9-inch cake pan with a parchment paper round. Split the cake in half horizontally. Return the bottom half of the cake to the cake pan. Spread 2 1/2 cups of the sherbet evenly over the cake. Top with the frozen raspberries. Spread the remaining 1 cup of sherbet over the raspberries. Set the remaining cake half on top
2. Cover with plastic wrap and freeze until the ice cream has hardened, at least 4 hours.
3. Using an electric hand mixer or hand whisk and large bowl, beat the cream and sugar together to stiff peaks.
4. Remove the ice cream cake from the pan and discard the parchment round. Use a knife to trim away any of the ice cream that seeped over the edge of the cake. Spread the whipped cream over the top of the cake and garnish with fresh raspberries. Serve immediately or return to the freezer until ready to serve.
