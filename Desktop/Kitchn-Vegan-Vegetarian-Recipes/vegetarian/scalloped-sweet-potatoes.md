# Scalloped Sweet Potatoes

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- Cooking spray or butter
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 teaspoon fresh thyme leaves
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 3 pounds sweet potatoes (about 4 to 5 medium), peeled and sliced into 1/4-inch thick rounds
- 1/2 cup shredded Gruyère cheese

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 400°F. Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with butter or cooking spray; set aside.
2. Place the cream, butter, garlic, and bay leaf in a small saucepan and bring to a simmer over low heat. Meanwhile, combine the thyme, nutmeg, salt, and pepper in a small bowl.
3. Evenly distribute about half of the potatoes over the bottom of the baking dish. Sprinkle with half of the salt and herb mixture. Add the remaining potatoes in an even layer. Sprinkle with the remaining seasoning.
4. Remove the bay leaf from the cream and discard. Pour the cream and garlic over the potatoes. Push down to submerge them as much as possible. The potatoes won't be fully submerged — that's okay. Cover tightly with aluminum foil.
5. Bake for 15 minutes. Uncover and bake until the sweet potatoes are tender, about 15 minutes more. Remove the baking dish from the oven. Turn on the oven's broiler. Sprinkle the cheese over the top of the potatoes. Broil until the cheese is melted and the top is lightly browned, 3 to 5 minutes. Let the sweet potatoes cool for about 10 minutes before serving.
