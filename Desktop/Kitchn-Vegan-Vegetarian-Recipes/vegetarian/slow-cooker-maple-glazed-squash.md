# Slow-Cooker Maple-Glazed Squash

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, at room temperature
- 1 (3-pound) winter squash, such as kabocha, buttercup, red kuri, or butternut
- 3 tablespoons maple sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- Pinch of freshly grated nutmeg
- Pinch of ground allspice
- Yogurt, chopped fruit, dukkah, or granola, for serving (optional)

## Method

1. Line the bottom of a 5-quart or larger slow cooker with a large piece of aluminum foil, making sure it comes up partway on the sides. Spread the butter over the foil.
2. Cut the squash into 8 wedges. Cut out the seedy centers and discard.
3. Stir together the maple sugar, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, and allspice in a medium bowl. Dredge each piece of squash in the maple sugar mixture until lightly coated, arranging each one, skin-side down, in the slow cooker as you work. Try to fit them in a single layer as best you can. Sprinkle any remaining sugar mixture over the squash.
4. Cover and cook on the LOW setting until the squash is tender, 4 to 6 hours.
5. Lift each piece of squash from the cooker and arrange on a platter. Carefully lift the foil out of the cooker and pour the collected juices over the squash pieces. Serve warm with yogurt, chopped fruit, and dukkah or granola.
