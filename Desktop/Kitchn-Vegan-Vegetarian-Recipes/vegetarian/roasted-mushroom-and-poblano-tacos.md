# Roasted Mushroom and Poblano Tacos

**Diet:** vegetarian

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- #### For the tacos:
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 1/2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lime juice
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/4 teaspoon chili powder
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 4 medium portobello mushrooms, stems removed and cut into 1/2-inch slices
- 2 poblano peppers, cored, seeded, and cut into 1/2-inch strips
- 1 medium red onion, cut into 1/4-inch slices
- 8 corn tortillas
- #### For serving:
- 3 ounces fresh goat cheese, crumbled (about 3/4 cup)
- 1/3 cup coarsely chopped fresh cilantro
- Lime wedges

## Method

1. Arrange 1 rack in the middle of the oven and 1 rack below it, then heat to 425°F.
2. Place the oil, lime juice, cumin, coriander, chili powder, and salt in a large bowl and whisk to combine. Add the mushrooms, peppers, and onion and toss to evenly coat the vegetables in the marinade. Transfer the vegetables to a rimmed baking sheet and spread into an even layer.
3. Roast for 10 minutes, stirring halfway through. Meanwhile, stack the tortillas on a large sheet of aluminum foil and wrap completely in the foil; set aside.
4. Place the foil-wrapped tortillas on the lower rack. Roast until the vegetables are tender and lightly charred, 12 to 15 minutes more.
5. Fill the tortillas with the mushroom and pepper mixture, then top each with goat cheese and cilantro. Serve with lime wedges.
