# Breakfast Baked Potato

**Diet:** vegetarian

Jerry runs the vegetarian and vegan blog, Cooking Stoned, and is the author of the cookbook, Holidazed.

## Ingredients

- 1 small sweet potato
- 1/2 cup leftover vegetarian chili, warmed (See Recipe Note)
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons shredded cheese
- 1 tablespoon sliced green onions

## Method

1. Pierce the sweet potato a couple of times with a fork, wrap it in foil, and bake it at 400°F for about 45 minutes, until tender.
2. Bring a small saucepan of water to a rapid simmer and poach the egg. (See How To Poach an Egg.)
3. Slice the baked potato in half, lengthwise, and add the chili, cheese, and green onions. Add the poached egg and top with some fresh-cracked pepper. Enjoy!
