# Cheesy Potato Breakfast Casserole with Cheddar and Sun-Dried Tomatoes

**Diet:** vegetarian

Title: Recipe: Cheesy Potato Breakfast Casserole with Cheddar and Sun-Dried Tomatoes

## Ingredients

- Cooking spray or olive oil
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 1 small yellow onion, diced
- 6 cloves
- garlic, minced
- 2/3 cup sun-dried tomatoes, preferably oil-packed, sliced into thin strips
- 9 large eggs
- 3 tablespoons spicy brown mustard
- 1 cup whole milk
- 2 teaspoons kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 2 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 1 pound shredded frozen potatoes (about 6 cups, do not thaw)

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 350°F. Lightly coat a 9x13-inch baking dish with cooking spray or olive oil.
2. Heat the butter in a large skillet over medium heat until it melts and foams up. Add the onion and garlic and cook until the onion has started to soften, about 5 minutes. Add the sun-dried tomatoes and cook until fragrant and the onion is nearly translucent and beginning to brown around the edges, about 5 minutes. Remove from the heat.
3. Place the eggs and mustard in a large bowl and whisk to combine. Whisk in the milk, salt, and pepper. Fold in the cheddar cheese, frozen potatoes, and sun-dried tomato mixture. Transfer the mixture to the baking dish.
4. Bake until the eggs are set and the top is slightly golden-brown, 35 to 45 minutes. Serve immediately with a fresh salad or fruit.
