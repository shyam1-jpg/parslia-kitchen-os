# Pineapple Casserole Is a Sweet and Salty Delight

**Diet:** vegetarian

If you like the sweet and salty combination of pineapple on a pizza, this casserole is right up your alley.

## Ingredients

- 4 ounces cheddar cheese
- 2 (20-ounce) cans
- pineapple chunks in juice
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- 5 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 32 Ritz or butter crackers (about 1 sleeve)
- 4 tablespoons (1/2 stick) unsalted butter

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 350°F. Grate 4 ounces cheddar cheese on the large holes of a box grater (about 1 cup).
2. Open 2 (20-ounce) cans pineapple chunks in juice. Drain through a fine-mesh strainer fitted over a bowl to catch the juice. Transfer 6 tablespoons of the juice to a medium bowl (save the remaining juice for another use).
3. Add 1/3 cup granulated sugar and 5 tablespoons all-purpose flour to the juice and whisk to combine. Add the pineapple and toss to combine. Transfer to an 8x8-inch baking dish. Sprinkle with the cheese.
4. Place 32 Ritz crackers in a gallon-size zip-top plastic bag. Press out the excess air and seal the bag. Crush the crackers with a rolling pin until coarsely crushed. Sprinkle over the cheese.
5. Place 4 tablespoons unsalted butter in a small microwave-safe bowl. Microwave in 15-second intervals until melted. Drizzle over the crackers.
6. Bake until the casserole is bubbly, about 30 minutes.
