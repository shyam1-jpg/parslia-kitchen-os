# These 2-Ingredient Cheese Straws Are a Hit at Every Holiday Party

**Diet:** vegetarian

Emma is a former editor for The Kitchn and a graduate of the Cambridge School for Culinary Arts. She is the author of True Brews and Brew Better Beer. Check out her website for more cooking stories

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups grated mixed hard cheese, such as Parmesan, Pecorino Romano, or aged cheddar
- 2 sheets
- thawed store-bought puff pastry, divided

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 375°F. Line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone baking mats and set aside.
2. Sprinkle a work surface with 1/4 cup of the grated cheese.
3. Unfold or unroll 1 of the puff pastry sheets onto the cheese. If the pastry isn't quite thawed, don't force it. Let it sit on the counter for about 10 minutes, and then try unrolling or unfolding again.
4. Sprinkle 1/4 cup of the cheese over the puff pastry.
5. Using a rolling pin, roll the pastry out until 1/8-inch thick. This presses the cheese into the pastry and also smoothes out any seams or wrinkles. Fold the sheet of pastry in half.
6. Sprinkle the pastry with 1/4 cup of the cheese. Roll the pastry out again until 1/8-inch thick. The exact dimensions of the puff pastry don't matter. Aim for one side to be about 10 inches long to make longer puff pastry straws, or aim for 6 to 8 inches for shorter straws.
7. Using a knife and cutting straight down (do not saw), cut the pastry into long strips about 1-inch wide. Again, dimensions don't really matter, but I like my long puff pastry straws to measure about 10 inches.
8. Transfer each strip to one of the baking sheets, spaced an inch or so apart. As you set them down, twist the ends in opposite directions to give the straws a spiraled look. If they start to untwist when you let go, gently press the ends into the parchment to make them stick.
9. Refrigerate at least 10 minutes or up to 1 hour. This helps firm up the straws and give them better puff in the oven. While they are chilling, prepare the rest of the straws using the second sheet of puff pastry and the remaining cheese.
10. Bake the first sheet of cheese straws until they are puffed and crispy, feel dry to the touch, and are deep golden brown. The exact baking time will depend on the size of your straws. Begin checking shorter straws after about 15 minutes, and longer straws after 20 minutes.
11. Let cool until the straws are firm enough to lift from the sheet without bending. Transfer to a cooling rack or serving plate. Meanwhile, bake the second baking sheet of cheese straws.
12. Serve the cheese straws warm, or let them cool and then keep them in an airtight container until ready to serve. Puff pastry straws are best served the same day they are made.
