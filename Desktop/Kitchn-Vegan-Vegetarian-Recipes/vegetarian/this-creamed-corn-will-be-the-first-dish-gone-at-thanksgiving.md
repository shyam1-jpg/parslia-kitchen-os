# This Creamed Corn Will Be the First Dish Gone at Thanksgiving

**Diet:** vegetarian

Sweet corn kernels simmered in butter and cream is better than anything from a can.

## Ingredients

- 2 ears
- fresh corn, 1 (10-ounce) bag thawed frozen corn kernels, or 1 (about 15-ounce) can drained corn kernels
- 1/2 small yellow onion, finely chopped (about 1/2 cup)
- 2 teaspoons finely chopped fresh chives (optional)
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more as needed
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 cup heavy cream or half-and-half

## Method

1. Shuck and cut the kernels from 3 ears (about 1 1/2 cups) into a bowl, then use the back of your knife to scrape against the cobs to release the moisture into the bowl (also known as “milk”).
2. Melt 2 tablespoons unsalted butter in an 8-inch cast-iron or other medium skillet over medium-high heat. Add 1/2 finely chopped small yellow onion, 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper. Cook until light golden brown, about 2 minutes.
3. Add the corn kernels (and “milk”) and 1/2 cup heavy cream or half-and-half. Simmer until the corn is tender and the cream has reduced slightly, 2 to 4 minutes. Taste and season with more kosher salt as needed. Top with 2 teaspoons finely chopped fresh chives, if using.
