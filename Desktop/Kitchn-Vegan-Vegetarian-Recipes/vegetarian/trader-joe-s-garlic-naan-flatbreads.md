# Trader Joe’s Garlic Naan Flatbreads

**Diet:** vegetarian

Sheela was the Senior Contributing Food Editor at The Kitchn. She is the author of Mediterranean Every Day: Simple, Inspired Recipes for Feel-Good Food.

## Ingredients

- 1 (12-ounce) package frozen Trader Joe's Garlic Naan
- 1 (10-ounce) package Trader Joe's Antipasto Mediterranean Vegetables, drained and coarsely chopped
- 1 (12-ounce) bag Trader Joe's Quattro Formaggio Shredded Cheese Blend

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 400°F.
2. Place the garlic naan in a single layer on a baking sheet. Top each naan with vegetables and shredded cheese. Bake until warm and bubbling, 5 to 10 minutes.
