# Saag Paneer Pizza

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 1 pound frozen chopped spinach, thawed
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 clove
- garlic, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon finely chopped fresh peeled ginger
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt, plus more for seasoning
- 1/3 cup heavy cream
- 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 4 pieces
- naan
- 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 500°F. Line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper.
2. Using your hands, squeeze the excess water out of the spinach and set aside. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium heat until shimmering. Add the onion, season with salt, and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Add the garlic and ginger and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the garam masala and pepper and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds. Add the spinach and 1/2 teaspoon salt and cook, stirring occasionally, until heated through and any moisture that comes out of the spinach is evaporated, about 2 minutes.
4. Remove from the heat, add the cream and lemon juice, and stir to combine until the spinach absorbs the cream. Taste and season with more salt as needed.
5. Place 2 naan on each baking sheet. Divide the spinach mixture among the naan and spread into an even layer, leaving a 1/2-inch border. Sprinkle with the cheese.
6. Bake 1 sheet at a time until the cheese is melted and the edges of the naan are golden-brown, 5 to 7 minutes per sheet. Let cool 2 minutes, then cut into slices and serve.
