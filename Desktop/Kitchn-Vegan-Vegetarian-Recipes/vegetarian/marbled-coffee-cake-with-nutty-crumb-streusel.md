# Marbled Coffee Cake with Nutty Crumb Streusel

**Diet:** vegetarian

A double layer of streusel makes this one-bowl coffee cake recipe swirl with delight.

## Ingredients

- #### For the streusel:
- Cooking spray
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 cup walnuts (3 ounces), coarsely chopped
- 3/4 cup packed light brown sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 1/8 teaspoons salt
- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) cold unsalted butter, cubed
- #### For the cake:
- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 3/4 cups granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 3/4 teaspoon salt
- 4 large eggs
- 3/4 cup canola oil, or melted and cooled unsalted butter
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- #### For the glaze:
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2 tablespoons whole milk

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat to 350°F. Line a 9x13-inch baking pan with a parchment paper sling that hangs over the 2 long sides. Coat with cooking spray; set aside.
2. Stir the flour, walnuts, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt together in a medium bowl. Incorporate the chilled, cubed butter with your fingertips until you have small- to medium-sized lumps and none of the flour-sugar mixture remains dry; set aside.
3. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together in a large bowl. Add the eggs, oil or butter, milk, and vanilla and mix with an electric hand mixer on medium speed until smooth, about 3 minutes.
4. Pour half of the batter into the pan and spread into an even layer. Sprinkle half of the streusel over the batter. Pour in the rest of the batter evenly over top, then distribute the remaining streusel over the top.
5. Bake until the cake is lightly browned and springs bake when gingerly pressed with a finger, 30 to 40 minutes.
6. Cool on a wire rack for 15 minutes. Grasping the parchment paper sling, remove the cake from the pan and cool completely before glazing.
7. Stir the powdered sugar and milk together in a small bowl until smooth. Drizzle the glaze over the cooled cake. Wait 10 minutes for the glaze to harden before cutting and serving.
