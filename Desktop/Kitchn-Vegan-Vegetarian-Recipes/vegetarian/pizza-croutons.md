# Pizza Croutons

**Diet:** vegetarian

Hali Bey Ramdene is the founder of StudioHalibey, a creative consultancy that tells stories around food, good living, and well-being.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3 tablespoons tomato paste
- 3 cups day-old bread cubes (about 1 inch)
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese

## Method

1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add the tomato paste and cook and stir until combined with oil.
2. Add the bread cubes in a single layer. Let bread toast for 1 minute, then toss again, every minute or so, until the cubes are crisp and and brown on all sides, about 5 minutes total.
3. Remove from heat and transfer to a large bowl. Immediately sprinkle with Italian seasoning and grated cheese. Toss to combine. Serve immediately or the same day they're made.
