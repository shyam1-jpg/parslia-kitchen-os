# My “Sheet Pan Salad” Trick Has Forever Changed the Way I Make Salad

**Diet:** vegetarian

Roast sweet potatoes, chickpeas, and croutons, then build a flavorful, satisfying salad — directly on top!

## Ingredients

- 1 (about 15-ounce) can
- chickpeas
- 1 tablespoon nonpareil capers, drained, plus 2 teaspoons brine
- 2 medium
- sweet potatoes (about 1 1/2 pounds total), cut into 1/2-inch-thick pieces
- 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided, plus more for drizzling
- 3 tablespoons nutritional yeast or 3 teaspoons any color miso paste, divided
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more as needed
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper, plus more as desired
- 1/4 medium loaf
- sourdough boule or ciabatta bread (about 4 ounces), torn into rough 1-inch pieces (about 2 1/2 to 2 3/4 cups)
- 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice (from 1 medium lemon)
- 1/3 cup tahini
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- 1 medium romaine lettuce heart, halved lengthwise, then cut into crosswise 1/2-inch-thick strips (about 4 cups)
- 4 red radishes, thinly sliced
- 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced (about 1/4 cup)
- 3 ounces feta cheese, crumbled (about 1/3 cup)
- 3 tablespoons roasted, salted, shelled pistachios, coarsely chopped
- 2 sprigs
- fresh mint, leaves torn
- 1/4 cup dried pitted cherries or cranberries (optional)

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 425ºF. Line a rimmed baking sheet with paper towels.
2. Drain 1 (about 15-ounce) can chickpeas, reserving the liquid. Arrange the chickpeas on the baking sheet in a single layer. Spread 1 tablespoon drained nonpareil capers on the same baking sheet. Pat dry with more paper towels.
3. Remove the paper towels from the baking sheet. Add 2 chopped medium sweet potatoes, 2 tablespoons of the olive oil, 2 tablespoons of the nutritional yeast or 2 teaspoons of the miso paste, 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper. Toss with your hands to evenly coat, then arrange into a single layer.
4. Bake until the sweet potatoes start to soften, about 15 minutes. Scatter 1/4 torn medium loaf sourdough or ciabatta bread on the baking sheet and drizzle with olive oil and a pinch of kosher salt. Bake until the chickpeas and bread are browned and crisp, 10 to 15 minutes more. Set aside until cool enough to handle, about 10 minutes. Meanwhile, make the dressing.
5. Place 2 tablespoons lemon juice, 3 tablespoons of the reserved chickpea liquid, remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil, 2 teaspoons reserved caper brine, 1/3 cup tahini, 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard, and the remaining the 1 tablespoon nutritional yeast or 1 teaspoon miso paste in a small bowl. Whisk until smooth. Whisk in more chickpea liquid 1 tablespoon at a time as needed until the mixture is pourable. Taste and season with kosher salt and black pepper as needed.
6. Add 1 sliced medium romaine lettuce to the baking sheet, drizzle with 3/4 of the dressing (about 2/3 cup), and gently toss to combine. Evenly sprinkle 4 thinly sliced red radishes and 1/2 thinly sliced small red onion over the top. Drizzle with the remaining dressing. Sprinkle 3 ounces crumbled feta cheese over the salad. Sprinkle with 3 tablespoons coarsely chopped pistachios, torn fresh mint leaves, and 1/4 cup dried pitted cherries or cranberries, if using.
