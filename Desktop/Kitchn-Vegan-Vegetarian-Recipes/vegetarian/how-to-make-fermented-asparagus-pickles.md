# How To Make Fermented Asparagus Pickles

**Diet:** vegetarian

Joel MacCharles is co-founder of WellPreserved, co-author of Batch Cookbook, and former TEDxToronto speaker. His passion is to make preserving food accessible.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 to 2 pounds
- asparagus (I use the stalks and reserve the spears for other cooking)
- 1 1/2 teaspoons coarse salt
- 2 green onions, sliced into 3-inch pieces
- 1 to 2
- thick orange slices
- Filtered water
- #### Equipment
- 1-quart Mason jar with lid
- Small plate
- Clean dish towel

## Method

1. Cut the stalks so that they stand 3/4 to 1 inch below where the jar neck begins to narrow.
2. Pack the jar tightly with asparagus, and then add the salt.
3. Add water to barely cover the contents.
4. Lay green onion on top of the asparagus.
5. "Seat belt” the ingredients into place by wedging an orange slice across the top to prevent floating.
6. Add additional water so that all ingredients are completely submerged, and place it on a small plate.
7. Loosely cover with a clean cloth and allow to ferment until you like the sourness (two to four days is typical) before securing with a lid and storing in fridge.
