# My Southern-Style Yellow Squash Casserole Will Have You Going for Seconds

**Diet:** vegetarian

A Southern-style side dish with mild yellow squash in a creamy cheddar sauce and topped with a crunchy cracker topping.

## Ingredients

- 6 tablespoons (3/4 stick) unsalted butter, divided, plus more for the baking dish
- 2 pounds yellow summer squash (about 4 medium), sliced into 1/4-inch thick rounds (about 7 cups)
- 1 medium sweet onion, such as Vidalia, diced (about 1 1/2 cups)
- 8 ounces sharp cheddar cheese, shredded (about 2 cups)
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 2 large eggs
- 1/4 cup whole or 2% milk
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 45 round butter crackers, such as Ritz ( about 1 1/2 sleeves)

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 350°F. Coat a 9x13-inch baking dish with butter.
2. Melt 2 tablespoons of the unsalted butter in a 12-inch or larger skillet over medium heat. Add 2 pounds sliced yellow squash, 1 diced medium sweet onion, 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt, and 1/2 teaspoon black pepper. The skillet will be quite full. Cook, stirring occasionally, until tender and just beginning to brown, about 10 minutes.
3. Transfer the squash and onions to a colander set over a bowl or placed in the sink; reserve the skillet. Set aside to drain for 5 minutes.
4. Place 2 large eggs, 1/4 cup whole or 2 % milk, 1/4 cup mayonnaise, 1/4 cup sour cream, 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper, and half of the cheese (about 1 cup) in a large bowl and whisk to combine.
5. Transfer the squash and onions into the egg mixture. Stir to coat the vegetables in the sauce. Transfer to the baking dish and spread into an even layer. Sprinkle with the remaining 1 cup cheese.
6. Place 45 round butter crackers in a large zip-top plastic bag and seal the bag. Use your hands to crush into coarse crumbs (about 2 1/4 cups).
7. Melt the remaining 4 tablespoons unsalted butter in the reserved skillet over medium heat. Add the crackers and stir to coat. Evenly sprinkle the cracker crumbs over the casserole.
8. Bake until browned and the edges bubble, 25 to 30 minutes. Let cool for 5 minutes before serving.
