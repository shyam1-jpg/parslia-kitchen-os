# The 6-Ingredient Holiday Cookie I Make Every Year

**Diet:** vegetarian

## Ingredients

- 2 sticks (8 ounces) unsalted butter
- 2 cups pecan halves
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 cup powdered sugar
- 1 tablespoon water
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Method

1. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 350ºF. Place 2 sticks unsalted butter in the bowl of a stand mixer (or large bowl if using an electric mixer). Let sit at room temperature until softened. Meanwhile, toast the pecans and prepare the flour mixture.
2. Measure out 2 cups pecan halves. Reserve 24 of the halves (about 1/2 cup) for topping the cookies. Place the remaining pecans on a rimmed baking sheet. Bake until lightly toasted and fragrant, about 9 minutes. Let cool completely. Finely chop the roasted pecans (alternatively, pulse in a food processor to chop). Reserve 1/4 cup, then place the remaining chopped pecans in a medium bowl. Add 2 cups all-purpose flour and 3/4 teaspoon kosher salt to the bowl and stir to combine.
3. Add 1/2 cup powdered sugar to the bowl of butter. Beat with the paddle attachment on medium speed until pale and fluffy, about 2 minutes. Add 1 tablespoon water and 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, and beat until combined.
4. Add half of the flour mixture and beat on the lowest speed until just combined. Repeat beating in the remaining flour mixture. If needed, use a flexible spatula to fold in any remaining flour by hand.
5. Transfer the dough onto a sheet of plastic wrap. Gather the dough together and shape into a 12-inch log about 2 inches wide. Sprinkle the reserved chopped pecans over the log and onto the plastic wrap, and roll the log to coat (it’s okay for it to be uneven). Wrap the log tightly in the plastic wrap. Refrigerate until firm, at least 2 or up to 24 hours.
6. Arrange a rack in the middle of the oven and heat the oven to 350ºF. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat. Unwrap the dough log. Cut crosswise into 1/2-inch thick rounds (about 24). Place on the baking sheet, spacing them evenly apart. Gently press 1 reserved pecan half into the center of each cookie.
7. Bake until dry to the touch and just barely golden brown around the edges, 16 to 18 minutes. Let cool for 5 minutes on the baking sheet. Transfer the cookies to a wire rack and let cool completely.
