# Carla Hall’s Black-Eyed Pea Salad with Hot Sauce Vinaigrette

**Diet:** vegetarian

Down South, this salad is called a sitting salad. It can sit on the summer picnic table without wilting, so it's the perfect potluck dish.

## Ingredients

- 2 garlic cloves, grated on a Microplane
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon yellow mustard
- 1 tablespoon hot sauce
- 1 teaspoon honey
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 6 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 (15-ounce) can black-eyed peas, rinsed and drained
- 2 mini cucumbers, cut into 1/2-inch dice
- 1/2 sweet onion, finely chopped
- 1 pint
- cherry tomatoes or grape tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 cup picked fresh dill

## Method

1. Whisk the garlic, vinegar, mustard, hot sauce, honey, 1/4 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon pepper in a large bowl until smooth. While whisking, add the oil in a slow, steady stream. Whisk until emulsified.
2. Add the peas, cucumbers, onion, tomatoes, dill, and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Toss until well mixed. You can serve this right away or let it sit at room temperature for up to 1 hour.
