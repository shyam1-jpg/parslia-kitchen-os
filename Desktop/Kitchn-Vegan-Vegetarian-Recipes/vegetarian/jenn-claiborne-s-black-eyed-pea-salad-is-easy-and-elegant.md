# Jenné Claiborne’s Black-Eyed Pea Salad Is Easy and Elegant

**Diet:** vegetarian

Credit: Photo: Kelly Marshall | Food Stylist: Micah Morton | Prop Stylist: Gerri Williams

## Ingredients

- 3 cups cooked black-eyed peas (equivalent to 2 cans)
- 1/2 cup celery, chopped (about 1 stalk)
- 1/4 cup red onion, chopped
- 1 medium red bell pepper, diced (about 1/4 cup)
- 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 2 teaspoons hot sauce (start with 1 if you aren't a spicy food fan)
- 1 teaspoon maple syrup or agave syrup
- 1 teaspoon fresh oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 1/4 teaspoons sea salt
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped

## Method

1. Place the cooked black-eyed peas, celery, red onion, and peppers into a large mixing bowl.
2. In another bowl whisk together the mustard, vinegar, hot sauce, maple syrup, oregano, thyme, and salt.
3. Drizzle in the olive oil while whisking vigorously until well blended and creamy.
4. Pour the dressing over the black-eyed peas, and stir well to combine.
5. Stir in the fresh parsley then serve. (This dish is best when made a few hours before or the night before serving.)
