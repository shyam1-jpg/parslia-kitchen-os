# Asian Pear, Fresh Date, and Pomegranate Salad

**Diet:** vegetarian

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 1 pomegranate
- 1 teaspoon honey
- 1 teaspoon grated ginger
- 1/8 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 16 fresh dates
- 1 Asian pear
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice

## Method

1. De-seed the pomegranate* and place seeds in a large bowl.
2. Take 1/4 cup of the pomegranate seeds to make juice. Press the seeds through a strainer over a small bowl. Discard the pulp. Add the honey, ginger, and cinnamon to the pomegranate juice and combine. Set dressing aside.
3. Cut each date, lengthwise or crosswise, into 4-6 pieces. Slicing them crosswise creates nice little discs, but it requires a slightly steadier hand than cutting lengthwise. Discard the date seeds and add the dates to the bowl with the pomegranate seeds.
4. Slice the pear into bite-size pieces and toss with lemon juice. Add to the bowl with the pomegranate seeds and dates.
5. Pour dressing over the fruit and gently toss to combine. Serve immediately.
