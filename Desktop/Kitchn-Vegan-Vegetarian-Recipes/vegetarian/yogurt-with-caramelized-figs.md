# Yogurt With Caramelized Figs

**Diet:** vegetarian

Emily Han is a Los Angeles-based recipe developer, educator, herbalist, and author of Wild Drinks & Cocktails and co-author of Wild Remedies. For recipes and classes, check out her personal site.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons honey
- 1 (4-inch) sprig fresh rosemary
- 6 figs, trimmed and halved
- 8 ounces plain or lightly sweetened yogurt
- 2 tablespoons toasted pistachios
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Warm the honey and rosemary in a skillet over medium-low heat for about a minute.
2. Add the figs cut-side down and cook until soft and caramelized, about 2-3 minutes. Turn figs over and cook for another minute.
3. Divide the yogurt among two dishes and arrange the figs on top. Spoon some of the remaining honey over the figs.
4. Sprinkle pistachios on top, and then add a light drizzle of olive oil and a hint of salt.
