# CK-00 — Brigade & kitchen standards

**Station:** Hygiene  
**Short video:** Brigade standards — 55 seconds (55s)  
**Kiteline commercial kitchen SOP** — not home cooking.

How a professional kitchen works: roles, kit, and the line.

## Short video scenes

- **Commercial kitchen** — This is a professional brigade — not a home cook-along.
- **Roles** — Head chef sets the pass. Sous runs the line. CDP owns a section. Commis support prep.
- **Kit** — Clean whites, hat or hairnet, apron, closed shoes. No rings, watches, or nail varnish on food work.
- **Stations** — Know your section: garnish, sauce, grill, pastry, pot-wash, pass.
- **Voice** — Call orders back. Say ‘behind’, ‘hot’, ‘sharp’. The pass is the only send authority.

## Purpose

Set the professional standard for every person who enters a commercial kitchen operated with Kiteline — hotels, restaurants, catering, schools, and production sites.

## Scope

Applies to chefs, kitchen porters, stewards, agency staff, and managers on shift. Front of house only enter the kitchen on the agreed walkway and never touch the pass without permission.

## Brigade roles

- Head chef / executive chef — menu, HACCP ownership, sign-off, discipline of the pass
- Sous chef — service control, section cover, temperature and allergen checks
- Chef de partie — owns one station (sauce, grill, veg, pastry, larder)
- Commis — mise en place, labelling, following the recipe card exactly. If the card is not found, stop and use Guideline B.
- Kitchen porter / steward — pot-wash, floors, bins, chemical store — not food prep unless trained

## Professional appearance

- Clean uniform at the start of every shift; change if soiled
- Hair fully restrained; beard net where policy requires
- Hands: short nails, no varnish, waterproof plaster + blue glove if a cut
- No personal phones on the pass during service

## Kitchen language

- Acknowledge every order: ‘Yes chef’
- Warn: ‘Behind’, ‘Hot pan’, ‘Sharp’, ‘Corner’
- Never send a plate that the pass has not checked
