# CK-07 — Pass & service

**Station:** Service  
**Short video:** Running the pass — 58 seconds (58s)  
**Kiteline commercial kitchen SOP** — not home cooking.

Tickets, timing, plate check, and the pass.

## Short video scenes

- **The pass** — One voice. The pass chef calls, checks, and sends. Sections do not self-send.
- **Ticket** — Read table, covers, allergens, and fire time. Call the fire in brigade order.
- **Plate check** — Heat, garnish, sauce, protein, allergen garnish, wipe the rim. Then it leaves.
- **Hold the line** — If a section is late, hold the table — do not send a split main.
- **Recovery** — Remake beats an argument. Log the incident after service, not on the pass.

## Purpose

Commercial service is timed production. The pass protects the guest and the brigade.

## Pass checks before send

- Correct dish and dietary notes
- Temperature — hot food hot, cold food cold
- Allergen confirmation spoken and heard
- Clean plate, correct garnish, no cloth fibres

## Never

- Never send a plate you have not seen
- Never shout at FOH across a full pass — step aside after the fire
- Never reuse a returned plate without a full remake
