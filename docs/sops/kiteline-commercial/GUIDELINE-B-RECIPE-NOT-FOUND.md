# Guideline B — Recipe not found

**Product:** Kiteline  
**Type:** Controlled kitchen guideline (works with CK-00, CK-04, CK-06)  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Effective date:** 31 August 2026  
**Review:** 31 August 2027, or earlier after a menu, allergen or recipe-file change  

This is **not a recipe**. It is the stop rule when a controlled recipe card cannot be found.

## When it applies

Use Guideline B if any of these are true:

- The dish is on the board or ticket but there is no current recipe card on the section
- Kiteline Recipes, the printed pack, or the allergen matrix does not list that dish
- Search in the pocket SOP app or recipe library returns no match
- The only information available is memory, a phone photo, or an unofficial note

## Immediate action

1. **Stop** — do not cook, garnish, label or send the dish from memory.
2. **Do not invent** ingredients, method, cook temperatures, allergens or a shelf life.
3. **Search the controlled sources** — current recipe file, Kiteline Recipes, section card pack, and the allergen matrix of the same menu version.
4. **If still not found** — 86 the dish, tell the chef or duty manager, and record the gap (time, dish name, person, action).
5. **Do not promise** the guest a substitute until a controlled recipe and allergen information exist.

## Why this is a safety control

A missing recipe card is a missing allergen, method and CCP record. Cooking without it breaks CK-00 (follow the card), CK-04 (mise from the board and cards) and CK-06 (recipe is law).

## Sign-off

Trainers confirm Guideline B with CK-00, CK-04 and CK-06. Retrain after a recipe-file or menu change.
