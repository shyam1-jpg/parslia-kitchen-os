# CK-04 — Mise en place

**Station:** Line  
**Short video:** Mise en place — 55 seconds (55s)  
**Kiteline commercial kitchen SOP** — not home cooking.

Section set-up so service can run at commercial pace.

## Short video scenes

- **Read the board** — Covers, 86’d dishes, allergens, and the function sheet before you touch a board.
- **Set the station** — Boards coded, knives sharp, sanitiser, probe, tasting spoons, labelled mise.
- **Batch smart** — Prep to the forecast. Over-prepping is waste. Under-prepping kills the pass.
- **Taste & temp** — Season, taste with a clean spoon, and check sauces are at hold temperature.
- **Ready for the pass** — When the brief starts, your section is lit, labelled, and you can fire on the first ticket.

## Purpose

A commercial line cannot cook from cold stores during service. Mise en place is the production system.

## Before service brief

- Recipe cards and allergen matrix on the section. If a dish has no current card, follow Guideline B — Recipe not found. Do not prep from memory.
- Colour-coded boards: red raw meat, blue fish, yellow raw poultry, green salad/fruit, white bakery/dairy, brown vegetables
- Hot hold units preheated; bain-marie water clean and at temperature
- Bins empty, cloths in the right bucket (sanitiser vs detergent)

## During prep

- Work one task at a time on a clean board
- Return high-risk food to the fridge within 30 minutes
- Do not use service cloths for floors or raw trays
