# CK-10 — Manual handling

**Station:** Safety  
**Short video:** How to lift — 50 seconds (50s)  
**Kiteline commercial kitchen SOP** — not home cooking.

Flour sacks, stock pots, and the walk-in — lift like a pro.

## Short video scenes

- **Assess** — Weight, path, floor, and whether you need a sack truck or a second person.
- **Set up** — Feet apart, load close, back straight, bend the knees — not the spine.
- **Move** — Turn with the feet. Never twist with a 20 kg pot of stock.
- **Team lift** — One person calls. Same height. Same pace. No hero lifts on the stairs.
- **Stop** — If it feels wrong, put it down. Get the truck.

## House limits

- Use a sack truck for 15 kg+ bags where the route allows
- Team-lift stock pots, full gastronorms, and oven trays
- Never catch a falling pot — step back

## Walk-in safety

Keep aisles clear. Do not climb shelves. Use the step stool. Prop the door only with the approved catch — never a box.
