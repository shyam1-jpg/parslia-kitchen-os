# CK-06 — Allergen control

**Station:** Line  
**Short video:** Allergens — 60 seconds (60s)  
**Kiteline commercial kitchen SOP** — not home cooking.

The 14 allergens — from recipe card to the pass.

## Short video scenes

- **14 allergens** — Celery, gluten, crustaceans, egg, fish, lupin, milk, molluscs, mustard, peanut, sesame, soya, sulphites, tree nuts.
- **Recipe is law** — Cook the card. A ‘secret’ garnish can hospitalise a guest.
- **Separate kit** — Dedicated boards, fryers, and tongs for allergen-free tickets. Change gloves.
- **The pass** — Allergen tickets get a verbal check: chef reads the allergen, CDP confirms the plate.
- **If unsure** — Do not send. Remake or 86 the dish. Never guess.
- **Recipe not found** — If the controlled recipe or allergen matrix does not list the dish, stop. Do not invent allergens. Follow Guideline B.

## Purpose

Prevent allergen harm in a commercial service. Natasha’s Law and Natasha’s legacy: accurate information, every time.

## The 14 (UK / EU)

- Celery
- Cereals containing gluten
- Crustaceans
- Eggs
- Fish
- Lupin
- Milk
- Molluscs
- Mustard
- Peanuts
- Sesame
- Soybeans
- Sulphur dioxide & sulphites
- Tree nuts

## On the line

- Allergen matrix printed and current — same version as the menu
- No unmarked squeeze bottles
- Fryer oil is an allergen if it cooked breaded or nut items
- FOH must not invent answers; they check the matrix or the chef
