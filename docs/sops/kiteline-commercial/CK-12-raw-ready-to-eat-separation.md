# CK-12 — Raw & ready-to-eat separation

**Station:** Safety  
**Short video:** Raw vs RTE — 58 seconds (58s)  
**Kiteline commercial kitchen SOP** — not home cooking.

Stop cross-contamination on a busy commercial line.

## Short video scenes

- **Two worlds** — Raw protein can carry bacteria. Ready-to-eat food is eaten as it is. They never share a board.
- **Colour code** — Yellow poultry, red meat, blue fish, green salad. If the board is scored and stained, replace it.
- **Hands** — Wash after raw. Change gloves. Do not touch the ticket, the pass, or a garnish with raw hands.
- **Cloths & oil** — A cloth that wiped a raw tray is contaminated. Fryer oil that cooked raw-breaded items is not for chips only — manage it.
- **If it mixes** — Discard the RTE food. Clean. Log. Do not ‘quick rinse and send’.

## Purpose

Cross-contamination is a top cause of commercial kitchen outbreaks. Separation is a CCP in most HACCP plans.

## Non-negotiable

- Separate fridge space or sealed boxes for raw vs RTE
- Separate knives, tongs, and probe wipes after raw
- Wash hands every time you switch
- Never store raw trays above garnish or dessert
