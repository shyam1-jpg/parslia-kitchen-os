# CK-08 — Cleaning & COSHH

**Station:** Hygiene  
**Short video:** Clean-as-you-go — 55 seconds (55s)  
**Kiteline commercial kitchen SOP** — not home cooking.

Clean as you go, chemical store, and contact time.

## Short video scenes

- **Two-stage clean** — Detergent to remove grease. Rinse. Sanitiser — and wait the contact time on the bottle.
- **Cloths** — Colour-coded. Never the floor cloth on a board. Change when dirty, not ‘at the end’.
- **COSHH** — Chemicals stay in the locked store. Dilute as the wall chart says. PPE for oven cleaner and descaler.
- **Machines** — Empty, clean, and dry fryers, slicers, and mixers before they go back on the line.
- **Floors last** — Food surfaces first. Floors and drains last so you do not splash clean benches.

## Clean as you go

A commercial kitchen that waits until close-down is already failing hygiene. Wipe, sanitise, and reset between tasks.

## COSHH

- Safety data sheets in the chemical folder
- Never mix bleach and acid
- Spray away from food; cover or remove product first
- Report spills; do not leave a wet chemical floor
