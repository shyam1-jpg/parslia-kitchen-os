# CK-03 — Storage, FIFO & labelling

**Station:** Stores  
**Short video:** Stores & labels — 58 seconds (58s)  
**Kiteline commercial kitchen SOP** — not home cooking.

Walk-in, dry store, and Natasha’s Law labels.

## Short video scenes

- **Zones** — Raw bottom. RTE top. Allergens in sealed, named containers — never open bags on a high shelf.
- **FIFO** — New stock behind old. Date-check every time you take a tray.
- **Label** — Name, date prepped, use-by, allergens, chef initials. No mystery cambros.
- **Natasha’s Law** — PPDS food needs its name and a full ingredients list, with every regulated allergen emphasised within that list.
- **Floor rule** — Nothing stored on the floor. 15 cm off the ground on racks.

## Walk-in layout

- Ready-to-eat and cooked above raw
- Raw poultry on the lowest shelf in a lidded tray
- Allergen-critical items (nuts, sesame, celery) in dedicated sealed boxes
- No cardboard on wet floors

## Label every container

- Dish or ingredient name as the guest will see it
- Prep date and use-by (follow site shelf-life table)
- For PPDS food: food name plus the full ingredients list, with regulated allergens emphasised within that list
- Initials of the person who prepped

## Dry store

Cool, dry, pest-proof. Open sacks decanted into lidded bins. Scoop stored clean and dry, not buried in flour.
