# CK-09 — Closing checks

**Station:** Close-down  
**Short video:** Close-down — 60 seconds (60s)  
**Kiteline commercial kitchen SOP** — not home cooking.

Food away, kit off, pests out, tomorrow set.

## Short video scenes

- **Food first** — Label and fridge all high-risk food. Discard anything that sat in the danger zone.
- **Line down** — Gas off or isolated per site. Fryers filtered. Ovens empty. Extract run to clear steam.
- **Wash-up** — No pans in the sink overnight. Machines emptied, arms clean, doors ajar to dry.
- **Pests & bins** — Bins out, lids on, floors dry, doors shut. No food scraps under the pass.
- **Sign out** — Closing checklist signed. Alarms set. Only the duty manager locks the last door.

## Close-down order

- Service equipment cleaned and stored
- Walk-in organised, door shut, temp recorded
- Dry store tidy, lights off
- Floors mopped, mats hung
- Pest proofing: gaps closed, bait stations not disturbed
- Checklist signed; keys to the agreed person
