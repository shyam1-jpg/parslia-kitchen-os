# CK-02 — Goods-in & deliveries

**Station:** Stores  
**Short video:** Goods-in in 60 seconds (60s)  
**Kiteline commercial kitchen SOP** — not home cooking.

Check, probe, reject, and put away — commercial receiving.

## Short video scenes

- **Bay ready** — Clear the receiving bench. Sanitise. Have probe, wipes, and the order sheet ready.
- **Check the van** — Clean vehicle, sealed packaging, no crushed boxes sitting in melt water.
- **Probe it** — Chilled ≤ 8 °C, frozen hard at −18 °C, hot deliveries ≥ 63 °C. Write the reading.
- **Reject** — Refuse damaged, leaking, bloated, unlabelled, or out-of-range stock. Note on the invoice.
- **Put away now** — High-risk chilled first, then frozen, then dry. Never leave a pallet in the corridor.

## Purpose

Stop unsafe food at the door. Commercial kitchens live or die on goods-in discipline.

## Accept only if

- Supplier is on the approved list
- Use-by / best-before dates allow a safe production window
- Chilled product is 8 °C or below (reject if warm)
- Frozen product is rock-hard with no thaw drip
- Packaging intact; allergen labelling present where required

## Reject and log

- Temperature fail, damaged seal, pest evidence, missing use-by
- Photograph if the supplier disputes
- Do not put rejected stock into the walk-in ‘to deal with later’
