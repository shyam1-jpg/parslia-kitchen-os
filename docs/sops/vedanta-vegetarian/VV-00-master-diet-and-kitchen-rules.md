# VV-00 — Master Diet & Kitchen Rules (Vedanta Vegetarian)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| SOP ID | VV-00 |
| Title | Master diet & kitchen rules |
| Standard | Vedanta vegetarian — commercial |
| Forbidden | Onion, garlic, all alliums; meat; fish; egg; animal gelatin |
| Out of scope | Chai / tea beverage SOPs |

## 1. Purpose

Define the diet and kitchen behaviour standard for all food prepared, stored, or served under the **Vedanta vegetarian** programme.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all staff, contractors, volunteers, and suppliers who enter food areas or handle ingredients for this programme.

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Vedanta vegetarian | Lacto-vegetarian food prepared in a sattvic spirit: no onion, no garlic, no other alliums, no eggs, no meat, no fish |
| Allium | Onion, garlic, shallot, leek, chives, spring onion / scallion, and any powder, paste, oil, or stock made from them |
| Hing | Asafoetida — approved aromatic substitute when recipes need onion/garlic character |
| Contaminated item | Any food, utensil, or surface that has contacted a forbidden ingredient |

## 4. Allowed (examples)

- Dairy: milk, yogurt, paneer, ghee, butter, cream (where kitchen policy allows dairy)
- Grains, pulses, rice, flours, breads without onion/garlic
- Vegetables and fruits (except alliums)
- Nuts, seeds, coconut
- Spices: cumin, coriander, turmeric, chilli, mustard, fenugreek, curry leaves, ginger, hing
- Oils and fats approved by the kitchen (ghee, sunflower, mustard, coconut, etc.)
- Verified allium-free stock pastes, sauces, and spice mixes only

## 5. Forbidden (absolute)

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Alliums | Onion, garlic, shallot, leek, chives, spring onion, garlic oil, onion powder |
| Animal flesh | Meat, poultry, fish, shellfish |
| Egg | Whole egg, egg powder, mayonnaise with egg unless vegan-verified |
| Hidden allium | Restaurant pastes, jarred sauces, “veg stock” cubes that list onion/garlic |
| Cross-use | Utensils or boards previously used for onion/garlic without deep clean |

## 6. Kitchen behaviour rules

1. Wash hands before starting work and after handling raw produce or waste.
2. Wear clean uniform, hair restraint, and closed shoes.
3. Do not bring personal food containing onion or garlic into prep or cook areas.
4. Read every label before opening a new ingredient.
5. If onion or garlic is found in store or on a delivery, **quarantine** and report to the Kitchen Manager — do not cook with it.
6. Speak calmly; treat food as service (prasad mindset where the site requires it).
7. No chai / tea station procedure is part of this SOP pack — beverage tea service follows a separate site rule if any.

## 7. Escalation

| Issue | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Allium found in recipe card | Stop dish; correct card in Parslia; remake if needed |
| Guest asks for onion/garlic | Explain diet standard politely; offer approved alternative |
| Cross-contamination | Discard affected food; deep-clean; log incident |

## 8. Records

- Induction sign-off sheet  
- Incident log (allium / diet breach)  
- Supplier specification sheets for pastes and stocks  

**End of VV-00**
