# VV-06 — Service & Plating

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| SOP ID | VV-06 |
| Title | Service & plating |
| Company | Vedanta Limited |
| Standard | Vedanta Limited vegetarian commercial |
| Related | VV-00, VV-01, VV-05 |

## 1. Purpose

Present food that looks professional and clearly meets the no-onion, no-garlic standard at the pass, buffet, and dining room under Vedanta Limited rules.

## 2. Pass checklist (every ticket)

1. Correct dish for ticket.  
2. Diet tag matches guest / event (Vedanta Limited vegetarian).  
3. No onion, garlic, shallot, or spring onion visible or in sauce.  
4. Allergen flags called.  
5. Temperature and garnish correct.  
6. Clean rim; wipe spills before service.  

## 3. Plating standards

- Follow the approved plating photo / standard for the dish.
- Garnish with coriander, microgreens (non-allium), lemon, edible flowers, toasted seeds — **never** fried onion or garlic chips.
- Buffet labels must state: `Vedanta Limited vegetarian · No onion · No garlic` plus main allergens.
- Use clean tongs and spoons; change utensils when moving between dishes.

## 4. Guest & host communication

| Situation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| “Can you add garlic?” | “Under Vedanta Limited vegetarian rules we do not use garlic or onion. We can increase ginger, chilli, or hing aroma instead.” |
| “Is this Jain / sattvic?” | Confirm against the specific site policy; this standard means no onion/garlic lacto-veg — clarify if root vegetables are also restricted on that site. |
| Allergic guest | Check the allergen sheet; do not improvise. |

## 5. Transport & events

1. Hot food in insulated boxes; cold food chilled.
2. Seal containers; label each pan.
3. On-site: reheat to safe temperature; refresh garnish without alliums.
4. Do not share serving utensils with a non–Vedanta Limited vegetarian catering line at the same event (VV-08).

## 6. Out of scope

Beverage chai / tea service is **not** covered by this SOP pack.

**End of VV-06**
