# VV-07 — Cleaning & Closing

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| SOP ID | VV-07 |
| Title | Cleaning & closing |
| Standard | Vedanta vegetarian commercial |
| Related | VV-01, VV-02, VV-08 |

## 1. Purpose

Leave the kitchen food-safe, allium-secure, and ready for the next shift.

## 2. During-shift clean-as-you-go

1. Wipe spills immediately (slip risk — see VV-02).
2. Change cloths when dirty; use sanitiser per chemical chart.
3. Scrapers and boards washed between raw and cooked tasks.
4. Keep floor aisles clear for safe lifting and trolley movement.

## 3. End-of-shift closing sequence

1. Turn off / safe-set unused equipment.  
2. Store food labelled (VV-04); discard anything past policy.  
3. Wash, rinse, sanitise utensils; air-dry.  
4. Clean cook line, pass, prep tables, sinks.  
5. Empty and clean waste bins; lift bags using VV-02 technique.  
6. Sweep then mop; dry wet patches before leaving.  
7. Check fridges closed; record final temperatures.  
8. Confirm no onion/garlic products left in Vedanta zones.  
9. Complete Parslia closing checklist.  
10. Lock stores / set alarm per site.  

## 4. Deep clean (scheduled)

| Item | Frequency (typical) |
|------|---------------------|
| Extractor filters | Per site schedule |
| Behind fridges | Weekly |
| Spice & hing tins exterior | Weekly |
| Floor drains | Weekly |
| Walk-in shelving | Monthly |

## 5. Chemicals

- Use only approved chemicals at correct dilution.
- Never store chemicals above food.
- PPE as COSHH / site sheet requires.

**End of VV-07**
