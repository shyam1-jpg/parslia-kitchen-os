# VV-08 — Cross-Contamination Control (Allium & Non-Veg)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| SOP ID | VV-08 |
| Title | Cross-contamination control |
| Standard | Vedanta vegetarian commercial |
| Related | VV-00, VV-03, VV-04, VV-05 |

## 1. Purpose

Stop onion, garlic, egg, meat, and fish from entering Vedanta vegetarian food through shared tools, staff habits, or supplier error.

## 2. Barrier methods

| Control | Practice |
|---------|----------|
| Colour coding | Dedicated boards, knives, cloths, and boxes for Vedanta line |
| Separate spice kit | Hing, cumin, chilli — sealed kit; never shared with garlic kitchen |
| Separate fryers / oils | Do not fry Vedanta food in oil used for garlic bread or onion bhaji |
| Separate storage | High shelves for sealed Vedanta dry goods; quarantine for unknowns |
| Staff flow | If staff work a garlic line first, change apron and wash hands before Vedanta prep |
| Documentation | Recipes in Parslia tagged; printouts match live cards |

## 3. Shared site (two concepts in one building)

1. Physical separation preferred (different rooms).
2. If same kitchen at different times: full clean-down and utensil change between concepts.
3. Never store onion nets in the Vedanta dry store “just for the other brand.”
4. Delivery receiving must sort allium goods away from Vedanta carts immediately (VV-03).

## 4. What counts as contamination

- Knife that cut onion, then used on tomato for Vedanta salad without wash.
- Spoon from garlic tadka dipped into Vedanta dal.
- Fryer oil after garlic naan.
- Hands after peeling garlic, before washing.
- Cutting board scratched and soaked with onion juice, then rinsed only under cold water without detergent/sanitiser.

## 5. Corrective action

1. **Stop** the affected batch.  
2. **Discard** contaminated food — do not scrape off garnish and serve.  
3. **Deep clean** tools and surfaces.  
4. **Remake** from clean mise.  
5. **Log** incident (time, dish, cause, action).  
6. **Retrain** if human error.  
7. **Supplier CAPA** if goods-in failure.  

## 6. Audit questions (manager weekly)

- Are hing kits intact and labelled?  
- Any onion/garlic in Vedanta fridges?  
- Do buffet labels still say No onion / No garlic?  
- Any shared fryer risk this week?  
- Staff sign-offs current for VV-00 and VV-02?  

**End of VV-08**
