# VV-05 — Prep & Cooking

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| SOP ID | VV-05 |
| Title | Prep & cooking |
| Standard | Vedanta vegetarian — no onion, no garlic |
| Related | VV-00, VV-01, VV-08 |
| Out of scope | Chai / tea brewing |

## 1. Purpose

Cook flavoursome Vedanta vegetarian food using hing and approved aromatics instead of onion and garlic.

## 2. Prep rules

1. Wash produce under running water; scrub root vegetables.
2. Use colour-coded boards for raw veg, dairy, and cooked food per site code.
3. Cut sizes match the recipe card (uniform for even cooking).
4. Hold cut veg chilled and covered until needed.
5. Measure spices; do not tip containers over steaming pots (moisture ruins hing and spices).

## 3. Building flavour without onion or garlic

| Technique | How |
|-----------|-----|
| Hing tadka | Heat ghee/oil → crackle cumin or mustard → pinch of hing off heat or briefly in oil → pour over dal/veg |
| Ginger–chilli | Fresh ginger and green chilli for sharpness |
| Tomato base | Cook tomatoes down for body and acidity |
| Spice toast | Dry-toast cumin, coriander, fennel for depth |
| Coconut / cashew | Creamy body for gravies |
| Curry leaf | South-Indian aroma without alliums |

**Never** use garlic paste, ginger-garlic paste, onion paste, or fried onion as a shortcut.

## 4. Cooking sequence (typical hot line)

1. Confirm recipe in Parslia is tagged Vedanta / allium-free.
2. Heat fat; add whole spices; add hing correctly (do not burn — bitter).
3. Add vegetables / pulses / liquids per method.
4. Season; simmer to correct texture.
5. Finish with fresh coriander, lemon, or ghee as recipe allows.
6. Hot-hold or plate immediately; core temperature check when required.
7. Taste with clean spoon; adjust salt/spice only — do not “fix” with onion/garlic.

## 5. Common dishes — compliance notes

| Dish type | Watch-outs |
|-----------|------------|
| Dal | Hing in tadka; no onion tempering |
| Paneer gravy | Tomato–cashew or spinach base; no onion masala |
| Pulao / fried rice | No spring onion garnish; use peas, beans, carrot |
| Chutneys | Coriander, mint, coconut, tomato — no raw onion |
| Indo-Chinese | No garlic sauce; build heat with chilli and ginger |
| Mithai | No egg unless vegan line approved |

## 6. Equipment

- Prefer stainless, iron, or non-stick as recipe states.
- Do not use pans that just cooked onion/garlic for another concept kitchen without deep clean (VV-08).
- Keep a dedicated hing tadka ladle if shared sites struggle with contamination.

## 7. Stop-work triggers

Stop cooking and call the lead if:

- A jar or paste lists onion or garlic mid-service.
- A garnish of fried onion arrives from pastry or another station.
- A guest allergy conflicts with a dish — do not guess; check Parslia allergen data.
