# VV-01 — How to Work (Shift & Roles)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| SOP ID | VV-01 |
| Title | How to work |
| Company | Vedanta Limited |
| Standard | Vedanta Limited vegetarian commercial kitchen |
| Related | VV-00, VV-02, VV-05, VV-07 |

## 1. Purpose

Set a clear, professional way of working from arrival to clock-out so every shift protects diet integrity, food safety, and calm service for Vedanta Limited.

## 2. Before you start (opening)

1. Clock in / sign the site rota.
2. Put on clean uniform, apron, hair restraint, closed non-slip shoes.
3. Wash hands for 20 seconds; dry with disposable towel.
4. Read the **day board**: menus, covers, allergens, diet tags (`Vedanta Limited vegetarian`, `No onion`, `No garlic`).
5. Check fridge / freezer temperatures and log them.
6. Confirm no onion, garlic, or other alliums are on the pass or prep tables.
7. Collect tools for your station only; do not borrow contaminated kit from other kitchens.

## 3. Role map (typical)

| Role | Primary duties |
|------|----------------|
| Head Chef / Kitchen Manager | Menu, diet compliance, briefing, final pass check |
| Sous / Lead cook | Hot line, hing tadka, timing, quality |
| Prep cook | Veg cut, dals, rice, sauces — allium-free only |
| Pastry / mithai | Sweets and desserts — verify no egg unless vegan line |
| Steward / KP | Wash-up, deep clean, waste segregation |
| Runner / service support | Plating assist, buffet replenishment, no plate stacking hazards |

## 4. During prep

1. Work from the approved recipe card or printed recipe — do not “add onion for taste.”
2. Mise en place: wash → peel → cut → weigh → label → cover → chill.
3. Keep hing, ginger, chilli, and spice boxes labelled and dry.
4. Taste only with a clean spoon; never double-dip.
5. If a recipe lists onion or garlic, **stop** and escalate (see VV-00). Replace with hing / ginger / tomato base as directed by Head Chef.
6. Batch-cook dals, rice, and sauces early; finish tadka close to service.

## 5. During service

1. Stand at your station; call when tickets change.
2. Plate to the approved plating standard / photo reference.
3. Call allergens and diet status clearly on the pass.
4. Never garnish with spring onion, fried onion, or garlic chips.
5. Keep walkways clear; use VV-02 for any heavy lift.
6. If you leave the line, wash hands before returning.

## 6. Communication

- Brief at start of shift: 5 minutes max — menu, allergens, 86’d items, VIP notes.
- Use calm, short calls: “Behind,” “Hot,” “Sharp,” “Allium check clear.”
- No arguing on the pass; take issues to the manager after the ticket is plated or held safely.

## 7. Breaks & personal conduct

1. Eat only in the designated staff area.
2. Do not eat onion/garlic foods before returning to the Vedanta Limited vegetarian line without washing hands, rinsing mouth if required by site policy, and changing apron.
3. Phones off the cook line unless required for approved kitchen systems.
4. Report illness (vomiting, diarrhoea, fever, open infected wounds) — do not work food.

## 8. End of your work block

1. Clean your station (VV-07).
2. Label and date leftovers correctly (VV-04).
3. Restock hing and verified spices.
4. Hand over open tickets and low stock to the next lead.
5. Sign closing checks; clock out.

## 9. Performance standard

You are working correctly when:

- Every dish on the pass is onion-free and garlic-free.
- Temperatures and labels are logged.
- The line is calm, stocked, and safe to walk through.
- Guests receive consistent Vedanta Limited vegetarian food.

**End of VV-01**
