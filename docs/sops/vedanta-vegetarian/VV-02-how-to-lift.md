# VV-02 — How to Lift (Manual Handling)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| SOP ID | VV-02 |
| Title | How to lift |
| Company | Vedanta Limited |
| Standard | Commercial kitchen manual handling |
| Related | VV-01, VV-03, VV-04 |

## 1. Purpose

Prevent back, shoulder, and hand injuries when moving stock, pots, gas cylinders, rice sacks, dairy crates, and waste in a Vedanta Limited vegetarian kitchen.

## 2. Scope

All staff who lift, carry, push, or pull anything heavier than a light tray. Applies in dry store, cold rooms, prep, cook line, dish pit, and delivery bay.

## 3. Before you lift — TILE check

| Letter | Check |
|--------|--------|
| **T**ask | Can the job be done without lifting (trolley, pump, two people)? |
| **I**ndividual | Are you fit, trained, and free of injury today? |
| **L**oad | Weight, shape, hot/cold, liquid spill risk, sharp edges? |
| **E**nvironment | Floor dry? Path clear? Door propped? Lights on? |

If any answer is unsafe — **do not lift alone**. Get help or use equipment.

## 4. Safe lifting technique (floor to waist)

1. Stand close to the load, feet shoulder-width, one foot slightly forward.
2. Bend hips and knees; keep the back naturally straight — do not round the spine.
3. Get a firm grip under the load; use gloves for rough sacks or cold crates.
4. Tighten core; lift smoothly with the legs — no jerk.
5. Keep the load close to the body; elbows tucked.
6. Turn by moving the feet — **do not twist** the spine while holding weight.
7. Set down by bending knees; keep control until the load is stable.

## 5. Kitchen-specific loads

| Load | Rule |
|------|------|
| Full stockpot / kadai | Two people if > ~10–12 kg or liquid is hot; call “hot pot moving” |
| Rice / flour sack (20–25 kg) | Prefer sack truck; if carried, hug to chest, short distance only |
| Dairy / yogurt crates | Use trolley; stack no higher than chin level |
| Oil / ghee tins | Cap secure; two hands; never lift by the spout alone |
| Gas cylinder | Trained staff only; chain when stored; never drag on side across kitchen floor |
| Hot trays from oven | Dry cloths / oven gloves; announce “hot behind” |
| Waste bins | Do not overfill; wheel to bay; lift bag with bent knees |

## 6. Team lifts

1. Agree who leads the count: “One, two, **lift**.”
2. Same height grip where possible; walk in step.
3. Communicate stops: “Door,” “Step,” “Set.”
4. Never surprise-release a shared load.

## 7. Pushing and pulling

- Push rather than pull when you can see the path.
- Keep handles at mid-torso height.
- Slow on wet floors; wipe spills immediately.

## 8. Never do this

- Lift a heavy pot of boiling liquid alone above waist height.
- Climb on crates or chairs to lift stock from high shelves.
- Carry loads that block your view of stairs or corners.
- Catch a falling hot pan with bare hands.
- Twist while holding a full hotel pan of dal or rice.

## 9. After an incident

1. Stop work if injured.
2. First aid / manager notify.
3. Log in the accident book and site incident record.
4. Do not continue heavy lifts until cleared.

## 10. Training

Demonstrate VV-02 at induction. Re-brief after any lifting injury on site. Sign-off required.

**End of VV-02**
