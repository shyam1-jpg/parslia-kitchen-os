# Green Mango Salad

**Continent:** South America & Caribbean — Trinidad  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Glass bowl

## Why this dish

Sharp salad that replaces onion kuchela on a sattvic table.

## Ingredients

- 2 green mangoes, julienned
- 1 cucumber, julienned
- Hot pepper, minced
- Lime
- Salt
- Roasted geera
- Coriander or culantro
- Pinch of sugar

## Method

1. Toss mango and cucumber with lime, salt, geera and pepper.
2. Rest 10 minutes. The mango’s acid is the ‘onion’.
