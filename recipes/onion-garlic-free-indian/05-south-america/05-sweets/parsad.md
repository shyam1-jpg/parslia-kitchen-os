# Parsad

**Continent:** South America & Caribbean — Trinidad Hindu prayer sweet  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Cream-of-wheat prasad. Already onion-garlic free; keep it in steel.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup ghee
- 1 cup sooji (cream of wheat)
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 cups milk
- 4 cardamom
- 2 tbsp raisins
- 2 tbsp chopped cherries or mixed peel (optional)

## Method

1. Roast sooji in ghee on low until fragrant.
2. Warm milk with sugar and cardamom separately in steel.
3. Pour into the sooji, stirring. Add raisins. Cook until it leaves the sides.
4. Offer, then serve warm.
