# Caribbean Sweet Rice

**Continent:** South America & Caribbean — Guyana / Trinidad  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel pot

## Why this dish

Indo-Caribbean kheer, often with coconut milk on the islands.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup rice
- 2 cups milk
- 1 cup coconut milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- Cardamom
- Cinnamon stick
- Raisins
- Vanilla (optional, ¼ tsp)

## Method

1. Simmer rice in milk and cinnamon in a steel pot until very soft.
2. Add coconut milk, sugar, cardamom and raisins. Cook until creamy.
3. Serve chilled or warm. No aluminium pot.
