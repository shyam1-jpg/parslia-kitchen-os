# Baigan Choka (no garlic)

**Continent:** South America & Caribbean — Trinidad  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron or open flame + steel bowl

## Why this dish

Smoky mashed eggplant. Classic choka is heavy on garlic — hing, cumin and hot pepper do that job.

## Ingredients

- 2 large baigan (eggplant)
- 2 tbsp oil
- ½ tsp toasted geera
- Pinch of hing
- 1 tomato, roasted
- Hot pepper to taste
- Salt
- Culantro or coriander

## Method

1. Roast whole baigan on a flame or in a cast-iron pan until collapsed and charred. Peel.
2. Mash with roasted tomato, oil, geera, hing, pepper and salt.
3. Taste. If it feels flat, add more toasted geera and lime — not garlic.
