# Sada Roti

**Continent:** South America & Caribbean — Trinidad / Guyana  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Thick Indo-Caribbean bread for scooping channa. Iron tawa only.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups atta
- Pinch of baking powder
- Pinch of salt
- Water

## Method

1. Knead a soft dough. Rest 20 minutes.
2. Pat into a thick round. Cook on a hot iron tawa, turning, until it puffs and spots.
3. Wrap in a cloth. Tear and scoop the curry.
