# Kalakand

**Continent:** Europe — British Indian mithai counter  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Milk-cake sweet sold in every UK Indian sweet shop. Naturally no allium.

## Ingredients

- 500 g full-fat ricotta or well-drained chenna
- 200 g condensed milk
- 1 tsp ghee
- ½ tsp cardamom
- Pistachios

## Method

1. Cook ricotta and condensed milk in a steel kadhai on medium-low, stirring, 15–20 minutes until thick.
2. Add ghee and cardamom. Spread in a greased steel/glass tray.
3. Top with pistachios. Chill 1 hour and cut.
