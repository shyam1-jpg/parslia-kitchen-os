# Kesar Shrikhand

**Continent:** Europe — UK / Maharashtrian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl — no cooking pan needed

## Why this dish

No-cook yogurt dessert. The calm end to a masala-heavy British Indian meal.

## Ingredients

- 500 g hung yogurt
- ⅓ cup powdered sugar
- 8 saffron strands in 1 tbsp warm milk
- ½ tsp cardamom
- Pistachios and almonds

## Method

1. Whisk hung yogurt with sugar, saffron milk and cardamom until satin-smooth.
2. Chill 2 hours. Serve with nuts.
3. Do not add fruit with onion-family garnishes (spring onion is sometimes used on fusion plates — skip it).
