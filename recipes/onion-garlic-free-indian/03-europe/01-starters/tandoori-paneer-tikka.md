# Tandoori Paneer Tikka

**Continent:** Europe — British Indian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 40 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron grill pan or steel oven tray

## Why this dish

The UK’s favourite Indian starter, marinade rewritten without garlic ginger paste full of garlic.

## Ingredients

- 300 g paneer, cubed
- 1 capsicum, chunks
- 1 tomato, petals (seeds removed)
- 200 g hung yogurt
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp kasuri methi
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp mustard oil
- Pinch of hing
- Salt
- Chaat masala to finish

## Method

1. Mix the marinade. Coat paneer and vegetables. Rest 30 minutes.
2. Skewer. Roast on a cast-iron grill or steel tray at 220°C for 12–15 minutes, turning once.
3. Finish with lemon and chaat masala.

## Notes

Skip bottled tikka paste — almost all of them list garlic.
