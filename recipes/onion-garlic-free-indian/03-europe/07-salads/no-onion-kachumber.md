# No-Onion Kachumber

**Continent:** Europe — British Indian table salad  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Glass bowl

## Why this dish

Pub-curry-house salad minus the raw onion.

## Ingredients

- 1 cucumber, diced
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- ½ green apple or raw mango, diced (for bite)
- 1 green chilli
- Coriander
- Lemon
- Roasted cumin
- Salt
- Pinch of sugar

## Method

1. Combine and dress just before serving.
2. Apple or raw mango replaces the crunch and bite of onion.
