# Vegetable Tikka Masala

**Continent:** Europe — United Kingdom  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Britain’s national curry, sattvic version. Smoked paprika stands in for tandoor and alliums.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp ghee or butter
- 1 bay leaf
- 4 cardamom
- Pinch of hing
- 3 tomatoes + 12 soaked cashews, blended
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- Cauliflower florets, capsicum, paneer cubes, peas (about 4 cups mixed, roasted)
- ½ cup water
- 3 tbsp cream
- 1 tsp kasuri methi
- ½ tsp garam masala
- Salt
- Pinch of sugar

## Method

1. Roast the vegetables and paneer on a steel tray until charred at the edges.
2. Make the gravy: ghee, whole spices, hing, tomato-cashew paste and ground spices. Cook until fat separates.
3. Add water, cream, kasuri methi, salt and sugar.
4. Fold in roasted vegetables. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
