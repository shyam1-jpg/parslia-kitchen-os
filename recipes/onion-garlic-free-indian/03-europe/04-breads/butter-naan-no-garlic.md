# Butter Naan (no garlic)

**Continent:** Europe — United Kingdom  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 70 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Restaurant naan without garlic butter.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- ½ cup yogurt
- ½ tsp sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp baking powder
- 2 tbsp milk
- 1 tbsp oil
- Butter and coriander for finishing

## Method

1. Knead a soft dough. Rest 1 hour.
2. Roll ovals. Cook on a screaming-hot iron tawa. Flip and blister the top over a flame.
3. Brush with butter (not garlic butter) and coriander.

## Notes

If you have no flame, finish 2 minutes under a hot steel-grill oven.
