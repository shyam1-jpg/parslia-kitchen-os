# Pazham Pori

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ripe plantain fritters.

## Ingredients

- Nendran banana
- Maida
- Rice flour
- Sugar
- Turmeric pinch
- Oil

## Method

1. Batter, fry in steel.
