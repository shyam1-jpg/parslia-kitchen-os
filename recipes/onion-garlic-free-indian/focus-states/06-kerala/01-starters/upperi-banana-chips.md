# Upperi Banana Chips

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Salted raw-banana chips of Kerala.

## Ingredients

- Raw banana, sliced
- Coconut oil
- Salt
- Turmeric water

## Method

1. Fry in coconut oil in a steel kadhai. Salt.
