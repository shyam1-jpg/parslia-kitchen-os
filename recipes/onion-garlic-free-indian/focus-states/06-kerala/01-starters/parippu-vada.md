# Parippu Vada

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Chana-dal vada. Skip onion; use curry leaf, ginger, chilli.

## Ingredients

- Chana dal, soaked coarsely crushed
- Ginger
- Green chilli
- Curry leaves
- Hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Shape, fry in steel.
