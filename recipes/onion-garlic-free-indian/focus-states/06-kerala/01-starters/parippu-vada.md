# Parippu Vada

**Recipe card** · Kerala kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Chana-dal vada. Skip onion; use curry leaf, ginger, chilli.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 100 | g | chana dal, soaked |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Shape, fry in steel.
