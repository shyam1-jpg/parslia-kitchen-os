## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | cabbage, shredded |

| 150 | g | urad dal |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Temper, cover briefly, mix coconut.
