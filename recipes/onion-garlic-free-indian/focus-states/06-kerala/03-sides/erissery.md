# Erissery

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Pumpkin and cowpeas with ground coconut.

## Ingredients

- Pumpkin
- Cowpeas
- Coconut-cumin-chilli paste
- Mustard
- Curry leaves
- Coconut oil
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook pumpkin and peas. Add coconut paste. Temper.
