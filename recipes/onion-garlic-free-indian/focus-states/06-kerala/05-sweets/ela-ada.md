# Ela Ada

| | |
|---|---|

| **Serves** | 4 |

Rice parcels with coconut-jaggery, steamed in banana leaf.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 100 | g | ground rice paste |
| 200 | g | coconut-jaggery filling |
| 2 | pieces | ripe bananas |

## Method

1. Spread, fill, fold, steam in steel.
