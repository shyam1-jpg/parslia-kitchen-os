# Unniyappam

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron unniyappam pan — not aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Kerala, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Rice flour
- Jaggery
- Banana
- Cardamom
- Coconut bits
- Ghee

## Method

1. Thick batter, fry in ghee wells.
