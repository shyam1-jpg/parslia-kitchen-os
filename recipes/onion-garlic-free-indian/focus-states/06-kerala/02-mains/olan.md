# Olan

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ash gourd and cowpeas in thin coconut milk — sadya main.

## Ingredients

- Ash gourd
- Red cowpeas, cooked
- Thin + thick coconut milk
- Green chilli
- Curry leaves
- Coconut oil
- Salt

## Method

1. Simmer gourd and peas in thin milk. Finish thick milk and curry leaves. No allium.
