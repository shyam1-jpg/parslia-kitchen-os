|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen |

| **Serves** | 4 |

Sadya mixed veg in coconut-yogurt. Never onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 600 | g | yam, ash gourd, carrot, beans, drumstick and raw banana |
| 80 | g | coconut-cumin-chilli paste (no garlic) |
| 200 | g | plain yogurt |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| 2 | tbsp | coconut oil |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook veg, add coconut, fold yogurt off the boil, coconut oil.
