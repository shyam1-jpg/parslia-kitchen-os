# Avial

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sadya mixed veg in coconut-yogurt. Never onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Yam, ash gourd, carrot, beans, drumstick, raw banana
- Coconut-cumin-chilli paste
- Yogurt
- Curry leaves
- Coconut oil
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook veg, add coconut, fold yogurt off the boil, coconut oil.
