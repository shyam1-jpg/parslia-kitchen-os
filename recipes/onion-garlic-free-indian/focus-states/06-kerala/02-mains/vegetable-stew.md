# Vegetable Stew

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Coconut-milk stew for appam. Ginger, pepper, curry leaf — no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- Potato, carrot, beans, peas
- Coconut milk
- Ginger
- Peppercorns
- Curry leaves
- Hing
- Coconut oil
- Salt

## Method

1. Simmer veg in thin milk with ginger and pepper. Thick milk at the end.
