# Vegetable Stew

Coconut-milk stew for appam. Ginger, pepper, curry leaf — no onion garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | potatoes |
| 150 | g | carrot, cut |
| 250 | g | french beans or local beans |
| 150 | g | green peas |
| 400 | ml | thick coconut milk |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| ½ | tsp | black peppercorns |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 2 | tbsp | coconut oil |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Simmer veg in thin milk with ginger and pepper. Thick milk at the end.
