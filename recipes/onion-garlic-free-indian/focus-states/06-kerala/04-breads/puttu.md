# Puttu

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Steamed rice cylinders with coconut. Steel or bamboo puttu maker — not aluminium.

## Ingredients

- Rice flour, roasted
- Grated coconut
- Salt
- Water

## Method

1. Layer flour and coconut. Steam 5–7 minutes.
