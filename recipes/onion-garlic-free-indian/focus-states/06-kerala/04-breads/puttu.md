# Puttu

**Recipe card** · Kerala kitchen · Bread

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen |
| **Course** | Bread |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 30 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Steamed rice cylinders with coconut. Steel or bamboo puttu maker — not aluminium.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | rice flour |
| 80 | g | fresh grated coconut |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 500 | ml | water |

## Method

1. Layer flour and coconut. Steam 5–7 minutes.
