# Appam

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 70 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Lace rice pancake in a cast-iron appam kadai.

## Ingredients

- Rice
- Coconut
- Yeast
- Sugar
- Salt

## Method

1. Ferment, swirl in iron pan, cover 1 minute.
