# Appam

**Recipe card** · Kerala kitchen · Bread

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen |
| **Course** | Bread |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 70 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 90 min |
| **Cookware** | Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium) |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Lace rice pancake in a cast-iron appam kadai.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | raw rice |
| 100 | g | fresh grated coconut |
| 1 | tsp | instant yeast |
| 100 | g | sugar |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Ferment, swirl in iron pan, cover 1 minute.
