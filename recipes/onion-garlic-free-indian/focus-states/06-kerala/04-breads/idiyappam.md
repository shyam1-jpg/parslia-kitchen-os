# Idiyappam

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 10 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Rice-string nests.

## Ingredients

- Roasted rice flour
- Hot water
- Salt
- Coconut to serve

## Method

1. Press noodles, steam in steel 5 minutes.
