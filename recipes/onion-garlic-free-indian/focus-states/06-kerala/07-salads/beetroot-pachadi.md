# Beetroot Pachadi

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature salad from Kerala, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Beetroot
- Coconut paste
- Yogurt
- Mustard tadka
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook beet, fold coconut-yogurt, temper.
