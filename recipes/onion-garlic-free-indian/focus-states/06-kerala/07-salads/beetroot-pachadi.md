# Beetroot Pachadi

|---|---|

| **Serves** | 4 |

| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |

Signature salad from Kerala, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | beetroot, cubed |
| 80 | g | fresh coconut paste |
| 200 | g | plain yogurt |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds, for tempering |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook beet, fold coconut-yogurt, temper.
