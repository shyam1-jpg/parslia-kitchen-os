# Pineapple Pachadi

**Recipe card** · Kerala kitchen · Salad

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen |
| **Course** | Salad |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 12 min |
| **Total** | 27 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Sadya pineapple-yogurt coconut relish.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | pineapple, cubed |
| 80 | g | coconut-chilli-mustard paste |
| 200 | g | plain yogurt |
| 1 | pinch | jaggery |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook pineapple, add paste and yogurt off the boil.
