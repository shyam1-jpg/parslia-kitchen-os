# Pineapple Pachadi

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sadya pineapple-yogurt coconut relish.

## Ingredients

- Pineapple
- Coconut-chilli-mustard paste
- Yogurt
- Jaggery pinch
- Curry leaves
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook pineapple, add paste and yogurt off the boil.
