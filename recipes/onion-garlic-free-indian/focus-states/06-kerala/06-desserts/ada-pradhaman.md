# Ada Pradhaman

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Jaggery-coconut-milk payasam with ada.

## Ingredients

- Ada
- Jaggery
- Thin and thick coconut milk
- Ghee
- Cashews
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Cook ada in jaggery. Coconut milk. Do not boil hard after thick milk.
