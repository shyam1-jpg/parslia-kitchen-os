# Ada Pradhaman

**Recipe card** · Kerala kitchen · Dessert

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Kerala — Kerala sadya kitchen |
| **Course** | Dessert |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 30 min |
| **Total** | 45 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Jaggery-coconut-milk payasam with ada.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 80 | g | rice ada |
| 100 | g | jaggery, grated |
| 400 | ml | thin coconut milk plus 200 ml thick |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 20 | g | cashews |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, crushed |

## Method

1. Cook ada in jaggery. Coconut milk. Do not boil hard after thick milk.
