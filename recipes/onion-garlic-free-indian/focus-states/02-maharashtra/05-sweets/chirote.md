Flaky fried pastry with sugar.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | maida (refined flour) |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 100 | g | sugar |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, crushed |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Layer with ghee, roll, cut, fry in steel, dust sugar.
