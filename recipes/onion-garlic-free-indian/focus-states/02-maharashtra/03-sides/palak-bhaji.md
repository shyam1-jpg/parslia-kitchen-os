# Palak Bhaji

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Maharashtra, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Spinach
- Cumin
- Hing
- Green chilli
- Ginger
- Salt

## Method

1. Wilt in a steel pan. No onion.
