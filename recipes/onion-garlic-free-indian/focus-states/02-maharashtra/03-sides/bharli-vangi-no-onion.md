# Bharli Vangi (no onion)

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Stuffed brinjal with coconut-peanut, no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 8 small brinjals
- Coconut
- Peanuts
- Sesame
- Tamarind
- Jaggery
- Goda masala garlic-free
- Salt

## Method

1. Fill slits. Cook covered until soft and the masala clings.
