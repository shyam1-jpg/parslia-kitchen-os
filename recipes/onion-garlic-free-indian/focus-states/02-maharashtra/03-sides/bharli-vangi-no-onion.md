# Bharli Vangi (no onion)

**Recipe card** · Maharashtra kitchen · Side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking |
| **Course** | Side |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 25 min |
| **Total** | 40 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Stuffed brinjal with coconut-peanut, no onion garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 8 | — | small brinjals |
| 100 | g | fresh grated coconut |
| 30 | g | roasted peanuts |
| 3 | tbsp | white sesame seeds |
| 20 | g | seedless tamarind |
| 100 | g | jaggery, grated |
| 2 | tsp | goda masala (no onion-garlic) |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Fill slits. Cook covered until soft and the masala clings.
