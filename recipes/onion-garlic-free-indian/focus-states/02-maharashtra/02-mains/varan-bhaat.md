# Varan Bhaat

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Plain toor varan with ghee and rice — the Maharashtrian comfort main.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup toor dal
- Turmeric
- Ghee
- Cumin
- Hing
- Salt
- Steamed rice

## Method

1. Cook dal very soft. Tadka ghee, cumin, hing. Pour over hot rice with more ghee.
