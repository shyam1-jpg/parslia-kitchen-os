Plain toor varan with ghee and rice — the Maharashtrian comfort main.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| ¾ | cup | toor dal |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 400 | g | steamed rice |

## Method

1. Cook dal very soft. Tadka ghee, cumin, hing. Pour over hot rice with more ghee.
