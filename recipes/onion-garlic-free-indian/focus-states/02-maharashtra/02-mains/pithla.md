Besan curry of rural Maharashtra. Hing, ginger, chilli — no onion.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | cup | besan |
| 3 | cups | water |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Temper, pour besan slurry, cook until the raw smell goes. Serve with bhakri.
