# Pithla

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Besan curry of rural Maharashtra. Hing, ginger, chilli — no onion.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup besan
- 3 cups water
- Mustard, cumin, hing, curry leaves, green chilli, ginger
- Turmeric
- Salt

## Method

1. Temper, pour besan slurry, cook until the raw smell goes. Serve with bhakri.
