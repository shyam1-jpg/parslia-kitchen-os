# Kothimbir Vadi

**Recipe card** · Maharashtra kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 40 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Coriander-besan steamed cake, sliced and fried.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | coriander |
| 1 | cup | besan |
| 1 | tbsp | ginger-green chilli paste (no garlic) |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 3 | tbsp | white sesame seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Steam 12 minutes, slice, pan-fry in steel until crisp.
