# Kothimbir Vadi

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Coriander-besan steamed cake, sliced and fried.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups coriander
- 1 cup besan
- Ginger-chilli
- Turmeric
- Sesame
- Hing
- Salt

## Method

1. Steam 12 minutes, slice, pan-fry in steel until crisp.
