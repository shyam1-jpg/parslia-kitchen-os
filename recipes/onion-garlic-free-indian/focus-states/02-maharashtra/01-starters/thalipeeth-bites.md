# Thalipeeth Bites

**Recipe card** · Maharashtra kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 30 min |
| **Cookware** | Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium) |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Multi-grain savoury pancake. Skip onion; use sesame, chilli, coriander.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | cup | jowar-bajra-atta mix (bhajani) |
| 3 | tbsp | white sesame seeds |
| 1 | tbsp | ginger-green chilli paste (no garlic) |
| 15 | g | fresh coriander leaves |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Pat small rounds on iron tawa. Cook both sides.
