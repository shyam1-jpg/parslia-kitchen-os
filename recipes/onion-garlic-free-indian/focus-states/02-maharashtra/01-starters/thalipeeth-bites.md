# Thalipeeth Bites

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Multi-grain savoury pancake. Skip onion; use sesame, chilli, coriander.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup jowar-bajra-atta mix (bhajani)
- Sesame
- Ginger-chilli
- Coriander
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Pat small rounds on iron tawa. Cook both sides.
