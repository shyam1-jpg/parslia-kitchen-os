# Sabudana Vada

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fasting vada — already no onion garlic if you skip peanut-onion mixes.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup soaked sabudana
- 2 boiled potatoes
- Roasted peanuts crushed
- Ginger-chilli
- Cumin
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Shape, fry in a steel kadhai. Serve with coconut chutney (no garlic).
