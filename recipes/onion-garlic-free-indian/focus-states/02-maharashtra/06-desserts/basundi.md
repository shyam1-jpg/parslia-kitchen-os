# Basundi

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Reduced sweet milk of Maharashtra.

## Ingredients

- 1 litre milk
- Sugar
- Cardamom
- Saffron
- Nuts

## Method

1. Reduce in a heavy steel pot, scraping cream back in.
