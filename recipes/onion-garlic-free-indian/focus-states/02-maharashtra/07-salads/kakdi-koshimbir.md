# Kakdi Koshimbir

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian vegetarian and vrat cooking  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Cucumber-coconut-peanut. Skip onion.

## Ingredients

- Cucumber
- Coconut
- Peanuts
- Lemon
- Green chilli
- Cumin
- Salt

## Method

1. Mix. Peanut and lemon replace onion.
