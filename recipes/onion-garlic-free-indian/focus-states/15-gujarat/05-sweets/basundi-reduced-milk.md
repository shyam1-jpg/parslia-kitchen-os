# Basundi (reduced milk)

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 50 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 L milk, ½ cup sugar, cardamom, saffron, almonds, pistachios
- Heavy kadai

## Method

1. Reduce milk. Sugar, nuts. Serve warm or chilled.
