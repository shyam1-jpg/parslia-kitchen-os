# Basundi (reduced milk)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style |
| **Course** | Sweet |

| **Cookware** | Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium |

Signature sweet from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | L | milk |
| ½ | cup | sugar, cardamom, saffron, almonds, pistachios |

## Method

1. Reduce milk. Sugar, nuts. Serve warm or chilled.
