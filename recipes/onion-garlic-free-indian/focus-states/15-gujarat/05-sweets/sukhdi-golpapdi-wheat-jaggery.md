# Sukhdi / golpapdi (wheat jaggery)

| **Course** | Sweet |

Signature sweet from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | atta |
| 1 | cup | ghee |
| 1 | cup | jaggery |

## Method

1. Roast atta in ghee. Mix jaggery off heat. Press in tray. Cut diamonds.
