# Mohanthal (besan fudge)

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups coarse besan, 1 cup ghee, 1½ cups sugar syrup, milk, cardamom, nuts
- Heavy kadai

## Method

1. Roast besan in ghee till grainy. Add syrup. Set in greased steel tray. Cut.
