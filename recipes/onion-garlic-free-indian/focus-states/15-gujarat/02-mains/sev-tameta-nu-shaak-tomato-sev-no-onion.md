# Sev tameta nu shaak (tomato sev, no onion)

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature main from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 5 tomatoes chopped
- 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp hing, chilli, turmeric, coriander, jaggery pinch, salt
- 1 cup thick sev, ghee in kadai

## Method

1. Cook tomato masala. Add sev last minute. Serve with rotli.
