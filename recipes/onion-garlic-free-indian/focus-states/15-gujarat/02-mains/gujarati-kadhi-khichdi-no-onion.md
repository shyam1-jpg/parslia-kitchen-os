# Gujarati kadhi + khichdi (no onion)

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature main from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Kadhi: yogurt, besan, ginger, green chilli, curry leaf, mustard, hing, jaggery pinch
- Khichdi: rice + moong dal, turmeric, ghee, cumin, hing
- Kadai

## Method

1. Simmer kadhi sweet-sour. Cook khichdi. Temper both. Serve together.
