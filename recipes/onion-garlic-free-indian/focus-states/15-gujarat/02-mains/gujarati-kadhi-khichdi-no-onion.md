# Gujarati kadhi + khichdi (no onion)

Signature main from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 400 | g | yogurt, plus 3 tbsp besan, ginger, chilli, curry leaf, mustard, hing, pinch jaggery |
| 150 | g | rice plus 100 g moong dal, turmeric, ghee, cumin, hing |

## Method

1. Simmer kadhi sweet-sour. Cook khichdi. Temper both. Serve together.
