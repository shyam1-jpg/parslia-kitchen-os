# Guvar fali salad (cluster bean, no onion)

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature salad from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups guvar steamed
- Lemon, chilli, cumin, salt, coconut

## Method

1. Steam in steamer. Toss.
