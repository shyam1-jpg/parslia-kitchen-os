Signature salad from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | guvar steamed |
| 1 | tbsp | lemon juice |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 100 | g | fresh grated coconut |

## Method

1. Steam in steamer. Toss.
