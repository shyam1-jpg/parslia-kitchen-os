# Kachumber no onion (Gujarati)

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Signature salad from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Cucumber, tomato, raw mango, chilli, coriander, lemon, salt, sugar pinch

## Method

1. Chop. Toss. No onion.
