# Batata nu shaak (dry potato, no onion)

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 4 potatoes cubed
- Mustard, cumin, hing, turmeric, chilli, coriander, salt, oil in kadai

## Method

1. Temper. Potatoes. Cover-cook. Slightly sweet optional jaggery pinch.
