# Fafda (crisp gram strips)

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature starter from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups coarse besan, 1 tsp ajwain, ½ tsp hing, salt, oil, water
- Oil in kadai

## Method

1. Knead stiff. Press strips. Fry. Serve with papaya sambharo and fried chillies.
