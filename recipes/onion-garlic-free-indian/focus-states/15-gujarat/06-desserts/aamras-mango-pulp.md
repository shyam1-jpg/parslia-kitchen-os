# Aamras (mango pulp)

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Gujarat, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 4 Alphonso or Kesar mangoes
- Pinch cardamom, saffron optional, sugar if needed

## Method

1. Pulp. Chill. Serve with puri.
