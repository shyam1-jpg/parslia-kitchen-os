# Rabri

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal sattvic  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Uttar Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Milk
- Sugar
- Saffron
- Pistachios
