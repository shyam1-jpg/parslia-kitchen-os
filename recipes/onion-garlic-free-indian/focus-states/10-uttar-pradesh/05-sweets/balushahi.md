Signature sweet from Uttar Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | maida (refined flour) |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 200 | g | plain yogurt |
| ¼ | tsp | baking soda |
| 250 | ml | sugar syrup (1-string) |

## Method

1. Fry on low, soak in syrup made in steel.
