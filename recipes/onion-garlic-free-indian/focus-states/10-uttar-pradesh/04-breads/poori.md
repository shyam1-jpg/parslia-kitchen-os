# Poori

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal sattvic  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature bread from Uttar Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Atta
- Ghee
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Fry in steel.
