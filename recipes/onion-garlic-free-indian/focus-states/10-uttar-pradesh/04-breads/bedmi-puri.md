# Bedmi Puri

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal sattvic  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Urad-stuffed puri of UP breakfasts.

## Ingredients

- Atta
- Urad grind with fennel, ginger, hing, chilli
- Oil

## Method

1. Roll thick, fry until brown and puffed.
