Signature main from Uttar Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | plain yogurt |
| 150 | g | besan (gram flour) |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| ½ | tsp | fenugreek seeds |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Simmer kadhi in steel, stirring. Tadka.
