# Aloo Tamatar

Braj temple potato-tomato curry.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 400 | g | potatoes |
| 2 | pieces | ripe tomatoes, chopped |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 15 | g | fresh coriander leaves |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Tadka, tomato, potatoes, simmer. No pyaz, no lehsun.
