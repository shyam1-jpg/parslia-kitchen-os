# Aloo Tamatar

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal sattvic  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Braj temple potato-tomato curry.

## Ingredients

- Potatoes
- Tomato puree
- Cumin
- Hing
- Ginger
- Turmeric
- Coriander
- Chilli
- Ghee
- Salt

## Method

1. Tadka, tomato, potatoes, simmer. No pyaz, no lehsun.
