# Matar Kachori

**Recipe card** · Uttar Pradesh kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal sattvic |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 30 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 50 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Mathura pea kachori, sattvic filling.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | maida (refined flour) |
| 150 | g | green peas, crushed |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| 1 | tsp | amchur (dry mango powder) |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Stuff, fry on medium in steel.
