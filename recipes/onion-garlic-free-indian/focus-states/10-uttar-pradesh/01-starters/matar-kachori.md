# Matar Kachori

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal sattvic  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Mathura pea kachori, sattvic filling.

## Ingredients

- Maida
- Crushed peas
- Ginger
- Cumin
- Amchur
- Chilli
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Stuff, fry on medium in steel.
