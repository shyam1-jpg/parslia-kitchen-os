# Palak Pakora

**Recipe card** · Uttar Pradesh kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal sattvic |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 25 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature starter from Uttar Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | spinach leaves |
| 150 | g | besan (gram flour) |
| ½ | tsp | ajwain (carom seeds) |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Dip leaves, fry in steel.
