# Palak Pakora

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal sattvic  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature starter from Uttar Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Spinach leaves
- Besan
- Ajwain
- Chilli
- Hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Dip leaves, fry in steel.
