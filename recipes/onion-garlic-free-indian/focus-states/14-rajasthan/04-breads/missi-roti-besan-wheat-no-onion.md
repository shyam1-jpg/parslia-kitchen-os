# Missi roti (besan-wheat, no onion)

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Signature bread from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta, 1 cup besan, 1 tsp ajwain, ½ tsp chilli, salt, ghee, water

## Method

1. Knead. Roll. Cook on tawa with ghee.
