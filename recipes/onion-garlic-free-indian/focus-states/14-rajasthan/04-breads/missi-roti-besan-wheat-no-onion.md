# Missi roti (besan-wheat, no onion)

**Recipe card** · Rajasthan kitchen · Bread

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian |
| **Course** | Bread |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium) |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature bread from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | atta |
| 1 | cup | besan |
| 1 | tsp | ajwain |
| ½ | tsp | chilli, salt, ghee, water |

## Method

1. Knead. Roll. Cook on tawa with ghee.
