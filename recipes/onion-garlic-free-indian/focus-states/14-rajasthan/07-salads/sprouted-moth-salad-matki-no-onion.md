# Sprouted moth salad (matki, no onion)

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Signature salad from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sprouted moth beans steamed lightly
- Lemon, chilli, cumin, salt, tomato

## Method

1. Toss warm sprouts with dressing.
