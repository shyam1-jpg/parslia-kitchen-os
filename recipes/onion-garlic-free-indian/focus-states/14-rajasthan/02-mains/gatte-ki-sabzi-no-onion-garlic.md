# Gatte ki sabzi (no onion garlic)

Signature main from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | besan, yogurt, ajwain, hing — for gatte |
| 1 | cup | yogurt gravy |
| 1 | tsp | cumin |
| ½ | tsp | hing |
| 1 | tsp | red chilli |
| 1 | tsp | coriander, turmeric, salt |

## Method

1. Steam gatte as above. Slice.
2. Whisk yogurt with spices. Temper cumin hing. Add yogurt on low, then gatte. Simmer.
