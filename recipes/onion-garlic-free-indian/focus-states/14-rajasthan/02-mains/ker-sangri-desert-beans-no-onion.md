# Ker sangri (desert beans, no onion)

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature main from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup dried ker + 1 cup dried sangri soaked overnight
- 2 tomatoes, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp hing, 1 tsp red chilli, 1 tsp amchur, 1 tsp coriander, salt
- Ghee in kadai

## Method

1. Boil soaked ker-sangri till tender. Drain.
2. Temper spices, tomato. Add beans. Cook dry-ish. Finish amchur.
