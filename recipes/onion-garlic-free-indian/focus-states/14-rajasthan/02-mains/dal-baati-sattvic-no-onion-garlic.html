Dal baati (sattvic, no onion garlic)

VEGETARIAN · NO ONION · NO GARLIC · NO ALLIUM · NO ALUMINIUM

4
Prep
30 min

Cookware: Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

Signature main from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

Ingredients · 4 servings

QtyUnitIngredient
2cupswhole wheat atta
½cupghee, salt, water
1cuptoor + ½ cup chana dal
1tspturmeric
1tspcumin
½tsphing, 2 tomatoes, ginger
2tbspghee

Method

  1. Knead stiff dough. Shape baati. Bake on tandoor/clay or roast in heavy kadhai 35 min, turning.
  2. Cook dals with turmeric. Temper cumin, hing, tomato, ginger in kadai. Mix.
  3. Crack baati, soak in ghee, serve with dal.
