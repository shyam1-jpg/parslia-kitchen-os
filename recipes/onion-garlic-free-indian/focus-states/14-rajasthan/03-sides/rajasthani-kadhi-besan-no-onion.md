# Rajasthani kadhi (besan, no onion)

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup yogurt, 3 tbsp besan, 3 cups water, turmeric, salt
- 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp methi, 4 curry leaves, 2 dry red chillies, ½ tsp hing
- Ghee in kadai

## Method

1. Whisk yogurt-besan. Simmer till thick.
2. Temper whole spices in ghee. Pour over kadhi.
