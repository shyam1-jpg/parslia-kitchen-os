# Rajasthani kadhi (besan, no onion)

**Recipe card** · Rajasthan kitchen · Side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian |
| **Course** | Side |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 25 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature side from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | cup | yogurt |
| 3 | tbsp | besan |
| 3 | cups | water, turmeric, salt |
| 1 | tsp | cumin |
| ½ | tsp | methi, 4 curry leaves, 2 dry red chillies |
| ½ | tsp | hing |

## Method

1. Whisk yogurt-besan. Simmer till thick.
2. Temper whole spices in ghee. Pour over kadhi.
