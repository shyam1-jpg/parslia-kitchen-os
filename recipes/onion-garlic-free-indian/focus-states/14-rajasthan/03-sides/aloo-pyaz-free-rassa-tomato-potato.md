## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 4 | — | potatoes cubed |
| 3 | — | tomatoes |
| 1 | tsp | cumin |
| ½ | tsp | hing |
| 1 | tsp | red chilli, coriander, turmeric, salt |

## Method

1. Temper spices. Tomato. Potatoes. Water. Simmer till potatoes melt into gravy.
