# Aloo pyaz-free rassa (tomato potato)

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 4 potatoes cubed
- 3 tomatoes, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp hing, 1 tsp red chilli, coriander, turmeric, salt
- Ghee in kadai

## Method

1. Temper spices. Tomato. Potatoes. Water. Simmer till potatoes melt into gravy.
