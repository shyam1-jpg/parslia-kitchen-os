# Papad ki sabzi (Rajasthani, no onion)

**Recipe card** · Rajasthan kitchen · Side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian |
| **Course** | Side |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 25 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature side from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 6 | — | urad papads broken |
| 1 | cup | yogurt |
| 1 | tsp | cumin |
| ½ | tsp | hing, turmeric, chilli, salt |

## Method

1. Temper cumin hing. Add yogurt spices on low. Add papad pieces. Simmer 5 min.
