# Papad ki sabzi (Rajasthani, no onion)

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 6 urad papads broken
- 1 cup yogurt, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp hing, turmeric, chilli, salt
- Ghee in kadai

## Method

1. Temper cumin hing. Add yogurt spices on low. Add papad pieces. Simmer 5 min.
