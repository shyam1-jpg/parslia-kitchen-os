Signature dessert from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | maida |
| ½ | cup | milk, pinch fennel, saffron |

## Method

1. Rest batter. Fry small pancakes. Soak in syrup.
