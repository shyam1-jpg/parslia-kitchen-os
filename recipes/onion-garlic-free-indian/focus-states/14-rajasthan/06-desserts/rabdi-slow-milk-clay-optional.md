# Rabdi (slow milk, clay optional)

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 50 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 L milk, ½ cup sugar, saffron, pistachios
- Heavy kadai or clay

## Method

1. Reduce milk stirring. Sugar, saffron, nuts. Chill.
