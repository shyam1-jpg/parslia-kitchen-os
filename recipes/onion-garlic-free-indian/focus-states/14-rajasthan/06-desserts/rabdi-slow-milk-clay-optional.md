# Rabdi (slow milk, clay optional)

**Recipe card** · Rajasthan kitchen · Dessert

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian |
| **Course** | Dessert |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 50 min |
| **Total** | 60 min |
| **Cookware** | Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature dessert from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | L | milk |
| ½ | cup | sugar, saffron, pistachios |

## Method

1. Reduce milk stirring. Sugar, saffron, nuts. Chill.
