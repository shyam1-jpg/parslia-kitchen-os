# Dal kachori (hing, no onion)

**Recipe card** · Rajasthan kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 40 min |
| **Cook** | 25 min |
| **Total** | 65 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature starter from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | yellow moong dal soaked 4h and drained |
| 3 | cups | atta |
| 2 | tsp | cumin |
| 1 | tsp | fennel |
| 1 | tsp | coriander powder |
| ½ | tsp | hing |
| 1 | tsp | red chilli |
| 1 | tsp | amchur, salt |

## Method

1. Grind dal coarse. Temper cumin, fennel, chilli, hing, amchur. Mix.
2. Knead atta with ghee and water. Stuff kachoris. Fry golden in kadai.
3. Serve with imli chutney.
