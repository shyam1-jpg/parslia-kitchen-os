# Gatte ki tikki (steamed gram flour, no onion)

**Recipe card** · Rajasthan kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 25 min |
| **Total** | 45 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature starter from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | besan |
| ½ | cup | yogurt |
| 1 | tsp | ajwain |
| ½ | tsp | hing, salt, ghee |
| 1 | tsp | red chilli |
| 1 | tsp | coriander powder |

## Method

1. Knead besan dough. Roll cylinders. Steam 15 min in steamer. Slice.
2. Pan-sear slices on tawa in ghee. Serve with dahi.
