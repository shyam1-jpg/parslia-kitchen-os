# Mirchi vada (stuffed chilli, no onion)

**Recipe card** · Rajasthan kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 25 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 45 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature starter from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 8 | — | large mild green chillies slit |
| 4 | — | boiled potatoes mashed |
| 1 | tsp | cumin |
| ½ | tsp | hing |
| 1 | tsp | amchur, salt |

## Method

1. Stuff chillies with spiced potato. Dip in besan. Fry in kadai.
2. Serve with imli.
