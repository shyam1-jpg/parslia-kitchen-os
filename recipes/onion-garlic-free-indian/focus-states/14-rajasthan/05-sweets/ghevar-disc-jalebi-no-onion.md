# Ghevar (disc jalebi, no onion)

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 40 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida, ½ cup ghee, ice water, pinch cardamom
- Sugar syrup 2 cups
- Ghee in tall kadai for pouring batter

## Method

1. Make thin batter. Pour in circular motion into hot ghee to form honeycomb disc. Fry.
2. Dip in syrup. Garnish pistachio.
