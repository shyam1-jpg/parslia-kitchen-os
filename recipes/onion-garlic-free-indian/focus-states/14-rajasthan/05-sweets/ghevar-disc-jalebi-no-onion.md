# Ghevar (disc jalebi, no onion)

| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature sweet from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | maida |
| ½ | cup | ghee, ice water, pinch cardamom |
| 250 | ml | sugar syrup (1-string) |

## Method

1. Make thin batter. Pour in circular motion into hot ghee to form honeycomb disc. Fry.
2. Dip in syrup. Garnish pistachio.
