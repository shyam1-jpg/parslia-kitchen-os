## Method

1. Mise en place. Weigh every ingredient on this card for 4 portions. Set Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium. Wash produce. Confirm spice blends have no onion or garlic powder. No onion, no garlic, no aluminium.
2. Cut vegetables to even size so they cook together.
3. Make thin batter. Work in Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
4. Pour in circular motion into hot ghee to form honeycomb disc. Work in Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
5. Fry. Work in Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
6. Dip in syrup. Work in Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
7. Garnish pistachio. Work in Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
8. Doneness: mixture leaves the sides of the pan, or milk is thick enough to coat a spoon, or syrup has taken the stated string.
9. Taste and adjust salt, lemon or chilli only — do not add onion or garlic at the finish. Garnish as listed. Yield is 4 portions.
10. Hold as the dish is eaten — hot sweets in a bain of hot water, chilled sweets in the fridge. Label date and time. Service: Hot.

## Chef notes

For Ghevar (disc jalebi, no onion): keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder.
