# Churma (crushed baati sweet)

**Recipe card** · Rajasthan kitchen · Sweet

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian |
| **Course** | Sweet |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 30 min |
| **Total** | 50 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature sweet from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 6 | — | baatis crushed |
| 1 | cup | ghee |
| 1 | cup | powdered jaggery or sugar |
| 1 | tsp | cardamom, almonds |

## Method

1. Fry crushed baati in ghee in kadai. Mix jaggery off heat. Cardamom, nuts.
