# Moong dal halwa (Marwari)

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari, Mewari and Jain vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Rajasthan, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups yellow moong dal soaked and ground coarse
- 1 cup ghee, 1½ cups sugar, 1 cup milk, cardamom, saffron, almonds
- Heavy kadai

## Method

1. Roast dal paste in ghee till grainy and aromatic (long stir in kadai).
2. Add milk then sugar. Cook till ghee separates. Garnish.
