# Pesarattu

**Recipe card** · Andhra Pradesh kitchen · Bread

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu |
| **Course** | Bread |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |

| **Cookware** | Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium) |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature bread from Andhra Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | whole green moong, soaked |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Grind, spread on iron tawa like a dosa.
