# Pesarattu

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Signature bread from Andhra Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Whole green moong, soaked
- Ginger
- Green chilli
- Cumin
- Salt

## Method

1. Grind, spread on iron tawa like a dosa.
