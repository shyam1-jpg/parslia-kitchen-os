# Bobbatlu

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 40 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Puran poli of Andhra (chana dal-jaggery).

## Ingredients

- Maida or atta dough
- Chana-jaggery-cardamom filling

## Method

1. Stuff, roll, cook on iron with ghee.
