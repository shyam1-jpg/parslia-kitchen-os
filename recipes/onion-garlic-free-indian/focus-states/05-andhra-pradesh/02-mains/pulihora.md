# Pulihora

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Tamarind rice of Andhra temples.

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice
- Tamarind pulp
- Mustard
- Chana dal
- Urad dal
- Peanuts
- Hing
- Curry leaves
- Turmeric
- Dry chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook tamarind with spices until thick. Mix into rice. Temple food — no allium.
