# Pulihora

**Recipe card** · Andhra Pradesh kitchen · Main

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu |
| **Course** | Main |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Tamarind rice of Andhra temples.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 400 | g | cooked rice |
| 20 | g | seedless tamarind |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| 100 | g | chana dal, soaked |
| 50 | g | urad dal |
| 30 | g | roasted peanuts |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 2 | pieces | dry red chillies |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook tamarind with spices until thick. Mix into rice. Temple food — no allium.
