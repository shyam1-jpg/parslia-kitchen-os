# Tomato Pappu

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu |
| **Course** | Main |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 25 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Everyday tomato dal.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | toor dal, rinsed |
| 3 | pieces | ripe tomatoes, chopped |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook dal with tomato. Temper.
