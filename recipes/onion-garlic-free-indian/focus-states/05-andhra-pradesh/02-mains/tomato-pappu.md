# Tomato Pappu

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Everyday tomato dal.

## Ingredients

- Toor dal
- Tomatoes
- Turmeric
- Mustard
- Hing
- Green chilli
- Curry leaves
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook dal with tomato. Temper.
