# Dondakaya Fry

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ivy gourd fry.

## Ingredients

- Dondakaya / tendli
- Chilli powder
- Coriander
- Mustard
- Hing
- Salt

## Method

1. Fry until the edges catch.
