Ivy gourd fry.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | dondakaya / tendli |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 15 | g | fresh coriander leaves |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Fry until the edges catch.
