# Punugulu

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Leftover-batter fritters.

## Ingredients

- Idli batter
- Sooji
- Ginger-chilli
- Hing
- Curry leaves
- Oil

## Method

1. Fry small blobs in steel.
