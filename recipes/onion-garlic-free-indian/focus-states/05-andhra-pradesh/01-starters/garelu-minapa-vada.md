# Garelu (Minapa Vada)

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Andhra urad vada with pepper and ginger, no onion.

## Ingredients

- Urad dal
- Ginger
- Pepper
- Curry leaves
- Hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Grind, shape rings, fry.
