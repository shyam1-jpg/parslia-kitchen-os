Signature dessert from Andhra Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | rava (semolina) |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 100 | g | sugar |
| 1 | pinch | saffron strands |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, crushed |
| 20 | g | cashews |

## Method

1. Roast rava in ghee, add water, sugar, saffron.
