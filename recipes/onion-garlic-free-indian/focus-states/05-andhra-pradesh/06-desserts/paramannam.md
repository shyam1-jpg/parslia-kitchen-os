# Paramannam

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Andhra Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Rice
- Milk
- Jaggery
- Cardamom
- Cashews

## Method

1. Temple rice pudding in steel.
