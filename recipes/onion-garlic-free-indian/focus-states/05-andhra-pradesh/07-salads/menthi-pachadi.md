# Menthi Pachadi

**Recipe card** · Andhra Pradesh kitchen · Salad

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu |
| **Course** | Salad |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 10 min |
| **Total** | 25 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Fenugreek-seed chutney, bitter-tangy.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| ½ | tsp | roasted fenugreek seeds |
| 20 | g | seedless tamarind |
| 1 | tsp | Kashmiri red chilli powder |
| 1 | pinch | jaggery |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Grind, mix. A little goes a long way.
