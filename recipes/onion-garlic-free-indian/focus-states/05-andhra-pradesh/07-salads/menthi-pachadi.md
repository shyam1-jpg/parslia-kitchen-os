# Menthi Pachadi

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Andhra vegetarian / temple pappu  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 10 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fenugreek-seed chutney, bitter-tangy.

## Ingredients

- Fenugreek seeds, roasted
- Tamarind
- Red chilli
- Jaggery pinch
- Salt

## Method

1. Grind, mix. A little goes a long way.
