# Singodi

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Khoa wrapped in maloo leaf — home version in steel cups.

## Ingredients

- Khoya
- Sugar
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Cook, set small cones.
