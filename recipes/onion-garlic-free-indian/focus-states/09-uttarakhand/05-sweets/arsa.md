# Arsa

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Rice-jaggery fried sweet of the hills.

## Ingredients

- Rice flour
- Jaggery syrup
- Sesame
- Oil

## Method

1. Pat discs, fry on medium.
