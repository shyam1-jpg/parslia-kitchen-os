Roasted black-gram (urad) gravy of Garhwal.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 50 | g | urad dal |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 2 | pieces | dry red chillies |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Roast dal, grind, cook with water and spices until thick and smoky.
