# Chainsoo

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Roasted black-gram (urad) gravy of Garhwal.

## Ingredients

- Urad dal, dry roasted and ground
- Cumin
- Hing
- Dry chilli
- Ginger
- Ghee
- Salt

## Method

1. Roast dal, grind, cook with water and spices until thick and smoky.
