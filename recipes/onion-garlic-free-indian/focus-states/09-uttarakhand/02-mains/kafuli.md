# Kafuli

**Recipe card** · Uttarakhand kitchen · Main

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian |
| **Course** | Main |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 25 min |
| **Total** | 40 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Spinach-methi mash thickened with rice paste.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | spinach leaves |
| 50 | g | fresh methi leaves |
| 100 | g | ground rice paste |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook greens, mash, thicken, tadka.
