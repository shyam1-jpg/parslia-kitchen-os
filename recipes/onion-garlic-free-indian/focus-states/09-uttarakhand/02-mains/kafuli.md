# Kafuli

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Spinach-methi mash thickened with rice paste.

## Ingredients

- Spinach
- Methi
- Rice paste
- Ginger
- Green chilli
- Cumin
- Hing
- Ghee
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook greens, mash, thicken, tadka.
