# Phaanu

Mixed-dal stew of Garhwal, slow-cooked, hing-cumin — no onion garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | gahat, bhatt or mixed dals, soaked |
| 100 | g | ground rice paste |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook dals very soft. Rice paste. Long simmer. Ghee tadka.
