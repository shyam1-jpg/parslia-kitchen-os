# Phaanu

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Mixed-dal stew of Garhwal, slow-cooked, hing-cumin — no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- Gahat, bhatt or mixed dals, soaked
- Rice paste
- Cumin
- Hing
- Ginger
- Chilli
- Ghee
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook dals very soft. Rice paste. Long simmer. Ghee tadka.
