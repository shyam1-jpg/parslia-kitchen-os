# Aloo ke Gutke

**Recipe card** · Uttarakhand kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 30 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Kumaoni potatoes with jakhiya. Already no onion garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 400 | g | boiled potatoes |
| 2 | tbsp | mustard oil |
| 1 | tsp | jakhiya or mustard seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 1 | tsp | Kashmiri red chilli powder |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | coriander powder |
| 1 | tbsp | lemon juice |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Crisp potatoes in mustard oil with jakhiya.
