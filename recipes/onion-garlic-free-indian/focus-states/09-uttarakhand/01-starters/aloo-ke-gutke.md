# Aloo ke Gutke

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Kumaoni potatoes with jakhiya. Already no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- Boiled potatoes
- Mustard oil
- Jakhiya or mustard
- Hing
- Red chilli
- Turmeric
- Coriander powder
- Lemon
- Salt

## Method

1. Crisp potatoes in mustard oil with jakhiya.
