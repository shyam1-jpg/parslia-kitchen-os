# Garhwali Kapa Bites

**Recipe card** · Uttarakhand kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 30 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Thick yogurt-besan gravy cubes / dunked bread — serve as a starter dip with roti pieces.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | plain yogurt |
| 150 | g | besan (gram flour) |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook a thick kadhi in steel. Serve with toasted roti strips.
