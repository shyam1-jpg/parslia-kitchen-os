# Garhwali Kapa Bites

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Thick yogurt-besan gravy cubes / dunked bread — serve as a starter dip with roti pieces.

## Ingredients

- Yogurt
- Besan
- Turmeric
- Cumin
- Hing
- Ginger
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook a thick kadhi in steel. Serve with toasted roti strips.
