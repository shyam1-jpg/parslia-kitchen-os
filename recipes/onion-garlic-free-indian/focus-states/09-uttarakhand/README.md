# Uttarakhand

**Kitchen:** Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian  
**Diet:** vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no aluminium cookware

Kafuli, phaanu, chainsoo, mandua roti, bal mithai. Jakhiya and mustard oil.
