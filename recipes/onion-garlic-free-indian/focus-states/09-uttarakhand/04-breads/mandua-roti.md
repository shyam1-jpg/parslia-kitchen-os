# Mandua Roti

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Signature bread from Uttarakhand, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Mandua/ragi flour
- Hot water
- Salt

## Method

1. Pat, cook on iron tawa.
