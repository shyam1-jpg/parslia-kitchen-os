# Mandua Roti

**Recipe card** · Uttarakhand kitchen · Bread

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian |
| **Course** | Bread |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 30 min |
| **Cookware** | Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium) |

Signature bread from Uttarakhand, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | mandua / ragi flour |
| 250 | ml | hot water |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Pat, cook on iron tawa.
