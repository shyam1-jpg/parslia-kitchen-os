# Bari Roti

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Thicker pahadi roti to scoop kafuli.

## Ingredients

- Atta
- Water
- Salt
- Ghee

## Method

1. Roll slightly thick, cook on iron.
