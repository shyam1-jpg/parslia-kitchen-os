# Mochar Chop (niramish)

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali niramish (vegetarian, no onion garlic)  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Banana-flower croquettes. Skip onion; use coconut, ginger, peanuts.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked banana flower
- 1 potato
- Ginger
- Coconut
- Peanuts
- Garam masala
- Besan batter
- Oil

## Method

1. Mash filling, shape, crumb in batter, fry in steel.
