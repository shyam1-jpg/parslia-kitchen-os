# Mochar Chop (niramish)

**Recipe card** · West Bengal kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | West Bengal — Bengali niramish (vegetarian, no onion garlic) |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 25 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 45 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Banana-flower croquettes. Skip onion; use coconut, ginger, peanuts.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | cooked banana flower |
| 1 | — | potato |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 100 | g | fresh grated coconut |
| 30 | g | roasted peanuts |
| ½ | tsp | garam masala (no onion-garlic powder) |
| 150 | g | besan (gram flour) |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Mash filling, shape, crumb in batter, fry in steel.
