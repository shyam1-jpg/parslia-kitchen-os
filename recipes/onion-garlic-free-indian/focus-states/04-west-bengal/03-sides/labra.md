Festival mixed vegetable of Durga Puja bhog — onion-garlic free.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | pumpkin, cubed |
| 1 | tsp | panch phoron |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 100 | g | sugar |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |

## Method

1. Temper, add veg, cook until they melt together. Bhog style.
