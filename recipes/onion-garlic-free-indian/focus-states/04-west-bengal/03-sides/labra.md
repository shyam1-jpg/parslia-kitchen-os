# Labra

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali niramish (vegetarian, no onion garlic)  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Festival mixed vegetable of Durga Puja bhog — onion-garlic free.

## Ingredients

- Pumpkin, beans, eggplant, cabbage, potato, radish
- Panch phoron
- Ginger
- Turmeric
- Salt
- Sugar
- Ghee

## Method

1. Temper, add veg, cook until they melt together. Bhog style.
