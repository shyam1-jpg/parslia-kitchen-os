# Aloo Posto

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali niramish (vegetarian, no onion garlic)  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Potato in poppy paste — niramish emblem.

## Ingredients

- Potatoes
- Posto paste
- Green chilli
- Kalonji
- Mustard oil
- Turmeric
- Salt
- Sugar pinch

## Method

1. Cook potatoes, add posto. Oil should shine. No peyaj, no rasun.
