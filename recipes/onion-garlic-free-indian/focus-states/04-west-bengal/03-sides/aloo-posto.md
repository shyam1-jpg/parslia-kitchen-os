# Aloo Posto

**Recipe card** · West Bengal kitchen · Side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | West Bengal — Bengali niramish (vegetarian, no onion garlic) |
| **Course** | Side |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 30 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Potato in poppy paste — niramish emblem.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 400 | g | potatoes |
| 4 | tbsp | posto (poppy) paste |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| ½ | tsp | kalonji (nigella) |
| 2 | tbsp | mustard oil |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 1 | pinch | sugar |

## Method

1. Cook potatoes, add posto. Oil should shine. No peyaj, no rasun.
