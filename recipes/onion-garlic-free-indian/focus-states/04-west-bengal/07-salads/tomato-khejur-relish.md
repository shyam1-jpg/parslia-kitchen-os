# Tomato Khejur Relish

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali niramish (vegetarian, no onion garlic)  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Tomato-date chutney of Bengali thalis.

## Ingredients

- Tomato
- Dates
- Panch phoron
- Panch phoron
- Chilli
- Sugar
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook to a jammy relish.
