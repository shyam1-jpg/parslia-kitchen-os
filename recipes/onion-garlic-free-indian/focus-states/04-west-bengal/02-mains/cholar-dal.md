# Cholar Dal

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali niramish (vegetarian, no onion garlic)  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Chana dal with coconut and raisins. Niramish.

## Ingredients

- Chana dal
- Coconut pieces
- Raisins
- Bay, cardamom, cinnamon
- Ginger
- Ghee
- Sugar pinch
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook dal. Temper whole spices, coconut, raisins. Slightly sweet.
