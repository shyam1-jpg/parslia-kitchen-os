# Dhokar Dalna

**Recipe card** · West Bengal kitchen · Main

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | West Bengal — Bengali niramish (vegetarian, no onion garlic) |
| **Course** | Main |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 25 min |
| **Cook** | 30 min |
| **Total** | 55 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Chana-dal cakes in ginger-tomato gravy. Classic niramish.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 100 | g | chana dal, soaked |
| 2 | pieces | ripe tomatoes, chopped |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| 2 | leaves | bay leaves |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| ½ | tsp | garam masala (no onion-garlic powder) |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 1 | pinch | sugar |

## Method

1. Make a tomato-ginger gravy in steel. Slide in dhoka pieces. Simmer 8 minutes.
