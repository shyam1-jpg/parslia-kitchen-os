# Dhokar Dalna

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali niramish (vegetarian, no onion garlic)  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Chana-dal cakes in ginger-tomato gravy. Classic niramish.

## Ingredients

- Chana dal cakes, steamed and fried
- Tomato
- Ginger
- Cumin
- Bay
- Ghee
- Garam masala
- Salt
- Sugar pinch

## Method

1. Make a tomato-ginger gravy in steel. Slide in dhoka pieces. Simmer 8 minutes.
