# Shukto

Bitter-sweet mixed veg in milk-poppy gravy — the niramish opener-main.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 600 | g | bitter gourd, potato, raw banana, drumstick, beans and eggplant |
| 4 | tbsp | poppy-mustard paste |
| 1 | L | full-fat milk |
| 1 | tsp | panch phoron |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 100 | g | sugar |

## Method

1. Fry bitter gourd first. Cook all veg with paste and milk until creamy. No onion, no garlic.
