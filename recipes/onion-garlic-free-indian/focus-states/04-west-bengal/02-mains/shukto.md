# Shukto

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali niramish (vegetarian, no onion garlic)  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Bitter-sweet mixed veg in milk-poppy gravy — the niramish opener-main.

## Ingredients

- Bitter gourd, potato, raw banana, drumstick, beans, eggplant
- Poppy-mustard paste
- Milk
- Panch phoron
- Ghee
- Ginger
- Salt
- Sugar

## Method

1. Fry bitter gourd first. Cook all veg with paste and milk until creamy. No onion, no garlic.
