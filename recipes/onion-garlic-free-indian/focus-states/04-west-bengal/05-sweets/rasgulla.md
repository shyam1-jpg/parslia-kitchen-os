Signature sweet from West Bengal, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | fresh chhena |
| 250 | ml | sugar syrup (1-string) |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, crushed |

## Method

1. Boil balls in syrup in a wide steel pot until they sponge.
