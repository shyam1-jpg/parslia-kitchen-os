# Idli sambar plate (South + national, no onion garlic sambar)

|---|---|

| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |

| **Cookware** | Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium |

Signature starter from Pan India, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 500 | g | idli batter, for steaming |
| 150 | g | toor dal, plus pumpkin, drumstick, tomato, tamarind, sambar powder (no onion-garlic) |

## Method

1. Steam idli. Cook sambar in kadai. Coconut chutney ginger-chilli.
