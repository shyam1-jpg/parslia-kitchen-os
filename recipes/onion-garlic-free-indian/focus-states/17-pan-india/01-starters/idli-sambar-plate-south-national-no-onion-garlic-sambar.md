# Idli sambar plate (South + national, no onion garlic sambar)

**Continent:** Pan-India — Dishes cooked in homes all over India  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature starter from Pan India, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Idli batter steamed
- Sambar: toor, pumpkin, drumstick, tomato, tamarind, sambar powder without onion garlic, mustard hing curry leaf

## Method

1. Steam idli. Cook sambar in kadai. Coconut chutney ginger-chilli.
