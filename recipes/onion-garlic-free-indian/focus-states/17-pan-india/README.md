# Pan-India

**Kitchen:** Dishes cooked in homes all over India  
**Diet:** vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no aluminium cookware

The nationwide vegetarian table: dal, paneer, chole, roti, jeera rice, gulab jamun, kheer — all without onion or garlic.
