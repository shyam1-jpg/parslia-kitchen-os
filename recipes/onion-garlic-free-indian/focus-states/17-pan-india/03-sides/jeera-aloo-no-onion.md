## Method

1. Mise en place. Boil 4 potatoes until just tender. Cool slightly. Cube. Steel kadhai — never aluminium.
2. Heat ghee. Bloom cumin and hing until the cumin darkens a shade, 20–30 seconds. Add chilli.
3. Toss potatoes with amchur, coriander and salt 3–4 minutes until edges take colour.
4. Doneness: potatoes hot, cumin fragrant, dry. Serve immediately.
5. Hold 15 minutes covered. Do not add water.

## Chef notes

For Jeera aloo (no onion): keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder.

## Service notes

Serve family-style in a steel bowl. Keep a lid on at the pass so it does not skin. Service temperature: Hot.
