## Method

1. Mise en place. Wipe 500 g okra dry — water makes slime. Cut 3 cm pieces. Steel kadhai — never aluminium.
2. Fry okra in oil on medium-high 6–8 minutes until the slime cooks off and edges colour. Lift out.
3. In the same pan bloom cumin and hing. Cook tomato, chilli, coriander, amchur and salt 4 minutes. Return okra. Toss 3 minutes.
4. Doneness: okra tender not mushy, masala dry on the pieces. Serve hot.
5. Hold uncovered a few minutes so steam does not return the slime.

## Chef notes

For Bhindi masala (no onion garlic): keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder.

## Service notes

Serve family-style in a steel bowl. Keep a lid on at the pass so it does not skin. Service temperature: Hot.
