## Method

1. Mise en place. Even florets and potato cubes. Steel kadhai — never aluminium.
2. Bloom cumin and hing in ghee. Add vegetables, turmeric, chilli, coriander and salt. Cover-cook 12–15 minutes, stirring.
3. Uncover and dry the masala 2–3 minutes so it clings.
4. Doneness: tender with dry spice coat, not mush. Serve hot as a side.
5. Hold covered 20 minutes.

## Chef notes

For Aloo gobi (national dry, no onion): keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder.

## Service notes

Serve family-style in a steel bowl. Keep a lid on at the pass so it does not skin. Service temperature: Hot.
