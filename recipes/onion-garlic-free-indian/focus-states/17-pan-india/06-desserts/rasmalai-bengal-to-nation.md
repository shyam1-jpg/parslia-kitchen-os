## Method

1. Mise en place. Fresh chhena discs, reduced milk (rabri) with saffron and pistachio. Steel pots — never aluminium.
2. Poach chhena discs in light sugar syrup 8–10 minutes until they swell. Squeeze gently.
3. Soak in warm rabri at least 1 hour, chilled after that.
4. Doneness: discs spongy, milk thick enough to nap, saffron aroma. Serve chilled, 2 discs per portion.
5. Hold refrigerated. Label milk and tree nuts.

## Chef notes

For Rasmalai (Bengal-to-nation): keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder.
