## Method

1. Mise en place. Soak and cook 2 cups chickpeas until soft. Steel kadhai — never aluminium. Check masala packets for onion or garlic powder.
2. Heat ghee. Bloom cumin and hing. Cook tomato with coriander, chilli, amchur, garam masala and salt until fat separates.
3. Add chickpeas and a ladle of cooking liquor. Simmer 12–15 minutes, crushing a few chana for body.
4. Doneness: gravy thick, chickpeas creamy. Serve hot with roti or rice.
5. Hold hot. Label legumes.

## Chef notes

For Chana masala (no onion garlic): keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder.
