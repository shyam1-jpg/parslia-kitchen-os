# Jeera rice (national, no onion)

**Continent:** Pan-India — Dishes cooked in homes all over India  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature bread from Pan India, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups basmati soaked, 1 tsp cumin, 2 bay, 4 cloves, ghee, salt, water
- Kadai

## Method

1. Temper whole spices. Rice. Water. Simmer.
