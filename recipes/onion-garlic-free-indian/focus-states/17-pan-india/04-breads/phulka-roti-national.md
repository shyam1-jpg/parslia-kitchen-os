## Method

1. Mise en place. Mix atta and water to a soft dough. Rest 10–15 minutes. Cast-iron tawa — not aluminium.
2. Divide. Roll 2 mm thick, even, round. Dust lightly.
3. Cook first side 20 seconds until bubbles. Flip. Cook until brown spots. Flip onto the flame or press to puff.
4. Optional ghee. Stack in a cloth.
5. Doneness: puffed or fully cooked, no raw patches. Serve hot.
6. Hold wrapped 20 minutes. Refresh on the tawa.

## Chef notes

For Phulka / roti (national): keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder.
