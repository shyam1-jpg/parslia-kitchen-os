# Phulka / roti (national)

**Continent:** Pan-India — Dishes cooked in homes all over India  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Signature bread from Pan India, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Atta, water, ghee

## Method

1. Roll. Tawa. Puff. Every Indian table.
