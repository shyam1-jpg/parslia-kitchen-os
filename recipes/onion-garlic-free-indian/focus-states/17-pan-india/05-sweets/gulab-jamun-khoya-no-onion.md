# Gulab jamun (khoya, no onion)

**Continent:** Pan-India — Dishes cooked in homes all over India  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Pan India, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Khoya + maida balls, sugar syrup rose cardamom, ghee in kadai

## Method

1. Fry on low. Soak.
