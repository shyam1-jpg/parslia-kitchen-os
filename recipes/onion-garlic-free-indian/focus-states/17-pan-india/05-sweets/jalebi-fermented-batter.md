## Method

1. Mise en place. Fermented maida batter in a piping cloth. One-string saffron sugar syrup, warm. Steel kadhai of ghee — never aluminium.
2. Pipe tight coils into 170 C fat. Fry until crisp and orange-gold, 1–2 minutes a side.
3. Dip in syrup 20–30 seconds. Lift. Drain on a steel rack.
4. Doneness: crisp, syrup in the tunnels, not soggy. Serve hot or at room temperature within 2 hours.
5. Label gluten. Do not hold in a closed box or they sweat.

## Chef notes

For Jalebi (fermented batter): keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder.
