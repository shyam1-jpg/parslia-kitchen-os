## Method

1. Mise en place. Whisk dahi smooth. Small dice cucumber and boiled potato. No onion.
2. Fold vegetables with cumin, salt, chilli powder and coriander. Chill 15 minutes. Stir.
3. Doneness: thick, seasoned, vegetables distinct. Serve cold.
4. Hold 2 hours chilled. Stir at the pass.
5. Label milk. Keep onion off this bowl even if the rest of the table has kachumber with onion.
