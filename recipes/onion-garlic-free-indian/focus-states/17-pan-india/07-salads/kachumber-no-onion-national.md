| 15 | g | — | fresh coriander leaves |
| 1 | tsp | ≈ 6 g | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Mise en place. Dice cucumber, tomato and chilli even. Steel or glass bowl. No onion.
2. Toss with lemon, cumin, coriander and salt just before service.
3. Doneness: crisp, bright, not watery. Taste salt and lemon.
4. Serve cold as a side salad.
5. Hold 20 minutes. Drain if it weeps. Do not add onion at the pass.
