# Rasiyaw

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min

Bihari rice pudding with jaggery.

- Rice
- Milk
