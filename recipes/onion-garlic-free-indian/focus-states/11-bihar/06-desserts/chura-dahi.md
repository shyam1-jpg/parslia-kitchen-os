# Chura Dahi

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Flattened rice in yogurt with jaggery — Bihar breakfast-dessert.

## Ingredients

- Chura / poha
- Yogurt
- Jaggery
- Banana optional

## Method

1. Soak chura, mix yogurt and jaggery.
