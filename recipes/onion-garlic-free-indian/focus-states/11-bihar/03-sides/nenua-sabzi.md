# Nenua Sabzi

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sponge gourd.

## Ingredients

- Nenua / turai
- Cumin
- Hing
- Turmeric
- Tomato
- Salt

## Method

1. Simmer until soft.
