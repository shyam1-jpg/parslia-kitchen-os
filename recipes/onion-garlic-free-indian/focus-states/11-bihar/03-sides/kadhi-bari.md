# Kadhi Bari

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Bihar, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Besan baris
- Yogurt kadhi
- Cumin
- Hing
- Turmeric
- Salt

## Method

1. Simmer baris in kadhi. Tadka.
