# Dal Pithaur

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Wheat dumplings simmered in dal.

## Ingredients

- Atta dumplings
- Toor or masoor dal
- Turmeric
- Cumin
- Hing
- Ghee
- Salt

## Method

1. Simmer pithaur in dal until cooked. Tadka.
