# Dal Pithaur

**Recipe card** · Bihar kitchen · Main

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Bihar — Magadh vegetarian |
| **Course** | Main |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 30 min |
| **Total** | 50 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Wheat dumplings simmered in dal.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | whole-wheat atta |
| 150 | g | toor or masoor dal |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Simmer pithaur in dal until cooked. Tadka.
