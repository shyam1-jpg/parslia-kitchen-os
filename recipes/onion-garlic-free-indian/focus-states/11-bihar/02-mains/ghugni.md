# Ghugni

**Recipe card** · Bihar kitchen · Main

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Bihar — Magadh vegetarian |
| **Course** | Main |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |

| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

White-peas curry of Bihar. Tomato, ginger, cumin — no onion.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | white peas, soaked overnight |
| 2 | pieces | ripe tomatoes, chopped |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 1 | tsp | amchur (dry mango powder) |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook peas soft. Tomato-ginger gravy. Simmer.
