# Ghugni

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

White-peas curry of Bihar. Tomato, ginger, cumin — no onion.

## Ingredients

- White peas, soaked
- Tomato
- Ginger
- Cumin
- Hing
- Turmeric
- Chilli
- Amchur
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook peas soft. Tomato-ginger gravy. Simmer.
