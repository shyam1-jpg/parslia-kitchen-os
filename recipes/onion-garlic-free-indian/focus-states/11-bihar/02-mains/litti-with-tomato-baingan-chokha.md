# Litti with Tomato Baingan Chokha

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron grill or steel oven tray — never aluminium foil

## Why this dish

Fire mash without onion; mustard oil and chilli.

## Ingredients

- Brinjal
- Tomato
- Potato
- Mustard oil
- Green chilli
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Roast, peel, mash. Serve with litti.
