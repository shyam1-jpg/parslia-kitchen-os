# Litti with Tomato Baingan Chokha

**Recipe card** · Bihar kitchen · Main

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Bihar — Magadh vegetarian |
| **Course** | Main |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Cast-iron grill or steel oven tray — never aluminium foil |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Fire mash without onion; mustard oil and chilli.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 400 | g | brinjal, cubed |
| 2 | pieces | ripe tomatoes, chopped |
| 300 | g | potatoes |
| 2 | tbsp | mustard oil |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 15 | g | fresh coriander leaves |

## Method

1. Roast, peel, mash. Serve with litti.
