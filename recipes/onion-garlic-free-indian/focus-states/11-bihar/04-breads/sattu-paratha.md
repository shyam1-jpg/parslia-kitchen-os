# Sattu Paratha

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Signature bread from Bihar, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Atta
- Sattu filling
- Ghee

## Method

1. Stuff, cook on iron tawa.
