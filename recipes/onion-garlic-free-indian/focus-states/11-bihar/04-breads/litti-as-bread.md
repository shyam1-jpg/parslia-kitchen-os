# Litti as Bread

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron grill or steel oven tray — never aluminium foil

## Why this dish

Serve whole litti as the bread of the meal.

## Ingredients

- Atta
- Sattu filling

## Method

1. Roast, crush slightly, pour ghee.
