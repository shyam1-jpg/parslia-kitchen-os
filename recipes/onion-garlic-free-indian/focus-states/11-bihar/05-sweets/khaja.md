# Khaja

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Flaky layered fried sweet.

## Ingredients

- Maida
- Ghee
- Sugar syrup

## Method

1. Layer, fry, dip in syrup.
