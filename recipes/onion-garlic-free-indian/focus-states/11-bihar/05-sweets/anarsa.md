Rice-jaggery sweet with poppy crust.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 100 | g | ground rice paste |
| 100 | g | jaggery, grated |
| 2 | tbsp | white poppy seeds |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |

## Method

1. Shape, dip in poppy, fry on low.
