# Aloo Chop (no onion)

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature starter from Bihar, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Potatoes
- Ginger-chilli
- Bhaja masala
- Besan batter
- Oil

## Method

1. Shape, fry in steel.
