# Sattu Cooler Chaat

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Sattu drink thickened and topped like chaat, minus onion.

## Ingredients

- Sattu
- Water
- Lemon
- Roasted cumin
- Green chilli
- Black salt
- Mint

## Method

1. Whisk a thick cooler. Top with chilli and cumin.
