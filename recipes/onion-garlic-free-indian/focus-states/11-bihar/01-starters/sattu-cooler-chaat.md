Sattu drink thickened and topped like chaat, minus onion.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | sattu (roasted gram flour) |
| 500 | ml | water |
| 1 | tbsp | lemon juice |
| 1 | tsp | roasted cumin powder |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| ½ | tsp | black salt |
| 10 | g | fresh mint leaves |

## Method

1. Whisk a thick cooler. Top with chilli and cumin.
