# Baked Sattu Litti

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron grill or steel oven tray — never aluminium foil

## Why this dish

Sattu-stuffed balls, no onion in the filling.

## Ingredients

- Atta
- Sattu
- Ginger-chilli
- Ajwain
- Mustard oil
- Amchur
- Salt

## Method

1. Stuff, roast on steel tray until charred spots. Dip in ghee.
