# Jaggery Rice

**Continent:** Meghalaya — Khasi and Garo vegetarian home food  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Meghalaya, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice
- Jaggery
- Sesame

## Method

1. Fold warm jaggery into rice.
