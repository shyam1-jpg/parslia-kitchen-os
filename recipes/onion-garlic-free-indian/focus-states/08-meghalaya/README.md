# Meghalaya

**Kitchen:** Khasi and Garo vegetarian home food  
**Diet:** vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no aluminium cookware

Rice cakes, sesame greens, pumpkin stew. No jadoh meat — fully vegetarian.
