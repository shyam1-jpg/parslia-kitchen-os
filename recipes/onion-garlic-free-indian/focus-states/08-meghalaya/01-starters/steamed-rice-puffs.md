# Steamed Rice Puffs

**Continent:** Meghalaya — Khasi and Garo vegetarian home food  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature starter from Meghalaya, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Rice flour
- Salt
- Water

## Method

1. Shape small cakes, steam.
