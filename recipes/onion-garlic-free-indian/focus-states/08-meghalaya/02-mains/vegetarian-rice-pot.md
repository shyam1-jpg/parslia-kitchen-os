# Vegetarian Rice Pot

**Continent:** Meghalaya — Khasi and Garo vegetarian home food  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Khasi-style veg rice with ginger and greens (jadoh without meat).

## Ingredients

- Rice
- Greens
- Tomato
- Ginger
- Mustard oil
- Green chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Temper ginger, chilli, tomato. Add rice, greens, water. Cook like a wet pulao.
