Khasi-style veg rice with ginger and greens (jadoh without meat).

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | raw rice |
| 300 | g | seasonal greens, washed |
| 2 | pieces | ripe tomatoes, chopped |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | tbsp | mustard oil |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Temper ginger, chilli, tomato. Add rice, greens, water. Cook like a wet pulao.
