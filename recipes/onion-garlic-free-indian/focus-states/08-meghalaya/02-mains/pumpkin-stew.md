# Pumpkin Stew

**Continent:** Meghalaya — Khasi and Garo vegetarian home food  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature main from Meghalaya, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Pumpkin
- Ginger
- Chilli
- Salt
- Sesame optional

## Method

1. Simmer until the pumpkin collapses into a stew.
