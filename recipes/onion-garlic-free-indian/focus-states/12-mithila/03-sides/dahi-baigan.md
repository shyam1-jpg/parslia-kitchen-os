# Dahi Baigan

**Continent:** Mithila — Maithil vegetarian (Bihar–Nepal border culture)  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Yogurt-eggplant of Maithil homes.

## Ingredients

- Brinjal, fried or roasted
- Yogurt
- Cumin
- Hing
- Green chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Fold roasted baigan into tempered yogurt.
