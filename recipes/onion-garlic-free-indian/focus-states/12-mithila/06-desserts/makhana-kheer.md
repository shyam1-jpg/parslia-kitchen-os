# Makhana Kheer

**Continent:** Mithila — Maithil vegetarian (Bihar–Nepal border culture)  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

The dessert Mithila is famous for.

## Ingredients

- Makhana, roasted and crushed
- Milk
- Sugar
- Cardamom
- Saffron
- Almonds

## Method

1. Simmer in steel until creamy.
