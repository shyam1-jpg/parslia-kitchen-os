Maithil sweet-savoury banana-wheat fritters for festivals.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | whole-wheat atta |
| 2 | pieces | ripe bananas |
| 1 | tsp | fennel seeds |
| 1 | pinch | sugar |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |

## Method

1. Drop batter in ghee. Fry until brown.
