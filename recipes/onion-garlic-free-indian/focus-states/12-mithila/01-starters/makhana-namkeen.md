# Makhana Namkeen

**Continent:** Mithila — Maithil vegetarian (Bihar–Nepal border culture)  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 10 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Roasted fox nuts — Mithila’s signature snack.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups makhana
- Ghee
- Hing
- Turmeric
- Chilli
- Black salt

## Method

1. Roast in ghee in steel until crisp. Spice.
