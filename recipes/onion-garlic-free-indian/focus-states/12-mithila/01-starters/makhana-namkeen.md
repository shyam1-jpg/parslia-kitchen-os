Roasted fox nuts — Mithila’s signature snack.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 4 | cups | makhana |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| ½ | tsp | black salt |

## Method

1. Roast in ghee in steel until crisp. Spice.
