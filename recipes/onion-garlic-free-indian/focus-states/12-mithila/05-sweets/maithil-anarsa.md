# Maithil Anarsa

**Continent:** Mithila — Maithil vegetarian (Bihar–Nepal border culture)  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 40 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Mithila, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Rice
- Jaggery
- Poppy
- Ghee

## Method

1. Shape, fry on low.
