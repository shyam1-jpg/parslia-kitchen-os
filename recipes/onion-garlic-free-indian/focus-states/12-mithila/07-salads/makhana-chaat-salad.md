# Makhana Chaat Salad

**Continent:** Mithila — Maithil vegetarian (Bihar–Nepal border culture)  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Signature salad from Mithila, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Roasted makhana
- Tomato
- Cucumber
- Lemon
- Chaat masala
- Coriander

## Method

1. Toss just before eating. No onion.
