## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 80 | g | roasted makhana |
| 2 | pieces | ripe tomatoes, chopped |
| 2 | pieces | cucumbers |
| 1 | tbsp | lemon juice |
| ½ | tsp | chaat masala (check no onion-garlic) |
| 15 | g | fresh coriander leaves |

## Method

1. Toss just before eating. No onion.
