# Maithil Ghugni

**Continent:** Mithila — Maithil vegetarian (Bihar–Nepal border culture)  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

White peas with ginger and cumin.

## Ingredients

- White peas
- Tomato
- Ginger
- Cumin
- Hing
- Amchur
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook peas, make a thick gravy.
