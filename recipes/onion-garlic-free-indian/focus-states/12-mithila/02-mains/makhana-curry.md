# Makhana Curry

**Continent:** Mithila — Maithil vegetarian (Bihar–Nepal border culture)  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fox-nut curry in tomato-yogurt. Maithil vegetarian royal dish.

## Ingredients

- Makhana, lightly fried
- Tomato
- Yogurt
- Ginger
- Cumin
- Hing
- Garam masala
- Ghee
- Salt

## Method

1. Make tomato-yogurt gravy in steel. Add makhana last so they stay slightly crisp.
