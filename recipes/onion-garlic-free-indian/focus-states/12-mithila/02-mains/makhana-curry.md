# Makhana Curry

|---|---|

| **Course** | Main |
| **Serves** | 4 |

Fox-nut curry in tomato-yogurt. Maithil vegetarian royal dish.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 80 | g | makhana, lightly fried |
| 2 | pieces | ripe tomatoes, chopped |
| 200 | g | plain yogurt |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| ½ | tsp | garam masala (no onion-garlic powder) |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Make tomato-yogurt gravy in steel. Add makhana last so they stay slightly crisp.
