# Maithil Kadhi

Yogurt kadhi with bari, Mithila-style.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | plain yogurt |
| 150 | g | besan (gram flour) |
| 12 | pieces | bari or besan pakora |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| ½ | tsp | fenugreek seeds |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Simmer, add bari, tadka. No onion.
