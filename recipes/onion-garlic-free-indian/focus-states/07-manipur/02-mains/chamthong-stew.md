# Chamthong Stew

| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Clear Manipuri vegetable stew — the home pot. No nga-ri, no onion, no garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | cabbage, large cubes |
| 150 | g | potato, large cubes |
| 100 | g | carrot, chunks |
| 100 | g | french beans, cut 4 cm |
| 80 | g | pumpkin, cubes |
| 25 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | pieces | green chilli, slit |
| 15 | g | fresh coriander leaves |
| 1 | tsp | salt |
| 1 | L | water |

## Method

1. Put water, ginger and chilli in a stainless steel pot and bring to a rolling boil.
2. Add potato and pumpkin first; cook 8 minutes. Add cabbage, carrot and beans.
3. Season with salt. Simmer until all vegetables are tender but hold their shape, about 12 minutes more.
4. Finish with coriander. Serve in steel bowls with sticky rice. Never aluminium.
