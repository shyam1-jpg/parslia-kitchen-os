# Chamthong Stew

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Clear Manipuri vegetable stew.

## Ingredients

- Mixed veg
- Ginger
- Green chilli
- Salt
- Local herb or coriander

## Method

1. Simmer until tender. Light, clean broth.
