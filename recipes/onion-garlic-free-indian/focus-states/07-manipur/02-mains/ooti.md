Creamy yellow-pea stew of Manipur. A pinch of soda is traditional; skip onion and garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | yellow peas (vatana), soaked 6 hours |
| 800 | ml | water |
| 25 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | pieces | green chilli, slit |
| ¼ | tsp | baking soda |
| 1 | tsp | salt |
| 2 | tbsp | cooked rice, to thicken (optional) |
| 1 | tbsp | mustard oil |

## Method

1. Drain the peas. Cook with water, ginger, chilli and soda in a clay or steel pot until very soft, 30 minutes.
2. Mash a ladle of peas against the pot. Stir in optional rice. Season with salt.
3. Finish with mustard oil. The stew should be creamy, not watery. Serve with sticky rice.
