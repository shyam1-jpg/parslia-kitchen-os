# Ooti

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Yellow pea stew of Manipur. Ginger and baking soda pinch, no allium.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup yellow peas, soaked
- Ginger
- Green chilli
- Salt
- Optional rice to thicken

## Method

1. Cook peas very soft. Ginger, chilli, salt. Should be creamy.
