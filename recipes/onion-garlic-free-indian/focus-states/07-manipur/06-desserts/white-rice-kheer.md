# White Rice Kheer

|---|---|

| **Course** | Dessert |
| **Serves** | 4 |

| **Cookware** | Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Everyday rice kheer for the Manipuri vegetarian table.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 80 | g | raw rice, rinsed |
| 1 | L | full-fat milk |
| 80 | g | sugar |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, crushed |
| 15 | g | raisins |

## Method

1. Simmer rice in milk in a steel pot until the grains burst and the milk is creamy, 30 minutes.
2. Add sugar, cardamom and raisins. Cook 5 minutes. Serve warm.
