# Chak-Hao Kheer

|---|---|

| **Serves** | 4 |

| **Cookware** | Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Black-rice pudding — the colour turns lilac-grey. Slow milk, steel pot only.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 80 | g | chak-hao (Manipur black rice), soaked 2 hours |
| 1 | L | full-fat milk |
| 80 | g | sugar |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, crushed |
| 10 | g | pistachios, slivered |

## Method

1. Drain rice. Simmer in milk in a heavy steel pot, stirring often, 35–40 minutes until thick.
2. Add sugar and cardamom. Cook 5 minutes more.
3. Serve warm or chilled, topped with pistachio. Never aluminium — milk reacts.
