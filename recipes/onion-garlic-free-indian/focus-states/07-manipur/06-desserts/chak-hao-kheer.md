# Chak-Hao Kheer

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Black-rice kheer.

## Ingredients

- Chak-hao
- Milk
- Sugar
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Simmer in steel until lilac-grey and thick.
