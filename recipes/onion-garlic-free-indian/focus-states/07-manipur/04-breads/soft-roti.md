# Soft Roti

**Recipe card** · Manipur kitchen · Bread

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table |
| **Course** | Bread |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 30 min |
| **Cookware** | Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium) |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Plain wheat roti for guests who want a North-Indian bread with the stew.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | whole-wheat atta |
| 180 | ml | water, or as needed |
| ½ | tsp | salt |
| 1 | tsp | ghee, for finishing |

## Method

1. Knead atta, salt and water 5 minutes. Rest 10 minutes.
2. Divide into 8 balls. Roll 15 cm discs. Cook on a cast-iron tawa until they puff.
3. Brush with ghee. Keep wrapped in a cloth.
