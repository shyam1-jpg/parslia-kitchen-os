# Chak-Hao Rice

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Black rice as the grain of the meal.

## Ingredients

- Chak-hao, soaked

## Method

1. Steam or cook in steel until tender.
