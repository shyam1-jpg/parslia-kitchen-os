Manipur black rice as the grain of the meal.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | chak-hao (Manipur black rice) |
| 700 | ml | water |
| ½ | tsp | salt |

## Method

1. Rinse until the water runs less purple. Soak 2 hours. Drain.
2. Steam or simmer in steel with salt until grains are tender, 25–30 minutes.
3. Rest 5 minutes. The rice should be nutty and slightly chewy.
