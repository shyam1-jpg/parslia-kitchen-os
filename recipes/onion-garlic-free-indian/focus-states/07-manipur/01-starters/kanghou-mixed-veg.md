# Kanghou Mixed Veg

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Quick dry-fried seasonal vegetables with chilli.

## Ingredients

- Cabbage, beans, carrot
- Green chilli
- Ginger
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. High-heat stir in steel. No onion.
