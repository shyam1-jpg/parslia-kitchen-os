# Kanghou Mixed Veg

**Recipe card** · Manipur kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 12 min |
| **Total** | 22 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

High-heat dry-fried seasonal vegetables. Ginger and chilli stand in for alliums.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | cabbage, shredded |
| 100 | g | french beans, cut 3 cm |
| 100 | g | carrot, batons |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, julienne |
| 2 | pieces | green chilli, slit |
| 2 | tbsp | mustard oil |
| 1 | tsp | salt |

## Method

1. Heat mustard oil in a steel kadhai until it just smokes, then lower the flame.
2. Add ginger and chilli, then cabbage, beans and carrot. Stir-fry 8–10 minutes.
3. Season with salt. Vegetables should stay bright, not soggy. Serve at once.
