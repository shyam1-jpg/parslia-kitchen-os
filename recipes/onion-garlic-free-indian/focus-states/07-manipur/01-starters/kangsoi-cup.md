# Kangsoi Cup

**Recipe card** · Manipur kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 25 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

A small bowl of clear vegetable soup to open the meal.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 100 | g | cabbage, cubed |
| 100 | g | potato, cubed |
| 80 | g | french beans, cut |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | pieces | green chilli, slit |
| 700 | ml | water |
| 1 | tsp | salt |

## Method

1. Bring water, ginger and chilli to a boil in a stainless steel pot.
2. Add cabbage, potato, beans and salt. Simmer 12 minutes until just tender.
3. Ladle into steel cups. Broth should be light and peppery, never cloudy from stock cubes.
