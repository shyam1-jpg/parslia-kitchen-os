# Kangsoi Cup

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Small bowl of vegetable soup as a starter.

## Ingredients

- Cabbage
- Potato
- Beans
- Ginger
- Chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Simmer a clear soup. Serve in steel cups.
