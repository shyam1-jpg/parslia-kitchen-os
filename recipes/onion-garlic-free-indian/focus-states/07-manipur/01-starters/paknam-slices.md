# Paknam Slices

**Recipe card** · Manipur kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 25 min |
| **Total** | 45 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Steamed gram-and-herb cake, sliced like a savoury terrine. No fermented fish, no onion, no garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | besan (gram flour) |
| 120 | ml | water |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, grated |
| 2 | pieces | green chilli, finely chopped |
| 30 | g | fresh coriander leaves, chopped |
| 1 | tsp | salt |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 1 | tbsp | mustard oil |
| 1 | pieces | banana leaf, to line the steamer |

## Method

1. Mix besan, water, ginger, chilli, coriander, salt, hing and mustard oil to a thick cake batter.
2. Line a steel steamer thali with the banana leaf. Pour in the batter 2 cm thick.
3. Steam 20 minutes. Cool 5 minutes, turn out and slice into 8 fingers. Serve warm.
