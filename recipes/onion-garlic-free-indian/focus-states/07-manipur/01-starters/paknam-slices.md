# Paknam Slices

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Steamed gram-herb cake.

## Ingredients

- Besan
- Coriander and herbs (no spring onion)
- Ginger
- Salt
- Banana leaf

## Method

1. Steam 20 minutes in steel, slice.
