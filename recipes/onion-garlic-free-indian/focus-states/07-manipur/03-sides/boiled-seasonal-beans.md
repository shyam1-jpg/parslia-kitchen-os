# Boiled Seasonal Beans

**Recipe card** · Manipur kitchen · Side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table |
| **Course** | Side |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 12 min |
| **Total** | 22 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Plain boiled beans finished with ginger and chilli — a clean Manipuri side.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | seasonal beans, trimmed |
| 15 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | pieces | green chilli, slit |
| 1 | tsp | salt |
| 600 | ml | water |

## Method

1. Boil beans in salted water with ginger and chilli for 8–10 minutes.
2. Drain. Toss with a spoon of the cooking liquor. Serve hot.
