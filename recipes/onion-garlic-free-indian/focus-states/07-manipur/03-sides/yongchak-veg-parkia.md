# Yongchak Veg (parkia)

**Recipe card** · Manipur kitchen · Side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table |
| **Course** | Side |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 25 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Stink beans with chilli. If yongchak is unavailable, use french beans.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | yongchak (parkia) or french beans, sliced |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, julienne |
| 3 | pieces | green chilli, slit |
| 2 | tbsp | mustard oil |
| 1 | tsp | salt |

## Method

1. Heat mustard oil in a steel kadhai. Add ginger and chilli.
2. Add beans and salt. Stir-fry 10–12 minutes until tender with a little bite.
