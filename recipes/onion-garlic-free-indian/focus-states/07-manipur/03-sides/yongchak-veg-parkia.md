# Yongchak Veg (parkia)

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Stink beans with chilli — skip if unavailable; use french beans.

## Ingredients

- Yongchak or french beans
- Chilli
- Ginger
- Salt

## Method

1. Stir-fry until tender.
