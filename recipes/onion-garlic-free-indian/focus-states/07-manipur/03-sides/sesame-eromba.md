# Sesame Eromba

**Recipe card** · Manipur kitchen · Side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table |
| **Course** | Side |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel or glass bowl |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Boiled vegetables pounded with roasted sesame and chilli — Manipur's mash, without fish paste.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | potatoes, boiled and peeled |
| 150 | g | french beans, boiled |
| 40 | g | white sesame seeds, roasted |
| 3 | pieces | dry red chilli, toasted |
| 1 | tsp | salt |
| 1 | tbsp | mustard oil |

## Method

1. Pound roasted sesame and chilli to a coarse paste in a stone mortar or crush in a steel bowl.
2. Mash potatoes and beans with the paste, salt and mustard oil. Keep some texture.
3. Serve at room temperature as a side to rice and stew.
