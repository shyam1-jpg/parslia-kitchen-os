# Sesame Eromba

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Boiled veg mashed with roasted sesame and chilli.

## Ingredients

- Potato
- Beans
- Roasted sesame
- Dry chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Pound sesame-chilli. Mash with boiled veg.
