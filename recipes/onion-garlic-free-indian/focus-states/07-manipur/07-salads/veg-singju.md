| 3 | tbsp | ≈ 24 g | white sesame seeds, roasted and crushed |
| 2 | tbsp | ≈ 16 g | besan (gram flour), dry-roasted |
| 2 | pieces | ≈ 16 g | green chilli, minced |
| 1 | tbsp | ≈ 8 g | lemon juice |
| 1 | tsp | ≈ 6 g | salt |

## Method

1. Mise en place. Weigh every ingredient on this card for 4 portions. Set Steel or glass bowl. Wash produce. Confirm spice blends have no onion or garlic powder. No onion, no garlic, no aluminium.
2. Cut vegetables to even size so they cook together.
3. Toss cabbage and beans with sesame, roasted besan, chilli, lemon and salt. Work in Steel or glass bowl. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
4. Rest 5 minutes so the besan clings. Work in Steel or glass bowl. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
5. Serve the same hour — it weeps if it sits. Work in Steel or glass bowl. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
6. Doneness: vegetables stay crisp; dressing coats evenly; seasoning is bright, not flat.
7. Taste and adjust salt, lemon or chilli only — do not add onion or garlic at the finish. Garnish as listed. Yield is 4 portions.
8. Hold chilled, covered, up to 2 hours. Stir before service. Discard if it weeps heavily or smells sour beyond yogurt character. Service: Cold.

## Chef notes

For Veg Singju: squeeze wet vegetables dry or the bowl weeps on the pass. Season slightly higher than you think — cold dulls salt. No onion, no garlic, no allium garnish. Use steel or glass, never aluminium. Dress at the last minute. Verify dahi and spice labels.
