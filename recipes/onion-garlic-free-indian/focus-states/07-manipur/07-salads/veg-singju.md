# Veg Singju

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Manipuri salad with roasted sesame and besan, no nga-ri.

## Ingredients

- Cabbage
- Beans or banana flower
- Roasted sesame
- Roasted besan
- Chilli
- Lemon
- Salt

## Method

1. Toss, rest 5 minutes.
