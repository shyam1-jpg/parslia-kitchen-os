Shredded cabbage with lemon and sesame.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | cabbage, shredded |
| 1 | tbsp | lemon juice |
| 2 | tbsp | white sesame seeds, toasted |
| ¾ | tsp | salt |

## Method

1. Toss cabbage with lemon, sesame and salt. Serve crisp and cold.
