# Cucumber Chilli Salad

| **Course** | Salad |
| **Serves** | 4 |

| **Total** | 6 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel or glass bowl |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Cool cucumber with chilli and lemon. Go easy if using king chilli.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | pieces | cucumbers, sliced thin |
| 1 | pieces | green chilli, minced |
| 1 | tbsp | lemon juice |
| ½ | tsp | salt |

## Method

1. Toss cucumber, chilli, lemon and salt. Serve immediately.
