# Steamed Rice Cake

**Recipe card** · Manipur kitchen · Sweet

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table |
| **Course** | Sweet |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Soft rice-flour cakes with jaggery and coconut.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | rice flour |
| 100 | g | jaggery, grated |
| 80 | g | fresh grated coconut |
| 150 | ml | warm water |
| ¼ | tsp | salt |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, powdered |

## Method

1. Mix rice flour, jaggery, coconut, salt, cardamom and water to a thick batter.
2. Spoon into a steel idli mould or thali. Steam 12–15 minutes.
3. Unmould. Serve warm.
