# Til-Gud Ladoo

Sesame and jaggery balls — festival sweet.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | white sesame seeds, roasted |
| 120 | g | jaggery, grated |
| 1 | tbsp | ghee |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, powdered |

## Method

1. Melt jaggery with ghee in a steel kadhai until it forms a soft ball in cold water.
2. Stir in sesame and cardamom. Off the heat, shape 12 ladoos while warm.
3. Cool on a steel plate. Store in an airtight tin.
