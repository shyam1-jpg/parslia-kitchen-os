# Til-Gud Ladoo

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Manipur, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Sesame
- Jaggery
- Ghee

## Method

1. Melt jaggery, add sesame, shape.
