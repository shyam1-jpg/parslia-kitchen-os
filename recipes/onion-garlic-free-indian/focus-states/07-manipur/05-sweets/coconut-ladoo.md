# Coconut Ladoo

|---|---|

| **Course** | Sweet |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 5 min |

| **Total** | 15 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Quick coconut balls with jaggery.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | fresh grated coconut |
| 120 | g | jaggery, grated |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, powdered |
| 1 | tsp | ghee, for the pan |

## Method

1. Cook coconut and jaggery in a steel pan 6–8 minutes until the mix holds together.
2. Add cardamom. Shape 12 ladoos. Cool.
