# Saga Bhaja

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Amaranth or spinach fry.

## Ingredients

- Greens
- Garlic skipped: use dry chilli and panch phoron
- Mustard oil
- Salt

## Method

1. Temper, wilt greens. No onion, no garlic.
