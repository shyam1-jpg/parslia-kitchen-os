# Besara Mixed Veg

**Recipe card** · Odisha kitchen · Side

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen |
| **Course** | Side |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Mustard-paste vegetable of Odisha.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 400 | g | mixed seasonal vegetables, cut |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| 1 | tsp | panch phoron |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 2 | tbsp | mustard oil |

## Method

1. Temper panch phoron. Add veg and mustard paste. Cook until the oil shines.
