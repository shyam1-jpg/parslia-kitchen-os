# Besara Mixed Veg

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Mustard-paste vegetable of Odisha.

## Ingredients

- Mixed veg
- Mustard seeds ground with chilli
- Panch phoron
- Turmeric
- Salt
- Mustard oil

## Method

1. Temper panch phoron. Add veg and mustard paste. Cook until the oil shines.
