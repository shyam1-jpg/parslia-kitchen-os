# Bara (no onion)

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Urad vada. Pepper, ginger, fennel — no onion.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup urad dal, soaked
- Ginger
- Fennel
- Pepper
- Hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Grind fluffy, fry rings in steel.
