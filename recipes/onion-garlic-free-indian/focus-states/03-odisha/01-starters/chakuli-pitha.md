# Chakuli Pitha

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Rice-urad pancake of Odisha.

## Ingredients

- Rice
- Urad dal
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Grind, spread on iron tawa, cook both sides.
