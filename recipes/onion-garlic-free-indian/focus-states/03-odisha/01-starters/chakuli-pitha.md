# Chakuli Pitha

**Recipe card** · Odisha kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium) |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Rice-urad pancake of Odisha.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | raw rice |
| 50 | g | urad dal |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Grind, spread on iron tawa, cook both sides.
