# Chhena Poda

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 50 min  
**Cookware:** Steel baking dish — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Odisha, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Chenna
- Sugar
- Sooji
- Cardamom
- Ghee

## Method

1. Bake until the top is burnt caramel.
