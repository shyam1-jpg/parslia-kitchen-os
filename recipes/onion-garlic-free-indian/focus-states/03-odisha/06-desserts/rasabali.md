# Rasabali

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Odisha, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Chenna patties
- Reduced milk
- Sugar
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Sear patties, soak in thickened steel-pot milk.
