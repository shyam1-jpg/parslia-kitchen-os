Signature dessert from Odisha, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | fresh chhena |
| 500 | ml | reduced full-fat milk |
| 100 | g | sugar |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, crushed |

## Method

1. Sear patties, soak in thickened steel-pot milk.
