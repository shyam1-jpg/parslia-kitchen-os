# Kheeri

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Odia rice kheer with bay leaf.

## Ingredients

- Rice
- Milk
- Sugar
- Bay
- Cardamom
- Raisins

## Method

1. Simmer in steel until creamy.
