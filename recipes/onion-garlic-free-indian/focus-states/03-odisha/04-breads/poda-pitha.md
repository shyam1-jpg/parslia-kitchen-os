# Poda Pitha

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or earthen pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Slow-baked rice-jaggery cake used as a dense bread-sweet.

## Ingredients

- Rice paste
- Jaggery
- Coconut
- Ginger powder
- Black pepper
- Ghee

## Method

1. Mix, bake in a greased steel pot on low until the crust is charred-caramel.
