## Method

1. Mise en place. Weigh every ingredient on this card for 4 portions. Set Steel or glass bowl. Wash produce. Confirm spice blends have no onion or garlic powder. No onion, no garlic, no aluminium.
2. Cut vegetables to even size so they cook together. For dough, add water gradually; rest covered 10–15 minutes before rolling.
3. Soak rice in water (or whey). Work in Steel or glass bowl. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
4. Season. Work in Steel or glass bowl. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
5. Serve with saga and badi. Work in Steel or glass bowl. Cook to the doneness below, tasting salt at the end. Do not add onion, garlic or aluminium cookware.
6. Doneness: bread is cooked through, no wet dough in the centre, light brown spots on the face.
7. Taste and adjust salt, lemon or chilli only — do not add onion or garlic at the finish. Garnish as listed. Yield is 4 portions.
8. Hold wrapped in a clean cloth in a covered steel box up to 30 minutes. Refresh on a hot tawa 20 seconds if needed. Service: Hot.

## Chef notes

For Pakhala Rice: keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder. Bring yogurt to a simmer gently and stir; a rolling boil can split it.
