# Pakhala Rice

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Fermented or instant soaked rice — Odisha’s summer staple instead of roti.

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice
- Water
- Curd optional
- Roasted cumin
- Green chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Soak rice in water (or whey). Season. Serve with saga and badi.
