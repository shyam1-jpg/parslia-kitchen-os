Fermented or instant soaked rice — Odisha’s summer staple instead of roti.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 400 | g | cooked rice |
| 500 | ml | water |
| 100 | g | plain yogurt, optional |
| 1 | tsp | roasted cumin powder |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Soak rice in water (or whey). Season. Serve with saga and badi.
