# Amba Khatta

Raw-mango sweet-sour relish.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | pieces | raw mango, grated |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 100 | g | jaggery, grated |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Temper, cook 5 minutes until glossy.
