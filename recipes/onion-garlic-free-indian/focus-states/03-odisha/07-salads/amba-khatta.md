# Amba Khatta

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 10 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Raw-mango sweet-sour relish.

## Ingredients

- Raw mango
- Mustard
- Hing
- Turmeric
- Chilli
- Jaggery
- Salt

## Method

1. Temper, cook 5 minutes until glossy.
