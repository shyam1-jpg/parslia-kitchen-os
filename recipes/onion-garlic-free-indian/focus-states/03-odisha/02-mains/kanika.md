Mildly sweet ghee rice of Odia feasts.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | cup | basmati |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 2 | leaves | bay leaves |
| 20 | g | raisins |
| 20 | g | cashews |
| 1 | pinch | sugar |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Fry spices and nuts in ghee. Cook rice. Slightly sweet, no onion.
