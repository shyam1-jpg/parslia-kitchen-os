# Kanika

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Mildly sweet ghee rice of Odia feasts.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup basmati
- Ghee
- Bay, cardamom, cinnamon, clove
- Raisins
- Cashews
- Sugar pinch
- Salt

## Method

1. Fry spices and nuts in ghee. Cook rice. Slightly sweet, no onion.
