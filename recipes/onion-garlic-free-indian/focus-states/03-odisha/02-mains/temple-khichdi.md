# Temple Khichdi

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Moong-rice khichdi as offered in Puri — only ghee, ginger, cumin, salt.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup rice
- ½ cup moong
- Ghee
- Cumin
- Hing
- Ginger
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook together very soft. Ghee tadka. This is mahaprasad style.
