# Temple Khichdi

Moong-rice khichdi as offered in Puri — only ghee, ginger, cumin, salt.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| ½ | cup | rice |
| ½ | cup | moong |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook together very soft. Ghee tadka. This is mahaprasad style.
