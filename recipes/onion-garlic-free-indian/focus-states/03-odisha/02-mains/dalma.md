# Dalma

Jagannath-kitchen dal with vegetables. Onion and garlic are forbidden.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | toor dal, rinsed |
| 500 | g | raw banana, papaya, brinjal, pumpkin and beans, cubed |
| 1 | tsp | roasted cumin powder |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 2 | pieces | dry red chillies |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook dal and veg together. Cumin-ginger-ghee tadka. Never aluminium, never allium.
