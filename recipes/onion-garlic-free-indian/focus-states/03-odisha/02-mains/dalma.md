# Dalma

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Jagannath-kitchen dal with vegetables. Onion and garlic are forbidden.

## Ingredients

- Toor dal
- Raw banana, papaya, brinjal, pumpkin, beans
- Roasted cumin
- Ginger
- Ghee
- Dry chilli
- Turmeric
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook dal and veg together. Cumin-ginger-ghee tadka. Never aluminium, never allium.
