# Ragi Roti

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Signature bread from Karnataka, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Ragi flour
- Hot water
- Ginger-chilli
- Coriander
- Salt

## Method

1. Pat onto iron tawa.
