# Neer Dosa

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian |
| **Course** | Bread |
| **Serves** | 4 |

| **Cook** | 15 min |

Lacy water dosa of Tulunadu.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | raw rice |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Pour on hot iron tawa. Do not spread like regular dosa. Fold.
