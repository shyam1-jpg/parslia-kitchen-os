Lentil-jaggery flatbread of Karnataka.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | maida (refined flour) |
| 250 | g | cooked chana or toor dal with jaggery and cardamom |

## Method

1. Stuff, roll thin, cook on iron with ghee.
