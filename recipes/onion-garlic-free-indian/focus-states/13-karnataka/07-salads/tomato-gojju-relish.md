# Tomato Gojju Relish

**Recipe card** · Karnataka kitchen · Salad

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian |
| **Course** | Salad |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 10 min |
| **Cook** | 12 min |
| **Total** | 22 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Sweet-sour tomato gojju served like a salad-relish.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | pieces | ripe tomatoes, chopped |
| 20 | g | seedless tamarind |
| 100 | g | jaggery, grated |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook to a relish. No onion.
