# Tomato Gojju Relish

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sweet-sour tomato gojju served like a salad-relish.

## Ingredients

- Tomato
- Tamarind
- Jaggery
- Mustard
- Hing
- Chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook to a relish. No onion.
