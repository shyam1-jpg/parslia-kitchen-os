# Bisi Bele Bath

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Udupi hot lentil-rice. Use garlic-free powder.

## Ingredients

- Rice
- Toor dal
- Mixed veg
- Bisi bele bath powder garlic-free
- Tamarind
- Ghee
- Mustard
- Hing
- Cashews
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook loose and hot. Temple style — no onion, no garlic.
