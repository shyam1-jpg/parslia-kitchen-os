# Bisi Bele Bath

**Recipe card** · Karnataka kitchen · Main

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian |
| **Course** | Main |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 40 min |
| **Total** | 60 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Udupi hot lentil-rice. Use garlic-free powder.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 200 | g | raw rice |
| 150 | g | toor dal, rinsed |
| 400 | g | mixed seasonal vegetables, cut |
| 2 | tbsp | bisi bele bath powder (no onion-garlic) |
| 20 | g | seedless tamarind |
| 2 | tbsp | ghee |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 20 | g | cashews |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Cook loose and hot. Temple style — no onion, no garlic.
