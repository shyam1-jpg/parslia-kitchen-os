# Thondekayi Palya

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian |
| **Course** | Side |
| **Serves** | 4 |

| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |

Ivy gourd coconut stir-fry.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 300 | g | thondekayi (ivy gourd) |
| 100 | g | fresh grated coconut |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Fry until the gourd is tender-crisp.
