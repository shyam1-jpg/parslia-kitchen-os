# Thondekayi Palya

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ivy gourd coconut stir-fry.

## Ingredients

- Thondekayi
- Coconut
- Mustard
- Chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Fry until the gourd is tender-crisp.
