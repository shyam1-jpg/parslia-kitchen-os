Banana-yogurt puri of coastal Karnataka.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | maida (refined flour) |
| 2 | pieces | ripe bananas |
| 200 | g | plain yogurt |
| 100 | g | sugar |
| ¼ | tsp | baking soda |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Rest dough, roll thick, fry in steel until they puff.
