# Mangalore Buns

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 80 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Banana-yogurt puri of coastal Karnataka.

## Ingredients

- Maida
- Banana
- Yogurt
- Sugar
- Baking soda pinch
- Oil

## Method

1. Rest dough, roll thick, fry in steel until they puff.
