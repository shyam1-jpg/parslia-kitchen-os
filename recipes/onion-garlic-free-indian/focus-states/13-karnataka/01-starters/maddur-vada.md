# Maddur Vada

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Rice-flour vada. Skip onion; use curry leaf, coconut, chilli.

## Ingredients

- Rice flour
- Atta
- Sooji
- Coconut
- Ginger-chilli
- Curry leaves
- Hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Flatten thin, fry crisp in steel.
