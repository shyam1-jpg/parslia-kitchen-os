# Maddur Vada

**Recipe card** · Karnataka kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Rice-flour vada. Skip onion; use curry leaf, coconut, chilli.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | rice flour |
| 300 | g | whole-wheat atta |
| 150 | g | sooji (semolina) |
| 100 | g | fresh grated coconut |
| 1 | tbsp | ginger-green chilli paste (no garlic) |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Flatten thin, fry crisp in steel.
