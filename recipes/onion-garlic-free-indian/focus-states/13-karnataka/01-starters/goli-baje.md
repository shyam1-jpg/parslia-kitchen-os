# Goli Baje

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Mangaluru bajji. Yogurt-maida batter with ginger, no onion.

## Ingredients

- Maida
- Yogurt
- Ginger-chilli
- Curry leaves
- Hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Drop blobs, fry. Serve with coconut chutney (no garlic).
