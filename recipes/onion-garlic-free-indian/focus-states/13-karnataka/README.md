# Karnataka

**Kitchen:** Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Diet:** vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no aluminium cookware

Udupi cooking is sattvic. Bisi bele, saaru, neer dosa, obbattu, kosambari.

Open first: [`index.html`](index.html) (recipe cards) or [`excel/karnataka-recipes.xlsx`](excel/karnataka-recipes.xlsx) (one sheet = one card).
