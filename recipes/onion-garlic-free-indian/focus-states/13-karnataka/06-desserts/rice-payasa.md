# Rice Payasa

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Karnataka, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Rice
- Milk or coconut milk
- Jaggery
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Simmer in steel.
