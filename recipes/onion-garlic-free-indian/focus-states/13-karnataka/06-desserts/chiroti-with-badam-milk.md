# Chiroti with Badam Milk

**Recipe card** · Karnataka kitchen · Dessert

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian |
| **Course** | Dessert |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 25 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 45 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Flaky chiroti served in almond milk.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 250 | g | maida (refined flour) |
| 100 | g | sugar |
| 500 | ml | badam milk |

## Method

1. Fry chiroti in steel. Serve soaking in warm almond milk.
