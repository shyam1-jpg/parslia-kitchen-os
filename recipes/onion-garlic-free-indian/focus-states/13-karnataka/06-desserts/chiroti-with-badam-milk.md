# Chiroti with Badam Milk

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Flaky chiroti served in almond milk.

## Ingredients

- Maida layered with ghee
- Sugar
- Badam milk

## Method

1. Fry chiroti in steel. Serve soaking in warm almond milk.
