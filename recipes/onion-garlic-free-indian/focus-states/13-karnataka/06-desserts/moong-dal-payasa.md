Signature dessert from Karnataka, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 150 | g | roasted moong |
| 400 | ml | thick coconut milk |
| 100 | g | jaggery, grated |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, crushed |
| 20 | g | cashews |

## Method

1. Cook dal, jaggery, coconut milk. Do not boil hard.
