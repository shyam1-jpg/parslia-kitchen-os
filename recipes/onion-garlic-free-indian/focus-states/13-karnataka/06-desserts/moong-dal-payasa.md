# Moong Dal Payasa

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Karnataka, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Moong, roasted
- Coconut milk
- Jaggery
- Cardamom
- Cashews

## Method

1. Cook dal, jaggery, coconut milk. Do not boil hard.
