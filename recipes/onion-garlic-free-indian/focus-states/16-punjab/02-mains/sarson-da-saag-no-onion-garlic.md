Signature main from Punjab, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | kg | mustard greens + 250 g spinach + 100 g bathua optional |
| 20 | g | fresh ginger, crushed |
| ½ | tsp | hing |

## Method

1. Boil greens. Mash. Simmer with makki atta. Temper ghee, ginger, chilli, hing.
2. Serve with makki roti and white butter.
