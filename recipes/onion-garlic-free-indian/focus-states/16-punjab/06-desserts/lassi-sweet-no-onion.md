# Lassi (sweet, no onion)

**Continent:** Punjab — Punjabi langar and home vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Punjab, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups dahi, 1 cup chilled water or milk, sugar, cardamom, ice
- Optional malai

## Method

1. Blend in steel glass. Serve cold.
