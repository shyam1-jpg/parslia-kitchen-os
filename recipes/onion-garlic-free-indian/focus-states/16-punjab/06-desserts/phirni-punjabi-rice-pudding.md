# Phirni (Punjabi rice pudding)

**Continent:** Punjab — Punjabi langar and home vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Punjab, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup soaked rice ground
- 1 L milk, sugar, saffron, cardamom
- Clay bowls to set

## Method

1. Cook rice paste in milk. Sugar, saffron. Pour in clay. Chill.
