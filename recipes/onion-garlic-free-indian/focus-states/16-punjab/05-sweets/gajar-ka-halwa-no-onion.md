Signature sweet from Punjab, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | kg | red carrots grated |
| 1 | L | milk |
| ¾ | cup | sugar |
| ½ | cup | ghee, cardamom, nuts |

## Method

1. Cook carrot in milk till dry. Ghee, sugar, nuts.
