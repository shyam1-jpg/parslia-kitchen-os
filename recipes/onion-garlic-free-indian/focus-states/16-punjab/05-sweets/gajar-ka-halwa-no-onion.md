## Method

1. Mise en place. Grate 1 kg red carrots. Measure 1 L milk, 3/4 cup sugar, 1/2 cup ghee. Set a heavy stainless steel kadhai — never aluminium (milk and carrot acid).
2. Cook carrot in the milk on medium, stirring, until the milk is absorbed and the mass is dry, 30–40 minutes.
3. Add ghee. Roast 8–10 minutes until the carrot shines and the raw smell is gone.
4. Add sugar. It will loosen. Cook until it leaves the sides of the pan, 8–12 minutes. Finish cardamom and nuts.
5. Doneness: glossy, spoon leaves a trail, no watery milk. Rest 5 minutes.
6. Serve hot or warm. Hold in a bain of hot water. Label milk and tree nuts.

## Chef notes

For Gajar ka halwa (no onion): keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder.
