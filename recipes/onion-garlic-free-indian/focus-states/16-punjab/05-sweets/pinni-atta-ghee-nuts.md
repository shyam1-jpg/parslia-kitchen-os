Signature sweet from Punjab, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | atta |
| 1 | cup | ghee |
| 1 | cup | powdered sugar or jaggery, almonds, gond optional, cardamom |

## Method

1. Roast atta in ghee. Mix sugar and nuts. Shape pinnis.
