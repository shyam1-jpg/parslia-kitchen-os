## Method

1. Mise en place. Weigh 2 cups atta, 1 cup ghee, 1 cup grated jaggery, sesame and a pinch of dry ginger. Steel kadhai — never aluminium.
2. Roast atta in ghee on low 18–22 minutes to biscuit-brown.
3. Cool 5 minutes. Mix jaggery, sesame and ginger. Jaggery should melt from residual heat, not from a blast of flame (it burns).
4. Shape pinnis while warm. Cool to set.
5. Doneness: holds shape, no raw atta, jaggery mixed through.
6. Store airtight. Serve at room temperature. Label gluten and sesame.

## Chef notes

For Atta gur pinni winter (jaggery): keep the mix slightly drier than you think; wet mixes split or go greasy. Bloom hing in hot fat 20–30 seconds; raw hing tastes medicinal. Commercial hing is often cut with wheat flour — use a gluten-free hing if you must mark Gluten Free. Never cook tomato, tamarind, lemon, yogurt or milk in aluminium; use stainless steel, iron, clay or glass. Spice blends: read the packet. Many garam masalas hide onion or garlic powder.
