# Mooli salad (white radish, no onion)

**Continent:** Punjab — Punjabi langar and home vegetarian  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Signature salad from Punjab, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 white radishes grated, 1 carrot
- Lemon, green chilli, salt, coriander, roasted cumin

## Method

1. Squeeze extra water. Toss.
