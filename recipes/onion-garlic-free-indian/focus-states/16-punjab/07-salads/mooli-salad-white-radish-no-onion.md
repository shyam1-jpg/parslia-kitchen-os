## Method

1. Mise en place. Peel 2 white radishes and 1 carrot. Set a steel or glass bowl. No onion.
2. Grate. Squeeze firmly in a cloth to remove water or the salad weeps on the pass.
3. Toss with lemon, slit green chilli, salt, coriander and roasted cumin. Taste. It should be sharp and clean.
4. Doneness: crisp, not wet; seasoning bright.
5. Serve cold at once. Hold 30 minutes covered and chilled. Drain if liquid collects. Label mustard if radish is an issue for the guest.
