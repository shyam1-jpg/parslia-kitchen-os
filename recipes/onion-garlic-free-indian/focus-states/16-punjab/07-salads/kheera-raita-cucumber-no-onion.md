## Method

1. Mise en place. Whisk 2 cups dahi until smooth. Grate 1 cucumber. Set a steel or glass bowl. No onion.
2. Squeeze cucumber dry. Fold into dahi with roasted cumin, salt and chopped mint.
3. Chill 20 minutes so the flavours settle. Stir. Taste salt — cold dulls it slightly, so season a touch higher than you think.
4. Doneness: thick enough to mound, cucumber crisp, no water pooling.
5. Serve cold. Hold chilled up to 2 hours. Stir at the pass. Label milk.
