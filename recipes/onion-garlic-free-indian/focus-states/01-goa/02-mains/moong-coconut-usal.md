Sprouted moong in coconut — Goan-Maharashtrian border breakfast.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | sprouted moong |
| ½ | cup | coconut paste |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 1 | tbsp | lemon juice |

## Method

1. Temper, add sprouts, coconut, splash of water. Cover 8 minutes. Lemon.
