# Moong Coconut Usal

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sprouted moong in coconut — Goan-Maharashtrian border breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sprouted moong
- ½ cup coconut paste
- Mustard
- Hing
- Curry leaves
- Green chilli
- Salt
- Lemon

## Method

1. Temper, add sprouts, coconut, splash of water. Cover 8 minutes. Lemon.
