# Vegetable Caldin

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Pale turmeric-coconut curry. Skip garlic; use pepper, cumin, kokum.

## Ingredients

- Cauliflower, beans, carrot
- 1½ cups coconut milk
- Turmeric
- Cumin
- Pepper
- Kokum
- Hing
- Curry leaves
- Salt

## Method

1. Temper spices. Simmer veg in coconut milk with kokum until coated.
