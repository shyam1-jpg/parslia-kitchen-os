# Vegetable Caldin

**Recipe card** · Goa kitchen · Main

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic |
| **Course** | Main |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 25 min |
| **Total** | 40 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Pale turmeric-coconut curry. Skip garlic; use pepper, cumin, kokum.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 400 | g | cauliflower, beans and carrot |
| 1½ | cups | coconut milk |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| ½ | tsp | black pepper, crushed |
| 6 | pieces | kokum petals |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 10 | leaves | fresh curry leaves |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Temper spices. Simmer veg in coconut milk with kokum until coated.
