# Sannas

**Recipe card** · Goa kitchen · Bread

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic |
| **Course** | Bread |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 70 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 85 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Steamed rice cakes, slightly sour. Steel moulds only.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 2 | cups | rice, soaked |
| ½ | cup | coconut |
| 1 | tsp | instant yeast (or toddy) |
| 100 | g | sugar |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Grind, ferment, steam 12 minutes in a steel idli stand.
