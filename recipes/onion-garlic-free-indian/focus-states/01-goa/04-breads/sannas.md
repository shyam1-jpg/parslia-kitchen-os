# Sannas

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 70 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Steamed rice cakes, slightly sour. Steel moulds only.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice, soaked
- ½ cup coconut
- Yeast or toddy
- Sugar
- Salt

## Method

1. Grind, ferment, steam 12 minutes in a steel idli stand.
