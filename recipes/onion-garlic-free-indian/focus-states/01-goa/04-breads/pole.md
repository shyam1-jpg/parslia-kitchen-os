# Pole

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Goan rice crepe, cousin of neer dosa.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice, soaked
- 2 tbsp coconut
- Salt

## Method

1. Grind thin. Pour on iron tawa. Do not flip hard — it should stay soft.
