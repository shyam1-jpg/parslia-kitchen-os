# Pole

Goan rice crepe, cousin of neer dosa.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | cup | rice, soaked |
| 2 | tbsp | coconut |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |

## Method

1. Grind thin. Pour on iron tawa. Do not flip hard — it should stay soft.
