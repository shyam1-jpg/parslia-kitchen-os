## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | pieces | raw mango, julienned |
| 100 | g | fresh grated coconut |
| 2 | pieces | green chillies, slit |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 1 | pinch | jaggery |

## Method

1. Toss. Rest 5 minutes.
