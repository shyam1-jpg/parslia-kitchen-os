# Doce de Grao

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Chana-dal coconut fudge of Goan feasts.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup chana dal, cooked mashed
- 1 cup coconut
- ¾ cup sugar
- Cardamom
- Ghee

## Method

1. Cook in steel until it leaves the sides. Press, cut diamonds.
