# Goa

**Kitchen:** Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Diet:** vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no aluminium cookware

Coconut, kokum, curry leaves. Khatkhate, caldin and sannas without onion or garlic.
