# Moong Ghavan

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Yellow-moong pancake eaten at Goan Hindu breakfasts.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup yellow moong, soaked
- Ginger-chilli
- Cumin
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Grind a thick batter. Spread on an iron tawa. Cook both sides.
