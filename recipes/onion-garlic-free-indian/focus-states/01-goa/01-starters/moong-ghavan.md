# Moong Ghavan

**Recipe card** · Goa kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 20 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 35 min |
| **Cookware** | Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium) |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Yellow-moong pancake eaten at Goan Hindu breakfasts.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | cup | yellow moong, soaked |
| 1 | tbsp | ginger-green chilli paste (no garlic) |
| 1 | tsp | cumin seeds |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Grind a thick batter. Spread on an iron tawa. Cook both sides.
