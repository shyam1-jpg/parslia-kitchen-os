# Jeev Kadgi Fritters

**Recipe card** · Goa kitchen · Starter

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic |
| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 15 min |
| **Cook** | 15 min |
| **Total** | 30 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Breadfruit slices in rice-flour batter — monsoon Goa.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 12 | — | breadfruit or raw banana slices |
| ½ | cup | rice flour |
| ½ | tsp | turmeric powder |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Dip and fry in steel until crisp. Serve with kokum dip.
