# Coconut Cucumber Cutlets

| | |
|---|---|

| **Course** | Starter |
| **Serves** | 4 |

| **Total** | 30 min |
| **Cookware** | Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Goan vegetarian starter. Coconut and chilli, no allium.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 1 | cup | coconut |
| 1 | — | cucumber, squeezed |
| 2 | tbsp | besan |
| 1 | tbsp | ginger-green chilli paste (no garlic) |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 2 | tbsp | oil |

## Method

1. Mix, shape, pan-fry in a steel kadhai until brown.
