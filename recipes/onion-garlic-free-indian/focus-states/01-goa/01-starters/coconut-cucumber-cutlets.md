# Coconut Cucumber Cutlets

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Goan vegetarian starter. Coconut and chilli, no allium.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup coconut
- 1 cucumber, squeezed
- 2 tbsp besan
- Ginger-chilli
- Hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Mix, shape, pan-fry in a steel kadhai until brown.
