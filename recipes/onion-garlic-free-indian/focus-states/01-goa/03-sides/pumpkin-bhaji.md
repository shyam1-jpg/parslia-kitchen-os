# Pumpkin Bhaji

|---|---|

| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |

Sweet pumpkin with coconut and curry leaves.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 4 | cups | pumpkin |
| 100 | g | fresh grated coconut |
| 1 | tsp | mustard seeds |
| ½ | tsp | hing (asafoetida) |
| 2 | pieces | green or dry red chillies |
| 1 | tsp | salt, or to taste |
| 1 | pinch | jaggery |

## Method

1. Temper, cover until soft, coconut and jaggery.
