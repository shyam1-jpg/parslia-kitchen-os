# Pumpkin Bhaji

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sweet pumpkin with coconut and curry leaves.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups pumpkin
- Coconut
- Mustard
- Hing
- Chilli
- Salt
- Jaggery pinch

## Method

1. Temper, cover until soft, coconut and jaggery.
