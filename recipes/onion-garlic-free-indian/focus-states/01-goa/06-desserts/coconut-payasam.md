# Coconut Payasam

**Recipe card** · Goa kitchen · Dessert

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian / sattvic |
| **Course** | Dessert |
| **Serves** | 4 |
| **Prep** | 5 min |
| **Cook** | 20 min |
| **Total** | 25 min |
| **Cookware** | Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium |
| **Diet** | Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium |

Signature dessert from Goa, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients *(for 4 servings)*

| Qty | Unit | Ingredient |
|-----|------|------------|
| 80 | g | roasted vermicelli |
| 400 | ml | thick coconut milk |
| 100 | g | jaggery, grated |
| 4 | pods | green cardamom, crushed |
| 20 | g | cashews |

## Method

1. Cook in steel. Do not boil coconut milk hard.
