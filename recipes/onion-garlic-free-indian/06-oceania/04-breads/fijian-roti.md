# Fijian Roti

**Continent:** Oceania — Indo-Fijian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Soft roti eaten with khatar and dhal.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- 1 tbsp oil
- Pinch of salt
- Warm water
- Ghee

## Method

1. Knead soft dough. Rest 15 minutes. Roll and cook on iron tawa. Ghee between layers of the stack.
