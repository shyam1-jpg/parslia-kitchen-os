# Almond Phirni

**Continent:** Oceania — Indo-Fijian festive table  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ground-rice milk dessert set in steel or clay pots.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup basmati, soaked and ground to a paste
- 3 cups milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- Saffron
- Cardamom
- Slivered almonds

## Method

1. Boil milk in steel. Stir in rice paste. Cook until it thickens and the raw taste goes.
2. Add sugar, saffron and cardamom. Pour into steel katoris. Chill. Top with almonds.
