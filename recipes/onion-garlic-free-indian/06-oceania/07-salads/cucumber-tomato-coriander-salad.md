# Cucumber Tomato Coriander Salad

**Continent:** Oceania — Fiji  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Glass bowl

## Why this dish

Simple island table salad, no onion.

## Ingredients

- 1 cucumber
- 2 tomatoes
- 1 green chilli
- Lemon
- Salt
- Coriander
- Roasted cumin

## Method

1. Dice, dress, serve at once beside the dhal and roti.
