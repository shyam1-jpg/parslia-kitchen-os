# Vegetable Pakora

**Continent:** Oceania — Indo-Fijian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying

## Why this dish

Girmitiya snack. Mixed veg, no onion — cabbage, spinach and potato bind with besan.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup gram flour
- 2 tbsp rice flour
- 1 cup shredded cabbage
- ½ cup chopped spinach
- 1 small potato, grated
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli paste
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- Salt
- Water
- Oil

## Method

1. Mix vegetables with salt and spices, then flours and a little water.
2. Fry medium-size pakoras in a steel kadhai until crisp.
3. Serve with tamarind chutney.
