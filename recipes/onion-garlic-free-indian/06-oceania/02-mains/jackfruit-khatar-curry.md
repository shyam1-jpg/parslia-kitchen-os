# Jackfruit Khatar Curry

**Continent:** Oceania — Indo-Fijian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fiji’s vegetarian jackfruit curry, originally onion-garlic heavy. Hing, curry leaves and ginger take over.

## Ingredients

- 400 g young jackfruit (fresh or tinned, drained)
- 3 tbsp oil
- ½ tsp mustard seeds
- ½ tsp cumin seeds
- 10 curry leaves
- Pinch of hing
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp garlic-free curry powder
- ½ cup water
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Heat oil. Splutter mustard, cumin, curry leaves and hing.
2. Add ginger and dry spices. Fry 30 seconds.
3. Add jackfruit, salt and water. Cook 15–20 minutes until almost dry and the edges catch colour.
4. Garnish with coriander.

## Notes

Tinned jackfruit in brine: rinse well. Tinned in syrup is for dessert, not this curry.
