# Milk-Powder Gulab Jamun

**Continent:** Oceania — Indo-Fijian / wider Indian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai + steel saucepan for syrup

## Why this dish

Festival sweet across Fiji. Already no allium. Syrup must not touch aluminium.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup milk powder
- ¼ cup maida
- ¼ tsp baking soda
- 2 tbsp ghee
- Milk to bind
- 1½ cups sugar
- 1½ cups water
- 4 cardamom
- Few saffron strands
- Oil or ghee for frying

## Method

1. Make a one-thread-adjacent syrup: sugar, water, cardamom, saffron. Keep warm in steel.
2. Rub ghee into milk powder, maida and soda. Bind with milk to a soft dough. Rest 10 minutes.
3. Shape smooth balls. Fry on low-medium until deep brown.
4. Drain and soak in warm syrup at least 30 minutes.

## Notes

If the syrup is made in aluminium it can crystallise oddly and taste sharp.
