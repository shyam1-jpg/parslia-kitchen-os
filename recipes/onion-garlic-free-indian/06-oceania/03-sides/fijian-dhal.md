# Fijian Dhal

**Continent:** Oceania — Indo-Fijian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

The everyday soup-dal of Fiji Indian homes.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup masoor or toor dal
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- 4 cups water
- Salt
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 dry chillies
- 1 tomato
- Lemon

## Method

1. Cook dal with turmeric until very soft. Whisk.
2. Add tomato and salt. Simmer 5 minutes.
3. Tadka: oil, mustard, cumin, hing, curry leaves, chilli. Pour over. Lemon at the table.
