# Punjabi Dal Tadka

**Continent:** Delhi — Delhi street and home vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Delhi, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Toor or moong dal
- Tomato
- Cumin
- Hing
- Ghee
- Chilli

## Method

1. Simmer, tadka.
