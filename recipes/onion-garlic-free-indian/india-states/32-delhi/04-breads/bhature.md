# Bhature

**Continent:** Delhi — Delhi street and home vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 70 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fermented fried bread of Delhi chole.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- ½ cup yogurt
- ½ tsp baking powder
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Rest 1 hour. Roll, fry in a steel kadhai until they balloon.
