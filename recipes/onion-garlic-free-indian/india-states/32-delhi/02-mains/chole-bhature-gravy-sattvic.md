# Chole Bhature Gravy (sattvic)

**Continent:** Delhi — Delhi street and home vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Delhi chole without onion-garlic paste. Tomato, tea, anardana, ginger.

## Ingredients

- Chickpeas
- Tomato-ginger paste
- Chole masala garlic-free
- Tea bag
- Amchur
- Cumin
- Hing

## Method

1. Cook a thick dark gravy. Serve with bhature.
