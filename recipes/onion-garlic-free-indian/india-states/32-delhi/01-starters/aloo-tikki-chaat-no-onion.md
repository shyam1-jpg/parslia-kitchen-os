# Aloo Tikki Chaat (no onion)

**Continent:** Delhi — Delhi street and home vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Delhi chaat minus raw onion. Yogurt, chutneys, sev.

## Ingredients

- Aloo tikki
- Yogurt
- Tamarind chutney (no garlic)
- Mint chutney (no onion garlic)
- Sev
- Pomegranate
- Chaat masala

## Method

1. Fry tikki on iron. Top with yogurt, chutneys, sev, pomegranate. No pyaz.
