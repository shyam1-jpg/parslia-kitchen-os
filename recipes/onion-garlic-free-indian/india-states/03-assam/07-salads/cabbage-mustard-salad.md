# Cabbage Mustard Salad

**Continent:** Assam — Assamese vegetarian table  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Shredded cabbage with mustard oil and lemon — no onion.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups shredded cabbage
- 1 tsp mustard oil
- Lemon
- Green chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Toss everything. Rest 5 minutes so the cabbage softens slightly.
