# Aloo Pitika

**Continent:** Assam — Assamese vegetarian table  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Mashed potato with mustard oil — Assam’s essential side. Skip the usual raw onion.

## Ingredients

- 4 potatoes, boiled
- 1 tbsp mustard oil
- 2 green chillies, crushed
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Mash hot potatoes with mustard oil, chilli and salt.
2. Coriander on top. The mustard oil bite replaces onion.
