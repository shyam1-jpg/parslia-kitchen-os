# Luchi

**Continent:** Assam — Assamese vegetarian table  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Small fried bread eaten with pitika and dal.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- 2 tbsp ghee
- Pinch of salt
- Water
- Oil for frying

## Method

1. Knead a soft dough. Rest 15 minutes. Roll small rounds.
2. Fry in a steel kadhai until they puff.
