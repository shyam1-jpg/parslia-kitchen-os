# Til Pitha

**Continent:** Assam — Assamese vegetarian table  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Sesame-jaggery stuffed rice pancake for Magh Bihu.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice flour
- ½ cup sesame, roasted and crushed
- ⅓ cup jaggery
- Water
- Ghee

## Method

1. Mix sesame and jaggery for filling.
2. Spread a thin rice-flour crepe on a hot iron tawa, add filling, roll.
3. Cook until the edges crisp.
