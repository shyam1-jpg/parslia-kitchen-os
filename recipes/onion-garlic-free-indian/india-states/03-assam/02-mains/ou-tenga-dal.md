# Ou Tenga Dal

**Continent:** Assam — Assamese vegetarian table  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Elephant-apple (or tomato) sour dal — Assam’s vegetarian tenga.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup masoor dal
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- 1 ou tenga slice or 2 tomatoes + 1 tsp lemon
- 1 tbsp mustard oil
- ½ tsp panch phoron
- Pinch of hing
- 2 dry chillies
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook dal with turmeric. Add ou tenga or tomato; simmer sour.
2. Temper mustard oil with panch phoron, hing and chilli. Pour over.
3. Lemon if you used tomato. No onion, no garlic — sour fruit does the work.
