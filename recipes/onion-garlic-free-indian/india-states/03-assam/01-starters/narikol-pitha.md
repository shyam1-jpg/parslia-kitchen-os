# Narikol Pitha

**Continent:** Assam — Assamese vegetarian table  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Assamese coconut rice cakes, savoury-sweet.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice flour
- 1 cup grated coconut
- 2 tbsp jaggery (optional for sweet) or salt for savoury
- Warm water
- Ghee

## Method

1. Mix rice flour with enough hot water to a soft dough.
2. Fill with coconut mixed with jaggery or salt.
3. Pat into discs. Cook on an iron tawa with ghee until speckled.
