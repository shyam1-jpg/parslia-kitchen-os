# Sandesh

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali vegetarian (niramish)  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fresh chenna sweet of Bengal.

## Ingredients

- 400 g chenna
- ⅓ cup sugar or date-palm jaggery
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Knead, cook in steel on low until it leaves the sides. Mould.
