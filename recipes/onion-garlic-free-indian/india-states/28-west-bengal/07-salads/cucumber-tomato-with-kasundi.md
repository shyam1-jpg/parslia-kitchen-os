# Cucumber Tomato with Kasundi

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali vegetarian (niramish)  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Mustard-oil salad. Kasundi instead of onion bite.

## Ingredients

- Cucumber
- Tomato
- ½ tsp kasundi or mustard oil
- Lemon
- Salt
- Green chilli

## Method

1. Toss. No peyaj.
