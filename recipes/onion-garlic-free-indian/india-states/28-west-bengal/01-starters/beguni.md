# Beguni

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali vegetarian (niramish)  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Brinjal slices in rice-flour batter — niramish if the batter has no onion.

## Ingredients

- 1 large brinjal, sliced
- ½ cup besan + 2 tbsp rice flour
- Turmeric
- Kalonji
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Dip, fry in a steel kadhai until crisp.
