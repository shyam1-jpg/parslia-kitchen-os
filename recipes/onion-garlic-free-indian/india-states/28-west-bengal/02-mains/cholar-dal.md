# Cholar Dal

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali vegetarian (niramish)  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Bengali chana dal with coconut and raisins. Niramish: no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup chana dal
- 2 tbsp coconut pieces
- 10 raisins
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 cardamom
- 1-inch cinnamon
- 1 tsp ginger
- Turmeric
- Ghee
- Sugar pinch
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook dal. Temper ghee with whole spices, ginger, coconut, raisins. Mix. Slightly sweet.
