# Aloo Posto

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali vegetarian (niramish)  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Potato in poppy-seed paste — the niramish emblem of Bengal.

## Ingredients

- 4 potatoes, cubed
- 4 tbsp posto (poppy), soaked and ground
- 2 green chillies
- ½ tsp kalonji
- Turmeric
- Mustard oil
- Salt
- Sugar pinch

## Method

1. Heat mustard oil, kalonji, chilli. Potatoes, turmeric, salt. Cover until almost done.
2. Add posto paste and a splash of water. Cook until the oil shines. No onion, no garlic.
