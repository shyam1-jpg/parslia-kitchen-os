# Luchi

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali vegetarian (niramish)  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Maida puri of Bengal.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- 2 tbsp ghee
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Roll small, fry until they balloon.
