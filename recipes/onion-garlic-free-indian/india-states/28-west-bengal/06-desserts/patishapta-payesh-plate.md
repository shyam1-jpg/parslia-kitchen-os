# Patishapta Payesh Plate

**Continent:** West Bengal — Bengali vegetarian (niramish)  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Rice crepes with coconut-jaggery, plus a bowl of payesh.

## Ingredients

- Rice-flour crepes
- Coconut-jaggery filling
- Payesh: milk, rice, sugar, bay, cardamom

## Method

1. Spread thin crepes on iron tawa, fill, roll. Serve with a ladle of steel-pot payesh.
