# Payasam

**Continent:** Puducherry — Tamil and Franco-Tamil vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Puducherry, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Moong or vermicelli
- Milk or coconut milk
- Jaggery
- Cardamom
- Cashews

## Method

1. Cook in steel.
