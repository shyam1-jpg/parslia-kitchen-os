# Appam or Idli

**Continent:** Puducherry — Tamil and Franco-Tamil vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 70 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Lace appam or idli to eat with stew.

## Ingredients

- Rice-coconut appam batter or idli batter

## Method

1. Cook appam in iron pan, or steam idli in steel.
