# Beans Coconut Poriyal

**Continent:** Puducherry — Tamil and Franco-Tamil vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Puducherry, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Beans
- Coconut
- Mustard
- Urad
- Curry leaves

## Method

1. Temper, cook, coconut.
