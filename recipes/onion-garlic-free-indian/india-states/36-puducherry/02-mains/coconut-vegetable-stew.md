# Coconut Vegetable Stew

**Continent:** Puducherry — Tamil and Franco-Tamil vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Franco-Tamil stew: coconut milk, pepper, ginger, curry leaves — no onion, no garlic.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups veg (potato, carrot, beans, cauliflower)
- 1½ cups thin coconut milk + ½ cup thick
- 1 tsp ginger
- 8 peppercorns
- Curry leaves
- Pinch of hing
- Coconut oil
- Salt

## Method

1. Simmer veg in thin coconut milk with ginger, pepper, salt.
2. Add thick coconut milk and curry-leaf hing tadka. Do not boil hard.
3. French-Tamil home stew without mirepoix alliums.
