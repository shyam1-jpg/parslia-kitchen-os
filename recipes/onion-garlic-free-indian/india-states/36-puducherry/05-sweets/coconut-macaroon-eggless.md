# Coconut Macaroon (eggless)

**Continent:** Puducherry — Tamil and Franco-Tamil vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or ceramic tray

## Why this dish

Pondicherry bakery coconut kisses, eggless: coconut, condensed milk, cardamom.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups coconut
- ½ cup condensed milk
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Mix, mound on a steel tray, bake 160°C until the edges brown.
