# Plain Roti / Rice

**Continent:** Jammu and Kashmir — Kashmiri Pandit and Dogra vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Roti for Jammu; rice for Kashmiri pandit thali.

## Ingredients

- Atta roti on iron tawa, or steamed rice

## Method

1. Cook roti on iron, or steam rice in steel.
