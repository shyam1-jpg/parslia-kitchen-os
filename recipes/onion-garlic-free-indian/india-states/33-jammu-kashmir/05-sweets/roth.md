# Roth

**Continent:** Jammu and Kashmir — Kashmiri Pandit and Dogra vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Kashmiri sweet bread for festivals.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- ½ cup sugar
- 4 tbsp ghee
- Fennel
- Cardamom
- Milk to bind

## Method

1. Knead, roll thick ovals, cook on iron tawa on low until cooked through. Optional ghee finish.
