# Nadru Chips

**Continent:** Jammu and Kashmir — Kashmiri Pandit and Dogra vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Lotus-stem chips of Kashmir.

## Ingredients

- 2 lotus stems, sliced thin
- Salt
- Chilli powder
- Oil

## Method

1. Soak slices in water. Drain, dry, fry in steel. Salt and chilli.
