# Kashmiri Dum Aloo

**Continent:** Jammu and Kashmir — Kashmiri Pandit and Dogra vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Kashmiri Pandit dum aloo: yogurt, fennel, ginger, Kashmiri chilli — no onion, no garlic.

## Ingredients

- 12 baby potatoes, peeled, fried
- 1 cup yogurt
- 1 tsp saunf (fennel) powder
- 1 tsp dry ginger
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 4 cloves
- 1-inch cinnamon
- Ghee
- Salt

## Method

1. Fry whole spices in ghee. Lower heat, add yogurt and powders, stir until oil shows.
2. Add potatoes and a little water. Cover, dum 15 minutes.
3. Pandit kitchens do not use onion or garlic. Do not add them.
