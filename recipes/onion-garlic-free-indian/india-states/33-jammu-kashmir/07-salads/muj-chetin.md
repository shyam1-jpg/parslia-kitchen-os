# Muj Chetin

**Continent:** Jammu and Kashmir — Kashmiri Pandit and Dogra vegetarian  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Kashmiri radish chutney-salad with yogurt. No onion.

## Ingredients

- 1 radish, grated
- ½ cup yogurt
- Dry mint
- Salt
- Chilli pinch

## Method

1. Mix. Rest 10 minutes.
