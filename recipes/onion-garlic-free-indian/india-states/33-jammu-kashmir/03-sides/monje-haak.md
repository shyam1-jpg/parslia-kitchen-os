# Monje Haak

**Continent:** Jammu and Kashmir — Kashmiri Pandit and Dogra vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Kashmiri kohlrabi greens in light ginger-asafoetida broth.

## Ingredients

- 1 kohlrabi with greens, sliced
- 1 tsp dry ginger
- Pinch of hing
- 2 dry red chillies
- Mustard oil 1 tbsp
- Salt

## Method

1. Heat oil, chilli, hing. Add haak, ginger, salt, water. Simmer until the stem is tender.
