# Phirni

**Continent:** Jammu and Kashmir — Kashmiri Pandit and Dogra vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Kashmiri ground-rice pudding with saffron.

## Ingredients

- Ground rice
- Milk
- Sugar
- Saffron
- Cardamom
- Almonds

## Method

1. Cook in steel. Set in bowls. Chill.
