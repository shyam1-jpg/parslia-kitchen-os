# Sattu Paratha

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh and Mithila  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Everyday stuffed paratha of Bihar.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- 1 cup sattu filling as for litti
- Ghee

## Method

1. Stuff atta rounds with sattu mix. Roll gently. Cook on iron tawa with ghee.
