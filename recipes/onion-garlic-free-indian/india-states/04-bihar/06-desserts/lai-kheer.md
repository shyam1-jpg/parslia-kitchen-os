# Lai Kheer

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh and Mithila  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Puffed-rice kheer for festivals.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups milk
- 1 cup lai (puffed rice)
- ⅓ cup sugar or jaggery
- Cardamom
- Raisins

## Method

1. Simmer milk in steel 10 minutes. Add lai, sweetener, cardamom. Cook 8 minutes.
