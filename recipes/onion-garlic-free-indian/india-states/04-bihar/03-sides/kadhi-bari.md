# Kadhi Bari

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh and Mithila  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Gram-flour dumplings in yogurt kadhi — Mithila favourite.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup besan (for bari)
- 1 cup yogurt
- 2 tbsp besan (for kadhi)
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- 2 green chillies
- Salt
- 1 tbsp ghee

## Method

1. Mix thick besan dough, shape small baris, fry or boil until cooked.
2. Whisk yogurt, 2 tbsp besan, turmeric and water. Simmer in steel, stirring.
3. Add baris. Tadka of ghee, cumin, hing, chilli.
