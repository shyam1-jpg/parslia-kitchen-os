# Litti with Tomato Baingan Chokha

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh and Mithila  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron grill or steel oven tray — never aluminium foil

## Why this dish

Fire-roasted mash served with litti. Skip onion; keep mustard oil and chilli.

## Ingredients

- 2 brinjals
- 2 tomatoes
- 1 boiled potato
- 1 tbsp mustard oil
- 2 green chillies
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Roast brinjal and tomato on flame or iron until collapsed. Peel.
2. Mash with potato, mustard oil, chilli and salt.
3. Coriander. Serve with litti. Do not add chopped onion.
