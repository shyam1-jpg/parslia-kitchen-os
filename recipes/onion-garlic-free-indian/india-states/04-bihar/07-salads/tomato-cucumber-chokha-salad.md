# Tomato Cucumber Chokha Salad

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh and Mithila  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Raw salad next to litti when you do not want another mash.

## Ingredients

- 2 tomatoes
- 1 cucumber
- Green chilli
- Lemon
- Salt
- Mustard oil ½ tsp

## Method

1. Dice and toss. No onion.
