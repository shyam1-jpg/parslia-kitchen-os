# Thekua

**Continent:** Bihar — Magadh and Mithila  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Chhath festival wheat-jaggery cookie.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- ¾ cup jaggery, melted
- 4 tbsp ghee
- ½ tsp cardamom
- Fennel ½ tsp
- Oil or ghee to fry

## Method

1. Mix atta, jaggery, ghee, cardamom and fennel to a stiff dough.
2. Press in a mould or pat discs. Fry on low-medium until brown.
