# Ariselu

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 40 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Festival rice-jaggery sweet of Andhra and Telangana.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice flour (from soaked ground rice, dried)
- 1 cup jaggery
- ½ cup water
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- Ghee or oil for frying
- 4 cardamom, crushed

## Method

1. Melt jaggery with water in steel; strain. Cook to a soft-ball syrup.
2. Mix in rice flour, sesame and cardamom to a soft dough.
3. Pat small discs. Fry in medium-hot ghee until deep golden.
