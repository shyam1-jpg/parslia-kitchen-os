# Paramannam

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Temple rice pudding cooked in milk and jaggery.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup rice
- 3 cups milk
- ½ cup jaggery
- 4 cardamom
- 10 cashews fried in ghee
- Saffron optional

## Method

1. Simmer rice in milk in a steel pot until very soft.
2. Add jaggery off a hard boil so milk does not split. Cardamom and cashews.
3. Never cook this in aluminium — milk and jaggery both react.
