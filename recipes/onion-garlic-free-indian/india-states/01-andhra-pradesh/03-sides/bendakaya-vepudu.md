# Bendakaya Vepudu

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Dry okra fry with sesame and chilli.

## Ingredients

- 400 g okra, sliced
- 2 tbsp oil
- ½ tsp mustard
- Pinch of hing
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds, roasted
- Salt

## Method

1. Dry the okra well. Heat oil, mustard, hing.
2. Add okra, turmeric and salt. Fry uncovered until the slime goes and edges brown.
3. Dust chilli, coriander and sesame.
