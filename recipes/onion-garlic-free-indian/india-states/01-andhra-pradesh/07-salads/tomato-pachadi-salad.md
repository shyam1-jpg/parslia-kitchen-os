# Tomato Pachadi Salad

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 5 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Fresh tomato-chilli pachadi served like a salad with pesarattu.

## Ingredients

- 3 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 green chilli
- 1 tsp oil
- ½ tsp mustard
- Pinch of hing
- 6 curry leaves
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Crush tomato, chilli and salt lightly.
2. Temper mustard, hing and curry leaves. Pour over. Coriander.
