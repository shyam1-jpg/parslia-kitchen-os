# Punugulu

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Leftover idli-dosa batter fritters from Andhra breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups leftover idli/dosa batter
- 2 tbsp sooji
- 1 tsp ginger-green chilli paste
- Pinch of hing
- 8 curry leaves, chopped
- Salt
- Oil for frying

## Method

1. Stir sooji, ginger-chilli, hing, curry leaves and salt into the batter.
2. Heat oil in a steel kadhai. Drop small batter blobs.
3. Fry on medium until golden. Serve with tomato-coconut chutney (no onion garlic).
