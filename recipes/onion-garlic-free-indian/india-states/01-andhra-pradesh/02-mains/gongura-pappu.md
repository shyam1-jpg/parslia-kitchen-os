# Gongura Pappu

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sorrel-leaf dal — Andhra’s signature sour main, already onion-garlic free in temple kitchens.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup toor dal
- 2 cups gongura (sorrel) leaves
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- 2 dry red chillies
- 8 curry leaves
- 2 green chillies
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook dal with turmeric until soft. Whisk.
2. In oil, splutter mustard, cumin, hing, chilli and curry leaves. Add gongura and green chilli; wilt 4 minutes.
3. Mix into dal with salt. Simmer 5 minutes. The leaves give the sourness — do not add onion.
