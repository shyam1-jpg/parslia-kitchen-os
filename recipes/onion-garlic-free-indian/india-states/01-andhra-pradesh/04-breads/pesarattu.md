# Pesarattu

**Continent:** Andhra Pradesh — Coastal Andhra and Rayalaseema  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Green-moong dosa from Andhra — no rice needed, no allium.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup whole green moong, soaked 4 hours
- 1-inch ginger
- 2 green chillies
- ½ tsp cumin
- Salt
- Oil for the tawa

## Method

1. Grind moong with ginger, chilli, cumin and salt to a slightly coarse batter.
2. Spread on a hot iron tawa like a dosa. Drizzle oil. Cook both sides.
3. Serve with ginger chutney (allium-free).
