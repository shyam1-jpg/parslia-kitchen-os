# Jeera Aloo

**Continent:** Chandigarh — Punjabi city kitchen  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Chandigarh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Potatoes
- Cumin
- Hing
- Turmeric
- Chilli
- Amchur
- Salt

## Method

1. Fry spices, toss potatoes.
