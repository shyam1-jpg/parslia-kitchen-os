# Kulcha (no onion)

**Continent:** Chandigarh — Punjabi city kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 40 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Potato kulcha without chopped onion in the filling.

## Ingredients

- Maida-yogurt dough
- Potato, ginger-chilli, amchur, coriander filling
- Butter

## Method

1. Stuff, cook on iron tawa with butter.
