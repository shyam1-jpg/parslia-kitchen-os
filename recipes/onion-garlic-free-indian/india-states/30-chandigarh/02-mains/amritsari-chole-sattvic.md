# Amritsari Chole (sattvic)

**Continent:** Chandigarh — Punjabi city kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Chickpea curry, tomato-ginger-anardana, no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups chickpeas, cooked
- 2 tomatoes + ginger, blended
- Chole masala (garlic-free)
- Amchur
- Tea bag for colour
- Cumin
- Hing
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook tomato-ginger with masala. Add chickpeas. Simmer 15 minutes. Ginger juliennes.
