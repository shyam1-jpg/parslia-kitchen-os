# Aloo Tikki

**Continent:** Chandigarh — Punjabi city kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

City tikki without onion in the mash.

## Ingredients

- 4 potatoes
- ½ cup peas
- Ginger-chilli
- Chaat masala
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Shape, pan-fry on iron. Chutneys without onion garlic.
