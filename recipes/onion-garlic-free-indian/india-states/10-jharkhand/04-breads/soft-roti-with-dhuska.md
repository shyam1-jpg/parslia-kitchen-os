# Soft Roti with Dhuska

**Continent:** Jharkhand — Nagpuri and tribal plateau kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Wheat roti to eat with rugra, plus leftover dhuska on the side.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- Water
- Salt
- Ghee

## Method

1. Knead, roll, cook on iron tawa. Ghee.
