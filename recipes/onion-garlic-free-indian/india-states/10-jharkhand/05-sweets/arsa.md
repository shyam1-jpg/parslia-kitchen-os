# Arsa

**Continent:** Jharkhand — Nagpuri and tribal plateau kitchen  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Rice-jaggery fried sweet of Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice flour
- 1 cup jaggery syrup
- Sesame
- Oil

## Method

1. Bind flour with warm jaggery syrup. Pat discs, sprinkle sesame, fry on medium.
