# Dhuska

**Continent:** Jharkhand — Nagpuri and tribal plateau kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Rice-chana dal fritters of Ranchi.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice, soaked
- ½ cup chana dal, soaked
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli
- Pinch of hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Grind to a thick batter. Fry small pancakes in a steel kadhai until crisp.
