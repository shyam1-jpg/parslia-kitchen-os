# Thekua Milk Soak

**Continent:** Jharkhand — Nagpuri and tribal plateau kitchen  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Broken thekua in warm sweet milk.

## Ingredients

- 6 thekua, crumbled
- 3 cups milk
- Sugar
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Warm milk with sugar and cardamom. Pour over thekua.
