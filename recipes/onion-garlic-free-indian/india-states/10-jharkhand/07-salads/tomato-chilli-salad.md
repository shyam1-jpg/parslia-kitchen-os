**Continent:** Jharkhand — Nagpuri and tribal plateau kitchen  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 6 min

Signature salad from Jharkhand, cooked without onion or garlic.

- 2 tomatoes
- Green chilli
- Lemon

- Coriander
