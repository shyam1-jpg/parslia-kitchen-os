# Palak and Bathua Sag

**Continent:** Jharkhand — Nagpuri and tribal plateau kitchen  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Mixed greens of the plateau.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups mixed greens
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- Pinch of hing
- Green chilli
- Salt
- Mustard oil

## Method

1. Temper, add greens, cover until wilted. Mash slightly.
