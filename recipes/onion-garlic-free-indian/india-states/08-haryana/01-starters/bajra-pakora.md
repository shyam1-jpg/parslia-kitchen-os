# Bajra Pakora

**Continent:** Haryana — Jat vegetarian farm kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Pearl-millet fritters of the Haryana winter.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup bajra flour
- ¼ cup atta
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli
- Pinch of hing
- Salt
- Water
- Oil

## Method

1. Mix a thick batter. Fry spoonfuls in a steel kadhai.
