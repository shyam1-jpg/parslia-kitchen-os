# Bajra Roti

**Continent:** Haryana — Jat vegetarian farm kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Pearl-millet roti eaten with kadhi and ghee.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups bajra flour
- Hot water
- Salt
- Ghee

## Method

1. Knead with hot water. Pat with wet hands. Cook on iron tawa. Smear ghee.
