# Chilled Lassi

**Continent:** Haryana — Jat vegetarian farm kitchen  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Sweet yogurt drink as dessert.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups yogurt
- ½ cup water or milk
- 3 tbsp sugar
- Cardamom
- Ice

## Method

1. Blend. Serve in steel glasses.
