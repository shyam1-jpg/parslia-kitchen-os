# Kadhi Pakora

**Continent:** Haryana — Jat vegetarian farm kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Haryanvi yogurt kadhi with besan pakoras — hing tadka, no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups yogurt
- 4 tbsp besan
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- Pakoras: ½ cup besan + ajwain + chilli + water, fried
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- 2 dry chillies
- 1 tbsp ghee
- Salt

## Method

1. Whisk yogurt, besan, turmeric and water. Simmer 15 minutes in steel, stirring.
2. Add pakoras. Tadka: ghee, cumin, hing, chilli.
