# Atta Pinni

**Continent:** Haryana — Jat vegetarian farm kitchen  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Wheat-ghee-jaggery laddoo of North Indian winters.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- ¾ cup ghee
- ¾ cup jaggery
- Cardamom
- Almonds

## Method

1. Roast atta in ghee until brown. Mix jaggery and cardamom. Shape pinnis.
