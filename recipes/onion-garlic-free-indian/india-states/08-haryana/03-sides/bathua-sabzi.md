# Bathua Sabzi

**Continent:** Haryana — Jat vegetarian farm kitchen  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Chenopodium greens of Haryana winters.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups bathua leaves
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- 1 tsp ginger
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- Salt
- 1 tbsp ghee

## Method

1. Temper cumin, hing, ginger. Add bathua, turmeric, salt. Cover 8 minutes. Mash slightly.
