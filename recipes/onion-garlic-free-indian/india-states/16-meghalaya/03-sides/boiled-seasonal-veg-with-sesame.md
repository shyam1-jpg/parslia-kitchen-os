# Boiled Seasonal Veg with Sesame

**Continent:** Meghalaya — Khasi and Garo vegetarian home food  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Meghalaya’s simple boiled vegetables with sesame smash.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups mixed veg
- 3 tbsp sesame, roasted
- Chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Boil veg. Pound sesame and chilli. Toss.
