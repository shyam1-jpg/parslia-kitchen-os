# Vegetarian Rice Pot

**Continent:** Meghalaya — Khasi and Garo vegetarian home food  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Jadoh is meat-rice; this is a Khasi-style veg rice cooked with ginger and local greens.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups rice
- 3 cups water
- 1 tsp ginger
- 2 cups chopped greens
- 1 tomato
- Salt
- 1 tbsp mustard oil
- Green chilli

## Method

1. Temper oil, ginger, chilli, tomato. Add rice, greens, water, salt. Cook like a wet pulao.
2. No pork fat, no onion, no garlic.
