# Pukhlein

**Continent:** Meghalaya — Khasi and Garo vegetarian home food  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Khasi rice-jaggery fritter, eaten as snack or sweet.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice flour
- ⅓ cup jaggery
- Water
- Oil

## Method

1. Mix a thick batter. Fry small discs in a steel kadhai.
