# Bamboo Shoot Fritters

**Continent:** Tripura — Tripuri vegetarian (no berma fish)  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Blanched bamboo shoot in besan.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups bamboo shoot, blanched
- ½ cup besan
- Turmeric
- Chilli
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Coat, fry in steel.
