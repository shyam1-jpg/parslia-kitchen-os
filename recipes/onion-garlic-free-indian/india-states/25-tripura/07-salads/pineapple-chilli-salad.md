# Pineapple Chilli Salad

**Continent:** Tripura — Tripuri vegetarian (no berma fish)  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Pineapple with chilli and salt — Tripura’s tropical bite instead of onion.

- 2 cups pineapple
- Green chilli
