# Payesh

**Continent:** Tripura — Tripuri vegetarian (no berma fish)  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Bengali-Tripuri rice pudding.

## Ingredients

- Rice
- Milk
- Sugar or jaggery
- Cardamom
- Bay leaf

## Method

1. Simmer in steel.
