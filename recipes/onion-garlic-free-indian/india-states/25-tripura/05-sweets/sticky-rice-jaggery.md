# Sticky Rice Jaggery

**Continent:** Tripura — Tripuri vegetarian (no berma fish)  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Tripura, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Sticky rice
- Jaggery
- Coconut

## Method

1. Steam rice, fold jaggery and coconut.
