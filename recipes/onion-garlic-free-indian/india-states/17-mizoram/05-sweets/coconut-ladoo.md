# Coconut Ladoo

**Continent:** Mizoram — Mizo vegetarian bai  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 10 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Island-and-hill festival sweet.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups coconut
- ¾ cup condensed milk or jaggery
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Cook in steel until thick. Shape.
