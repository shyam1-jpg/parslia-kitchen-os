# Boiled Veg Fritters

**Continent:** Mizoram — Mizo vegetarian bai  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Leftover boiled veg bound with besan.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups mashed boiled veg
- ½ cup besan
- Ginger-chilli
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Mix, fry small pakoras in steel.
