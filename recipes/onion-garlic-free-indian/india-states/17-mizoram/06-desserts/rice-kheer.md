# Rice Kheer

**Continent:** Mizoram — Mizo vegetarian bai  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Mizoram, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup rice
- 1 litre milk
- Sugar
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Simmer in steel.
