# Boiled Bamboo Shoot

**Continent:** Mizoram — Mizo vegetarian bai  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sliced bamboo shoot boiled and tossed with chilli.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups bamboo shoot, sliced and rinsed
- Green chilli
- Salt
- 1 tsp oil

## Method

1. Boil until tender. Drain. Toss with chilli, salt, oil.
