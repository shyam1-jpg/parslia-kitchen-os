# Bamboo Shoot Salad

**Continent:** Mizoram — Mizo vegetarian bai  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Rinsed tender bamboo with lemon and chilli.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup tender bamboo shoot, blanched
- Lemon
- Chilli
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Toss. No onion.
