# Steamed Rice

**Continent:** Mizoram — Mizo vegetarian bai  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Plain rice, the Mizo staple.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice
- Water
- Salt pinch

## Method

1. Steam or boil in steel.
