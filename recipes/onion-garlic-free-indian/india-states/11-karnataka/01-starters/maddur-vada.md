# Maddur Vada

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Rice-flour vada of Maddur. Skip onion; use curry leaf, coconut, chilli.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice flour
- 2 tbsp atta
- 2 tbsp sooji
- 2 tbsp grated coconut
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli
- 8 curry leaves
- Pinch of hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Mix a stiff dough with hot water. Flatten thin vadas. Fry crisp.
