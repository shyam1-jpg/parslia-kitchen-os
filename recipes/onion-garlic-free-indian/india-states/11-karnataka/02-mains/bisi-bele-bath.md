# Bisi Bele Bath

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Udupi-Mysuru hot lentil-rice. Use bisi bele bath powder without garlic.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup rice
- ⅓ cup toor dal
- 2 cups mixed veg (beans, carrot, peas, capsicum)
- 2 tbsp bisi bele bath powder (garlic-free)
- 2 tbsp tamarind
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp ghee
- Mustard, curry leaves, hing, cashews
- Salt
- Jaggery pinch

## Method

1. Cook rice and dal soft. Cook veg separately.
2. Simmer tamarind, powder, turmeric, salt, jaggery. Mix rice, dal, veg. Loose, hot, ghee tadka.
3. Udupi temples never use onion or garlic in this dish.
