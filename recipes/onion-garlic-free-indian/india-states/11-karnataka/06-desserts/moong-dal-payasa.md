# Moong Dal Payasa

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Karnataka dessert of fried moong in coconut milk.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup moong dal, dry roasted
- 2 cups coconut milk
- ⅓ cup jaggery
- Cardamom
- Cashews in ghee

## Method

1. Cook moong soft. Add jaggery, coconut milk, cardamom. Do not boil hard.
