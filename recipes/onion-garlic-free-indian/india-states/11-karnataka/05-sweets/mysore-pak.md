# Mysore Pak

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ghee-besan-sugar fudge of Mysuru.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup besan
- 1 cup ghee
- 1½ cups sugar
- ½ cup water

## Method

1. Make syrup. Add roasted-with-ghee besan. Cook until it pores. Pour into a greased steel tray.
