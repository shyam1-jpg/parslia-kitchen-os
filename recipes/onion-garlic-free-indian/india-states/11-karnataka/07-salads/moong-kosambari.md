# Moong Kosambari

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 2 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Festival salad of Karnataka — already sattvic.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup split moong, soaked
- Cucumber
- Coconut
- Lemon
- Mustard-hing-curry-leaf tadka
- Salt

## Method

1. Mix, temper, toss.
