# Beans Palya

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Dry beans coconut stir-fry.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups chopped beans
- 2 tbsp coconut
- Mustard, urad dal, curry leaves, hing
- Turmeric
- Salt
- Coconut oil

## Method

1. Temper, add beans, turmeric, salt, splash of water. Cover 8 minutes. Coconut.
