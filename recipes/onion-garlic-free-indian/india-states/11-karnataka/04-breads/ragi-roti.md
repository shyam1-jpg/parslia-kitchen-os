# Ragi Roti

**Continent:** Karnataka — Udupi, Mysuru and North Karnataka  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Finger-millet roti of South Karnataka.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups ragi flour
- Hot water
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli
- Coriander
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Knead, pat on banana leaf or wet cloth onto iron tawa. Cook both sides.
