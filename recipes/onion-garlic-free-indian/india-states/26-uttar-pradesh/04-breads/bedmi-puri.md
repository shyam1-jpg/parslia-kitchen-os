# Bedmi Puri

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Urad-dal stuffed puri of UP breakfasts.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- ½ cup urad dal, soaked and coarsely ground with ginger, chilli, fennel, hing
- Oil

## Method

1. Knead dal into atta or stuff. Roll thick. Fry until puffed and brown.
