# Agra Petha

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ash-gourd candy of Agra. Already no allium.

## Ingredients

- 500 g ash gourd, peeled cubed
- Lime water to soak
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup water
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Soak cubes in lime water 15 minutes, rinse well. Boil until translucent.
2. Cook in sugar syrup in steel until the syrup coats and candies.
