# Bhindi ki Sabzi

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Dry okra with amchur.

## Ingredients

- 400 g bhindi
- Oil
- Cumin
- Hing
- Turmeric
- Coriander
- Chilli
- Amchur
- Salt

## Method

1. Fry uncovered until no slime. Spices at the end.
