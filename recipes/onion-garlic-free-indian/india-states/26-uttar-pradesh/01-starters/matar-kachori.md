# Matar Kachori

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Lucknow-Mathura pea kachori. Sattvic filling: peas, ginger, cumin, amchur — no onion.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- Filling: 1 cup crushed peas + ginger + cumin + amchur + chilli + salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Stuff, fry on medium until flaky. Serve with aloo tamatar.
