# Rabri

**Continent:** Uttar Pradesh — Awadhi, Braj and Purvanchal vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Malai-rich reduced milk of Awadh.

## Ingredients

- 1 litre milk
- Sugar
- Saffron
- Pistachios

## Method

1. Reduce in a heavy steel pot, scraping cream back in.
