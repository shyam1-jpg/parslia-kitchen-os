# Tingmo

**Continent:** Ladakh — Ladakhi Buddhist vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 80 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Tibetan-Ladakhi steamed folded bread.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- Yeast ½ tsp
- Sugar 1 tsp
- Salt
- Water

## Method

1. Ferment 1 hour. Roll, brush oil, fold, cut, steam 12 minutes in steel.
