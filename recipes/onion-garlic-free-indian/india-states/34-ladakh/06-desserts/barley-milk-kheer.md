# Barley Milk Kheer

**Continent:** Ladakh — Ladakhi Buddhist vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Tsampa or cracked barley in milk.

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup cracked barley or tsampa
- 3 cups milk
- Sugar
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Simmer in steel.
