# Dried Apricot Compote

**Continent:** Ladakh — Ladakhi Buddhist vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ladakh grows apricots. Stew them with a little sugar.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dried apricots
- Water
- 2 tbsp sugar
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Stew in steel until syrupy.
