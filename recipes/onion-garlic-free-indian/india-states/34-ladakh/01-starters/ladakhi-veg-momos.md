# Ladakhi Veg Momos

**Continent:** Ladakh — Ladakhi Buddhist vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Cabbage-ginger momos of Leh.

## Ingredients

- Maida
- Cabbage, carrot, ginger filling

## Method

1. Steam in steel. Chilli-tomato dip without garlic.
