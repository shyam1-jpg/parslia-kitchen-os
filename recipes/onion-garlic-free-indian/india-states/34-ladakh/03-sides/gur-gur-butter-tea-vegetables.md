# Gur-Gur Butter Tea Vegetables

**Continent:** Ladakh — Ladakhi Buddhist vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Plain boiled root veg with salt and a knob of butter — high-altitude side.

## Ingredients

- Mixed root veg
- Salt
- Butter

## Method

1. Boil until soft. Butter and salt. No onion.
