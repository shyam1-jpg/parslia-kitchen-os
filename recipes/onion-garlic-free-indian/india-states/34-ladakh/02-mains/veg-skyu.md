# Veg Skyu

**Continent:** Ladakh — Ladakhi Buddhist vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Thumb-pressed wheat pasta stew. Vegetable skyu: root veg, ginger — no meat, no allium.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups atta dough, pinched into skyu shapes
- 2 potatoes
- 1 turnip or carrot
- 1 tsp ginger
- Green chilli
- Salt
- 1 tbsp oil

## Method

1. Simmer veg and ginger in water. Add skyu pieces. Cook until the pasta is soft and the stew is thick.
