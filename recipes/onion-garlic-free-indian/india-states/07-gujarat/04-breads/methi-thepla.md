# Methi Thepla

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Travel roti of Gujarat.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- 1 cup methi
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli
- 2 tbsp yogurt
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Knead, roll thin, cook on iron tawa with oil until speckled.
