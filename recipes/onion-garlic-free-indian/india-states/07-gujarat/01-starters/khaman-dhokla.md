# Khaman Dhokla

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Gujarati steamed gram cake — already onion-garlic free.

## Ingredients

- 1½ cups besan
- 2 tbsp sooji
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli paste
- 1 tsp sugar
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp lemon
- 1 tsp Eno
- Mustard-sesame-curry-leaf temper
- Coconut
- Coriander

## Method

1. Mix batter, add Eno, steam 12 minutes in steel.
2. Temper and pour sweet-sour water. Coconut and coriander.
