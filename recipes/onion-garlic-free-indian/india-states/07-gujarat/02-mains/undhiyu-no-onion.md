# Undhiyu (no onion)

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Surti winter mixed veg. Jain/sattvic undhiyu skips onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups mixed: surti papdi, candied beans, brinjal, banana, yam, peas
- Muthiya: ½ cup besan + methi + ginger-chilli + ajwain, steamed or fried
- 1 tsp ajwain
- Pinch of hing
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp coriander-cumin powder
- ½ cup grated coconut
- 2 tbsp oil
- Salt
- Sugar pinch
- Lemon

## Method

1. Temper ajwain and hing. Add vegetables, spices, salt and a splash of water. Cover 20 minutes.
2. Tuck in muthiya. Coconut and lemon at the end.
3. Do not add onion — coconut, methi and ajwain are the flavour.
