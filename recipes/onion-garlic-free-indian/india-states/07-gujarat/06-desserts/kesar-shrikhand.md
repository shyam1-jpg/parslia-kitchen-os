# Kesar Shrikhand

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Hung-yogurt dessert of Gujarati feasts.

## Ingredients

- 500 g hung yogurt
- ⅓ cup powdered sugar
- Saffron
- Cardamom
- Pistachios

## Method

1. Whisk, chill 2 hours.
