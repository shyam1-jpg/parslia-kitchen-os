# Mohanthal

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Gram-flour ghee fudge.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups besan
- 1 cup ghee
- 1¼ cups sugar
- ⅓ cup water
- Cardamom
- Pistachios

## Method

1. Roast besan in ghee until nutty. Add one-thread syrup, cardamom. Set in a steel tray.
