# Ringan no Olo

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron grill or steel oven tray — never aluminium foil

## Why this dish

Charred brinjal mash with green chilli — Kathiawad, no onion.

## Ingredients

- 2 large brinjals
- 2 green chillies
- 1 tbsp oil
- Salt
- Coriander
- Lemon

## Method

1. Roast brinjal on flame. Peel, mash with chilli, oil, salt, lemon, coriander.
