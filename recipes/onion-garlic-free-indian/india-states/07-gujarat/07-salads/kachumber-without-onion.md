# Kachumber without Onion

**Continent:** Gujarat — Gujarati and Kathiawadi, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Cucumber, tomato, raw mango, lemon, roasted cumin.

## Ingredients

- 1 cucumber
- 2 tomatoes
- ½ raw mango
- Lemon
- Cumin
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Dice and toss. Mango replaces onion bite.
