# Bal Mithai

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Roasted-khoya sweet coated in sugar balls — Almora.

## Ingredients

- 400 g khoya, roasted dark
- ½ cup sugar
- Sugar balls (khurchan) to roll

## Method

1. Cook khoya and sugar in steel until it holds shape. Cut squares, roll in sugar balls.
