# Kafuli

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Spinach-fenugreek mash thickened with rice paste — Garhwal.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups spinach
- 1 cup methi
- 2 tbsp rice paste or besan
- 1 tsp ginger
- Green chilli
- Cumin
- Hing
- Salt
- Ghee

## Method

1. Cook greens with ginger and chilli. Mash. Add rice paste. Simmer thick. Cumin-hing ghee tadka.
