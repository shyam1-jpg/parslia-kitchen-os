# Aloo ke Gutke

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Kumaoni spiced potatoes with jakhiya seeds. Already no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 4 boiled potatoes, cubed
- 2 tbsp mustard oil
- 1 tsp jakhiya or mustard seeds
- Pinch of hing
- Red chilli
- Turmeric
- Coriander powder
- Salt
- Lemon

## Method

1. Heat mustard oil, jakhiya, hing, chilli. Add potatoes and spices. Crisp the edges. Lemon.
