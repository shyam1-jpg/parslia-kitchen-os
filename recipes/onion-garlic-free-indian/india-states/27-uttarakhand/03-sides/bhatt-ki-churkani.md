# Bhatt ki Churkani

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Black-soybean curry of Kumaon.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup bhatt (black soy), soaked
- Rice paste 2 tbsp
- Cumin
- Hing
- Ginger
- Chilli
- Salt
- Ghee

## Method

1. Cook bhatt until very soft. Add rice paste and spices. Tadka.
