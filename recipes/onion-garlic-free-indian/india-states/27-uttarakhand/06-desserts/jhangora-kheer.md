# Jhangora Kheer

**Continent:** Uttarakhand — Kumaoni and Garhwali  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Barnyard-millet kheer of Uttarakhand fasts.

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup jhangora (barnyard millet), soaked
- 3 cups milk
- Sugar
- Cardamom
- Nuts

## Method

1. Simmer in steel until creamy.
