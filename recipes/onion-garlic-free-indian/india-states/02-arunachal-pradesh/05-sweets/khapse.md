# Khapse

**Continent:** Arunachal Pradesh — Monpa, Nyishi and Adi home kitchens  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Festival fried pastry shared across the Buddhist Himalaya.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 3 tbsp ghee
- Warm water
- Oil for frying

## Method

1. Rub ghee into flour and sugar. Bind a firm dough. Rest 15 minutes.
2. Roll, cut strips or discs. Fry in a steel kadhai until golden.
