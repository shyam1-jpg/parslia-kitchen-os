# Steamed Sticky Rice

**Continent:** Arunachal Pradesh — Monpa, Nyishi and Adi home kitchens  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

The staple bread-substitute of the hills.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sticky or short-grain rice, soaked 2 hours
- Pinch of salt

## Method

1. Drain rice. Steam in a steel steamer 20–25 minutes until translucent.
2. Serve with thukpa and greens. No aluminium steamer.
