# Steamed Vegetable Momos

**Continent:** Arunachal Pradesh — Monpa, Nyishi and Adi home kitchens  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Arunachali and Tibetan-border momos with cabbage-ginger filling, no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- 2 cups shredded cabbage
- 1 carrot, grated
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1 green chilli, minced
- Salt
- 1 tsp oil

## Method

1. Knead a firm dough. Rest 20 minutes.
2. Salt the cabbage, squeeze dry, mix carrot, ginger, chilli and oil.
3. Fill, pleat, steam 12 minutes in a steel steamer. Serve with tomato-chilli dip (no garlic).
