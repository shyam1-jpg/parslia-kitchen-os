# Vegetable Thukpa

**Continent:** Arunachal Pradesh — Monpa, Nyishi and Adi home kitchens  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Himalayan noodle soup. Ginger and pepper replace garlic.

## Ingredients

- 150 g wheat noodles
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp ginger
- Pinch of hing
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 2 cups mixed veg (cabbage, carrot, beans)
- 4 cups water or light veg stock
- 1 tsp soy sauce optional (garlic-free)
- Salt
- Pepper
- Coriander

## Method

1. Heat oil, ginger, hing, tomato. Add vegetables, water and salt. Simmer 10 minutes.
2. Add noodles; cook until just done.
3. Pepper and coriander. Do not add garlic paste.
