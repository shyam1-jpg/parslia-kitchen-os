# Cucumber Green-Chilli Salad

**Continent:** Arunachal Pradesh — Monpa, Nyishi and Adi home kitchens  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Cool crunch next to momos.

## Ingredients

- 2 cucumbers
- 1 green chilli
- Lemon
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Slice cucumber. Toss with chilli, lemon, salt and coriander. No onion.
