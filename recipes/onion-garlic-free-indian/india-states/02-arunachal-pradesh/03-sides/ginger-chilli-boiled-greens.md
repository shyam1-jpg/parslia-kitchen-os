# Ginger-Chilli Boiled Greens

**Continent:** Arunachal Pradesh — Monpa, Nyishi and Adi home kitchens  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Local lai/mustard greens boiled and tossed — Arunachal’s everyday sabzi.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups mustard or spinach greens
- 1 tsp ginger, julienned
- 2 green chillies, slit
- 1 tsp mustard oil
- Salt

## Method

1. Boil greens in salted water 5 minutes. Drain.
2. Toss with ginger, chilli and mustard oil in a steel pan 2 minutes.
