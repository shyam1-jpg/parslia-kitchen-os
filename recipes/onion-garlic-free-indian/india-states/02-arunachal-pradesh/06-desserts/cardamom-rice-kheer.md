# Cardamom Rice Kheer

**Continent:** Arunachal Pradesh — Monpa, Nyishi and Adi home kitchens  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Plain hill kheer after a chilli-heavy meal.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup rice
- 1 litre milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 4 cardamom
- Raisins

## Method

1. Simmer rice in milk in steel 35 minutes. Sugar, cardamom, raisins.
