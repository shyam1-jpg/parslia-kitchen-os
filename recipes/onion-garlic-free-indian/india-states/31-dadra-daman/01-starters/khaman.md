# Khaman

**Continent:** Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu — Gujarati and Indo-Portuguese vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Gujarati khaman of the UT.

- Besan batter
- Eno
- Mustard-sesame temper
