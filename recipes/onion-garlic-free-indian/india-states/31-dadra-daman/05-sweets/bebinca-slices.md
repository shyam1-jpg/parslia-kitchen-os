# Bebinca Slices

**Continent:** Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu — Gujarati and Indo-Portuguese vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 50 min  
**Cookware:** Steel baking dish

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Dadra Daman, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Coconut milk
- Maida
- Jaggery
- Ghee

## Method

1. Layer-bake in steel.
