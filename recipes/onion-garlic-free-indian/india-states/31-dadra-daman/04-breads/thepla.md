# Thepla

**Continent:** Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu — Gujarati and Indo-Portuguese vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Signature bread from Dadra Daman, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Atta
- Methi
- Turmeric
- Yogurt
- Ginger-chilli

## Method

1. Roll thin, cook on iron.
