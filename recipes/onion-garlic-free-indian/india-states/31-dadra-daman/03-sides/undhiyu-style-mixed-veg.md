# Undhiyu-Style Mixed Veg

**Continent:** Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu — Gujarati and Indo-Portuguese vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Dadra Daman, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Papdi, brinjal, potato, banana
- Coconut-coriander stuffing
- Ajwain
- Hing

## Method

1. Cook covered until soft.

## Notes

Gujarati winter veg of the UT.
