# Coconut Caldin (veg)

**Continent:** Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu — Gujarati and Indo-Portuguese vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Indo-Portuguese coconut curry, vegetarian, no garlic. Kokum and pepper.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups veg (pumpkin, beans, cauliflower)
- 1½ cups coconut milk
- Turmeric
- Pepper
- Cumin
- Kokum
- Curry leaves
- Hing
- Salt
- Coconut oil

## Method

1. Temper, add veg and spices, coconut milk, kokum. Simmer until the veg is coated.
