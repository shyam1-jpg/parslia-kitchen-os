# Rabri

**Continent:** Madhya Pradesh — Malwa, Bundelkhand and Mahakoshal  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Reduced sweet milk of North and Central India.

## Ingredients

- 1 litre full-fat milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- Saffron
- Cardamom
- Pistachios

## Method

1. Reduce milk in a heavy steel pot, scraping malai back in, until thick. Sugar, saffron.
