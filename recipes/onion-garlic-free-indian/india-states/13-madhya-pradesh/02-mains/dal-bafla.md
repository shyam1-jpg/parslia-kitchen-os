# Dal Bafla

**Continent:** Madhya Pradesh — Malwa, Bundelkhand and Mahakoshal  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Malwa dal with baked wheat bafla. Dal is toor with hing tadka.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup toor dal
- Turmeric
- Tomato 1
- Ghee tadka: cumin, hing, chilli, coriander powder
- Bafla: 2 cups atta + 2 tbsp ghee + ajwain + salt, boiled then baked/roasted on iron

## Method

1. Cook dal, whisk, tomato, tadka.
2. Boil bafla discs 15 minutes, then roast on iron until cracked. Dunk in ghee. Serve with dal.
