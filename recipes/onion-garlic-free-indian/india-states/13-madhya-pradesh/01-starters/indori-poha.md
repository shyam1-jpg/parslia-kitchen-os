# Indori Poha

**Continent:** Madhya Pradesh — Malwa, Bundelkhand and Mahakoshal  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 10 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Indore’s flattened-rice breakfast. Skip onion; use sev, lemon, pomegranate.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups thick poha, washed
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp mustard
- Pinch of hing
- Curry leaves
- Turmeric
- Green chilli
- Salt
- Sugar pinch
- Lemon
- Sev
- Pomegranate

## Method

1. Temper, add poha, turmeric, salt, sugar. Steam 2 minutes. Lemon, sev, pomegranate — no onion.
