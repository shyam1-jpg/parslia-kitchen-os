# Bhutte ka Kees

**Continent:** Madhya Pradesh — Malwa, Bundelkhand and Mahakoshal  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Grated corn cooked in milk — Indore monsoon dish.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups grated corn
- ½ cup milk
- 1 tbsp ghee
- Cumin
- Hing
- Green chilli
- Ginger
- Salt
- Lemon
- Coconut

## Method

1. Temper ghee, cumin, hing, chilli, ginger. Add corn, milk, salt. Cook 10 minutes. Lemon, coconut.
