# Pomegranate Cucumber Salad

**Continent:** Madhya Pradesh — Malwa, Bundelkhand and Mahakoshal  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Signature salad from Madhya Pradesh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 1 cucumber
- ½ cup pomegranate
- Lemon
- Black salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Toss. No onion.
