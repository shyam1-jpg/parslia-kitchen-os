# Bafla

**Continent:** Madhya Pradesh — Malwa, Bundelkhand and Mahakoshal  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

The bread of dal bafla, eaten dunked in ghee.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- 2 tbsp ghee
- Ajwain
- Salt
- Water

## Method

1. Knead, shape discs, boil, then roast on iron tawa until brown cracks.
