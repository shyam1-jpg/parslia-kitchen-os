# Mawa Bati

**Continent:** Madhya Pradesh — Malwa, Bundelkhand and Mahakoshal  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Stuffed fried khoya balls in sugar syrup — Malwa.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup khoya
- 2 tbsp maida
- Chopped nuts
- Cardamom
- Sugar syrup 1 cup sugar + ½ cup water

## Method

1. Stuff khoya-maida dough with nuts. Fry on low. Soak in warm syrup.
