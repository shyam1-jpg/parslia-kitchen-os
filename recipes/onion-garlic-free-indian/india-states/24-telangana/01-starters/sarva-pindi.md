# Sarva Pindi

**Continent:** Telangana — Telangana and Hyderabadi vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Sesame-chilli rice pancake of Telangana, pressed on the tawa.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice flour
- 2 tbsp sesame
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli
- Curry leaves
- Pinch of hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Make a stiff dough. Press onto a hot iron tawa with a hole in the centre. Cook both sides.
