# Beans Coconut Fry

**Continent:** Telangana — Telangana and Hyderabadi vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature side from Telangana, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Beans
- Coconut
- Mustard
- Chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Temper and fry.

## Notes

Dry side for sarva pindi.
