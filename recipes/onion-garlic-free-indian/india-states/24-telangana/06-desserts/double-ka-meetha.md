# Double ka Meetha

**Continent:** Telangana — Telangana and Hyderabadi vegetarian  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fried bread in saffron milk — Hyderabadi dessert.

## Ingredients

- 6 slices bread, fried in ghee
- 2 cups milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- Saffron
- Nuts

## Method

1. Reduce milk with sugar and saffron in steel. Soak fried bread. Nuts.
