# Tomato Cashew Kurma

**Continent:** Telangana — Telangana and Hyderabadi vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Hyderabadi-style veg kurma without onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 3 tomatoes + 12 cashews, blended
- 2 cups mixed veg and paneer
- Ginger
- Hing
- Coriander powder
- Chilli
- Garam masala
- Cream 2 tbsp
- Ghee
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook paste in ghee with spices until fat separates. Add veg, water, cream.
