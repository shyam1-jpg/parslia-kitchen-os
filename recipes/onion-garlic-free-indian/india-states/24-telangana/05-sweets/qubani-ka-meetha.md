# Qubani ka Meetha

**Continent:** Telangana — Telangana and Hyderabadi vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Hyderabadi stewed apricots. Already no allium.

## Ingredients

- 200 g dried apricots, soaked
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 1 cup soak-water
- Saffron
- Almonds
- Cream to serve

## Method

1. Stew apricots with sugar until jammy. Saffron, almonds, a ribbon of cream.
