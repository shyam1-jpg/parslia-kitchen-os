# Sweet Madra Kheer

**Continent:** Himachal Pradesh — Pahari dham (temple feast)  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Milk-rice dessert after dham.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup rice
- 1 litre milk
- Sugar
- Cardamom
- Saffron

## Method

1. Simmer in steel until thick.
