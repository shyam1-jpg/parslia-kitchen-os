# Siddu

**Continent:** Himachal Pradesh — Pahari dham (temple feast)  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 80 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

The steamed bread of Kullu and Shimla, eaten with ghee.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- ½ tsp yeast
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt
- Walnut-poppy filling as above

## Method

1. Same method as the starter, made larger for the meal.
