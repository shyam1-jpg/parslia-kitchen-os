# Mittha

**Continent:** Himachal Pradesh — Pahari dham (temple feast)  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sweet rice of Himachali dham with raisins and dry fruit.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup basmati
- 2 tbsp ghee
- ⅓ cup sugar
- Raisins
- Cashews
- Cardamom
- Saffron

## Method

1. Fry nuts in ghee. Add soaked rice, water 2 cups, cook. Sugar, saffron, cardamom.
