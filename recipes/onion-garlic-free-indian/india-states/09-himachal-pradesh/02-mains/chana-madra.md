# Chana Madra

**Continent:** Himachal Pradesh — Pahari dham (temple feast)  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Yogurt-chickpea curry of Himachali dham — traditionally no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked chickpeas
- 1½ cups yogurt, whisked
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 4 cloves
- 1-inch cinnamon
- 4 cardamom
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp garam masala
- Pinch of hing
- Salt

## Method

1. Heat ghee, whole spices, hing. Lower heat, stir in yogurt and dry spices until it does not split.
2. Add chickpeas and a little water. Simmer 15 minutes until the gravy is creamy.
3. This is temple food — do not add onion or garlic.
