# Sepu Vadi

**Continent:** Himachal Pradesh — Pahari dham (temple feast)  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Urad-dal dumplings in spinach-yogurt gravy, dham side.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup urad dal, soaked, ground, steamed as cakes, cubed
- 2 cups spinach puree
- ½ cup yogurt
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- Ginger
- Salt

## Method

1. Fry vadi cubes lightly. Make a gravy of spinach, yogurt, cumin, hing, ginger.
2. Simmer vadis 8 minutes.
