# Coconut Barfi

**Continent:** Lakshadweep — Island coconut and rice kitchen  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Lakshadweep, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Coconut
- Sugar
- Cardamom
- Ghee

## Method

1. Cook, set in steel tray, cut.
