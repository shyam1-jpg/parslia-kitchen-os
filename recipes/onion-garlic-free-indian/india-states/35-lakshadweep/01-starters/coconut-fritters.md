# Coconut Fritters

**Continent:** Lakshadweep — Island coconut and rice kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fresh coconut and rice-flour fritters.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup coconut
- ½ cup rice flour
- Green chilli
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Mix, fry in steel.
