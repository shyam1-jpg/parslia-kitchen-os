# Coconut Milk Vegetable Curry

**Continent:** Lakshadweep — Island coconut and rice kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Atoll curry: coconut milk, curry leaves, kokum. Skip fish; skip alliums.

## Ingredients

- Tropical veg
- Coconut milk
- Turmeric
- Curry leaves
- Mustard
- Hing
- Kokum
- Coconut oil
- Salt

## Method

1. Temper, simmer veg in coconut milk. Kokum for sour.
