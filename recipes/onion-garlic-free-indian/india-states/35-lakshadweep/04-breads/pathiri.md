# Pathiri

**Continent:** Lakshadweep — Island coconut and rice kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Soft rice-flour flatbread of the islands and Malabar.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice flour
- Hot water
- Salt

## Method

1. Scald flour, knead, roll thin, cook on iron tawa without browning too hard.
