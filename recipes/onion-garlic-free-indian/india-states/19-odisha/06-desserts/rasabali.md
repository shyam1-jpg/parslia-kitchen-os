# Rasabali

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Flattened chenna patties in thickened sweet milk.

## Ingredients

- 300 g chenna
- 1 tbsp sooji
- 3 cups milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Shape patties, pan-sear. Reduce milk with sugar in steel. Soak patties.
