# Santula

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Light Odia mixed vegetable, boiled then tossed.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups mixed veg
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- Green chilli
- Salt
- 1 tbsp oil

## Method

1. Boil veg with turmeric and salt. Drain extra water.
2. Temper cumin, hing, chilli. Toss.
