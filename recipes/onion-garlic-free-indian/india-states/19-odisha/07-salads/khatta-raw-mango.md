# Khatta (raw mango)

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 10 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Tangy raw-mango relish-salad of Odia thalis.

## Ingredients

- 2 raw mangoes, chopped
- ½ tsp mustard seeds
- Pinch of hing
- Turmeric
- Chilli
- Salt
- Sugar pinch
- Oil

## Method

1. Temper, add mango and spices. Cook 5 minutes until glossy but still tart.
