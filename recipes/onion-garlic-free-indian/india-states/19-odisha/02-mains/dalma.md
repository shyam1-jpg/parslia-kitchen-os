# Dalma

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

The Jagannath-kitchen dal with vegetables. Onion and garlic are forbidden in the temple.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup toor dal
- 2 cups mixed veg (raw banana, papaya, brinjal, pumpkin, beans)
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp cumin, roasted and crushed
- 1 tsp ginger
- Ghee
- Dry chilli
- Salt
- Grated coconut optional

## Method

1. Cook dal and veg together with turmeric until soft.
2. Add roasted cumin, ginger, salt, ghee tadka with dry chilli.
3. This is Puri temple food — never onion, never garlic, never aluminium.
