# Chhena Poda

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 50 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or earthen baking dish — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Caramelised chenna cake of Odisha.

## Ingredients

- 400 g fresh chenna (drained paneer)
- ½ cup sugar
- 2 tbsp sooji
- Cardamom
- Ghee to grease

## Method

1. Knead chenna, sugar, sooji, cardamom. Press into a greased steel tin.
2. Bake 180°C 40–50 minutes until the top is burnt-caramel. Cool, slice.
