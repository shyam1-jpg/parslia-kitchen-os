# Luchi

**Continent:** Odisha — Odia temple and home kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Odia-Bengali fried bread for dalma.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- 2 tbsp ghee
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Knead, roll small, fry in steel until they puff.
