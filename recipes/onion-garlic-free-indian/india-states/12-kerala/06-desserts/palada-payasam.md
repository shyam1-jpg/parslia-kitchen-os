# Palada Payasam

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya (feast) kitchen  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Wedding payasam of cooked ada in milk.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup rice ada
- 1 litre milk
- ½ cup sugar
- Ghee
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Cook ada. Reduce milk in steel with sugar until pale pink-beige. Mix ada, ghee, cardamom.
