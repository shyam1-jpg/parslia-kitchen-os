# Cucumber Coconut Salad

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya (feast) kitchen  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Sadya-style kichadi without onion.

## Ingredients

- 1 cucumber
- 2 tbsp coconut
- Green chilli
- Yogurt ½ cup
- Mustard tadka
- Salt

## Method

1. Mix, temper, serve at once.
