# Avial

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya (feast) kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sadya mixed veg in coconut-yogurt. The definition of onion-garlic-free Kerala food.

## Ingredients

- 5 cups mixed veg (yam, ash gourd, carrot, beans, drumstick, raw banana)
- 1 cup coconut ground with cumin and green chilli
- ½ cup yogurt
- Curry leaves
- Coconut oil
- Salt
- Turmeric

## Method

1. Cook vegetables with turmeric and salt until just done, little water.
2. Add coconut paste. Fold yogurt off the boil. Coconut oil and curry leaves.
3. Never add onion or garlic to avial.
