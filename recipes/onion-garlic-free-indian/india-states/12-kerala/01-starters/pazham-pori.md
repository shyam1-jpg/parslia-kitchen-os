# Pazham Pori

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya (feast) kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ripe-plantain fritters of Kerala tea time.

## Ingredients

- 2 ripe nendran or plantains
- 1 cup maida
- 2 tbsp rice flour
- 1 tsp sugar
- Pinch of turmeric
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Batter the slices. Fry in a steel kadhai until golden.
