# Appam

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya (feast) kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 70 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Lacy coconut rice pancake. Use a cast-iron appam kadai, not aluminium.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice, soaked
- ½ cup coconut
- ½ tsp yeast or toddy
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt

## Method

1. Grind, ferment. Pour into a hot iron appam pan, swirl. Cover 1 minute. Soft centre, crisp lace.
