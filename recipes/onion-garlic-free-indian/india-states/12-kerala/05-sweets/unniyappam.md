# Unniyappam

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya (feast) kitchen  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron unniyappam pan — not aluminium

## Why this dish

Jaggery-rice-banana dumplings.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice flour
- ½ cup jaggery
- 1 banana, mashed
- Cardamom
- Coconut bits
- Ghee

## Method

1. Mix a thick batter. Rest 15 minutes. Fry in ghee in an unniyappam pan.
