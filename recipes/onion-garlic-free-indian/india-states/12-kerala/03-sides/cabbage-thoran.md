# Cabbage Thoran

**Continent:** Kerala — Kerala sadya (feast) kitchen  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Coconut stir-fry of the sadya.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups shredded cabbage
- ½ cup coconut
- Mustard, urad, curry leaves, hing, green chilli
- Turmeric
- Coconut oil
- Salt

## Method

1. Temper, add cabbage, turmeric, salt. Cover 6 minutes. Mix coconut.
