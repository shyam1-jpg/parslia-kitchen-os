# Vegetable Momos

**Continent:** Nagaland — Naga vegetarian (axone + greens)  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Naga-style cabbage momos with ginger, no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- Cabbage-carrot-ginger filling as Arunachal
- Salt

## Method

1. Fill, pleat, steam 12 minutes in steel. Tomato-chilli dip without garlic.
