# Galho

**Continent:** Nagaland — Naga vegetarian (axone + greens)  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Naga rice porridge with vegetables — the everyday vegetarian bowl.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice
- 4 cups water
- 2 cups mixed veg and greens
- 1 tsp ginger
- 2 green chillies
- Salt

## Method

1. Cook rice extra-soft with veg, ginger and chilli until porridge-like.
2. Salt. No smoked meat, no garlic.
