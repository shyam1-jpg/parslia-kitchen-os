# Cabbage Chilli Salad

**Continent:** Nagaland — Naga vegetarian (axone + greens)  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Signature salad from Nagaland, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cabbage
- Chilli
- Lemon
- Salt

## Method

1. Toss. No onion.
