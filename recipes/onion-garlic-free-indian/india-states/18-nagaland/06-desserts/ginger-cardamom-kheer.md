# Ginger Cardamom Kheer

**Continent:** Nagaland — Naga vegetarian (axone + greens)  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Milk kheer with a thin slice of ginger — Naga-adjacent comfort.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup rice
- 1 litre milk
- Sugar
- 2 slices ginger, removed at the end
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Simmer in steel. Fish out ginger.
