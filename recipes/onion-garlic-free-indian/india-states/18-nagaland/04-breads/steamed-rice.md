# Steamed Rice

**Continent:** Nagaland — Naga vegetarian (axone + greens)  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature bread from Nagaland, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice

## Method

1. Steam in steel.

## Notes

Naga meals are rice-based.
