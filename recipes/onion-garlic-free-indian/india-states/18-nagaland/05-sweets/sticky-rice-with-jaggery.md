# Sticky Rice with Jaggery

**Continent:** Nagaland — Naga vegetarian (axone + greens)  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Festival sticky rice sweetened with jaggery.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sticky rice, soaked
- ½ cup jaggery
- Sesame

## Method

1. Steam rice. Fold warm jaggery and sesame.
