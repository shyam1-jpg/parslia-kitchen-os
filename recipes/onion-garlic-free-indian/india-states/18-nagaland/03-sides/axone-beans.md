# Axone Beans

**Continent:** Nagaland — Naga vegetarian (axone + greens)  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fermented soybean (axone/akhuni) with beans. Use ginger, not garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups beans
- 1 tsp axone (fermented soybean)
- 1 tsp ginger
- Chilli
- Salt
- 1 tbsp oil

## Method

1. Fry ginger, chilli, axone briefly. Add beans and a splash of water. Cover until tender.
2. Axone is soybean, not allium — but it is strong; start with 1 tsp.
