# Sel Roti

**Continent:** Sikkim — Sikkimese Nepali, Bhutia and Lepcha vegetarian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fermented rice ring-bread of Sikkim and Nepal.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice, soaked and ground
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tbsp ghee
- Cardamom
- Oil

## Method

1. Ferment batter 2 hours. Pipe rings into hot oil in a steel kadhai. Fry until deep brown.
