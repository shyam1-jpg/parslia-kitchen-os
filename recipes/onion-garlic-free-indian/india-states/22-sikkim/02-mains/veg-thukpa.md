# Veg Thukpa

**Continent:** Sikkim — Sikkimese Nepali, Bhutia and Lepcha vegetarian  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sikkimese noodle soup.

## Ingredients

- Noodles
- Cabbage, carrot, beans
- Tomato
- Ginger
- Hing pinch
- Salt
- Pepper

## Method

1. Simmer a ginger-tomato broth with veg. Add noodles. No garlic paste.
