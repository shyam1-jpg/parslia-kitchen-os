# Sikkimese Veg Momos

**Continent:** Sikkim — Sikkimese Nepali, Bhutia and Lepcha vegetarian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Cabbage-ginger momos of Gangtok.

## Ingredients

- Maida dough
- Cabbage, ginger, chilli filling
- Salt

## Method

1. Steam 12 minutes. Achar of tomato-chilli-sesame, no garlic.
