# Gundruk

**Continent:** Sikkim — Sikkimese Nepali, Bhutia and Lepcha vegetarian  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fermented leafy-green soup of the eastern Himalaya.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup gundruk (fermented greens), rinsed
- 1 tomato
- Ginger
- Chilli
- Salt

## Method

1. Simmer 10 minutes until sour and soft.
