# Sel Roti with Sugar

**Continent:** Sikkim — Sikkimese Nepali, Bhutia and Lepcha vegetarian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

The same sel roti dusted with sugar as a sweet.

## Ingredients

- Sel roti batter as above
- Sugar to dust

## Method

1. Fry rings. Dust with sugar while warm.
