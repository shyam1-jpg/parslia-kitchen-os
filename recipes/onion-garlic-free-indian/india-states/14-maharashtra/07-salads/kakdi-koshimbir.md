# Kakdi Koshimbir

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Cucumber-coconut-peanut salad. Traditional koshimbir often has onion — skip it.

## Ingredients

- 2 cucumbers
- 2 tbsp coconut
- 2 tbsp crushed peanuts
- Lemon
- Green chilli
- Cumin
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Mix. Peanut and lemon replace onion.
