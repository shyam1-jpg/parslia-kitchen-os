# Jowar Bhakri

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Sorghum roti of Maharashtra.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups jowar flour
- Hot water
- Salt

## Method

1. Knead with hot water. Pat, cook on iron tawa, finish on flame to puff.
