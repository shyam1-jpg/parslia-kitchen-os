# Bharli Vangi (no onion)

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Stuffed small brinjals with coconut-peanut masala, no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 8 small brinjals
- ½ cup coconut
- ¼ cup peanuts, roasted
- 1 tsp sesame
- 1 tsp goda masala (garlic-free) or coriander-cumin-coconut
- Tamarind
- Jaggery pinch
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Grind stuffing. Slit brinjals, fill.
2. Cook covered with a little water and oil until soft and the masala clings.
