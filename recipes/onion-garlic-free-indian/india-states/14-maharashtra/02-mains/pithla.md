# Pithla

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Besan curry of rural Maharashtra. Skip onion; use hing, ginger, green chilli.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup besan
- 3 cups water
- 2 tbsp oil
- Mustard, cumin, hing, curry leaves, green chilli, ginger
- Turmeric
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Temper spices. Pour a thin besan slurry, turmeric, salt. Cook stirring until it thickens and the raw smell goes.
2. Serve with bhakri. Do not add onion.
