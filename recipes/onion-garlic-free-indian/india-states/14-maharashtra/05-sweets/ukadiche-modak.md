# Ukadiche Modak

**Continent:** Maharashtra — Maharashtrian Hindu vegetarian / sattvic  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ganesh festival steamed modak — coconut-jaggery filling, no allium.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice flour
- 1 cup water + 1 tsp ghee + salt (for dough)
- Filling: 1 cup coconut + ¾ cup jaggery + cardamom

## Method

1. Cook filling until sticky. Make hot-water rice dough. Shape modaks. Steam 10–12 minutes in steel.
