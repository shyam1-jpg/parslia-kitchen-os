# Coconut Vegetable Curry

**Continent:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands — Island coconut kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Island veg in coconut milk, curry leaves, kokum — no fish, no allium.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups mixed tropical veg
- 1½ cups coconut milk
- Curry leaves
- Mustard
- Hing
- Turmeric
- Kokum or lemon
- Salt
- Coconut oil

## Method

1. Temper, add veg, turmeric, salt. Coconut milk, simmer. Kokum.
