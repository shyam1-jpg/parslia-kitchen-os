# Coconut Ladoo

**Continent:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands — Island coconut kitchen  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 10 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature sweet from Andaman Nicobar, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Coconut
- Condensed milk or jaggery
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Cook, shape.
