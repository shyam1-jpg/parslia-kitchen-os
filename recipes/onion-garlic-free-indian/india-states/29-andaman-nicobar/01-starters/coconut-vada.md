# Coconut Vada

**Continent:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands — Island coconut kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Island coconut-dal fritters.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup urad dal, soaked
- ½ cup coconut
- Ginger-chilli
- Hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Grind, fry in steel.
