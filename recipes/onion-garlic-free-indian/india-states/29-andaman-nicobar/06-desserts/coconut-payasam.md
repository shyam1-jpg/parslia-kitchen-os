# Coconut Payasam

**Continent:** Andaman and Nicobar Islands — Island coconut kitchen  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Signature dessert from Andaman Nicobar, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- Vermicelli
- Coconut milk
- Jaggery
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Cook in steel.
