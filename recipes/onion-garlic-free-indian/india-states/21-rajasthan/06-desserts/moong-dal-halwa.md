# Moong Dal Halwa

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari and Mewari, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Rich moong-ghee-milk halwa of Rajasthani weddings.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup moong dal, soaked and ground
- ¾ cup ghee
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 cup milk
- Cardamom
- Nuts

## Method

1. Cook dal paste in ghee in steel on low until it leaves ghee. Add milk, then sugar. Cardamom, nuts.
