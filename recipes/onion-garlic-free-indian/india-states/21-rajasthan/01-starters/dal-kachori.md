# Dal Kachori

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari and Mewari, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Moong-dal kachori without the pyaz of street pyaz-kachori.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- Filling: ½ cup cooked mooor crushed moong + fennel + coriander + chilli + amchur + hing
- Oil

## Method

1. Stuff, seal, fry on medium in a steel kadhai until flaky.
