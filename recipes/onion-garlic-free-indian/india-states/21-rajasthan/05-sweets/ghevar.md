# Ghevar

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari and Mewari, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Honeycomb Rajasthani disc soaked in syrup, topped with rabri.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup maida
- ¼ cup ghee
- Ice water
- Pinch of baking soda
- Sugar syrup
- Rabri to top

## Method

1. Make a thin batter. Pour in a thin stream into hot ghee in a steel kadhai to lace a disc. Fry, drain, soak in syrup.
