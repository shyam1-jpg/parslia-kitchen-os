# Ker Sangri

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari and Mewari, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Desert berries and beans of Rajasthan.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup dried ker, soaked
- 1 cup dried sangri, soaked
- 2 tbsp oil
- Cumin
- Hing
- Red chilli
- Amchur
- Coriander powder
- Salt

## Method

1. Boil ker and sangri until tender. Drain.
2. Temper spices, toss. Tart, spicy, no onion.
