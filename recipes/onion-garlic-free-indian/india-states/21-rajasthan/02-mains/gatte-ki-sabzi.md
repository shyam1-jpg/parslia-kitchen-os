# Gatte ki Sabzi

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari and Mewari, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Gram-flour dumplings in yogurt gravy — Marwari, often onion-garlic free.

## Ingredients

- Gatte: 1 cup besan + 2 tbsp yogurt + ajwain + chilli + oil, boiled as logs, sliced
- Gravy: 1 cup yogurt + 1 tsp coriander + turmeric + chilli + cumin + hing tadka
- Salt

## Method

1. Boil gatte logs, slice.
2. Make yogurt gravy in steel, stirring so it does not split. Add gatte. Simmer 8 minutes.
