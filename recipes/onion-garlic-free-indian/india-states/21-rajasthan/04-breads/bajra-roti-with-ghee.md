# Bajra Roti with Ghee

**Continent:** Rajasthan — Marwari and Mewari, including Jain-style  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Pearl-millet roti of the desert.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups bajra flour
- Hot water
- Salt
- Ghee

## Method

1. Pat and cook on iron tawa. Ghee.
