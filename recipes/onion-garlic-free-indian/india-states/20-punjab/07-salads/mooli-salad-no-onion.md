# Mooli Salad (no onion)

**Continent:** Punjab — Punjabi langar and home  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Radish-cucumber-lemon. Skip onion.

## Ingredients

- 1 radish, julienned
- 1 cucumber
- Lemon
- Green chilli
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Toss just before serving.
