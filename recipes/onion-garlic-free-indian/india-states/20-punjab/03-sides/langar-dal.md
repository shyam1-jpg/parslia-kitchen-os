# Langar Dal

**Continent:** Punjab — Punjabi langar and home  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 50 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Gurdwara-style whole-urad-chana dal. No onion, no garlic — ever.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup whole urad
- ¼ cup chana dal
- Turmeric
- 1 tsp ginger
- 2 tomatoes
- Ghee tadka: cumin, hing, chilli powder
- Salt

## Method

1. Pressure-cook dals until creamy. Add tomato, ginger, salt. Simmer.
2. Heavy ghee tadka with cumin and hing. This is langar — do not add onion or garlic.
