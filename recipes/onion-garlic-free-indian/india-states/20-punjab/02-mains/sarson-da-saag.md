# Sarson da Saag

**Continent:** Punjab — Punjabi langar and home  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Mustard-greens mash of Punjab. Langar-style: ginger, green chilli, makki atta — no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 500 g sarson (mustard greens)
- 100 g spinach
- 2 green chillies
- 1-inch ginger
- 2 tbsp makki atta
- Salt
- 2 tbsp ghee
- Pinch of hing
- 1 tsp cumin

## Method

1. Cook greens, chilli and ginger with a little water until very soft. Mash.
2. Stir in makki atta. Simmer 10 minutes.
3. Tadka: ghee, cumin, hing. Serve with makki roti and white butter.
