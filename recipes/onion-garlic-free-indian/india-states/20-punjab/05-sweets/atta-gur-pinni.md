# Atta Gur Pinni

**Continent:** Punjab — Punjabi langar and home  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Punjabi winter ladoo.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- ¾ cup ghee
- ¾ cup jaggery
- Gond optional
- Almonds
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Roast atta (and gond) in ghee. Mix jaggery. Shape.
