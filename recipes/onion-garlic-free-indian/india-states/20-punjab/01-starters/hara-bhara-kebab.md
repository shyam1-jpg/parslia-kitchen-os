# Hara Bhara Kebab

**Continent:** Punjab — Punjabi langar and home  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Spinach-pea-potato kebab. Skip onion; use ginger, chilli, chaat masala.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups spinach, blanched
- ½ cup peas
- 2 potatoes, boiled
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli
- ½ tsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp besan
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Mash, shape, pan-fry on iron or steel until crusted.
