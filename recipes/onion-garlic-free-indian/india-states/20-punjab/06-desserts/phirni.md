# Phirni

**Continent:** Punjab — Punjabi langar and home  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Ground-rice milk dessert set in katoris.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup basmati, soaked and ground
- 3 cups milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- Saffron
- Cardamom
- Almonds

## Method

1. Cook rice paste in milk in steel until thick. Sugar, saffron. Pour into steel bowls. Chill.
