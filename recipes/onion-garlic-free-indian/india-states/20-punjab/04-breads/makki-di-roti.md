# Makki di Roti

**Continent:** Punjab — Punjabi langar and home  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Maize roti eaten with saag.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups makki atta
- Hot water
- Salt
- Ghee

## Method

1. Knead with hot water. Pat with wet hands. Cook on iron tawa. Ghee.
