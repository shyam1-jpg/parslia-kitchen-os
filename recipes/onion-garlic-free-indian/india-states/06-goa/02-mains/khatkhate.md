# Khatkhate

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 30 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Goan Hindu mixed-vegetable coconut stew — festival main, traditionally no onion garlic.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups mixed veg (pumpkin, beans, colocation, cucumber, brinjal)
- 1 cup coconut, ground with 2 dry red chillies and ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- Pinch of hing
- 8 curry leaves
- 1 tbsp tamarind
- Salt
- 1 tbsp coconut oil

## Method

1. Cook vegetables with salt and tamarind until just tender.
2. Add coconut paste. Simmer 5 minutes.
3. Temper coconut oil with mustard, hing and curry leaves.
