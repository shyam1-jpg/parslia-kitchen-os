# Coconut Cucumber Cutlets

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Goan vegetarian starter with coconut and chilli, no allium.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup grated coconut
- 1 cucumber, grated and squeezed
- 2 tbsp besan
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli
- Pinch of hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Mix, shape small cutlets, pan-fry on iron or steel until brown.
