# Sannas

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 70 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Steamed rice cakes of Goa, slightly sweet-sour from toddy or yeast.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice, soaked
- ½ cup coconut
- ½ tsp yeast or ¼ cup toddy
- 1 tsp sugar
- Salt

## Method

1. Grind rice and coconut. Ferment 1 hour with yeast and sugar.
2. Pour into steel idli moulds. Steam 12 minutes. Never an aluminium steamer.
