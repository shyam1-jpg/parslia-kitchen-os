# Kokum Cucumber Salad

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Kokum syrup, cucumber, chilli — Goan bite without onion.

## Ingredients

- 1 cucumber
- 4 kokum petals soaked
- Green chilli
- Salt
- Pinch of sugar
- Coriander

## Method

1. Toss cucumber with kokum water, chilli, salt and sugar.
