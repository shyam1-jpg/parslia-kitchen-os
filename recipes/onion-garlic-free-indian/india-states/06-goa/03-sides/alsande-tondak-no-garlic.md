# Alsande Tondak (no garlic)

**Continent:** Goa — Goan Hindu vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Yard-long beans in coconut. Classic tondak uses garlic — hing and kokum replace it.

## Ingredients

- 3 cups alsande or French beans
- 1 cup coconut paste
- 3 kokum petals
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp chilli powder
- Pinch of hing
- Salt
- 1 tbsp oil

## Method

1. Cook beans with kokum and salt.
2. Add coconut, spices and hing. Simmer until thick and coating.
