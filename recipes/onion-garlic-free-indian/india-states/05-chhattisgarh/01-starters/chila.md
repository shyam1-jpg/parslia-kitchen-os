# Chila

**Continent:** Chhattisgarh — Chhattisgarhi village kitchen  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Rice-flour savoury pancake of Chhattisgarh.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice flour
- ¼ cup yogurt
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli
- Pinch of hing
- Salt
- Water
- Oil

## Method

1. Mix a pouring batter. Rest 10 minutes.
2. Spread on a hot iron tawa. Cook both sides with a little oil.
