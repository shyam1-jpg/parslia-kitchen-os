# Red Rice Kheer

**Continent:** Chhattisgarh — Chhattisgarhi village kitchen  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Local red rice cooked down in milk.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup red or white rice
- 1 litre milk
- ⅓ cup jaggery
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Simmer in steel until creamy. Jaggery off the boil.
