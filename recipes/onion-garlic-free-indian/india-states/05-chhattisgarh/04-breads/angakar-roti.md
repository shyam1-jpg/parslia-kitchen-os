# Angakar Roti

**Continent:** Chhattisgarh — Chhattisgarhi village kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Thick rice roti cooked on iron.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice flour
- Hot water
- Salt

## Method

1. Make a soft dough with hot water. Pat thick rotis. Cook on iron tawa until brown spots.
