# Dehrori

**Continent:** Chhattisgarh — Chhattisgarhi village kitchen  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Rice-flour dumplings soaked in thickened milk.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice flour
- Milk to bind
- 3 cups milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- Cardamom
- Ghee for frying

## Method

1. Shape rice-flour balls. Fry golden in ghee.
2. Simmer milk and sugar in steel until slightly thick. Soak dehrori 20 minutes.
