# Farra

**Continent:** Chhattisgarh — Chhattisgarhi village kitchen  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Rice-flour stuffed steam cakes.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup rice flour
- Hot water
- Salt
- Filling: ½ cup spiced chana dal paste as bafauri

## Method

1. Scald rice flour with hot salted water to a dough.
2. Flatten, fill, seal. Steam 12 minutes.
