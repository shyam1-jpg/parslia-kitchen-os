# Tomato Lemon Salad

**Continent:** Chhattisgarh — Chhattisgarhi village kitchen  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 8 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Signature salad from Chhattisgarh, cooked without onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- 2 tomatoes
- Green chilli
- Lemon
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Chop and toss. No onion.
