# Bafauri

**Continent:** Chhattisgarh — Chhattisgarhi village kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Steamed chana-dal dumplings — the protein main of village Chhattisgarh.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup chana dal, soaked 2 hours
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli
- ½ tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- Salt
- Coriander

## Method

1. Grind dal coarsely with spices. Shape logs or balls.
2. Steam 12–15 minutes in a steel steamer.
3. Temper optional: oil, cumin, hing, curry leaves. Pour over.
