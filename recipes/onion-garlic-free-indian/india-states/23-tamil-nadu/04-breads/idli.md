# Idli

**Continent:** Tamil Nadu — Tamil Brahmin and Chettinad vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Steamed rice-urad cakes. Steel idli stand, never aluminium.

## Ingredients

- Idli batter (rice + urad, fermented)
- Salt

## Method

1. Pour into steel moulds. Steam 10–12 minutes.
