# Beans Poriyal

**Continent:** Tamil Nadu — Tamil Brahmin and Chettinad vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Dry beans coconut.

## Ingredients

- Beans
- Coconut
- Mustard, urad, curry leaves, hing
- Turmeric
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Temper, cook beans, finish coconut.
