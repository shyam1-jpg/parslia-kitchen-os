# Medu Vada

**Continent:** Tamil Nadu — Tamil Brahmin and Chettinad vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Urad-dal doughnut. Tamil Brahmin version: pepper, curry leaf, ginger — no onion.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup urad dal, soaked 2 hours
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 tsp pepper
- Curry leaves
- Pinch of hing
- Salt
- Oil

## Method

1. Grind fluffy, mix spices, shape rings, fry in steel until golden.
