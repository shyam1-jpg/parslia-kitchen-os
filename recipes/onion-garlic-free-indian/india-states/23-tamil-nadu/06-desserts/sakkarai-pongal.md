# Sakkarai Pongal

**Continent:** Tamil Nadu — Tamil Brahmin and Chettinad vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sweet jaggery rice-moong of Pongal.

## Ingredients

- ½ cup rice
- ¼ cup moong
- ¾ cup jaggery
- Ghee
- Cashews
- Raisins
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Cook rice and moong soft. Melt jaggery, mix, add lots of ghee and nuts.
