# Adhirasam

**Continent:** Tamil Nadu — Tamil Brahmin and Chettinad vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 40 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Rice-jaggery disc of Tamil Nadu festivals.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rice flour from soaked ground rice
- 1 cup jaggery syrup
- Cardamom
- Sesame
- Ghee

## Method

1. Bind dough with warm jaggery syrup. Rest. Pat discs, fry in ghee on medium.
