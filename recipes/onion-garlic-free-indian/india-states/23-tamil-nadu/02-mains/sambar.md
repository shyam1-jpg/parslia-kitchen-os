# Sambar

**Continent:** Tamil Nadu — Tamil Brahmin and Chettinad vegetarian (sattvic)  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 35 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel or clay pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Toor-dal tamarind vegetable stew. Brahmin sambar has no onion or garlic.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup toor dal
- 2 tbsp tamarind
- 2 cups veg (drumstick, pumpkin, brinjal, beans)
- 2 tbsp sambar powder (garlic-free)
- Turmeric
- Mustard, curry leaves, hing, dry chilli
- Salt
- Jaggery pinch

## Method

1. Cook dal. Cook veg with tamarind, powder, turmeric, salt.
2. Mix dal. Tadka. This is Tamil Brahmin food — no allium.
