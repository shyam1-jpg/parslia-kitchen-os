# Veg Singju

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Manipuri salad. Skip fermented fish; use roasted sesame and chickpea flour.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups shredded cabbage and banana flower or beans
- 2 tbsp roasted sesame, crushed
- 1 tbsp roasted besan
- Chilli flakes
- Salt
- Lemon

## Method

1. Toss. Rest 5 minutes. No onion, no nga-ri.
