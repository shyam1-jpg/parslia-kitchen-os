# Paknam Slices

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Manipuri savoury gram-and-herb steamed cake, sliced as a starter.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup besan
- 1 cup chopped herbs (spring-onion skipped: use coriander + lakadong chilli)
- 1 tsp ginger
- Salt
- Banana leaf for wrapping

## Method

1. Mix a thick batter. Spread on banana leaf, steam 20 minutes in steel.
2. Cool, slice, pan-sear if you like.
