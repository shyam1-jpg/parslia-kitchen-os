# Veg Eromba (sesame)

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stone sil-batta or steel bowl (no aluminium)

## Why this dish

Eromba is often fish; this version is boiled veg mashed with roasted sesame and chilli.

## Ingredients

- 2 potatoes
- 1 cup beans or broad beans, boiled
- 3 tbsp sesame, roasted
- 2 dry red chillies, toasted
- Salt

## Method

1. Boil veg. Pound sesame and chilli. Mash with veg and salt.
2. Should be smoky and hot — not garlicky.
