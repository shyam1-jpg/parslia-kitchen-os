# Steamed Sticky Rice

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel steamer and steel thali — never aluminium

## Why this dish

The Manipuri staple.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sticky rice, soaked
- Pinch of salt

## Method

1. Steam 20–25 minutes in a steel steamer.
