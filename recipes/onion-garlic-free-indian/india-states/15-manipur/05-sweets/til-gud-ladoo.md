# Til-Gud Ladoo

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Sesame-jaggery balls common at Manipuri festivals.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup sesame, roasted
- ¾ cup jaggery
- Ghee 1 tsp

## Method

1. Melt jaggery, add sesame, shape ladoos.
