# Chak-Hao Kheer

**Continent:** Manipur — Meitei vegetarian table  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Black-rice kheer of Manipur.

## Ingredients

- ⅓ cup chak-hao (black rice), soaked
- 3 cups milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- Cardamom

## Method

1. Simmer black rice in milk in steel until the milk is lilac-grey and thick. Sugar, cardamom.
