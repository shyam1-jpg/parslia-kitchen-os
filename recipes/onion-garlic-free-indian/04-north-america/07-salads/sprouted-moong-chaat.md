# Sprouted Moong Chaat

**Continent:** North America — US / Canada  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

Protein salad that replaced onion-heavy bhel at many North American vegetarian tables.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups sprouted moong
- 1 tomato, diced
- ½ cup pomegranate
- Coriander
- Lemon
- Chaat masala
- Black salt
- 1 green chilli
- Optional: diced cucumber

## Method

1. Toss just before eating. Taste for lemon and black salt.
2. No onion. Pomegranate gives the pop.
