# Restaurant Chana Masala

**Continent:** North America — Punjabi-Canadian / US  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

The vegetarian main of Indian North America.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups cooked chickpeas (or 1 can, rinsed)
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- Pinch of hing
- 2 tomatoes blended with 1-inch ginger
- 1 tsp chana masala powder (check: no garlic)
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp chilli powder
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp amchur
- Salt
- Lemon
- Julienned ginger
- Coriander

## Method

1. Temper cumin and hing. Add tomato-ginger paste and dry spices. Cook until thick and glossy.
2. Add chickpeas and a splash of water. Simmer 12 minutes. Mash a few for body.
3. Finish with amchur, lemon, ginger strips and coriander.

## Notes

Many North American 'curry pastes' are garlic-heavy — make the gravy from tomatoes instead.
