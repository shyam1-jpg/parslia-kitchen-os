# Palak Paneer (no onion garlic)

**Continent:** North America — Punjabi North America  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Saag paneer without the usual onion-garlic fry-up.

## Ingredients

- 300 g spinach, washed
- 200 g paneer
- 1 tbsp ghee + 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- 1 tsp ginger paste
- 1 green chilli
- 1 small tomato
- ½ tsp coriander powder
- ¼ tsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp cream or yogurt
- Salt
- Pinch of sugar

## Method

1. Blanch spinach 1 minute, plunge in cold water, blend with chilli and tomato.
2. Heat ghee and oil. Cumin, hing, ginger. Add spinach puree and spices. Cook 6–8 minutes.
3. Add paneer, salt, sugar and cream. Do not boil hard after the cream goes in.
