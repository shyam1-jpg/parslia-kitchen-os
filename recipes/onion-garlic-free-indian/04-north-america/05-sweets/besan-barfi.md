# Besan Barfi

**Continent:** North America — US / Canada Indian mithai  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Gram-flour fudge. The sweet box staple of North American Indian homes.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup ghee
- 2 cups besan
- 1¼ cups sugar
- ⅓ cup water
- ½ tsp cardamom
- Pistachios

## Method

1. Roast besan in ghee on low 12–15 minutes until nutty and the ghee surfaces. Steel kadhai only.
2. Make one-thread sugar syrup with sugar and water.
3. Mix syrup into the besan off the heat. Add cardamom. Spread in a steel/glass tray. Set and cut.
