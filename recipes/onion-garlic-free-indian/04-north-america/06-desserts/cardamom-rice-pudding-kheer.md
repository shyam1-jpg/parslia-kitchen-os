# Cardamom Rice Pudding Kheer

**Continent:** North America — US Indian home cooking  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 40 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel pot

## Why this dish

Same as Indian kheer, often finished with maple or extra nuts in North America. Keep it classic here.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup basmati
- 1 litre milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- Cardamom
- Saffron optional
- Almonds
- Raisins

## Method

1. Simmer rice in milk in a steel pot 35–40 minutes, stirring.
2. Add sugar, cardamom and nuts. Serve warm or cold.

## Notes

Do not cook this in a slow-cooker with an aluminium insert.
