# Aloo-Pea Samosa (no onion)

**Continent:** North America — Punjabi-Canadian  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 40 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Steel kadhai for frying + steel bowl for filling

## Why this dish

The starter of every North American Indian party. Filling is potato, pea and spice — no onion.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- 4 tbsp oil (for dough)
- Salt
- Water
- 3 boiled potatoes, mashed
- ½ cup green peas
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- Pinch of hing
- 1 tsp ginger-green chilli paste
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp garam masala
- ½ tsp amchur
- Oil for frying

## Method

1. Rub oil into maida and salt. Knead a firm dough. Rest 20 minutes.
2. Filling: heat 1 tbsp oil, cumin and hing. Add ginger-chilli, peas, potato and spices. Cool.
3. Roll dough into ovals, cut in half, form cones, fill, seal with water.
4. Fry in medium-hot oil in a steel kadhai until golden. Serve with tamarind and mint chutney (mint chutney without onion/garlic).
