# Potato Kulcha (no onion)

**Continent:** North America — Punjabi-Canadian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 40 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Amritsari-style stuffed bread minus chopped onion in the filling.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups maida
- ½ cup yogurt
- ½ tsp sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp baking powder
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 boiled potatoes
- 1 tsp ginger-chilli paste
- ½ tsp amchur
- ½ tsp cumin
- Coriander
- Butter

## Method

1. Knead dough with yogurt, sugar, salt, baking powder and oil. Rest 30 minutes.
2. Mash potato with ginger-chilli, amchur, cumin, salt and coriander — no onion.
3. Stuff, roll gently, cook on a hot iron tawa with butter until brown spots appear.
