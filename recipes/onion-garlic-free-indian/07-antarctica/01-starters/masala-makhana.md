# Masala Makhana

**Continent:** Antarctica — Polar / expedition snack  
**Category:** Starter  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 10 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fox-nut snack from dry stores. No fresh onion, no aluminium wok needed — steel kadhai on a camp stove.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups makhana (fox nuts)
- 1 tbsp ghee
- Pinch of hing
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ½ tsp chilli powder
- ½ tsp chaat masala
- Salt

## Method

1. Warm ghee. Add hing, then makhana. Roast on medium-low until crisp (5–8 minutes).
2. Dust with turmeric, chilli, chaat masala and salt. Cool — they crisp further.
3. Store in a steel or glass tin, not foil.
