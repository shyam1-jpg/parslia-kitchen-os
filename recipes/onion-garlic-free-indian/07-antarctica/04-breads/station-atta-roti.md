# Station Atta Roti

**Continent:** Antarctica — Polar kitchen  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Flour, water, salt, iron skillet. Works on a research-station range.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta (packed dry)
- Warm water
- Pinch of salt
- Ghee if available

## Method

1. Knead, rest 10 minutes if you can, roll, cook on a cast-iron skillet. Keep stacked in a cloth.
2. No aluminium griddle — it warps and cooks pale rotis.
