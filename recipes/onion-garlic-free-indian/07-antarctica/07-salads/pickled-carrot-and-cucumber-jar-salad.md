# Pickled Carrot and Cucumber Jar Salad

**Continent:** Antarctica — Polar kitchen  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 15 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Glass jar (never aluminium tin for storage)

## Why this dish

Fresh salad is scarce. Quick-pickle dry-store carrot and any cucumber from hydroponics, or use jarred gherkin plus carrot.

## Ingredients

- 2 carrots, julienned (fresh, hydroponic, or rehydrated strips)
- 1 cucumber or 4 crunchy pickles, sliced
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar
- 3 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
- ½ tsp roasted cumin
- Pinch of chilli flakes
- Coriander if available

## Method

1. Toss with salt, sugar and acid. Rest 20 minutes (or keep in a glass jar 1 day).
2. Store only in glass or steel. Acid plus aluminium jars is unsafe.

## Notes

This is the polar stand-in for kachumber when onion is both forbidden and unavailable.
