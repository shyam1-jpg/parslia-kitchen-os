# Milk-Powder Saffron Kheer

**Continent:** Antarctica — Polar kitchen  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

No litres of fresh milk. Milk powder plus a handful of rice (or broken vermicelli).

## Ingredients

- ½ cup milk powder mixed with 3 cups water (or as pack directs for whole milk)
- 3 tbsp rice or roasted vermicelli
- 3 tbsp sugar
- Saffron or cardamom from the spice kit
- Dried raisins and nuts

## Method

1. Bring reconstituted milk to a simmer in steel. Add rice or vermicelli; cook until soft.
2. Add sugar, saffron/cardamom and dried fruit. Serve in steel mugs.

## Notes

Do not reduce reconstituted milk in aluminium — it scorches and tastes of metal faster than fresh milk.
