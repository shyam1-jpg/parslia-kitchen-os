# Condensed-Milk Coconut Ladoo

**Continent:** Antarctica — Polar kitchen  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 10 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Desiccated coconut + condensed milk. Both survive the journey south.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups desiccated coconut
- ¾ cup condensed milk
- ½ tsp cardamom powder
- 1 tsp ghee

## Method

1. Cook coconut, condensed milk and ghee in a steel pan until thick.
2. Add cardamom. Shape. Roll in extra coconut.
