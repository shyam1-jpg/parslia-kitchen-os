# Tomato-Cashew Dal with Dried Vegetables

**Continent:** Antarctica — Polar kitchen  
**Category:** Main  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 25 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Shelf-stable main: dal + tomato puree + cashew powder + freeze-dried mixed veg. Tastes like shahi dal.

## Ingredients

- ¾ cup toor or moong dal (dry)
- 3 tbsp tomato puree (tin or tube)
- 2 tbsp cashew powder
- 1 cup freeze-dried mixed vegetables, rehydrated
- 2 tbsp ghee
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- 1 tsp ginger powder or 1 tsp ginger paste (tube)
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chilli
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- Salt
- 1 tbsp milk powder + a little water (for creaminess)

## Method

1. Cook dal in a steel pot until soft (pressure cooker if the station has one).
2. Tadka: ghee, cumin, hing, ginger, tomato puree and spices. Cook until thick.
3. Add dal, rehydrated veg, cashew powder and milk-powder cream. Simmer 5 minutes.

## Notes

Do not cook tomato puree in aluminium mess tins — use the station’s stainless insert.
