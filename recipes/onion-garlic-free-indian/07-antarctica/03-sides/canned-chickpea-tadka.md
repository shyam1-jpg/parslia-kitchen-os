# Canned Chickpea Tadka

**Continent:** Antarctica — Polar kitchen  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Emergency chana from cans. Drain, rinse, tadka.

## Ingredients

- 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin
- Pinch of hing
- 2 tbsp tomato puree
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp coriander
- ½ tsp chilli
- ½ tsp amchur or lemon crystals
- Salt

## Method

1. Bloom cumin and hing in oil. Add puree and spices. Cook 2 minutes.
2. Add chickpeas. Simmer 8 minutes. Finish with amchur.
