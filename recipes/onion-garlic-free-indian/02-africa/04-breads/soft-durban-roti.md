# Soft Durban Roti

**Continent:** Africa — Durban Indian  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 25 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

The bread Durban curry is eaten with. Cooked on iron, not aluminium.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta
- 2 tbsp oil
- Pinch of salt
- Warm water
- Ghee for brushing

## Method

1. Knead a soft, slightly oily dough. Rest 20 minutes.
2. Roll medium-thick rounds. Cook both sides on a hot iron tawa.
3. Smear ghee and stack in a cloth. Serve with brinjal curry.
