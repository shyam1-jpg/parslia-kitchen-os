# Tomato Cucumber Limbu Salad

**Continent:** Africa — Durban  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 0 min  
**Cookware:** Steel or glass bowl

## Why this dish

No-onion kachumber with extra lemon, the way Durban tables serve it next to hot curry.

## Ingredients

- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 1 green chilli, sliced
- Coriander
- Juice of 1 lemon
- ½ tsp roasted cumin
- Black salt
- Pinch of sugar

## Method

1. Toss everything just before serving so the cucumber stays crisp.
2. Do not add onion — chilli and lemon give the bite.
