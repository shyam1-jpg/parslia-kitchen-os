# Coconut Cardamom Burfi

**Continent:** Africa — East African Indian  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Coastal East African Indian mithai. Coconut, milk and cardamom only.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups grated coconut
- 1 cup full-fat milk
- ¾ cup sugar
- 1 tsp ghee
- 4 cardamom pods, crushed
- Pistachios to garnish

## Method

1. Cook coconut, milk and sugar in a steel kadhai on low until thick.
2. Add ghee and cardamom. Cook until it leaves the sides.
3. Press into a greased steel or glass tray. Top with pistachios. Cool and cut.
