# Semiya Payasam

**Continent:** Africa — East African / South Indian diaspora  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Vermicelli milk pudding served after East African Indian thalis.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp ghee
- ½ cup roasted vermicelli
- 3 cups milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 8 saffron strands
- 4 cardamom
- 10 cashews
- 10 raisins

## Method

1. Fry cashews and raisins in ghee; set aside. In the same ghee toast vermicelli until golden.
2. Add milk and simmer 10–12 minutes until the semiya is soft.
3. Add sugar, saffron and cardamom. Cook 3 minutes. Top with nuts.

## Notes

Steel saucepan only — milk plus aluminium tastes of the pan.
