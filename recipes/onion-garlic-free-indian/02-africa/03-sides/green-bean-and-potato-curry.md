# Green Bean and Potato Curry

**Continent:** Africa — East African Gujarati  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 20 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Weeknight sabzi from Kenyan and Ugandan Gujarati kitchens.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- Pinch of hing
- 2 cups green beans, cut
- 2 potatoes, cubed
- 1 tomato, chopped
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp chilli powder
- Salt
- ½ cup water
- Lemon
- Coriander

## Method

1. Temper cumin and hing in oil.
2. Add potato, beans, tomato and spices. Stir 2 minutes.
3. Add water, cover, cook 12–15 minutes until potatoes are soft.
4. Crush a few potato cubes into the gravy. Finish with lemon.
