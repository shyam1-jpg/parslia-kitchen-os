# Cabbage Poriyal

**Continent:** Asia — Tamil Nadu, India  
**Category:** Side  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Fast South Indian dry vegetable. Coconut and mustard seeds do the flavour work.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp coconut oil
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp urad dal
- 8 curry leaves
- Pinch of hing
- 2 dry red chillies
- 4 cups shredded cabbage
- ¼ tsp turmeric
- Salt
- ¼ cup grated coconut
- Coriander

## Method

1. Heat coconut oil. Splutter mustard, urad dal, curry leaves, chilli and hing.
2. Add cabbage, turmeric and salt. Cover 6–8 minutes until just tender.
3. Uncover, stir in coconut and coriander. Do not overcook — keep a little bite.
