# Phulka

**Continent:** Asia — North India  
**Category:** Bread  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 20 min  
**Cook:** 15 min  
**Cookware:** Cast-iron tawa (not aluminium)

## Why this dish

Everyday wholewheat bread. Puffs only on a properly hot iron tawa, not aluminium.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups atta (wholewheat flour)
- Warm water as needed
- Pinch of salt
- Ghee for brushing

## Method

1. Knead a soft dough. Rest 15 minutes under a cloth.
2. Divide into 10 balls. Roll thin rounds.
3. Cook the first side on a hot iron tawa 20 seconds. Flip.
4. When small bubbles appear, flip onto the open flame (or press with a cloth) until it puffs.
5. Brush with ghee. Keep wrapped in a cotton cloth.

## Notes

Aluminium tawas do not hold heat — the phulka will not puff.
