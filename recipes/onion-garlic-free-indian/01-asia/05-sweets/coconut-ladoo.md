# Coconut Ladoo

**Continent:** Asia — India (coastal / festival)  
**Category:** Sweet  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 5 min  
**Cook:** 12 min  
**Cookware:** Stainless steel kadhai or saucepan — never aluminium

## Why this dish

The easiest mithai that is naturally onion-garlic free.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups fresh or desiccated coconut
- ¾ cup condensed milk (or ½ cup jaggery + 2 tbsp milk)
- 1 tsp ghee
- 4 green cardamom, crushed
- Extra coconut for rolling

## Method

1. Warm ghee in a steel kadhai. Add coconut and condensed milk (or jaggery and milk).
2. Cook on low, stirring, until the mix leaves the sides.
3. Add cardamom. Cool until handleable. Shape into ladoos and roll in coconut.
