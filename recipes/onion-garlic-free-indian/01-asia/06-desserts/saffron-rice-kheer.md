# Saffron Rice Kheer

**Continent:** Asia — North India  
**Category:** Dessert  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 10 min  
**Cook:** 45 min  
**Cookware:** Heavy stainless steel milk pot — never aluminium

## Why this dish

Festival dessert. Milk cooked in aluminium tastes metallic — steel only.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup basmati rice, rinsed and soaked 20 minutes
- 1 litre full-fat milk
- ⅓ cup sugar
- 8 saffron strands soaked in 1 tbsp warm milk
- 4 green cardamom, crushed
- 10 raisins
- 10 slivered almonds

## Method

1. Bring milk to a simmer in a heavy steel pot.
2. Add drained rice. Cook on low 35–40 minutes, stirring often, until creamy.
3. Add sugar, saffron milk, cardamom, raisins and almonds. Cook 5 minutes more.
4. Serve warm or chilled.

## Notes

Never reduce milk in aluminium — it greys the kheer and dulls saffron.
