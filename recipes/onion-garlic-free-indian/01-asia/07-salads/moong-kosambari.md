# Moong Kosambari

**Continent:** Asia — Karnataka, India  
**Category:** Salad  
**Diet:** Vegetarian · no onion · no garlic · no allium · no aluminium  
**Servings:** 4  
**Prep:** 30 min  
**Cook:** 2 min  
**Cookware:** Steel mixing bowl + small steel tadka pan

## Why this dish

India’s best salad — already sattvic. Protein from soaked moong.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cup split yellow moong, soaked 30 minutes and drained
- ½ cucumber, diced
- 2 tbsp grated coconut
- 2 tbsp coriander, chopped
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Salt
- 1 tsp oil
- ½ tsp mustard seeds
- 6 curry leaves
- Pinch of hing
- 1 dry red chilli

## Method

1. Mix moong, cucumber, coconut, coriander, lemon and salt.
2. Temper mustard, curry leaves, hing and chilli in oil. Pour over the salad. Toss.
