# Black Bean Chilli

Black Bean Chilli is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, stalks of celery finely chopped, carrots finely chopped. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Lunch, Main Course, Side Dish, Snack
- **Cuisine:** American, Mediterranean, Mexican
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 60 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 stalks of celery finely chopped
- 2 carrots finely chopped
- 1 tbsp organic chilli flakes
- 2 tsp organic dried thyme
- 2 tsp organic cayenne pepper
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp organic fresh lemon juice
- 400 g organic chopped tomatoes
- 500 ml organic vegetable bouillon
- 250 g organic black turtle beans (cooked)
- fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Fry the celery, celery and carrots in the olive oil with salt and pepper for 20 minutes on a medium to low heat.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Add the chilli flakes, thyme and ginger and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Add the cayenne pepper and the tomato paste and cook for a further 2 minutes.
5. Add the lemon juice and reduce for 2 minutes.
6. Add the tinned tomatoes and vegetable stock, bring to the boil then reduce and simmer and cook for 30 minutes.
7. Add the black beans and cook for 5 minutes.
8. Serve with yoghurt, sliced red chillis and coriander.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
