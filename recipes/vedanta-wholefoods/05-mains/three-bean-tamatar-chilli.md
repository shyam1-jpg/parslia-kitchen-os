# Three-Bean Tamatar Chilli

Three-Bean Tamatar Chilli is a sattvic vegetarian dish of carrot, celery stalk, olive oil. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Mexican
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 60 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 carrot
- 1 celery stalk
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 300 g cooked brown lentils, drained (cooked brown lentils, drained)
- 1/2 tsp organic chilli powder
- 1 tsp organic ground cumin
- 1 tsp organic paprika
- 1 tbsp tomato puree
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 organic canned tomatoes
- 500 g organic vegetable bouillon
- 100 g cooked black beans
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Soak the cooked lentils for 10 minutes.
2. Finely dice the celery, carrot & celery.
3. Add to a saucepan with the olive oil, season generously with salt and pepper and fry on a medium heat for 10 - 15 minutes.
4. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
5. Add the cooked lentils and cook for 4-5 minutes or until it takes on some colour.
6. Grate a little ginger and add to the pan along with the chilli powder, cumin, paprika and tomato puree. Cook for for 2-3 minutes.
7. Now add the lemon juice, let reduce for a minute or so then add the tomatoes and vegetable stock and gently simmer for 30 minutes to an hour.
8. Add the cooked black beans, heat through and serve with plenty of plant based yoghurt, sliced red chillies and coriander.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
