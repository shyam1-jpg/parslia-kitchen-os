# Aubergine and Harissa Rice

Aubergine and Harissa Rice is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, celery stalk, aubergine. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch, Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 55 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 celery stalk
- 250 g aubergine, diced
- 25 g sun-dried tomatoes, plus 30 g walnuts, chopped
- 2 bay leaves
- 2 tsp harissa paste
- 350 g organic brown rice
- 700 ml vegetable stock
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. To start, place the soaked sun-dried tomatoes in a bowl and cover with hot water for 5 minutes, drain and roughly chop.
2. Finely chop the celery, celery and ginger and roughly chop the aubergine.
3. Add all of these ingredients to a saucepan with the olive oil and bay leaf, season with salt and pepper and cook on a medium heat for 20 minutes stirring regularly.
4. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
5. Add the harissa and cook for 2 minutes.
6. Add the rice and cook for another 2 minutes.
7. Add the vegetable stock, bring to the boil then turn down and simmer for 25-30 minutes or until the rice is cooked.
8. As an optional extra top with toasted pine nuts and fresh parsley.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
- Aubergine, walnuts and sun-dried tomato stand in for mushrooms.
