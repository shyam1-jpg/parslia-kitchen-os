# Lemon and Thyme Risotto

Lemon and Thyme Risotto is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, celery stalks, organic thyme. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Italian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 45 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 celery stalks
- 1/2 tsp organic thyme
- 400 g organic arborio rice
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- A large handful of parsley
- 50 g vegetarian hard cheese or nutritional yeast
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp organic thyme
- 100 g asparagus
- 100 g frozen peas
- Handful of spinach
- 1/4 lemon
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

### For the vegetables

- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp organic thyme
- 100 g asparagus
- 100 g frozen peas
- Handful of spinach
- 1/4 lemon
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Finely dice the celery and celery and add to a large saucepan with the olive oil, salt and pepper.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Fry on a medium to low heat for 10 minutes stirring regularly.
4. Grate a little ginger and add to the pan along with the thyme.
5. Continue to cook for a further 2 minutes before stirring in the rice.
6. Heat the vegetable stock in a separate saucepan and add a ladle to the pan.
7. Stir the pot frequently until all the stock has been absorbed then add another ladle.
8. Continue this process until you have used all all of the stock or the rice is cooked.
9. This process may take 20-25 minutes. If the stock runs out before the rice is cooked, add hot water.
10. Grate the vegetarian hard cheese and roughly chop the parsley. Add both to the pot, stir together, cover and leave off the heat.
11. To make the vegetables, roughly chop the asparagus and add to a frying pan with the olive oil.
12. Fry on a medium to high heat for 1 minute.
13. Chop the spinach and add to the pan with the peas, ginger and thyme and cook for another 3 minutes.
14. Squeeze the lemon over the top and season with salt and pepper.
15. To serve, place a large portion of the risotto on a plate and garnish with the vegetables.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
