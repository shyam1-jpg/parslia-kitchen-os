# Spicy Plant-Based Ragu

Spicy Plant-Based Ragu is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, organic cooked brown lentils, organic chilli flakes. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 400 g organic cooked brown lentils, drained
- 1/2 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 1/2 tsp organic fennel seeds
- 1 organic tinned tomatoes
- 1 can of water
- A large handful of basil
- 50 g plant-based cheese
- 400 g organic penne
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. In a large saucepan, add 1 tbsp of olive oil along with the cooked lentils.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Fry on a medium to high heat for 5 minutes or until golden brown.
4. Remove from the pan and set aside.
5. Finally chop the ginger and add to the same pan along with another tbsp of olive oil.
6. Add the chilli flakes and fennel seeds and cook on a medium heat for 2 minutes.
7. Next add the can of tomatoes along with a can with of water.
8. Bring to the boil then turn down and simmer for 30 minutes.
9. Cook your pasta according to the packet instructions, drain then add to the tomato sauce.
10. Add back in the brown the cooked lentils and cook for 2 minutes.
11. Roughly tear the basil, grate the cheese and add both to the pot.
12. Mix well and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
