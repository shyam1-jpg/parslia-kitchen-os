# Penne Arrabbiata

Penne Arrabbiata is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, organic chilli flakes, organic chopped tomatoes. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 30 min
- **Yield:** 2

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 1 organic chopped tomatoes
- 1/2 can water
- Natural Fine Sea Salt
- Organic Ground pepper

## Method

1. Grate a little ginger and fry in olive oil along with the chilli flakes for 2-3 minutes taking care not to burn the ginger.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Add the tinned tomatoes along with half a can of water.
4. Add salt and pepper generously, bring to the boil then turn down and simmer for half an hour.
5. Stir frequently whilst breaking the down the tomatoes to create a chunky sauce.
6. Cook the penne according to the packet instructions, strain and add to the arrabbiata sauce, mix well and serve with your favourite cheese.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
