# Crispy Potatoes with Lemon and Feta

Crispy Potatoes with Lemon and Feta is a sattvic vegetarian dish of new potatoes, organic extra virgin olive oil, organic dried oregano. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 45 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

### For the potatoes :

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 kg new potatoes
- 3 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- tsp organic dried oregano
- 1 unwaxed lemon, thinly sliced
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

### For the pickled onions :

- 2 tbsp organic fresh lemon juice
- Pinch natural fine sea salt

### To serve :

- 100 g crumbled paneer or plant feta, crumbled
- Small handful fresh mint, finely chopped

## Method

1. Start by placing the new potatoes in a pan of cold, salted water.
2. Bring to the boil, then simmer until just tender.
3. Drain well, then leave them to steam dry and cool slightly so they’re easier to handle.
4. While the potatoes are cooling, add the a pinch of hing to a small bowl with the lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
5. Toss together and leave to soften and lightly pickle.
6. Once cooled, place the potatoes on a large roasting tray and gently crush each one with the back of a spoon or a glass so they flatten and split slightly.
7. This helps create lots of crispy edges.
8. Drizzle over the olive oil, scatter with oregano, salt and pepper, then add the lemon slices to the tray.
9. Toss everything gently so the potatoes are coated without breaking them up too much.
10. Roast in a hot oven until the potatoes are deeply golden and crisp on the edges, turning once during cooking.
11. The lemon slices will soften and caramelise, becoming sweet and slightly jammy.
12. Transfer the hot potatoes to a serving dish, then scatter with crumbled feta so it softens slightly.
13. Finish with the fresh mint and the lightly pickled celery over the top.
14. Serve straight away while warm, with extra roasted lemon on the side for squeezing over.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
