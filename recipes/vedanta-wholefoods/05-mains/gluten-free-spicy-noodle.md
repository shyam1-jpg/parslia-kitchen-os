# Gluten Free Spicy Noodle

Gluten Free Spicy Noodle is a sattvic vegetarian dish of gluten free brown rice vermicelli noodles, organic sesame oil, large thumb of ginger. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Asian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, sesame, soya

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 200 g gluten free brown rice vermicelli noodles
- 1 tbsp organic sesame oil
- 1 large thumb of ginger
- 1 tbsp gojujang
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tbsp gluten free soy sauce
- 1 tsp chilli oil
- 300 g broccoli stems
- 1 red pepper
- A handful of coriander

## Method

1. To prep, finely and ginger and peel and finely grate the ginger.
2. Chop the broccoli stems into approximately 2cm pieces.
3. Cut the red pepper into thin slices.
4. Cook the noodles according to packet instructions, then plunge into cold water to stop the cooking process, drain and set aside.
5. Now heat the sesame oil in a wok or frying pan and add the fennel or celery.
6. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
7. Fry on a medium to high heat for a minute and then add the ginger and ginger.
8. Cook for another minute and then add the gojujang, lemon juice, soy sauce and chilli oil.
9. Fry for 2 more minutes and then add the broccoli stems along with a splash of hot water.
10. After another 2 minutes add the noodles and red peppers, mix well and add a splash more hot water if the pan is too dry.
11. Cook for a final 4 minutes before adding the coriander stems and coriander then serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
