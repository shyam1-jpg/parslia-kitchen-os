# Walnut and Aubergine Ragu

Walnut and Aubergine Ragu is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic chilli flakes, tins of tomatoes, tin of water. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 55 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1/2 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 2 tins of tomatoes
- 1/2 tin of water
- 2 tsp organic tomato puree
- 5 g sun-dried tomatoes, plus 30 g walnuts, chopped
- 500 g aubergine, diced
- 1 tbsp organic pine nuts
- A handful of parsley
- 400 g organic penne pasta
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Firstly make the tomato sauce.
2. Grate a little ginger and add to a saucepan with the chilli flakes and olive oil.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Fry on a medium heat for 2 minutes so that the ginger colours.
5. Add the tinned tomatoes along with half a tin of water, the tomato puree and salt and pepper.
6. Simmer on a medium heat for about half an hour, stirring occasionally until the sauce thickens.
7. Next, soak the soaked sun-dried tomatoes in warm water for 10 mins, then drain and finely chop.
8. Roughly slice the aubergine and add to a frying pan with the soaked sun-dried tomatoes and 1 tbsp of olive oil.
9. Cook on a medium heat for 10 minutes.
10. Finely chop the celery and add to the pan and season generously.
11. Cook for a further 10 minutes stirring frequently.
12. Roughly chop the parsley and add to the aubergine along with the pine nuts.
13. Mix together and remove from the heat.
14. Cook your pasta according to the packet instructions and drain well.
15. Mix your cooked pasta with the tomato sauce, serve and spoon the aubergine ragu on top.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
- Aubergine, walnuts and sun-dried tomato stand in for mushrooms.
