# Walnut Tempeh Burger

Walnut Tempeh Burger is a sattvic vegetarian dish of tomato puree mixed with chilli powder and a little maple, soy sauce, organic sesame oil. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Main Course
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Prep:** 40 min
- **Cook:** 5 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, sesame, soya

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp tomato puree mixed with chilli powder and a little maple
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp organic sesame oil
- 200 g organic tempeh
- 2 aubergine, cut into 1 cm slices
- organic chilli sauce
- 2 organic bread rolls
- 80 g walnuts, finely chopped

## Method

1. Firstly put the chilli-tomato paste, soy sauce and sesame oil in a bowl and mix together.
2. Cut the tempeh into approximately 0.5cm slices, add to the bowl and coat with the chilli-tomato paste mix.
3. Now cut the aubergine into 1cm slices and coat with the chilli-tomato paste as well.
4. Leave to marinade for half an hour.
5. Take a large frying pan and cook the tempeh and aubergine on a high heat for 5 minutes turning occasionally.
6. Cut the rolls in half and add some of the sriracha.
7. Divide the tempeh and aubergine between the rolls and top with your favourite salad.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Aubergine, walnuts and sun-dried tomato stand in for mushrooms.
