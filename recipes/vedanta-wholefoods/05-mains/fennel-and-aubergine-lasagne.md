# Fennel and Aubergine Lasagne

Fennel and Aubergine Lasagne is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, thyme sprigs, aubergine. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 20 min
- **Cook:** 120 min
- **Yield:** 6 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 3 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 thyme sprigs
- 500 g aubergine, diced
- 25 g sun-dried tomatoes, plus 30 g walnuts, chopped
- 1/2 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 2 cans organic tinned tomatoes
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 500 g cooked organic french lentils
- 2 tsp fresh lemon juice
- 100 g pitted black olives
- 100 g organic pinenuts
- Large handful of parsley
- 150 g cheese grated or plant-based alternative
- 150 ml cream or plant-based alternative
- 1 packet organic lasagne sheets
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Chop the leaks into 1cm rounds and add to a frying pan with 1 tbsp of olive oil.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Add the thyme, season with salt and pepper and cook on a low heat for 20-25 minutes stirring regularly.
4. Roughly chop the aubergine and place in a baking tray, add the thyme, season with salt and pepper and drizzle with olive oil.
5. Finely chop the soaked sun-dried tomatoes and scatter over the aubergine then bake in a 200c oven for 20 minutes.
6. Next, grate a little ginger and add to a saucepan with the chilli flakes and 1 tbsp of olive oil.
7. Cook on a medium heat for 2 minutes then add the tinned tomatoes, tomato paste, lemon juice and vegetable stock.
8. Season generously with salt and pepper and gently simmer for 30 minutes.
9. Roughly chop the olives, pine nuts and parsley then you are ready to assemble the lasagne.
10. Take a large baking dish and add a layer of the cook fennel followed by aubergine, lentils then a sprinkling of the chopped olives, pine nuts and parsley.
11. Place a layer of lasagne sheets on top then cover with the tomato sauce.
12. Repeat the process 2 more times then cover with foil and bake in an oven at 200c for 35 minutes.
13. Remove from the oven and sprinkle with the grated cheese and cream and cook for a final 10 minutes uncovered.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
- Aubergine, walnuts and sun-dried tomato stand in for mushrooms.
