# Butterbean Stew

Butterbean Stew is a sattvic vegetarian dish of carrot, celery stalks, bay leaf. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 50 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** nuts

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 carrot
- 2 celery stalks
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/2 tsp rosemary, chopped
- 250 g potato
- 250 g organic butter beans (cooked)
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 150 ml cashew cream or oat cream
- large handful of parsley
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Chop the celery, leak, carrot and celery stalks into approximately 1cm pieces.
2. Place the vegetable in a large saucepan, season with salt and pepper and fry in 1 tbsp of of olive oil on a medium to heat for 10 minutes stirring frequently.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Grate a little ginger and add to the pot along with the rosemary, ginger and bay leaf. Cook for a further 10 minutes and continue to stir.
5. Peel and chop the potatoes into approximately 2cm pieces and add to the pot along with the vegetable stock.
6. Bring to the boil then turn down and simmer for 20 minutes.
7. Add the butter beans and cook for another 5 minutes.
8. Remove the bay leaf and half of the stew from the pan.
9. Blitz the remaining half until smooth.
10. Return the non blitzed half to the pan along with the cream and bring back to a simmer.
11. Roughly chop the parsley, add to the pot and serve the stew with sourdough croutons.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
