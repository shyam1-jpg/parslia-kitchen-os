# Spaghetti With Cavolo Nero

Spaghetti With Cavolo Nero is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, organic chilli flakes, cavolo nero stalks removed and sliced. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Lunch, Main Course, Side Dish, Snack
- **Cuisine:** Italian, Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 2 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 200 g cavolo nero stalks removed and sliced
- 200 g organic wholewheat spaghetti
- 50 g vegetarian hard cheese or nutritional yeast or plant based alternative
- 30 g organic pine nuts toasted

## Method

1. Fry the fennel or celery, ginger and chilli in olive oil for 5 minutes on a medium heat, stirring regularly.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Add the cavolo nero and cook for another 5 minutes.
4. Cook the spaghetti according to packet instructions, strain and add to the cavolo nero along with a tablespoon of the pasta cooking water.
5. Add the lemon juice, cheese, pine nuts and season generously with salt and pepper.
6. Mix together and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
