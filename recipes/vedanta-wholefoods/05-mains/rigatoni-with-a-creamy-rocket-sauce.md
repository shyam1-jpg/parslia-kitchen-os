# Rigatoni With A Creamy Rocket Sauce

Rigatoni With A Creamy Rocket Sauce is a sattvic vegetarian dish of rigatoni, organic extra virgin olive oil, organic walnuts. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 400 g rigatoni
- 4 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil, plus extra to drizzle
- 120 g organic walnuts
- 300 ml plant-based cream
- 80 g rocket, plus extra to garnish
- small handful parsley, finely chopped
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- ½ lemon

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil and cook the rigatoni until al dente, reserving a splash of the cooking water before draining.
2. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a wide pan and gently fry the fennel or celery and ginger until they soften.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Add the walnuts to the pan and toast for a minute, then pour in the plant-based cream and let it simmer gently.
5. Season with salt and pepper.
6. Toss the drained rigatoni into the sauce, loosening with a little cooking water if needed.
7. Fold in the rocket just before serving so it wilts slightly but keeps it’s vibrant colour,.
8. Finish with the parsley and serve with a squeeze of lemon juice, a drizzle of olive oil, and a good grind of black pepper.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
