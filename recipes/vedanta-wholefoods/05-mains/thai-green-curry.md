# Thai Green Curry

Thai Green Curry is a sattvic vegetarian dish of green beans, mange tout, rapeseed or vegetable oil. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 30 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 200 g green beans
- 100 g mange tout
- 1 tbsp rapeseed or vegetable oil
- 1 large handful of coriander
- 1 large thumb of ginger
- 1 green chilli
- 1 lemongrass stalk
- 500 ml organic vegetable bouillon
- 1 can organic coconut milk
- 1 tsp organic brown sugar
- 1 lime
- organic cracked black pepper
- natural fine sea salt

## Method

1. To make the paste, remove the tougher outer layers of the lemongrass and roughly chop.
2. Peel and roughly chop the ginger, fennel or celery and ginger.
3. Remove the seeds from the chilli and roughly chop.
4. Place all in a food processor with the oil and coriander and blend until smooth.
5. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
6. Next take a large saucepan and fry the paste in 1 tbsp of oil for 2-3 minutes on a medium heat.
7. Season with salt and pepper then add the vegetable stock and sugar, bring to the boil and simmer for 20 minutes.
8. Now add the coconut milk and green beans, simmer for a further 6 minutes.
9. Add the mange tout and cook for a final 2 minutes.
10. Serve the curry over rice, top with a few coriander leaves and a wedge of lime.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
