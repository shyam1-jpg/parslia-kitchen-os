# Kale and Fennel Pasta

Kale and Fennel Pasta is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, organic cooked brown lentils, red chilli. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 300 g organic cooked brown lentils, drained
- 1/2 red chilli
- 1/2 tsp organic fennel seeds
- 150 g kale
- 1 tbsp organic pinenuts
- 100 g vegetarian hard cheese or nutritional yeast
- A large handful of basil
- 400 g organic penne pasta

## Method

1. Add the cooked lentils to a pan with the olive oil and cook for 5 minutes on a medium heat.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Add to the pan and season generously with salt and pepper.
4. Finely chop the chilli. Lightly crush the fennel seeds.
5. Add all to the pan and continue to cook for another 5 minutes whilst stirring regularly.
6. Next, remove the stems from the kale. Cook in boiling salted water, drain and roughly chop then add to the pan.
7. Cook the pasta according to packet instructions, drain and add as well.
8. Finally, grate the cheese and add along with the basil and pine nuts.
9. Mix well and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
