# Cumin Spiced Potatoes

Cumin Spiced Potatoes is a sattvic vegetarian dish of new potatoes, organic extra virgin olive oil, organic ground cumin. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Indian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 kg new potatoes
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tsp organic ground cumin
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- A handful of coriander
- 1/2 lemon
- 200 g yoghurt or plant-based alternative
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Place the potatoes in a saucepan of cold salted water.
2. Bring to the boil and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Drain then allow to cool.
4. Place the potatoes on a baking tray and drizzle with the olive oil and season with salt and pepper.
5. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
6. Roast at 220c for 20 minutes then remove and lightly squash the potatoes with a fork.
7. Lightly crush the cumin seeds in a pestle and mortar.
8. Add the cumin seeds and roast for a further 10-15 minutes or until the potatoes are golden brown.
9. To make the dip, grate a little ginger and coriander and juice the lemon.
10. Mix with the yoghurt, olive oil and season well.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
