# Plant-Based Black Bean Burrito

Plant-Based Black Bean Burrito is a sattvic vegetarian dish of aubergine, organic thyme, organic paprika. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Snack
- **Cuisine:** Mexican
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 500 g aubergine, diced
- 1/2 tsp organic thyme
- 1 tsp organic paprika
- 200 g organic brown rice (cooked)
- 200 g organic black turtle beans (cooked)
- 1 cos lettuce
- 100 g cherry tomatoes
- 2 avocado
- 1 tsp organic jalapeños
- 1 lime
- 2 tbsp plant-based yoghurt
- 50 g plant-based cheese, grated
- 4 large tortilla wraps
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Roughly chop the aubergine and add to a large frying pan with the olive oil.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Fry on a medium to high heat for 5 minutes stirring regularly.
4. Grate a little ginger and add to the aubergine along with the thyme and paprika.
5. Turn down the heat and cook for a further 5 minutes.
6. Add the brown rice and black beans and season generously with salt and pepper.
7. Cook for a final 5 minutes then set aside.
8. Shred the cos lettuce, finely chop the coriander stems and roughly chop the tomato and avocado.
9. To assemble the burritos, firstly warm the tortillas in a dry frying pan.
10. Place a large portion on the aubergine, rice and beans on the centre of the tortilla.
11. Top with the lettuce, coriander stems and tomato and avocado.
12. Add some of the jalapeños, grated cheese, plant-based yoghurt and a squeeze of lime juice.
13. Fold the side of the tortilla over the filling then carefully roll up from the bottom.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
- Aubergine, walnuts and sun-dried tomato stand in for mushrooms.
