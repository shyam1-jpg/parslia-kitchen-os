# Orecchiette with Broccoli and Hing

Orecchiette with Broccoli and Hing is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, sourdough bread, broccoli stems. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 100 g sourdough bread
- 200 g broccoli stems
- 1 red chilli
- 2 large handfuls of spinach
- 1/2 lemon
- 400 g orecchiette
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Take the bread and cut as finely as possible then fry in a pan with 1 tbsp of olive oil until golden brown.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Next, take a large pot and cook for orecchiette in boiling salted water for 6 minutes.
4. Chop the broccoli into 2cm pieces, add to the pot and cook for a further 3 minutes.
5. Drain the pasta and broccoli reserving a mug full of the cooking liquor.
6. Grate a little ginger, remove the seeds from the chillis and finely slice.
7. Take a large frying pan and add 1 tbsp of olive oil and gently fry the ginger and chilli for 2 minutes.
8. Add the pasta and broccoli to the frying pan along with the spinach and juice of half a lemon.
9. Season generously with salt and pepper then mix and gently cook for a final 2 minutes.
10. Add a splash of the cooking liquor if the pasta becomes too dry.
11. Serve with your favourite cheese and the sourdough breadcrumbs scattered on top.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
