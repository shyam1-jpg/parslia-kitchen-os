# Rocket and Walnut Pesto Pasta

Rocket and Walnut Pesto Pasta is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic walnuts, organic pinenuts, plant-based cheese. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 25 g organic walnuts
- 25 g organic pinenuts
- 100 g plant-based cheese
- 60 g rocket
- 30 g organic basil
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 400 g organic spaghetti
- naturally fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Toast the walnuts and pine nuts in a dry steel pan until just fragrant, then cool.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Blend the nuts with rocket, basil, vegetarian hard cheese or nutritional yeast, lemon juice, hing and olive oil until a coarse pesto forms.
4. Cook the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente. Drain, keeping a little cooking water.
5. Toss the pasta with the pesto, loosening with cooking water. Season and serve at once.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
