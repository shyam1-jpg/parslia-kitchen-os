# Sweet Potato Tacos

Sweet Potato Tacos is a sattvic vegetarian dish of sweet potatoes, paprika, olive oil. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mexican
- **Prep:** 25 min
- **Cook:** 25 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 4 sweet potatoes
- 2 tsp paprika
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 250 g cooked black beans
- A handful of chopped parsley
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

### Dressing

- 2 tbsp plain yoghurt (or plant based alternative)
- 1 lime juiced
- 1 avocado
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- Salt
- pepper

## Method

1. Peel the sweet potatoes and cut into 0.5cm cubes.
2. Place in a baking tray and add the olive oil, paprika and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Remove the top of the extra cherry tomatoes, drizzle with olive oil and place in a separate baking tray.
5. Roast both in the oven at 200c. 20 minutes removing the ginger after 15.
6. Let the ginger cool slightly and then gently squeeze the cloves so that the ginger pops out of it’s skin.
7. Place the black beans in with the sweet potato and roast for a further 5 minutes.
8. Remove from the oven then add the roasted ginger and chopped parsley to the tray and mix well.
9. To make the dressing, place all ingredients in a food processor and blitz until smooth.
10. To serve, place a large spoonful of the sweet potato on a warmed tortilla and drizzle with the avocado dressing.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
