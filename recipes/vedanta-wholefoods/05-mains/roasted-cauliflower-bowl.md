# Roasted Cauliflower Bowl

Roasted Cauliflower Bowl is a sattvic salad of large cauliflower, organic extra virgin olive oil, organic paprika. Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Lunch, Main Course, Salad, Side Dish, Snack
- **Cuisine:** French, Italian, Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 25 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- 1 large cauliflower
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp organic paprika
- 1/2 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 50 g organic pumpkin seeds
- 250 g organic tricolour quinoa (cooked)
- 4 large handfuls of washed spinach
- 8 radish finely sliced
- Half large cucumber sliced
- fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

### Dressing

- 2 tbsp plant based natural yoghurt
- 1 organic chilli flakes
- 6 mint leaves
- 2 tbsp organic lemon juice
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. Remove any leaves from the base of the cauliflower and cut into 4 pieces. Take each quarter and cut in another 3 or 4 equal sized pieces and place on a baking tray.
2. Add the paprika, chilli flakes, salt and pepper then drizzle with olive oil and toss together.
3. Place in an oven at 220c and roast for 20 minutes (turning over the cauliflower every 5 minutes). Add the pumpkin seeds and roast for a further 5 minutes.
4. Now add all of the dressing ingredients to a food processor and blend until smooth.
5. To serve, place a generous portion of the cooked cauliflower in a bowl along with a large spoonful of grains and spinach plus some sliced radish and cucumber.
6. Drizzle over the dressing and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
