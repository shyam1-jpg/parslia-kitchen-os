# Broccoli and Aubergine Quesadilla

Broccoli and Aubergine Quesadilla is a sattvic vegetarian dish of small tortillas, aubergine, tenderstem broccoli. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mexican
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 35 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 8 small tortillas
- 250 g aubergine, diced
- 250 g tenderstem broccoli
- 1 red chilli
- 30 g organic hazelnuts
- 50 g paneer or plant cheese, grated
- organic vegan mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Warm olive oil in a steel pan. Bloom the hing, then fry diced aubergine, tenderstem broccoli and chilli until golden.
2. Season, then stir through chopped toasted hazelnuts.
3. Lay a tortilla in a dry pan, scatter plant cheese or paneer, the vegetables, then a second tortilla.
4. Toast both sides until crisp. Rest 1 minute, cut into wedges, and serve with a lemon-yogurt dip.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
- Aubergine, walnuts and sun-dried tomato stand in for mushrooms.
