# Thai Basil Stir Fry

Thai Basil Stir Fry is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic tempeh, large thumb of ginger, red chilli. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Asian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, sesame, soya

## Ingredients

- 400 g organic tempeh
- 1 large thumb of ginger
- 1 red chilli
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp tamari with a squeeze of lemon
- 1 tbsp ketjap manis
- 50 g peas
- 30 g organic peanuts (toasted)
- A handful of Thai basil
- 1 tbsp sesame oil

## Method

1. Heat a large wok or frying pan and add the sesame oil.
2. Add the cooked lentils, break up in the pan and cook on a high heat for 4-5 minutes or until lightly browned.
3. Peel and grate the ginger. finely chop the chilli.
4. Add to the pan and cook for 2 minutes.
5. Add the soy, tamari and lemon and ketjap manis and continue to cook for another 2 minutes.
6. Add the peas and cook for a final 2 minutes.
7. Finely chop the toasted peanuts and add to the pan along with the Thai basil.
8. Give a final mix and serve over rice.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
