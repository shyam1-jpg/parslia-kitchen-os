# Chana Masala

Chana Masala is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, organic mustard seeds, small ginger thumb. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Main Course, Side Dish
- **Cuisine:** Indian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 45 min
- **Yield:** 2 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** mustard

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp organic mustard seeds
- 1 small ginger thumb
- 1 organic chilli
- 1 tsp organic cumin
- 1 tsp organic coriander
- 1 tsp organic turmeric
- 1 dsp tomato paste
- 250 g organic chickpea scooked
- 250 ml water
- natural sea salt crystals
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Fry the mustard seeds in oil, on a medium heat until they start to pop.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Finely chop the celery, add to the mustard seeds and cook for 5 minutes.
4. Now peel the ginger and grate, finely dice the ginger and chilli.
5. Add to the celery and cook for a further 10 minutes.
6. Next add the spices and tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes, stirring regularly, taking care to not burn the spices.
7. Add 250ml water, bring to a gentle simmer and cook for half an hour.
8. Add 250g cooked chickpeas, cook for a final 5 minutes and you are ready to serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
