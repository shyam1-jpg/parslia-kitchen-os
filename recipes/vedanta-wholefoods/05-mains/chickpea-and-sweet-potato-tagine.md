# Chickpea and Sweet Potato Tagine

Chickpea and Sweet Potato Tagine is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, sweet potato, large thumb of ginger. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Main Course, Side Dish
- **Cuisine:** moroccan
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 30 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 sweet potato
- 1 large thumb of ginger
- 2 cloves of
- 1 tsp organic ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp organic ground cinnamon
- 2 tsp tagine paste
- 2 tsp tomato paste
- 2 tsp organic maple syrup
- 1 tin organic tomatoes
- 500 ml organic vegetable bouillon
- 250 g cooked chickpeas
- Handful of coriander

## Method

1. Finely and add to a large saucepan with the olive oil.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Season with salt and pepper and fry on a medium heat for 2 minutes.
4. Peel the sweet potato and dice into 1cm cubes.
5. Grate a little ginger then peel and grate the ginger and add to the pan along with the sweet potato.
6. Cook for another 2 minutes then add the cumin, cinnamon, tomato paste and maple syrup and cook for a further 2 minutes.
7. Next add the tinned tomatoes and vegetable stock, bring to the boil then turn down to simmer for 20 minutes.
8. Add the chickpeas and cook for a final 5 minutes.
9. Serve with a scattering of chopped coriander.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
