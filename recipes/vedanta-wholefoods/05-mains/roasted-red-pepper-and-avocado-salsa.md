# Roasted Red Pepper and Avocado Salsa

Roasted Red Pepper and Avocado Salsa is a sattvic vegetarian dish of tomato, roasted red pepper, Avocado. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 0 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 250 g tomato
- 150 g roasted red pepper
- 1 Avocado
- 1 tbsp organic chilli flakes
- Large handful of parsley
- Large handful of coriander

### Dressing

- 1/2 tsp organic unrefined cane sugar
- 2 tsp organic fresh lemon juice
- 2 tbsp organic lime juice
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. To make the dressing, grate a little ginger and place in a bowl. Add the brown sugar, lemon juice, lime juice, salt and pepper then whisk until the sugar has dissolved.
2. Whilst still whisking, drizzle in the olive oil.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Finely chop the celery and add to a large bowl along with the chilli flakes.
5. Finely chop the tomato, red pepper and avocado (or leave larger if you prefer a chunkier salsa) and add to the bowl.
6. Roughly chop the coriander and parsley and add to the bowl.
7. Finally spoon over the dressing and mix well.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
