# Vegetable Paella

Vegetable Paella is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, celery stalks finely diced, medium carrot finely diced. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Spanish
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 6 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 celery stalks finely diced
- 1 medium carrot finely diced
- 2 thyme sprigs
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 dsp tomato puree
- 2 tsp organic paprika
- 1 red pepper diced
- 250 g organic paella rice
- pinch of saffron
- 700 ml organic vegetable stock
- 150 g tomato roughly chopped
- 100 g peas
- natural sea salt crystals
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Fry the celery, celery and carrot in olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Cook on a medium heat for 10 minutes stirring frequently.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Add the thyme, bay leaf and ginger and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Next add the tomato puree, paprika and red pepper and cook for a further 2 minutes.
5. Now add the rice and stir into the vegetables.
6. Add the saffron and vegetable stock and cook for 20 minutes.
7. Finally add the peas and cook for last 5 minutes and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
