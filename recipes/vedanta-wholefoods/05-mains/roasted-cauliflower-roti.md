# Roasted Cauliflower Roti

Roasted Cauliflower Roti is a sattvic vegetarian dish of roti or flatbread, large cauliflower, organic extra virgin olive oil. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 35 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 8 roti or flatbread
- 1 large cauliflower
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp organic cumin
- 1 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 1 tsp organic paprika
- 1 tbsp natural yoghurt or plant-based alternative
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

### For the salsa:

- 200 g cherry tomatoes
- 1/2 red chilli
- 1/2 lime
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- A large handful of coriander
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Cut the cauliflower into approximately 3cm pieces and place in a baking tray.
2. Sprinkle with the cumin, paprika and chilli flakes then drizzle with the olive oil.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Mix well then roast in a 200c oven for 25-30 minutes until the cauliflower is golden.
5. For the salsa, finely chop the cherry tomatoes, celery, red chilli, ginger and place in a bowl.
6. Juice the lime, roughly chop the coriander then place both in the same bowl along with the olive oil.
7. Season generously with salt and pepper and mix well.
8. To serve, heat the roti then place a portion of the cauliflower on top, followed by a large spoonful of the salsa and a drizzle of yoghurt.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
