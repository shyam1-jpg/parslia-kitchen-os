# Sesame Noodle

Sesame Noodle is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic skinny soba noodles, organic peanut butter, organic tamari soya sauce. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Asian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, nuts, sesame, soya, dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 200 g organic skinny soba noodles
- 1 tbsp organic peanut butter
- 2 tbsp organic tamari soya sauce
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tbsp organic maple syrup
- 1 thumb of ginger
- 1/2 red chilli
- A large handful of spinach
- 25 g organic peanuts
- 25 g organic sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp organic toasted sesame oil

## Method

1. Firstly cook the noodles according to packet instructions, then plunge into cold water to stop the cooking process, drain and set aside.
2. Place the peanut butter in a small bowl along with 2 tbsp of hot water.
3. Mix together to loosen, then add the tamari, lemon juice and maple syrup.
4. Mix again and put aside.
5. Peel the ginger and finely grate.
6. Finely chop the chilli.
7. Heat the sesame oil in a wok or frying pan and add the ginger, ginger and chilli.
8. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
9. Cook on a high heat for 2 minutes and then add the noodles.
10. Continue to cook for another 2 minutes and then add the peanut butter mixture.
11. Add a dash more water if the sauce is too thick.
12. Cook for a final 2 minutes and then fold in the spinach.
13. Serve topped with chopped coriander stems, peanuts and sesame seeds.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
