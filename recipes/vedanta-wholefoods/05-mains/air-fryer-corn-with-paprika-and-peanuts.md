# Air Fryer Corn with Paprika and Peanuts

Air Fryer Corn with Paprika and Peanuts is a sattvic vegetarian dish of fresh corn cobs, organic extra virgin olive oil, organic paprika. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- 4 fresh corn cobs
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp organic paprika
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- 2 tbsp water

### For the garnish:

- Lime wedges
- Chopped coriander
- Sliced red chillies
- roasted peanuts (crushed)

## Method

1. Line the air fryer basket with parchment paper made for air fryers.
2. Pour a small splash of water (about 2 tbsp) onto the parchment to create a touch of steam and keep the corn juicy.
3. Brush the corn evenly with olive oil, making sure all the kernels get a good coating.
4. Sprinkle over the paprika, then season generously with salt and pepper.
5. Arrange the cobs in a single layer on top of the parchment.
6. Air fry at 200°C for 12 minutes, turning once halfway through, until the corn is lightly charred and golden in spots.
7. Serve hot straight from the fryer with a squeeze of lime, a scattering of coriander, fresh sliced red chillies, and a generous sprinkle of crushed roasted peanuts.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
