# Coconut Laksa

Coconut Laksa is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, organic coconut milk, litre vegetable stock. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Asian
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 35 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, soya

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 400 ml organic coconut milk
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 250 g tofu
- 1 tbsp organic tamari soya sauce
- 200 g cooked noodles
- 1 thumb fresh ginger, grated
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped
- 1 lemongrass stalk, bruised
- 1 lime, juiced
- handful fresh coriander

## Method

1. Bloom the hing in hot oil for a few seconds, then stir in grated ginger, chopped chilli and bruised lemongrass until fragrant.
2. Pour in the coconut milk and vegetable stock. Simmer gently for 10 minutes.
3. Add the tofu cubes, tamari and a pinch of unrefined sugar. Warm through for 5 minutes.
4. Divide cooked noodles between steel bowls, ladle over the laksa, and finish with lime and coriander.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
