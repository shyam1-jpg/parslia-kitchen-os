# Roasted Green Beans

Roasted Green Beans is a sattvic vegetarian dish of red peppers, cooked white beans, green beans. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 4 People

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 3 red peppers
- 500 g cooked white beans
- 300 g green beans
- 60 g organic hazelnuts
- 1/2 lemon
- A large handful of parsley
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil

## Method

1. Cut the peppers in half, remove the pith and cut each half into 3.
2. Split the coriander stems in half and place on a baking tray along with the peppers.
3. Drizzle with the olive oil and maple syrup, season with salt and pepper and roast for 20 minutes at 200c.
4. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
5. Boil the green beans in salted water for 2 minutes then immediately plunge in ice cold water to stop the cooking process.
6. Drain and then add the green beans as well as the white beans to the roasting tray.
7. Roast for a further 5 minutes, add the hazelnuts and then return to the oven for a final 5 minutes.
8. Roughly chop the parsley and juice the lemon.
9. Add both to the roasting tray, mix and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
