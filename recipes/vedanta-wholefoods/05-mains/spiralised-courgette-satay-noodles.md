# Spiralised Courgette Satay Noodles

Spiralised Courgette Satay Noodles is a sattvic vegetarian dish of courgettes spiralised, organic peanut butter, organic tamari soy sauce. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Asian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 5 min
- **Yield:** 2
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, nuts, soya, dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 2 courgettes spiralised
- 3 tbsp organic peanut butter
- 2 tbsp organic tamari soy sauce
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tsp organic maple syrup
- 2 to 3 tbsp warm water, to reach the desired consistency
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil, to fry
- pinch organic chilli flakes
- lime wedge to garnish

## Method

1. Spiralise the courgettes and place them in a bowl.
2. Combine all the sauce ingredients in a blender and pulse until smooth.
3. Gently heat the olive oil and then add the spiralised courgette.
4. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
5. Fry the courgette for a few minutes.
6. Take off the heat and stir through the satay sauce.
7. Garnish with chilli and lime.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
