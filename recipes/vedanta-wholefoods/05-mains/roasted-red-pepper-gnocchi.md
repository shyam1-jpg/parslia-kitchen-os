# Roasted Red Pepper Gnocchi

Roasted Red Pepper Gnocchi is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic tomatoes (canned), organic chilli flakes, organic thyme. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 organic tomatoes (canned)
- 1/2 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 1/2 tsp organic thyme
- 200 g roasted red peppers
- 500 g gnocchi
- A handful of parsley
- 50 g vegetarian hard cheese or nutritional yeast
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Grate a little ginger and add to a saucepan with the olive oil and chilli flakes.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Fry on a medium heat for 2 minutes then add the can of tomatoes.
4. Roughly chop the roasted red peppers and add to the pan reserving a tablespoon for garnish.
5. Season with salt and pepper.
6. Bring the sauce to the boil then then simmer for 30 minutes stirring regularly breaking up the tomatoes as you go.
7. To prepare the gnocchi, take a frying pan and add 1 tbsp of olive oil and fry the gnocchi on a medium to high heat for 4-5 minutes or until golden.
8. Grate the cheese and roughly chop the parsley.
9. To serve, put a large portion of the sauce on a plate, top with the fried gnocchi and garnish with some chopped parsley, grated cheese and the reserved roasted red peppers.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
