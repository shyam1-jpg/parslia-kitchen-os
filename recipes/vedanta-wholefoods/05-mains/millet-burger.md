# Millet Burger

Millet Burger is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic hulled millet, organic sun dried tomatoes (soaked), organic unblanched almonds. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Lunch, Main Course, Snack
- **Prep:** 20 min
- **Cook:** 20 min
- **Yield:** 4
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- 130 g organic hulled millet
- 50 g organic sun dried tomatoes (soaked)
- 50 g organic unblanched almonds
- 50 g organic roasted pumpkin seeds
- 50 g organic tapioca flour
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp organic cracked black pepper
- 1 tsp organic dried basil
- 1 tsp organic dried oregano
- 5 tbsp organic olive oil

## Method

1. Rinse the grains twice with boiling water over the sieve. Place in the pot and cover with freshly boiled water around 1cm above the level of millet, add ½ teaspoon of salt, cover with a lid and check after 10 minutes if millet is soft. If it needs a bit more cooking but water has been absorbed, add just a little bit. When millet is soft, take the lid off and let the steam out for a bit.
2. In the meantime, put the almonds into a blender, blitz until coarse, add sundried tomatoes and keep blending till you get a smooth paste.
3. In a large bowl, mix cooked millet- it will bind better if it’s still warm, sundried tomato paste, toasted seeds, add a bit of starch and keep mixing, add spices, salt, taste, try to make a patty now, by forming a ball and flattening it. It should be really easy to make a burger, so if it is too crumbly, add more starch. The mixture should be a bit wet and oily. This is where you need to trust your hands and feel the textures of food, be intuitive and don’t be scared!
4. You can keep formed burgers in the fridge, so they firm up a bit, but if you are in a hurry, not to worry, burgers are ready to be either shallow fried or baked!
5. You can cover burgers in breadcrumbs now, if you choose to.
6. Warm up some rapeseed oil on the pan or heat up your oven to 180 degrees. Fry till golden and flip, if you are using the oven, bake for approx. 15 min and flip on the other side just for 5.
7. Eat cold, hot, in a bun or with salads, fries or whatever else you fancy! These are just so nice! Yum.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
