# Salsa Verde

Salsa Verde is a sattvic vegetarian dish of new potatoes, green beans, edamame. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch, Main Course, Side Dish
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 20 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** mustard

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 800 g new potatoes
- 250 g green beans
- 150 g edamame
- A large handful each of basil, parsley & mint
- 1 dsp capers
- 1 large tsp dijon mustard
- 1 tsp olive oil
- Half a lemon juiced
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
2. Place all of the salsa verde ingredients into a food processor and blend until smooth.
3. Boil the green beans & edamame in salted water for 6 minutes then drain and plunge into cold iced water to cool. Drain and set aside.
4. Put the new potatoes in a large pot of cold water and bring to the boil. Add a large pinch of salt, turn down to a gentle simmer and cook for 15 minutes or until tender.
5. Let the potatoes cool slightly, then slice and place in a bowl and spoon over the salsa verde and mix well. Add the Green Beans and Edamame, mix again and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
