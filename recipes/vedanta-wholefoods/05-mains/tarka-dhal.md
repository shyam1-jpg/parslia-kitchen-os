# Tarka Dhal

Tarka Dhal is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic red split lentils, large thumb of ginger grated, organic extra virgin olive oil. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch, Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Indian
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 60 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** mustard

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 250 g organic red split lentils
- 1 large thumb of ginger grated
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 medium red chilli finely chopped
- 2 tbsp organic granules
- 1 tsp organic yellow mustard seeds
- 1 tsp organic ground coriander
- 1 tsp organic ground cumin
- 1 tsp organic ground turmeric
- A handful of fresh coriander
- 500 ml water
- fine sea salt

## Method

1. Wash the lentils then place in a large saucepan with the water and grated ginger.
2. Bring to the boil then reduce to a simmer. Remove any scum that comes to the surface.
3. Cook for 1 hour (stirring regularly) or until the lentils soften. You may need to add more water. Season generously with salt.
4. Next put the oil in a frying pan and fry the mustard seeds until they start to pop open.
5. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
6. Then add the celery and cook for 2 minutes until it starts to soften.
7. Next add the ginger and chilli and cook for a further 2 minutes.
8. Now add the coriander, cumin, turmeric and tomato paste and cook gently for 5 minutes taking care to not burn the spices.
9. Finally, empty the pan into the pot of cooked lentils and stir throughly.
10. Serve topped with chopped fresh coriander.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
