# Broccoli and Olive Pasta

Broccoli and Olive Pasta is a sattvic vegetarian dish of broccoli stems, organic extra virgin olive oil, organic chilli flakes. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 200 g broccoli stems
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 100 g black olives
- 50 g organic walnuts
- A large handful of parsley
- 1/2 lemon
- 400 g organic penne pasta
- 100 g vegetarian hard cheese or nutritional yeast
- Natural fine Sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Chop the broccoli into 2cm pieces and add to a saucepan along with 2 tbsp of water.
2. Cook over a medium to high heat for 3 minutes or until the water has boiled away.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Grate a little ginger and add to the pan along with the olive oil and chilli flakes.
5. Cook for 2 more minutes.
6. Cook the pasta according to packet instruction, drain (reserving some of the cooking water) and add to the broccoli.
7. Finely chop the olives and walnuts and roughly chop the parsley.
8. Place all in the pan along with a squeeze of lemon juice.
9. Add a few tbsps of the pasta cooking water along with the grated cheese.
10. Mix together and you are ready to serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
