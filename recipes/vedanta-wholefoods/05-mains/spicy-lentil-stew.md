# Spicy Lentil Stew

Spicy Lentil Stew is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, medium carrots medium diced, sun-dried tomatoes. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Main Course, Side Dish, Soup
- **Cuisine:** world
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 50 min
- **Yield:** 6 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 medium carrots medium diced
- 30 g sun-dried tomatoes, plus 30 g walnuts, chopped
- 250 g aubergine, diced
- 1 organic coconut milk can
- 1 litre organic vegetable stock
- 250 g organic french type lentils
- 200 g chopped kale
- natural sea salt crystals
- organic cracked black pepper

### For the paste:

- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 medium thumb ginger skin removed
- 1 dsp tomato puree
- 1 tsp organic cumin
- 1 tsp organic coriander
- 1 tsp organic turmeric
- 50 ml water

## Method

1. Blend all of the paste ingredients together and fry in a pan on a medium heat for 5 minutes. Remove the cooked paste, set aside and clean the pan out.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Add the oil and fry the carrots, soaked sun-dried tomatoes, aubergine, season generously with salt and pepper and fry for 20 minutes.
4. Now add back in the cooked paste, coconut milk, lentils and vegetable stock and simmer for 40 minutes.
5. Finally add the lentils and kale and cook for a final 5 minutes and you are ready to serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
- Aubergine, walnuts and sun-dried tomato stand in for mushrooms.
