# Vegetable, Ginger and Lime Noodles

Vegetable, Ginger and Lime Noodles is a sattvic vegetarian dish of thumb of ginger, lime, organic coconut aminos. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Asian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** sesame

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 thumb of ginger
- 1 lime
- 1 tbsp organic coconut aminos
- 1 tbsp organic toasted sesame oil
- 400 g broccoli stems
- A large handful of spinach
- 250 g organic soba noodles

## Method

1. To make the sauce, peel the ginger and finely grate. Do the same with the ginger.
2. Juice the lime.
3. Add all to a small bowl along with the soy sauce and sesame oil. Whisk together and set aside.
4. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
5. Chop the broccoli stems into approximately 2cm pieces and boil in salted water for 2 minutes.
6. Drain and set aside.
7. Cook the noodles according to packet instructions, drain and then add back to the pan.
8. Pour the sauce over the noodles and add the broccoli and spinach.
9. Cook on a medium heat for 2 minutes and then serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
