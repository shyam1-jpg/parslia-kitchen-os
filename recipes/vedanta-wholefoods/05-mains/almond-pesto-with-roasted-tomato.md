# Almond Pesto With Roasted Tomato

Almond Pesto With Roasted Tomato is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, cherry tomatoes, organic spaghetti. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 500 g cherry tomatoes
- 400 g organic spaghetti
- Natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

### For the pesto:

- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 50 g organic almonds
- 100 g vegetarian hard cheese or nutritional yeast or plant-based cheese
- handful of basil
- 1/2 lemon
- natural fine sea salt

## Method

1. To make the pesto, place the almonds in a frying pan and roast over a medium to high heat until they start to colour.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. This should take 3-4 minutes, then remove from the pan and allow to cool.
4. Roughly chop the ginger and juice the lemon then place in a food processor with the almonds and the rest of the pesto ingredients.
5. Blitz until roughly chopped but not completely smooth.
6. Next, place the tomatoes in a baking tray, drizzle with the olive oil and season generously with salt and pepper.
7. Roast in the oven for 20 minutes at 200c then set aside in a warm place.
8. Cook the spaghetti according to packet instructions, drain (reserving 2 tbsp of the cooking water) then add back to the saucepan.
9. Mix in the pesto along with the cooking water and serve with the roasted tomatoes on top.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
