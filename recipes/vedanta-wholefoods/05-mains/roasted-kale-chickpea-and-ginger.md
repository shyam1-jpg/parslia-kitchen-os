# Roasted Kale, Chickpea and Ginger

Roasted Kale, Chickpea and Ginger is a sattvic vegetarian dish of carrots, cooked chickpeas, kale. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Side Dish
- **Cuisine:** world
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 kg carrots
- 250 g cooked chickpeas
- 250 g kale
- 50 g almonds
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Firstly cut the tops from your carrots and then slice in 2 lengthways. If you have any larger carrots, make a second slice across so that you have 4 pieces.
2. Place in a baking tray, drizzle with 2 tbsp’s of olive oil and season generously with salt and pepper.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Place in the oven and roast at 200c for 20 minutes turning over occasionally.
5. Add the chickpeas and cook for a further 10 minutes.
6. Roughly chop the kale removing any larger stems then finely slice the ginger.
7. Add to baking tray along with the almonds and lemon juice and roast for a final 10 minutes.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
