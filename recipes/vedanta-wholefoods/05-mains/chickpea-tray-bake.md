# Chickpea Tray Bake

Chickpea Tray Bake is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, large sweet potato, organic chickpeas (cooked). No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 0 min
- **Cook:** 0 min
- **Yield:** 0

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 large sweet potato
- 250 g organic chickpeas (cooked)
- 250 g broccoli stems
- Large handful of parsley
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

### For the dressing:

- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 tsp organic tahini
- 1/2 lemon
- 1 tbsp water
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Chop the sweet potato into roughly 2cm chunks and place in a baking tray.
2. Drizzle over the olive oil, season with salt and pepper and bake in the oven at 200c for 20 minutes.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Add the chickpeas and broccoli stems to the tray.
5. Roast for another 10 minutes, remove and keep warm.
6. To make the dressing, grate a little ginger and juice the lemon.
7. Place both in a mixing bowl then add the tahini and water and season with salt and pepper.
8. Whisk together, then slowly drizzle in the olive oil whilst still whisking.
9. To serve, place a large portion of the chickpeas and vegetables on a plate, drizzle over some of the dressing and garnish with chopped parsley.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
