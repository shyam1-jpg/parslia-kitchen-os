# Tandoori Baked Potato Curry

Tandoori Baked Potato Curry is a sattvic vegetarian dish of baking potatoes, organic coconut milk, frozen peas. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Indian
- **Prep:** 20 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 4 baking potatoes
- 400 ml organic coconut milk
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 2 cups fresh spinach
- natural fine sea salt, to taste

### Organic Masala Paste

- 1 tsp organic fenugreek
- 1 tbsp organic coriander
- 1 tbsp chilli powder
- 1 tbsp organic garam masala
- 1 tbsp organic turmeric
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 inches fresh ginger

## Method

1. Make a cross on the potatoes and bake them at 180 degrees, until nearly done, switch off the oven and keep the potatoes inside.
2. Combine the fenugreek, coriander, chilli powder, garam masala, turmeric, olive oil and fresh ginger in a blender and combine to make a paste.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Heat up some oil in a heavy bottom duty pan.
5. Sweat the celery until soft, add the curry paste and fry for one minute.
6. Add coconut milk and bring to a gentle boil, stirring occasionally.
7. Add frozen peas, spinach and taste for salt.
8. Transfer the curry to a deep tray, place the potatoes in the middle. bake for another 10 minutes until potatoes are soft and fluffy.
9. Before serving, squeeze each potato from the bottom so the cross opens up, place indian pickles inside, grab a big spoon to pour the curry over and enjoy!

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
