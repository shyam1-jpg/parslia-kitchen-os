# Creamy Aubergine and Walnut Stew

Creamy Aubergine and Walnut Stew is a sattvic vegetarian dish of olive oil, aubergine, stalks of celery. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Italian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 45 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, nuts

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 500 g aubergine, diced
- 2 stalks of celery
- 50 g sun-dried tomatoes, plus 30 g walnuts, chopped
- 2 tsp flour
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- 100 ml of cashew cream or oat cream
- A large handful of parsley
- Salt and pepper

## Method

1. Soak the sun-dried tomatoes in hot water for 15 minutes, then chop. Keep the soaking liquor.
2. Brown the aubergine cubes in olive oil in a steel casserole until golden. Add walnuts and cook 2 minutes more.
3. Stir in celery and bloom the hing in the hot oil. Sprinkle over the flour and cook 1 minute.
4. Add stock, the soaking liquor and chopped tomatoes. Simmer 20 minutes until thick and silky.
5. Stir in the plant cream and parsley. Rest 2 minutes and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
- Aubergine, walnuts and sun-dried tomato stand in for mushrooms.
