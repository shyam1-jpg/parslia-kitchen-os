# Sweet Potato Curry

Sweet Potato Curry is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, organic chilli flakes, large thumb of ginger. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Indian
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 35 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** nuts, dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 1 large thumb of ginger
- 1 tsp organic cumin
- 1 tsp organic coriander
- 1 tsp organic turmeric
- 1 tbsp tomato puree
- 100 g tomatoes
- 100 ml water
- 500 g sweet potato
- A large handful of spinach
- A large handful of fresh coriander
- 30 g organic cashew nuts
- 4 tbsp natural yoghurt
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Chop the celery into approximately 1cm pieces and place in a large saucepan with the oil.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Season with salt and pepper and fry on a medium heat for 5 minutes stirring frequently.
4. Peel and grate the ginger and finely chop the chilli and ginger.
5. Add all to the pan and cook for another 5 minutes.
6. Turn down the heat and add the cumin, coriander and turmeric as well as the tomatoes and tomato puree.
7. Cook for 5 more minutes take care not to burn the spices.
8. Peel the sweet potato and cut into 3cm chunks.
9. Turn the heat back up to medium, add the sweet potato and water, mix well and simmer for 20 minutes or until the sweet potato has cooked through.
10. Serve over rice with chopped cashew nuts, fresh coriander and a spoonful of sweet potato.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
