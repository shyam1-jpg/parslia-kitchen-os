# Slow-Roasted Tomato Spaghetti

Slow-Roasted Tomato Spaghetti is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic wholewheat spaghetti pasta, organic extra virgin olive oil, organic chilli flakes. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Lunch, Main Course, Side Dish, Snack
- **Cuisine:** American, Italian, Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 25 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 400 g organic wholewheat spaghetti pasta
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp organic chilli flakes
- 500 g cherry tomatoes
- A large handful of chopped parsley
- 100 g vegetarian hard cheese or nutritional yeast
- fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Halve the cherry tomatoes and spread on a steel tray.
2. Toss with olive oil, chilli flakes, a pinch of hing, salt and pepper.
3. Roast for 20 minutes until the tomatoes burst and catch at the edges.
4. Cook the spaghetti in salted water until al dente, then drain, keeping a little cooking water.
5. Tip the roasted tomatoes into a steel pan, add the pasta, parsley and cheese, and loosen with cooking water.
6. Toss well and serve at once.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
