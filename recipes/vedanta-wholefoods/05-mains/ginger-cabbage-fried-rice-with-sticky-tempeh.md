# Ginger-Cabbage Fried Rice with Sticky Tempeh

Ginger-Cabbage Fried Rice with Sticky Tempeh is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic sesame oil, dsp of tomato puree mixed with chilli powder and a little maple, tempeh cut into 1cm cubes. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch, Main Course, Side Dish
- **Cuisine:** korean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 35 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, sesame, soya

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 2 tbsp organic sesame oil
- 1 dsp of tomato puree mixed with chilli powder and a little maple
- 200 g tempeh cut into 1cm cubes
- 2 tbsp tamari soya sauce
- 1 thumb size piece ginger grated
- 1 medium chilli finely chopped
- 300 g organic brown jasmine rice
- 150 g peas
- 200 g organic finely shredded cabbage tossed with ginger, lemon and a pinch of chilli
- A handful of fresh coriander chopped
- 50 g organic cashews toasted
- 1 tbsp organic sesame seeds toasted

## Method

1. Cook the brown rice according to the packet instructions, then strain and let cool completely.
2. Heat 1 tbsp of sesame oil in a wok, add the chilli-tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Now add the tempeh and 1 tbsp of tamari soya sauce and fry for about 5 minutes until the tempeh takes on a deep red colour and the moisture has absorbed.
5. Remove from the pan and set aside.
6. Add another tbsp of sesame oil to the pan and fry the ginger, ginger and chilli for 2 minutes.
7. Now add the brown rice and cook on a high heat for 3-4 minutes stirring frequently.
8. Next add the ginger cabbage and 1 tbsp of tamari soya sauce and cook for a further 2 minutes.
9. Add the peas and cook for a final 2 minutes.
10. To serve, place the rice in a bowl, top with the tempeh and garnish with toasted cashews & sesame seeds.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
