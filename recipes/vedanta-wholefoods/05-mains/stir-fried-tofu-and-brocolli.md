# Stir Fried Tofu and Brocolli

Stir Fried Tofu and Brocolli is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic firm tofu, broccoli florets, thumb of ginger. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch, Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Asian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, sesame, soya

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 400 g organic firm tofu
- 300 g broccoli florets
- 1 thumb of ginger
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp organic toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tbsp organic maple syrup
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- organic sesame seeds

## Method

1. Drain the tofu and pat dry.
2. Using your hands or a fork, crumble it into small, bite-sized pieces.
3. Heat a wok or frying pan with 1 tbsp of oil and fry the tofu for 5 minutes until lightly golden and starting to crisp.
4. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
5. Remove from the pan and set aside.
6. In the same pan, add the remaining oil.
7. Peel and grate a little ginger and ginger, then fry for 1–2 minutes.
8. Add the broccoli florets and stir-fry on a high heat for 3–4 minutes until just tender.
9. Whisk together the soy sauce, sesame oil, lemon juice and maple syrup.
10. Pour into the pan, stir well, then return the crumbled tofu and toss everything together so it absorbs the sauce.
11. Serve hot, sprinkled with sesame seeds, over rice or noodles.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
