# Lemon and Poppy Seed Drizzle Cake

Lemon and Poppy Seed Drizzle Cake is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic self-raising flour, organic golden cane sugar, organic baking powder. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert
- **Cuisine:** World Cuisine
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 45 min
- **Yield:** 10 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- 250 g organic self-raising flour
- 150 g organic golden cane sugar
- 1 tsp organic baking powder
- Pinch natural fine sea salt
- 2 tbsp organic poppy seeds
- Zest of 2 lemons
- 80 ml organic coconut oil
- 200 ml organic oat milk, or any plant milk
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp organic vanilla extract
- Juice of 2 lemons
- 80 g organic icing sugar

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180c and line a loaf tin with baking paper.
2. In a large bowl mix together the flour, golden cane sugar, baking powder, salt, poppy seeds and lemon zest.
3. In a jug whisk the melted coconut oil, oat milk, lemon juice and vanilla until smooth.
4. Pour the wet mixture into the dry and gently fold until combined, being careful not to over mix.
5. Tip the batter into the prepared tin and smooth the top.
6. Bake for 40–45 minutes until golden and a skewer inserted in the centre comes out clean.
7. While the cake is baking, stir the lemon juice into the icing sugar until smooth.
8. As soon as the cake comes out of the oven, prick the top all over with a skewer and spoon over the drizzle so it soaks in.
9. Leave the cake to cool in the tin before slicing, allowing the drizzle to set slightly.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
