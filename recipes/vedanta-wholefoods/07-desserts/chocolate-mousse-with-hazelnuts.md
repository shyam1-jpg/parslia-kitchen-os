# Chocolate Mousse with Hazelnuts

Chocolate Mousse with Hazelnuts is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic dark chocolate courvature, organic coconut milk, organic maple syrup. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 0 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- 200 g organic dark chocolate courvature, 70% cocoa, dairy-free
- 400 ml organic coconut milk, from a chilled can, using mostly the thick cream
- 3 tbsp organic maple syrup
- 1 tsp organic vanilla extract
- natural fine sea salt
- 50 g organic hazelnuts (roasted), roughly chopped

## Method

1. Break the chocolate into pieces and melt gently over a pan of simmering water or in short bursts in the microwave.
2. Set aside to cool slightly.
3. Scoop the thick cream from the top of the chilled coconut milk can into a mixing bowl and whisk until light and fluffy.
4. Reserve any watery liquid at the bottom for smoothies.
5. Stir the melted chocolate into the whipped coconut cream along with the maple syrup, vanilla, and salt, folding gently until smooth and fully combined.
6. Spoon the mousse into small glasses and chill for at least two hours, or until set.
7. Before serving, scatter with the roasted hazelnuts.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
