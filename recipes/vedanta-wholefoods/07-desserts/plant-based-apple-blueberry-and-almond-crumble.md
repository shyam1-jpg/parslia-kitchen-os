# Plant-Based Apple, Blueberry and Almond Crumble

Plant-Based Apple, Blueberry and Almond Crumble is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around medium cooking apples, blueberries, organic brown sugar. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 6
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, nuts, dairy

## Ingredients

### For the fruit base:

- 4 medium cooking apples, peeled, cored, and cut into chunks
- 250 g blueberries
- 50 g organic brown sugar
- 1 tsp lemon juice

### For the crumble topping:

- 75 g organic plain flour
- 75 g organic almond flour
- 100 g vegan block butter, cold, diced
- 75 g organic brown sugar
- 50 g organic rolled oats
- 60 g organic flaked almonds
- Pinch natural fine sea salt

## Method

1. Firstly, mix together the apple chunks and blueberries with the lemon juice and sugar.
2. Tip the fruit into a baking dish then prep the topping.
3. Rub the butter into the plain and almond flour with your fingertips until it looks like breadcrumbs, leaving a few small lumps for texture.
4. Stir in the oats, sugar, flaked almonds, and a pinch of salt.
5. Scatter the crumble mixture evenly over the fruit without pressing it down.
6. Bake for 35–40 minutes at 180C, until the topping is golden and crisp and the fruit juices bubble up around the edges.
7. Leave to rest for 5 minutes, then serve with your favourite plant-based vanilla ice cream, custard or cream.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
