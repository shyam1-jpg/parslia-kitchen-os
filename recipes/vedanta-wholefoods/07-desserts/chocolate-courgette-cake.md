# Chocolate Courgette Cake

Chocolate Courgette Cake is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic self-raising flour, organic cocoa powder, organic extra virgin olive oil. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 8 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- 330 g organic self-raising flour
- 70 g organic cocoa powder
- 175 ml organic extra virgin olive oil
- 350 g organic cane sugar
- 3 flax eggs (1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water per egg)
- 2 tsp organic vanilla extract
- 500 g grated courgette

### Icing

- 300g organic icing sugar
- 150g vegan butter
- 2 tbsp organic cacao powder
- 2 tbsp plant milk
- 1 tsp organic vanilla essence

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180C/160C fan/gas 4.
2. Combine the flour, cocoa powder in a large bowl.
3. In another bowl, combine the olive oil, sugar, flax eggs, vanilla essence and grated courgette.
4. Mix the dry and wet mixture until combined.
5. Line a 24cm cake tin with baking paper and pour your mixture in.
6. Bake for 40 mins (or until a knife inserted into the middle comes out clean).
7. Turn out onto a wire rack and leave to cool.
8. Icing.
9. Blend the butter and sugar in a mixer until light and creamy.
10. Add the vanilla, cocoa and combine.
11. Add the milk slowly until you reach your desired consistency.
12. Once the cake is cool, smooth the icing on, for a perfect finish.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Each egg is replaced with a flax egg.
