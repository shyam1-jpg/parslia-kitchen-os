# Protein Flapjack

Protein Flapjack is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic butter, organic maple syrup, organic peanut butter. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Prep:** 0 min
- **Cook:** 25 min
- **Yield:** 12
- **Allergens (estimate):** nuts, dairy

## Ingredients

- 125 g organic butter
- 130 g organic maple syrup
- 125 g organic peanut butter
- 350 g organic gluten free porridge oats
- 2 tbsp organic pumpkin protein powder
- 2 tbsp organic hulled hemp seed

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180c/gas mark 4.
2. Line an 8inch/20cm square tin with baking paper.
3. Melt the butter in the saucepan over a low heat and stir in the maple syrup.
4. Remove pan from the heat and stir in the peanut butter.
5. Combine the oats, hemp seed and protein powder in a bowl.
6. Stir in the liquid mixture and combine.
7. Pour the mixture into the baking tray and press down firmly.
8. Bake for 25mins or until golden.
9. Allow to cool and then cut into 12 squares.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
