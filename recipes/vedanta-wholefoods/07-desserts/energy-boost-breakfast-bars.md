# Energy Boost Breakfast Bars

Energy Boost Breakfast Bars is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around banana, gluten free organic jumbo oats, organic desiccated coconut. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert, Snack
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 12 min
- **Yield:** 6
- **Allergens (estimate):** nuts, dairy

## Ingredients

- 1/2 banana
- 150 g gluten free organic jumbo oats
- 4 tbsp organic desiccated coconut
- 7 organic apricots
- 2 tbsp organic goji berries
- 2 tbsp organic cacao nibs
- 1/2 tsp organic ceylon cinnamon powder
- 50 g peanut butter
- 2 tbsp date syrup

## Method

1. Preheat over to 180C (360F). Line a baking tray with a little coconut oil.
2. In a bowl mash the banana. Add the dry ingredients: oats, coconut, dried fruit (chop the apricots into smaller chunks), cacao nibs and spice. Mix.
3. Add the peanut butter and date syrup. Mix until everything is combined and clumping together.
4. Dollop spoonfuls of the mixture onto the baking tray. Use your hands to spread it out into a rough rectangle. Press lightly to form an even layer.
5. Bake in the oven for 10 – 12 minutes.
6. Allow to cool, then slice into 8 squares.
7. Serve alone as a grab and go snack, enjoy for breakfast with yogurt, or top with some dark chocolate for an afternoon pick me up.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
