# Peanut Butter Chocolate Cookie

Peanut Butter Chocolate Cookie is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic plain flour, organic almond flour, organic baking powder. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch
- **Cuisine:** World Cuisine
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 12 min
- **Yield:** 12 Cookies
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, nuts

## Ingredients

- 80 g organic plain flour
- 70 g organic almond flour
- 1 tsp organic baking powder
- Pinch natural fine sea salt
- 125 g organic golden cane sugar
- 120 g organic smooth peanut butter
- 60 ml organic coconut oil
- 1 tsp organic vanilla extract
- 2 tbsp organic plant-based milk
- 100 g organic dark chocolate
- 50 g organic peanuts (roasted and chopped)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180°C and line a steel baking tray with baking paper.
2. In a bowl, whisk together the plain flour, almond flour, baking powder and salt.
3. Gently melt the dark chocolate until smooth, then set aside to cool slightly.
4. In a separate large bowl, stir together the peanut butter, melted coconut oil, sugar and vanilla until smooth and glossy.
5. Pour in the melted chocolate and mix until fully combined, then add the plant-based milk to loosen the mixture slightly.
6. Tip the dry ingredients into the wet mixture and stir until just combined into a thick, soft dough.
7. Fold through the chopped roasted peanuts so they’re evenly distributed.
8. Scoop heaped tablespoons of dough, roll lightly in your hands, and place onto the tray with space between each for spreading.
9. Gently flatten the tops with a fork.
10. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the edges are set but the centres remain soft.
11. Allow to cool slightly on the tray before serving so the texture stays fudgy and rich.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
