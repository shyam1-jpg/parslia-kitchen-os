# Vegan Dorset Apple Cake

Vegan Dorset Apple Cake is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around apples, organic self raising flour, organic blanched almond flour. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Breakfast, Dessert, Snack
- **Prep:** 20 min
- **Cook:** 35 min
- **Yield:** 8
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, nuts

## Ingredients

- 2 apples
- 100 g organic self raising flour
- 100 g organic blanched almond flour
- 100 g organic unrefined cane sugar
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp organic ceylon cinnamon powder
- 150 ml dairy free milk
- 75 ml organic olive oil
- 2 tsp vanilla essence

## Method

1. Preheat your oven to 180C (360F).
2. Mix together all the dry ingredients.
3. Add the wet ingredients and combine.
4. Peel and chop the apples in to 2cm cubes.
5. Mix the apple cubes into the batter.
6. Line and grease the tin with oil.
7. Pour the mixture in and spread evenly.
8. Bake for 35mins.
9. Remove and let the cake cool and sprinkle with unrefined cane sugar.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
