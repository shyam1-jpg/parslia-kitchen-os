# Peanut Butter Flapjack

Peanut Butter Flapjack is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic oats, organic pumpkin seeds, organic pecans. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Snack
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 25 min
- **Yield:** 12 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** nuts, dairy

## Ingredients

- 250 g organic oats
- 50 g organic pumpkin seeds
- 150 g organic pecans
- 100 g butter or plant-based alternatives
- 50 g organic brown sugar
- 3 tbsp agave
- 150 g organic peanut butter
- natural fine sea salt

## Method

1. Roughly chop the pecans and pumpkin seeds and place in a baking tray along with the oats.
2. Roast at 200c for approximately 10 minutes until they lightly colour.
3. Melt the butter and sugar together in a large saucepan.
4. Add the agave, peanut butter and salt, combine then remove from the heat.
5. Next, add the oats, pecans and pumpkin seeds and mix together well.
6. Line a steel baking tray with baking paper and turn the mixture out into it.
7. Bake at 200c for 20-25 minutes then remove and let cool completely before cutting into bars.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
