# Raisin and Vanilla Muffin

Raisin and Vanilla Muffin is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic Self Raising Flour, flax eggs (1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water per egg), organic ground flax seed. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 12
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, nuts

## Ingredients

- 170 g organic Self Raising Flour
- 2 flax eggs (1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water per egg)
- 2 tbsp organic ground flax seed
- 60 g organic mixed vine fruits
- 80 g organic raw light cane sugar
- 2 tsp organic vanilla extract
- 2 tbsp organic almond milk
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 40 ml organic extra virgin olive oil

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180 degrees and line a cake tray with 12 paper cases.
2. Soak the raisins in hot water for 30 minutes, then drain well.
3. Combine the golden cane sugar, vanilla, and plant milk, oil and flax egg until smooth.
4. Add the flour, cinnamon, flax seed and combing until light and fluffy, ideally in a mixer.
5. Add the raisins and combine well.
6. Cook for 15 minutes and then leave to cool before enjoying.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Each egg is replaced with a flax egg.
