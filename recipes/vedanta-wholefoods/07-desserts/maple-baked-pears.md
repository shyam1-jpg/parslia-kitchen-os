# Maple Baked Pears

Maple Baked Pears is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around pears, butter or plant-based alternative, brown sugar. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 35 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- 4 pears
- 50 g butter or plant-based alternative
- 50 g brown sugar
- 2 tbsp organic maple syrup
- 50 g organic pecans

## Method

1. In a small saucepan, melt the butter and add the sugar.
2. Mix until the sugar has dissolved.
3. Add the maple syrup, mix again and remove from the heat.
4. Peel the pears, cut in half and remove the cores.
5. Place a piece of baking paper on a baking tray and lay the pears cut side up.
6. Drizzle over the maple syrup mixture and bake at 200c for 25-30 minutes.
7. Roughly chop the pecans and roast in a dry frying pan for 2-3 minutes until they lightly colour.
8. To serve, place each pear on a plate, drizzle over the cooking liquor and scatter with some roasted pecans.
9. Goes wonderfully with ice-cream!

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
