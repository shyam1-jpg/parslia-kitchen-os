# Grain Free Banana Cake

Grain Free Banana Cake is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around Bananas, organic chestnut flour, organic coconut oil. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Dessert, Snack
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 12
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- 3 Bananas
- 150 g organic chestnut flour
- 2 tbsp organic coconut oil
- 30 g organic tapioca flour
- 2 tsp vanilla essence
- 1 tsp organic fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp organic ceylon cinnamon powder

## Method

1. Line a square 8″ bake tin and Pre-heat the oven to 150c.
2. Mash the banana and add all the other ingredients.
3. Combine well and use a food processor for a smoother batter.
4. Cook at 150° for 40 minutes, or until brown and firm.
5. Allow to cool and then slice into squares.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
