# Acai Coconut Pudding

Acai Coconut Pudding is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic chia seeds, coconut yoghurt, organic coconut milk. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Dessert, Snack
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 720 min
- **Yield:** 1
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp organic chia seeds
- 2 tbsp coconut yoghurt
- 150 ml organic coconut milk
- 1 tbsp organic date sugar

## Method

1. Soak the chia seed in the coconut milk overnight.
2. Mix the soaked chia seed, yogurt and date sugar together.
3. To create the layers, separate the mixture and add increasing amounts of acai powder.
4. Layer it up.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
