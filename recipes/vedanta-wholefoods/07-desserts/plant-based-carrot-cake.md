# Plant-Based Carrot Cake

Plant-Based Carrot Cake is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around carrots, organic plain flour, organic baking powder. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert
- **Cuisine:** World Cuisine
- **Prep:** 25 min
- **Cook:** 50 min
- **Yield:** 10
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, nuts

## Ingredients

- 250 g carrots, grated
- 180 g organic plain flour
- 1 tsp organic baking powder
- ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
- ½ tsp natural fine sea salt
- 1 tsp organic ceylon cinnamon
- ½ tsp organic nutmeg
- 1 tbsp freshly grated ginger
- 100 g organic aseel dates, pitted and chopped
- 60 g organic walnuts, roughly chopped
- 100 g organic cane sugar
- 80 ml organic coconut oil, melted
- 200 ml organic almond milk
- 2 tbsp organic orange juice
- 1 tsp organic vanilla extract

### For the frosting:

- 200 g plant-based cream cheese
- 60 g plant-based butter, softened
- 200 g organic icing sugar, sifted 1 tsp orange zest
- 1 tsp organic vanilla extract

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 180c and line a standard 2lb loaf tin with parchment, letting it overhang for easy lifting.
2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, bicarbonate of soda, salt, cinnamon and nutmeg.
3. Stir in the grated carrots, dates, ginger and walnuts so they’re coated in the flour mixture.
4. In another bowl, whisk the sugar with the coconut oil, milk, orange juice and vanilla until smooth.
5. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and gently fold until just combined to make a thick batter.
6. Spoon the mixture into the prepared tin, smooth the top and bake for 45–50 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
7. Leave to cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a wire rack and cool completely before frosting.
8. To make the frosting, beat the cream cheese and butter together until smooth, then gradually mix in the icing sugar.
9. Stir through the orange zest and vanilla then spread generously over the cooled cake.
10. Finally scatter a few extra chopped walnuts on top.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
