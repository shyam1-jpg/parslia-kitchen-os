# Vegan Blueberry Ice Cream

Vegan Blueberry Ice Cream is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around frozen bananas, tin organic coconut milk, organic maqui powder. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Prep:** 20 min
- **Cook:** 12 min
- **Yield:** 6 people

## Ingredients

- 3 frozen bananas, chopped roughly before freezing so they can blend quicker
- 1 tin organic coconut milk, see notes
- 2 tbsp organic maqui powder
- frozen fruit of your choice
- vegan white or dark chocolate, optional

## Method

1. In a high-speed blender, combine frozen bananas and coconut milk, and blend well till completely smooth.
2. Pour the mixture into the ice cream moulds till half of the height and place some frozen fruit in the middle.
3. Now, mix the maqui powder with the rest of the ice cream mixture, it doesn't have to be completely combined, so you can create a ripple effect.
4. Pour it onto the ice cream moulds and freeze.
5. Once frozen, you will be able to decorate them.
6. Simply melt the chocolate and pour it over your ice cream, and put it in a freezer again.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
