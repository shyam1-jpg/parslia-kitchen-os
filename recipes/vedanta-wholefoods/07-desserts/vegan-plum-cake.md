# Vegan Plum Cake

Vegan Plum Cake is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic plain flour, baking powder, baking soda. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Prep:** 0 min
- **Cook:** 0 min
- **Yield:** 6
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- 200 g organic plain flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1.5 tsp baking soda
- 150 g organic coconut oil
- 100 g organic coconut sugar
- 1/4 cup aquafaba- water from tinned chickpeas
- 4-6 ripe organic plums
- 1/4 cup organic crystalised ginger
- 1/2 cup organic chocolate chips

## Method

1. Combine the melted coconut oil, sugar and aquafaba. Whisk well until combined.
2. In a large bowl mix the flour, baking powder and baking soda.
3. Combine wet mixture with flour using a spatula, add chocolate chips and chopped ginger.
4. To make fan-shaped plums, simply cut them in halves, remove the stone, place them on the chopping board. Use a really sharp knife cut each half nearly all the way through, ensuring they hold together.
5. Line a round baking tin with grease proof paper (diameter 20cm). Pour the mixture in, and place the plums on top.
6. Bake at 180 degrees for approx. 45 min, check with a toothpick - if it comes out dry, the cake is ready.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
