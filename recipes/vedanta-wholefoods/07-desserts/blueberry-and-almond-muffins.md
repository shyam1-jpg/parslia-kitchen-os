# Blueberry and Almond Muffins

Blueberry and Almond Muffins is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around blueberries, organic almond flour, organic white cake flour. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 17 min
- **Yield:** 12 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, nuts, dairy

## Ingredients

- 200 g blueberries
- 150 g organic almond flour
- 150 g organic white cake flour
- 1 tsp organic baking powder
- 1 tsp bicarb
- 1/2 natural fine sea salt
- 1 tsp organic vanilla powder
- 150 g plant-based yoghurt
- 100 ml organic extra virgin olive oil
- 6 tbsp golden syrup
- 50 g organic flaked almonds

## Method

1. In a large mixing bowl, add the vanilla extract, yoghurt, vegetable oil and golden syrup and whisk well.
2. Next, sift the ground almonds, flour, baking powder, bicarb and salt.
3. Add to the mixing bowl and gently fold in.
4. Now add the blueberries and continue to fold the mixture.
5. Spoon the mixture into 12 muffin cases and bake at 200c.
6. After 5 minutes, remove the muffins and sprinkle with the flaked almonds.
7. Return to the oven and bake for a further 12 minutes or until completely cooked through.
8. Allow to cool and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
