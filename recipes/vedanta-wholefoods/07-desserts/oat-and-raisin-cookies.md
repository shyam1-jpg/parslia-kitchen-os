# Oat and Raisin Cookies

Oat and Raisin Cookies is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic raisins, organic extra virgin olive oil, organic cane sugar. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Prep:** 25 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 25
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- 100 g organic raisins
- 150 ml organic extra virgin olive oil
- 100 g organic cane sugar
- 1 flax egg (1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water per egg)
- 1 tsp organic cinnamon
- 1 tsp organic vanilla extract
- 140 g organic plain flour
- ¼ tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 300 g organic oats

## Method

1. Heat oven to 180C/160C fan/gas 4 and line 2 baking trays with baking paper.
2. Soak the raisins in 50ml of hot water for 15 minutes and drain.
3. Mix together the oil and sugar, beat in the flax egg, along with the raisin water, the cinnamon and vanilla extract.
4. Sift the flour, bicarbonate of soda then add the oats.
5. Mix in the raisins.
6. Drop heaped tbsps of the cookie dough onto the baking trays.
7. Bake for 12-15 mins until golden.
8. Leave to cool on the trays for 10 mins.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Each egg is replaced with a flax egg.
