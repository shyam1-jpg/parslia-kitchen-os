# Pistachio Chicory Layer Pudding

Pistachio Chicory Layer Pudding is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around oat or almond biscuits, roasted chicory decoction (brewed), organic maple syrup. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Dessert
- **Cuisine:** World Cuisine
- **Prep:** 30 min
- **Cook:** 4 min
- **Yield:** 8 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** nuts

## Ingredients

- 250 g oat or almond biscuits
- 250 ml roasted chicory decoction (brewed), cooled
- 2 tbsp organic maple syrup
- 250 g organic raw pistachios, unsalted, shelled
- 200 ml organic coconut cream, thick part from a chilled tin
- 250 g vegan cream cheese
- 100 g organic raw light cane sugar
- 2 tsp organic vanilla extract
- 2 tbsp organic almond milk
- 2 tbsp organic cacao powder, for dusting
- Extra chopped pistachios, to garnish

## Method

1. Soak the pistachios in hot water for 30 minutes, then drain well.
2. Blend with coconut cream, cream cheese, golden cane sugar, vanilla, and plant milk until smooth and the sugar has dissolved.
3. Chill the mixture while you prepare the rest.
4. Mix the cooled chicory decoction with maple syrup in a shallow dish.
5. Dip each biscuit quickly into the chicory decoction mixture so they soften but don’t collapse.
6. Line a dish (approx. 20 x 25cm) with a layer of the soaked biscuits.
7. Spread half the pistachio cream evenly over the top.
8. Repeat with another layer of dipped biscuits, then spread on the remaining pistachio cream, smoothing the surface neatly.
9. Dust generously with cacao powder and scatter chopped pistachios.
10. Chill for at least 4 hours, ideally overnight, for the tiramisu to firm up.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
