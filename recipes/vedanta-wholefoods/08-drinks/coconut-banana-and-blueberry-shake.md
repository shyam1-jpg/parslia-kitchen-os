# Coconut, Banana and Blueberry Shake

Coconut, Banana and Blueberry Shake is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around -3 small/medium bananas, -1.5 cup your favourite plant milk, coconut milk. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Breakfast, Snack
- **Cuisine:** world
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 0 min
- **Yield:** 2

## Ingredients

- 2-3 small/medium bananas, chopped roughly and frozen
- 1-1.5 cup your favourite plant milk
- 100 ml coconut milk
- 1 tbsp organic blueberry powder
- 1/4 cup frozen blueberries, optional
- 1 tsp organic maple syrup, optional
- 1/2 tsp organic vanilla powder, optional
- 2 tsp organic pumpkin protein powder

## Method

1. Place the bananas, plant milk, coconut milk, oats, vanilla, pumpkin powder and liquid sweetener (optional) in a high-speed blender.
2. Blend till completely smooth with no chunks of frozen bananas.
3. Taste for sweetness and pour half of the smoothie into a tall glass.
4. Now add blueberry powder and frozen berries.
5. Pour gently over the first layer to create a two colour effect.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
