# Healthy Dog Treats

Healthy Dog Treats is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around medium banana, peanut butter, flax eggs (1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water per egg). Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Side Dish, Snack
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 24
- **Allergens (estimate):** nuts, dairy

## Ingredients

- 1 medium banana
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 flax eggs (1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water per egg)
- 1 tbsp organic coconut oil
- 15 g organic pumpkin seeds
- 1/2 tsp organic peppermint
- 1/2 tsp organic dried rosemary
- 100 g organic rolled oats
- 50 g organic potato flakes

## Method

1. 1. Combine the banana, peanut butter, coconut oil and flax eggs in a bowl.
2. 2. Roughly blend all the dry ingredients.
3. 3. Add the dry ingredients to the wet mixture and mix well.
4. 4. If needed add more potato flakes to form a dough.
5. 5. Roll out onto a flat surface and cut into squares or make some fun shapes!
6. 6. Cook in the middle of the oven at 180° for 10 minutes or until golden.
7. 7. Allow to cool and start treating your dog!

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Each egg is replaced with a flax egg.
