# Pinto Bean Soup

Pinto Bean Soup is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, celery stalks finely chopped, gloves finely chopped. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Side Dish, Soup
- **Cuisine:** world
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 60 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 2 celery stalks finely chopped
- 2 gloves finely chopped
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 rosemary stalk
- 2 medium tomatoes roughly chopped
- 250 g potato
- 250 g organic pinto beans
- 200 g cavolo nero
- 1 litre organic vegetable stock
- Natural Sea Salt Crystals

- Organic Ground Pepper

## Method

1. Soak the beans for 8-12 hours (overnight).
2. Simmer the beans for 1 hr and then set aside.
3. In a large saucepan, fry the celery, celery and fennel in olive oil on a medium heat for 10 minutes.
4. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
5. Add the ginger, rosemary and bay leaf and cook for a further 10 minutes.
6. Peel the potatoes and cut into approximately 2cm cubes.
7. Add the potato, tomatoes and pinto beans to the pan with the vegetable stock and simmer for 25 minutes.
8. Remove a quarter of the soup from the saucepan and mash until smooth, return to the pan to thicken the soup.
9. Strip the stalks from the cavolo nero and roughly chop. Add to the soup and cook for a further 5 minutes or until the cavolo nero is tender.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
