# Roasted Carrot and Coriander Soup

Roasted Carrot and Coriander Soup is a sattvic vegetarian dish of carrots, organic extra virgin olive oil, organic ground coriander. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 35 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 600 g carrots, peeled and cut into chunks
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp organic ground coriander
- 1 tsp organic ground cumin
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 1 tbsp yoghurt or plant-based alternative
- A large handful of fresh coriander
- Natural Fine Sea Salt
- Organic Cracked Black Pepper
- A few extra coriander leaves
- 2-3 organic walnuts (crushed), chopped

### To Garnish

- A few extra coriander leaves
- 2-3 organic walnuts (crushed), chopped

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 200°C.
2. Place the carrots, celery and ginger cloves on a baking tray, drizzle with olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and roast for 25 minutes until golden and tender.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Reserve a few of the roasted carrots for garnish. Chop and set aside.
5. Squeeze the roasted ginger from its skins and place in a large saucepan with the rest of the roasted carrots and celery.
6. Sprinkle over the ground coriander and cumin, stir well and cook gently for a couple of minutes to release the flavour.
7. Pour in the vegetable stock, bring to the boil and simmer for 10 minutes.
8. Add the fresh coriander and yoghurt, then blend the soup until smooth using a stick blender or food processor. Adjust the seasoning to taste.
9. Ladle into bowls and garnish with fresh coriander leaves, chopped walnuts and the reserved roasted carrots. Serve with warm crusty bread.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
