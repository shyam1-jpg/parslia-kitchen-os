# Creamy Chickpea and Vegetable Soup

Creamy Chickpea and Vegetable Soup is a sattvic vegetarian dish of celery stalk, organic thyme, bay leaves. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 45 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** nuts

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 celery stalk
- 1/2 tsp organic thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 100 g cavolo nero
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 250 g organic chickpeas (cooked)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 150 ml cashew cream or oat cream
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Dice the celery, celery and fennel into approximately 1cm pieces and add to a large saucepan.
2. Add the olive oil, season with salt and pepper and cook on a medium heat for 10 minutes stirring regularly.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Grate a little ginger and add to the pan along with the thyme and bay leaves. Cook for another 2 minutes.
5. Add the vegetable stock, bring to the boil and simmer for 20 minutes.
6. Remove the stalks from the cavolo nero, roughly chop and add to the pan along with the chickpeas.
7. Cook for a further 10 minutes.
8. Remove the bay leaves then blitz with a stick blender.
9. Add the cream and cook for a final 2 minutes then serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
