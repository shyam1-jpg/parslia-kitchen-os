# Cauliflower Cheese Soup

Cauliflower Cheese Soup is a sattvic vegetarian dish of celery stalks, organic thyme, cauliflower. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 35 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, dairy, mustard

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 2 celery stalks
- 1/2 tsp organic thyme
- 1 cauliflower
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- 100 ml milk or plant-based alternative
- 100 g cheddar cheese or plant-based alternative
- 1 tsp organic dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- organic nutmeg
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

### For the breadcrumbs:

- 100 g sourdough bread
- 1 thyme sprig
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil

## Method

1. Roughly chop the celery, celery and fennel into 2cm pieces and add to a large saucepan along with the olive oil and thyme.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Season generously and cook on a medium to low heat for 15 minutes stirring regularly.
4. Cut the cauliflower into approximately 2cm pieces and add to the pot along with the vegetable stock.
5. Bring to the boil then turn down to simmer for 15 minutes.
6. Blend with a stick blender until smooth then add the Dijon mustard along with the oat milk and a rasp or 2 of nutmeg.
7. Cook for a further 2 minutes and then your soup is ready.
8. To make the breadcrumbs, roughly chop the sourdough and fry in a pan with 1 tbsp of olive oil and a sprig of thyme.
9. Fry until breadcrumbs are golden then sprinkle over your soup.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
