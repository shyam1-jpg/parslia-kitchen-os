# Creamy Watercress Soup

Creamy Watercress Soup is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic extra virgin olive oil, celery stalk, bay leaf. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch
- **Cuisine:** world
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 celery stalk
- 1 bay leaf
- 100 g watercress
- 200 g potato
- 1 litre organic vegetable bouillon
- 100 ml cream or plant based alternative
- 25 g organic hazelnuts
- 1/2 lemon
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Roughly chop the celery and celery and add to a saucepan with the olive oil, bay leaf and salt and pepper.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Cook on a medium heat for 5 minutes stirring regularly.
4. Slice the ginger.
5. Remove the larger stalks from the watercress and roughly chop.
6. Peel and roughly chop the potatoes and add to the pan along with the ginger and watercress stalks.
7. Cook for 2 minutes then add the vegetable stock and simmer on a medium heat for 25 minutes.
8. Remove the bay leaf then add the watercress and cream and cook for a final 2 minutes before blitzing with a stick blender.
9. Finally, place the hazelnuts in a dry frying pan and toast on a medium heat for 2-3 minutes until they stat to turn golden brown.
10. Remove from the pan, allow to cool then roughly chop.
11. Serve the soup with some hazelnuts scattered on top and a few drops of lemon juice.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
