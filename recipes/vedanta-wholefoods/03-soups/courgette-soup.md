# Courgette Soup

Courgette Soup is a sattvic vegetarian dish of celery stalks, bay leaf, courgettes. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 50 min
- **Yield:** 4 People

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 2 celery stalks
- 1 bay leaf
- 3 courgettes
- 100 g frozen peas
- 1 litre vegetable stock
- A handful of parsley
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Finely and celery stalks and place in a pan with the olive oil.
2. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
3. Season generously with salt and pepper and fry on a medium heat for 5 minutes.
4. Cut the stalks from the parsley (keep the leaf aside for later), finely chop and add to the pan along with the bay leaf.
5. Cook for another 5 minutes.
6. Cut the courgettes into quarters lengthways and then finely across.
7. Set a quarter of the courgette aside and add the remainder to the pan.
8. Cook for 5 minutes and then add the vegetable stock.
9. Bring to the boil then turn down and simmer for 30 minutes.
10. Use a stick blender to thicken the soup slightly then add the remaining courgette and peas.
11. Simmer for a final 3 minutes.
12. Finely chop the parsley leaf, add to the pan then serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
