# Buckwheat and Hemp Porridge

Buckwheat and Hemp Porridge is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic buckwheat flakes, organic ceylon cinnamon powder, plant milk. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Breakfast, Lunch, Snack
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 0 min
- **Yield:** 1

## Ingredients

- 60 g organic buckwheat flakes
- 1 tsp organic ceylon cinnamon powder
- 50 ml plant milk
- 200 ml water
- 1 tbsp organic hemp protein powder
- 1 tsp organic dried mixed berries
- 1 tsp organic raw hazelnuts
- 1 tsp organic coconut chips
- 1 tbsp organic chia seeds
- 1 tbsp organic sultanas

## Method

1. Simmer the buckwheat flakes with water, plant milk and cinnamon until creamy, about 8 minutes.
2. Stir in the hemp powder.
3. Spoon into a bowl and top with berries, hazelnuts, coconut chips, chia and sultanas.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
