# Fruit and Nut Granola

Fruit and Nut Granola is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around sunflower or vegetable oil, organic gluten free jumbo oats, organic pecan nuts. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Dessert, Side Dish, Snack
- **Cuisine:** American
- **Prep:** 30 min
- **Cook:** 25 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** nuts

## Ingredients

- 100 ml sunflower or vegetable oil
- 500 g organic gluten free jumbo oats
- 200 g organic pecan nuts
- 200 g organic walnut pieces
- 100 g organic brazil nuts pieces
- 100 g organic sunflower seeds
- 100 g organic pumpkin seeds
- 200 ml agave syrup
- 200 g organic chopped dates

## Method

1. Place all of the ingredients in a baking tray and mix throughly.
2. Bake in an oven at 200c for 25-30 minutes (or until golden brown) mixing the ingredients every 4-5 minutes so they don’t burn.
3. Take out of the oven, stir through the chopped dates and allow to cool.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
