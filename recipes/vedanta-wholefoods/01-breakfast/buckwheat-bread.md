# Buckwheat Bread

Buckwheat Bread is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic blanched almond flour, organic buckwheat flour, organic chia seeds. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Breakfast, Lunch, Snack
- **Prep:** 20 min
- **Cook:** 75 min
- **Yield:** 8
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, nuts

## Ingredients

- 150 g organic blanched almond flour
- 140 g organic buckwheat flour
- 25 g organic chia seeds
- 3 tbsp organic psyllium husk
- 50 g organic seed mix
- 2 tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 2 tbsp maple syrup
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 500 ml warm water

## Method

1. Put all the dry ingredients together into a big bowl and mix it.
2. Add the liquids to the mixture and combine. (The “dough” has a creamy texture at the beginning, but do not add more flour to the mixture, as it will swell.).
3. Cover the mixture with a tea towel for about an hour.
4. Preheat the oven for 180C and line a loaf tin with baking paper.
5. Put the “dough” to the loaf tin and bake it for about 1 hour and 15 minutes.
6. Let the bread cool, and you can slice it.
7. Add any topping you like, we added guacamole.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
