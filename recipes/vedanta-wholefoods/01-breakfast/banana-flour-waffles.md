# Banana Flour Waffles

Banana Flour Waffles is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic self raising flour, organic banana flour, organic baking powder. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- 100 g organic self raising flour
- 50 g organic banana flour
- 1 tsp organic baking powder
- 2 teaspoon organic unrefined cane sugar
- 1 flax egg (1 tbsp ground flax + 3 tbsp water per egg)
- 240 ml oat milk
- 1 orange, zest and juice

## Method

1. 1. Preheat the waffle maker.
2. 2. Put the flours, baking power, sugar and orange zest into a large bowl.
3. 3. Add the milk, flax egg and juice from the orange into a measuring jug and whisk together.
4. 4. Gradually whisk in the liquid to the dry ingredients. Top tip: add the liquid in small amounts whisking well after each addition to avoid lumps.
5. 5. Grease the waffle irons and put some of the batter into the waffle machine and cook as per waffle maker instructions.
6. 6. Remove once cooked add your toppings and enjoy.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Each egg is replaced with a flax egg.
