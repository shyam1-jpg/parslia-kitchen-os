# Spiced Pecan Nut Butter

Spiced Pecan Nut Butter is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic pecans, organic ceylon cinnamon powder, sea salt. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Breakfast, Snack
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 5 min
- **Yield:** 10

## Ingredients

- 250 g organic pecans
- 1 tsp organic ceylon cinnamon powder
- 1/2 tsp sea salt

## Method

1. Gently toast pecan nuts in a dry pan over a low heat.
2. Allow to cool.
3. Place toasted nuts into a food processor.
4. Blend on high until smooth and silky.
5. Add cinnamon and salt.
6. Blend for a further few minutes.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
