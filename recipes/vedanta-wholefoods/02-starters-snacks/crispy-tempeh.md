# Crispy Tempeh

Crispy Tempeh is a sattvic vegetarian dish of organic sesame oil, crumbled tempeh, large thumb of ginger. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Side Dish, Snack
- **Cuisine:** Japanese, korean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** sesame, soya

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 1 tbsp organic sesame oil
- 200 g crumbled tempeh
- 1 large thumb of ginger
- 1 tbsp organic maple syrup
- 1 tsp chilli oil
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice

## Method

1. Remove the tempeh from its packaging and pat dry with kitchen paper removing as much moisture as possible then roughly crumble.
2. Take a wok or heavy bottomed frying pan and fry the tempeh in sesame oil on a high heat for 4-5 minutes until it starts to colour.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Grate a little ginger, peel and grate the ginger and add to the tempeh. Cook for a further 2 minutes.
5. Next add the maple syrup, chilli oil and lemon juice and fry for a final 4-5 minutes or until the tempeh becomes golden brown and crispy.
6. Serve over rice with steamed green vegetables.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
