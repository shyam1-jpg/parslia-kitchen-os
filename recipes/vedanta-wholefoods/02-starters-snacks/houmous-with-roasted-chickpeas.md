# Houmous With Roasted Chickpeas

Houmous With Roasted Chickpeas is a sattvic vegetarian dish of cooked chickpeas, dsp organic tahini, lemon juiced. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Side Dish, Snack
- **Cuisine:** moroccan
- **Prep:** 5 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 350 g cooked chickpeas
- 1 dsp organic tahini
- 1 lemon juiced
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 50 g organic walnuts
- 50 g organic pumpkin seeds
- 1 tsp organic paprika
- Chopped parsley
- Natural fine sea Salt
- Organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. Grate a little ginger and juice the lemon.
2. Add 250g of the chickpeas to a food processor along with the tahini, lemon juice, 1 clove of chopped ginger and 1 tbsp of olive oil.
3. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
4. Season with plenty of salt and pepper and blend until smooth.
5. Spoon out into a bowl and set aside.
6. Take a frying pan and fry the remaining 100g of chickpeas in 1 tbsp of olive oil for 3 minutes on a medium heat.
7. Add the walnuts and pumpkin seeds along with the paprika and 1 clove of chopped ginger and cook for a further 2 minutes.
8. Allow to cool slightly then sprinkle over the houmous along with a drizzle of olive oil and some chopped parsley.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
