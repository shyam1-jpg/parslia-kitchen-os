# Sesame Ginger Slaw

Sesame Ginger Slaw is a sattvic vegetarian dish of carrot, cabbage, organic edamame beans. No onion or garlic — hing bloomed in hot oil gives the savoury depth. Cook fresh and serve as prasad-style kitchen food.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Side Dish
- **Cuisine:** Asian
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 3 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, sesame, soya

## Ingredients

- ¼ tsp hing (asafoetida)
- 500 g carrot
- 500 g cabbage
- 100 g organic edamame beans, cooked
- 1 red pepper
- 8 radish
- 1/2 cucumber
- 50 g organic sesame seeds

### For the dressing:

- 1 thumb of ginger
- 1 lime
- 1 tsp organic brown sugar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp organic toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. To make the dressing, peel and finely grate the ginger and ginger and place in a mixing bowl.
2. Juice the lime and add to the bowl.
3. Add the sugar, soy sauce and oil and season with salt and pepper.
4. Warm a little ghee or olive oil and bloom the hing for a few seconds until fragrant, then continue.
5. Whisk together well and put aside.
6. Take a frying pan or wok, add the sesame seeds.
7. Roast on a medium heat until they are golden then remove from the pan. This should take 2-3 minutes.
8. Next, grate the carrot and finely shred the cabbage and place in a large mixing bowl along with the edamame.
9. Finely slice the red pepper, radish, coriander stems and cucumber and add to the bowl also.
10. Pour over the dressing, mix well and then top with the toasted sesame seeds.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Hing is bloomed in hot ghee or oil; a pinch is enough.
