# Nectarine and Quinoa Salad

Nectarine and Quinoa Salad is an egg-free vegetarian sweet for a Vedanta kitchen — built around organic quinoa, organic extra virgin olive oil, half a lemon juiced. Offer it fresh, without onion, garlic or eggs.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Salad, Side Dish
- **Cuisine:** world
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** dairy

## Ingredients

- 250 g organic quinoa
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- half a lemon juiced
- natural sea salt crystals
- organic cracked black pepper
- 1 cucumber finely diced
- 1 little gem or cos lettuce roughly chopped
- 2 nectarines finely diced
- 100 g walnuts roughly chopped
- 1 dsp organic maple syrup
- 3 tbsp yoghurt
- 1 tsp chopped mint
- half a lemon juiced
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- natural sea salt crystals
- organic cracked black pepper

## Method

1. For the dressing, stir the lemon juice, mint and salt and pepper into the yoghurt, then drizzle in the olive oil whilst still stirring.
2. Next, gently fry the nectarine and walnuts in olive oil for 2 minutes.
3. Add the maple syrup and fry for a further 2 minutes then put aside.
4. Place the cooked whole grains in a large bowl, add the olive oil, lemon juice, cucumber, coriander stems and lettuce then mix throughly.
5. To serve, divide the grains into 4 portions, place the nectarine and walnuts on top and drizzle with the dressing.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
