# Spinach And Olive Pasta Salad

Spinach And Olive Pasta Salad is a sattvic salad of large handfuls of spinach, organic fusilli, cherry tomatoes. Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten

## Ingredients

- 2 large handfuls of spinach
- 200 g organic fusilli
- 200 g cherry tomatoes
- 100 g black olives
- 1 tsp capers
- 50 g organic walnuts
- A large handful of basil
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. Cook the fusilli in salted water until just tender. Drain and cool slightly.
2. Whisk lemon juice, maple, olive oil, roasted cumin and grated ginger into a dressing.
3. Toss the pasta with spinach, tomatoes, olives, capers, walnuts and basil.
4. Dress, toss once more, and serve fresh.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
