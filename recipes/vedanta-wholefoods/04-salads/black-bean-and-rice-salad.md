# Black Bean and Rice Salad

Black Bean and Rice Salad is a sattvic salad of cooked organic brown rice, cooked organic black beans, cucumber. Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 20 min
- **Cook:** 0 min
- **Yield:** 4

## Ingredients

- 400 g cooked organic brown rice
- 200 g cooked organic black beans
- 1/2 cucumber
- 1 avocado
- 1/4 tsp organic chilli flakes
- 1/4 sliced fresh red chilli
- 100 g lamb lettuce
- 1 handful coriander

### For the dressing:

- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 lime
- 1 tsp organic brown sugar
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. Roughly chop the cucumber, coriander stems, avocado and coriander and place in a large mixing bowl.
2. Remove the seeds from the chilli, finely dice and add to the bowl along with the brown rice and black beans.
3. To make the dressing, finely dice the ginger and add to a bowl.
4. Juice the lime and add to the same bowl along with the sugar and salt and pepper.
5. Mix well and then whilst still mixing drizzle in the olive oil.
6. Pour the dressing over the rice and black beans and mix well.
7. Serve the salad over a bed of lambs lettuce.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
