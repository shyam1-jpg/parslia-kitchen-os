# Roasted Carrot and Parsnip Salad with Tahini

Roasted Carrot and Parsnip Salad with Tahini is a sattvic salad of organic extra virgin olive oil, carrots, parsnips. Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Lunch
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** sesame

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 kg carrots
- 1 kg parsnips
- 1 tbsp organic maple syrup
- 1 tbsp organic fresh lemon juice
- 50 g organic pumpkin seeds
- 25 g organic sesame seeds
- 200 g cherry tomatoes
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper

### For the dressing

- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp organic tahini
- 1 tsp organic maple syrup
- 1 tsp organic lemon juice with a drop of maple
- 1/2 lemon
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. Peel the carrots and parsnips, place in a large saucepan of cold salted water and bring to the boil.
2. Simmer for 5 minutes, drain and then allow to cool.
3. When cool, cut the carrots in half across and then into quarters. Repeat the process with the parsnips.
4. Place on a baking tray and drizzle with the olive oil.
5. Next, add the lemon juice, maple syrup and season with salt and pepper.
6. Roast at 220c for 30 minutes.
7. Remove the tray from the oven, add the pumpkin and sesame seeds and roast for a further 5 minutes.
8. Take the tray from the oven and set aside.
9. To make the dressing, place all of the ingredients (except the oil) into a bowl and whisk together.
10. Next, drizzle in the oil whilst still whisking.
11. Finely slice the tomatoes and fennel or celery.
12. To assemble the salad, place a large portion of the roasted veg on a plate, top with sliced tomato and fennel or celery, then drizzle with the dressing.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
