# Roasted Root Salad with Lemon-Tahini

Roasted Root Salad with Lemon-Tahini is a sattvic salad of carrots, parsnips, organic extra virgin olive oil. Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course, Side Dish
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 20 min
- **Cook:** 40 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** sesame

## Ingredients

- 4 carrots
- 4 parsnips
- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp organic maple syrup
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 50 g organic pumpkin seeds
- 25 g organic sesame seeds
- 100 g cherry tomatoes
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. A pinch of hing.
2. 1 tsp tahini.
3. 1 tsp maple syrup.
4. 1 tbsp lemon juice with a little maple.
5. Half a lemon juiced.
6. 2 tbsp olive oil.
7. Salt & pepper.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
