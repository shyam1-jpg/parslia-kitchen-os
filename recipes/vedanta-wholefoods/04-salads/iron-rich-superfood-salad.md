# Iron Rich Superfood Salad

Iron Rich Superfood Salad is a sattvic salad of tenderstem broccoli, organic kidney beans cooked, cooked edamame beans. Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Salad
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 10 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** nuts, sesame, mustard

## Ingredients

- 300 g tenderstem broccoli
- 150 g organic kidney beans cooked
- 150 g cooked edamame beans
- 50 g organic cashew nuts
- 50 g organic pumpkin seeds
- 25 g organic sesame seeds

### Dressing

- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp organic dijon mustard
- 1/2 lime
- 1/2 lemon
- handful of parsley
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. Cook the tenderstem broccoli in salted water for 2 minutes and then plunge into cold water to stop the cooking process. Strain and place aside.
2. Put the cashew nuts into a frying pan and roast on a medium to high heat until golden.
3. Repeat the process for the pumpkin seeds and then sesame seeds.
4. To make the dressing, juice the lemon and lime and place in a bowl.
5. Grate a little ginger and add to the bowl along with the mustard and salt and pepper.
6. Whisk together and then slowly drizzle in the oil olive while still whisking.
7. To assemble the salad, place the broccoli on a large serving plate, put the kidney and edamame beans on top.
8. Roughly chop the parsley and scatter over the salad along with the nuts and seeds.
9. Finally, drizzle over the dressing and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
