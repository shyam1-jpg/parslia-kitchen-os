# Lentil Potato and Rocket Salad

Lentil Potato and Rocket Salad is a sattvic salad of organic extra virgin olive oil, new or waxy potatoes, organic french lentils cooked. Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course, Salad, Snack
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 45 min
- **Yield:** 4 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** mustard

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 500 g new or waxy potatoes
- 250 g organic french lentils cooked
- 100 g rocket
- 1 tsp dijon mustard
- 1/2 lemon
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. Place the potatoes in a saucepan, cover with cold salted water and bring to the boil.
2. Turn down the heat, simmer for 10 minutes then drain and allow to cool.
3. Cut the potatoes into approximately 2cm pieces and place in a baking tray.
4. Drizzle with 1 tbsp of olive oil then season with salt and pepper.
5. Roast in an oven at 220c for 25-30 minutes until the potatoes are golden brown.
6. Finely chop 1 clove of ginger and add to the tray.
7. Add the dijon mustard, rocket and the juice of half a lemon.
8. Season generously with salt and pepper, mix well and serve while the potatoes are still warm.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
