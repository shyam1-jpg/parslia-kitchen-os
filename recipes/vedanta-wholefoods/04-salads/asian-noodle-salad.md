# Asian Noodle Salad

Asian Noodle Salad is a sattvic salad of organic gluten free brown rice noodles, radishes finely sliced, Half a cucumber diced in 1cm cubes. Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** dinner, Lunch, Side Dish
- **Cuisine:** Asian
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 5 min
- **Yield:** 2 people
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, sesame, soya

## Ingredients

- 150 g organic gluten free brown rice noodles
- 4 radishes finely sliced
- Half a cucumber diced in 1cm cubes
- 50 g Organic roasted peanuts chopped
- Handful of chopped coriander

### For the dressing:

- 1 small thumb ginger grated
- 1 tsp organic unrefined cane sugar
- 1 lime juiced
- 1 tbs organic tamari soy sauce
- 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp organic sesame oil
- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. To make the dressing, combine all of the ingredients except the oil and whisk together until the sugar has dissolved.
2. Now slowly drizzle in the oil whilst still whisking.
3. Cook the noodles according to packet instructions then drain and run under cold water. Drain again and pat dry.
4. In a large bowl, add the noodles, radishes, coriander stems and cucumber.
5. Combine all ingredients together then pour over the dressing.
6. Mix again and serve topped with roasted peanuts and coriander.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
