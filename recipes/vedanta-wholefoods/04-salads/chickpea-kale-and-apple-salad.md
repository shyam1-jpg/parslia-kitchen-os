# Chickpea, Kale and Apple Salad

Chickpea, Kale and Apple Salad is a sattvic salad of organic extra virgin olive oil, organic chickpeas (cooked), organic unblanched almonds. Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Appetizer, Lunch, Main Course, Salad, Side Dish, Snack
- **Cuisine:** American, Chinese, French, Italian, Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 0 min
- **Yield:** 4 people

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 100 g organic chickpeas (cooked)
- 30 g organic unblanched almonds
- 30 g organic pumpkin seeds
- 4 radish finely sliced
- 1 apple finely sliced
- 200 g kale stalks removed and chopped

### For the dressing:

- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 organic light raw tahini
- 2 tsp organic maple syrup
- 2 tsp organic lemon juice with a drop of maple
- 3 tbsp organic lemon juice
- fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. Fry the chickpeas, almonds and pumpkin seeds in the olive oil for 5 minutes and put aside.
2. In a bowl, add all of the dressing ingredients except the oil and whisk. Next slowly drizzle in the olive oil whilst still whisking.
3. Place the kale in a large bowl and massage for 2-3 minutes to soften.
4. Place the sliced radish, apple and fennel or celery on top along with the fried chickpeas, almonds and pumpkin seeds.
5. Drizzle with the dressing and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
