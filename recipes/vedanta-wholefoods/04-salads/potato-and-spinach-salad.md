# Potato and Spinach Salad

Potato and Spinach Salad is a sattvic salad of new potatoes, organic chickpeas (cooked), celery sticks. Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Main Course
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 15 min
- **Cook:** 20 min
- **Yield:** 4 People

## Ingredients

- 500 g new potatoes
- 240 g organic chickpeas (cooked)
- 3 celery sticks, finely sliced
- 2 large handfuls baby spinach

### For the dressing:

- 3 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tbsp mayonnaise, or plant-based alternative
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. Bring a pan of salted water to the boil and cook the new potatoes for around 15 to 20 minutes until just tender.
2. Drain well and leave them to cool slightly then cut in half.
3. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice and mayo until smooth.
4. Season generously with salt and black pepper.
5. In a large bowl, combine the warm potatoes with the chickpeas, celery, coriander stems and spinach.
6. Pour over the dressing while the potatoes are still a little warm so they soak up the flavour.
7. Toss well until everything is coated and serve.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
