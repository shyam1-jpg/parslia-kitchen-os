# Plant-Based Caesar Salad

Plant-Based Caesar Salad is a sattvic salad of organic extra virgin olive oil, organic tempeh, organic chickpeas (cooked). Alliums are left out; lemon, ginger and roasted cumin carry the dressing.

- **Diet:** vegetarian, sattvic, no onion, no garlic, egg-free
- **Course:** Salad
- **Cuisine:** Mediterranean
- **Prep:** 10 min
- **Cook:** 15 min
- **Yield:** 4 People
- **Allergens (estimate):** gluten, soya

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 200 g organic tempeh
- 100 g organic chickpeas (cooked)
- 100 g sourdough bread
- 2 large cos lettuce

### For the dressing:

- 2 tbsp organic extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp organic mayonnaise
- 1 lemon
- natural fine sea salt
- organic cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
- ½ tsp fresh ginger, grated

## Method

1. Cut the tempeh into roughly 0.5cm slices then place in a frying pan with 1 tbsp of olive oil.
2. Cook on a medium heat for 2 minutes on each side then add the chickpeas.
3. Continue to cook for a further 2-3 minutes until the tempeh is golden then remove from the pan.
4. Cut the sourdough into approximately 1cm piece then place in the same pan with 1 tbsp of olive oil.
5. Fry on a medium to high heat until the bead becomes golden. Remove from the pan.
6. To make the dressing, place all of the ingredients in a bowl (except the oil) and whisk well.
7. Slowly drizzle in the olive oil whilst still whisking.
8. Cut across the cos lettuce in roughly 2cm slices and finely chop the coriander stems.
9. To serve, place a large handful of the cos in a bowl, top with a portion on the tempeh and chickpeas.
10. Scatter across some of the coriander stems, place a few pieces of sourdough on top then drizzle generously with the dressing.

## Vedanta kitchen notes

- Vedanta kitchen: vegetarian, no onion, no garlic, no eggs, no mushrooms, no alcohol.
- Use stainless steel, cast iron, glass or clay — never aluminium.
- Cold dressings use lemon, ginger and roasted cumin instead of garlic.
